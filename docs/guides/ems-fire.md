# EMS, fire and hazardous materials

Three [toolkits](toolkits.md) let any exercise bring in a realistic street response: **Mass casualty and triage**, **Hazardous materials** and **Dispatch and command traffic**. The **EMS, fire and hazardous materials** kind of organization (EMS agency, fire department, hazmat team, dispatch center, healthcare coalition liaison) suggests all three. A chemical plant, a stadium, a railroad or a hospital can turn them on too.

Everything here is exercise material. It is not clinical guidance, hazmat guidance or fire tactics for a real incident, and every patient, hospital and agency is fictional.

## Mass casualty and triage

- **Products:** triage tag, patient list, Medical Branch worksheet, treatment areas, transportation log and a hospital call record. Every priority is printed as a word (Immediate, Delayed, Minor, Expectant) with a pattern, so nothing depends on color.
- **Triage method:** name the method your players use (START, SALT or your local method) when you set up the scenario. No single method is required everywhere, so the exercise does not pick one for you.
- **Charts:** patients tagged by priority over time, a patient tracking board and a hospital capacity board, each with a text summary and a table.
- **Decisions:** declare an MCI, set the triage method, report triage complete, assign a patient to a hospital, open or close a hospital, and issue a public statement.
- **Evaluation:** time to triage complete, patients sent to a hospital that can treat their injuries, and whether any hospital was sent more than it said it could take. Message consistency checks the casualty counts that command, the PIO and the hospitals gave.

## Hazardous materials

- **Products:** placard, shipping paper, entry team briefing, air monitoring log, decontamination corridor, chemical emergency call record, and shelter in place or evacuation orders and recommendations.
- **Isolation and protective action map:** a plan that draws the isolation circle and the downwind protective action square from the wind, with a scale, the places near the release and a table of the numbers.
- **Guidebook distances:** the EMS, fire and hazmat reference library carries the 2024 Emergency Response Guidebook, but its distances are not copied into exercise data. Type the distances from the guidebook and name its edition, or use a fictional material with invented distances. Every order and map says which. Players use their own guidebook for real decisions.
- **Decisions:** set isolation and protective actions (with the area drawn on the map), and set the protective level and authorize entry.
- **Evaluation:** whether the distances players set match the scenario's, and whether the action was in place before the plume reached each place.

## Dispatch and command traffic

- **Products:** incident detail, unit status board, pending calls, radio traffic across dispatch, command and tactical channels, station alerting and mutual aid requests, shown from the responders' side. The 911 center's own view of a dispatch failure belongs to the 911 and emergency communications kind of organization.
- **Decisions:** call for mutual aid, transfer command (from, to, time and briefing), and declare a mayday.
- **Evaluation:** command transfers that were briefed and announced, and how quickly a mayday was answered.

## Simulated incidents

On a scenario's case data you can generate three kinds of simulated data, always labeled simulated:

- **A mass casualty incident:** patients whose findings your chosen triage method sorts into the mix you ask for (for example 28 patients: 4 Immediate, 9 Delayed, 12 Minor, 3 Expectant), when each is reached, triaged, treated and transported, and the best hospital for each within what the hospitals said they can take.
- **A hazmat release:** the zones for each stretch of wind, so a wind shift moves the protective action square, and when the plume front would reach each place (distance divided by wind speed; not a dispersion model).
- **A dispatch call load:** calls per minute with an optional surge, drawn from a mix of call types.

Each also writes the ground truth the evaluation measures compare your players' decisions with.
