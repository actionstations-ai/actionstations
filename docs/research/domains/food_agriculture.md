## 1. Scope

- **Covers:** row-crop and specialty-crop farms, livestock and poultry operations (feedlots, dairies, ranches), agricultural cooperatives (grain elevators, farm supply: seed, fertilizer, fuel, crop protection), grain merchandisers and feed mills, meat and poultry slaughter / processing plants (USDA FSIS jurisdiction), food manufacturers and processors (FDA jurisdiction), cold storage and food distribution centers, dairy processors, and the agricultural input supply chain. Restaurants and grocery retail are covered from the retail side in `commercial_facilities`.
- **Aliases users might type:** farm, ranch, agriculture, ag, agribusiness, dairy, feedlot, poultry, grain elevator, co-op, cooperative, farmer cooperative, grain, feed mill, food processor, food plant, meatpacking, packing plant, slaughter plant, food manufacturer, beverage plant, cold storage, food distribution, produce packer, fertilizer, seed.
- **CISA sector:** **Food and Agriculture Sector**. **Co-Sector Risk Management Agencies: U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) and the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), through FDA.** _The 2015 Food and Agriculture Sector-Specific Plan (fetched) is authored by FDA, USDA and DHS; search snippets of CISA's sector page used the "co-Sector Risk Management Agencies" wording; the current page text fetched this session does not restate it._
- National policy: **NSM-16** _Strengthening the Security and Resilience of United States Food and Agriculture_ (Nov 2022) (_FDA hosts a page on it; text not read this session_).
- Dependencies: Water (processing water, wastewater permits), Energy (refrigeration, grain drying propane), Transportation (rail, truck, barge), Chemical (anhydrous ammonia for refrigeration and fertilizer).

## 3. Systems

**IT / business**

- ERP and accounting: SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, JD Edwards; ag-specific: Agvance, AgTrax, Merchant Ag (grain accounting, agronomy dispatch, patronage).
- Grain / scale systems: truck scales and scale-house software, grain grading (moisture/test-weight analyzers), bin inventory, grain contracts and futures hedging links.
- Food plants: manufacturing execution system (MES), warehouse management (WMS), lot/batch traceability, label printing, LIMS (lab results), HACCP / food-safety records software, supplier approval portals, EDI with retailers.
- Farm: precision-ag platforms (John Deere Operations Center, Climate FieldView), farm management software, telematics on tractors and combines, autonomous/robotic milking systems with cloud dashboards.
- Corporate: email, identity, payroll (seasonal labor), EDI/customer portals, MSP-managed networks at small co-ops.

**OT / ICS**

- PLC / HMI-controlled processing lines (Allen-Bradley, Siemens), batching and mixing systems, pasteurizers (HTST) with recorder-controllers, clean-in-place (CIP) systems, metal detectors and X-ray inspection, checkweighers.
- **Ammonia refrigeration** systems (compressors, evaporators, ammonia detectors, emergency shutdown).
- Grain elevator controls: bucket elevators, conveyors, dryer controls (propane/natural gas), bin temperature cables, aeration fans, dust-collection and hazard monitors (bearing temperature, belt alignment).
- Feed mill batching (micro-ingredient dosing), pellet mills.
- Livestock environment controllers (barn ventilation, heating, feed and water systems with alarm dialers), milking parlor controls.
- Wastewater pretreatment (DAF), boilers, compressed air, CCTV, access control.

**Identifiers the documents state (candidate `factCategories`)**

- _new_ `lines`: "Line 3", "Packaging Line 2" (noun Line; lint true, complete true for a plant).
- _new_ `rooms` / `areas`: "Room 104", "Blast Freezer 2", "Cooler 5", "Kill Floor" (lint false).
- _new_ `elevators` / `sites`: co-op locations "Elevator 4", "Site 12", town-named locations (lint true when listed).
- _new_ `bins` / `silos` / `tanks`: "Bin 17", "Silo 3", "Tank 6" (noun Bin|Silo|Tank; lint true).
- _new_ `barns` / `pens` / `houses`: "Barn 2", "House 6" (poultry), "Pen 41" (lint false).
- _new_ `establishment_numbers`: USDA establishment numbers "Est. 12345" / "EST. P-1234" (lint true; must be fictional).
- _new_ `lots`: production lot codes (lint false; free-form).

## 6. Common operational workflows

**Intentional adulteration suspected at a processor**: employee or monitoring finds a tamper indicator (broken seal at an actionable process step, unexplained access) → line stopped, product on hold → Food Defense Coordinator and FSQA convene, apply **121.145 corrective actions** (contain product, find cause, re-establish mitigation) → law enforcement (local police, FBI WMD coordinator) and FDA or FSIS notified → if product is in commerce: **FSIS within 24 h (9 CFR 418.2)** or **Reportable Food Registry within 24 h** (FDA-regulated) → recall committee, customer notices, press release → public health and CDC if illnesses. (FREE-B _Insider Addition_; 21 CFR 121.)

**Ransomware at an agricultural cooperative during harvest**: scale-house and grain accounting systems down → elevators revert to paper scale tickets or stop receiving → farmers queue with trucks; grain spoilage and contract-delivery risk → GM decides on ransom with insurer and counsel (OFAC screening) → FBI, CISA, Food and Ag-ISAC → member communications and local ag radio → grain inventory reconciliation for the warehouse regulator. (FBI PIN 20220420-001; FBI PIN 20210901-001.)

**Ammonia release**: detector alarm → evacuate, ESD → fire/hazmat → NRC / SERC / LEPC notifications if above RQ → OSHA (hospitalization within 24 h, fatality within 8 h under 29 CFR 1904.39, _not re-verified_) → RMP accident history.

**Foreign animal disease suspicion** (e.g., FMD or HPAI-like signs): herd veterinarian → state veterinarian and USDA → quarantine and movement standstill → depopulation, carcass disposal → indemnity → biosecurity for neighbors; intentional-introduction suspicion brings FBI. (FREE-B _High Plains Harbinger_.)

**Recall** (FDA-regulated): health hazard evaluation → recall strategy (class, depth) → firm press release → FDA posting → effectiveness checks → close-out. FSIS recalls follow FSIS Directive 8080.1 (_not read_).

**Forms used**: ICS 201, 202, 205, 213, 214; food defense monitoring and corrective-action records (21 CFR 121 subpart D); FSIS Form 5420-5 (General Food Defense Plan); FDA Reportable Food Registry electronic report (Safety Reporting Portal); FDA Form 3500 (MedWatch) for consumer complaints _(not verified)_; recall notification letters; scale tickets; bills of lading; animal movement permits; APHIS/CDC Form 3; OSHA 301 / 300; HSEEP AAR/IP.

## 7. Exercise scenarios (seeds)

1. **Cyber (co-op)**: ransomware hits a grain cooperative's scale-house and accounting systems in the first week of harvest; trucks back up onto a state highway and the regional ethanol plant asks about deliveries (FBI PIN 20220420-001: six grain cooperatives attacked during the fall 2021 harvest, two early-2022 attacks threatening planting supply).
2. **Cyber (processor)**: ransomware on a meat processor's corporate network forces plant shutdowns across several states; FSIS inspectors on site, retailers asking about supply (FBI PIN 20210901-001; public reporting of 2021 processor ransomware; no victim named).
3. **Insider / intentional adulteration**: disgruntled employee adds a chemical to a liquid ingredient tank at an actionable process step (FREE-B _Insider Addition_; 21 CFR 121 vulnerability assessment).
4. **Cyber-physical**: a compromised remote-access tool at a feed mill changes micro-ingredient dosing setpoints; elevated levels found after cattle sicken (FREE-B _Foul Fodder_ for feed contamination; CISA OT remote-access advisories).
5. **Animal disease / agroterrorism**: suspected foreign animal disease at a feedlot with signs of deliberate introduction; movement standstill in the region (FREE-B _High Plains Harbinger_).
6. **Mass gathering**: foodborne illness cluster after a county fair or large event traced to one vendor and one supplier lot (FREE-B _Mass Mayhem_; CTEP _Food and Agriculture Supply_).
7. **Natural hazard**: derecho flattens bins and knocks out power across a co-op's 12 elevator sites during harvest; grain dryers offline (NWS derecho events; no specific co-op named).
8. **Infrastructure failure**: ammonia refrigeration compressor failure plus a valve leak at a cold-storage warehouse at night; product temperature climbing and a hazmat response (EPA RMP / OSHA PSM context; CTEP _Food Manufacturing Facility_).
9. **Physical security**: activists or trespassers enter a poultry operation at night and post video; biosecurity breach during HPAI season (FSIS/APHIS biosecurity guidance; no farm named).
10. **UAS**: drones observed over feedlots and a processing plant over several nights; pilots unknown, possible reconnaissance (FBI/CISA UAS awareness material; public reports of unexplained drone sightings over farms).
