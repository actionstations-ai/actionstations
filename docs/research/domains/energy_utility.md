An existing profile is at `src/shared/domains/profiles/energy_utility.ts` (roles: ops, it, pio, security, field; aliases include `gas`). With a separate `gas_pipeline` domain proposed, this profile should become electric-only: drop the `gas` alias and the gas wording in its description.

## 1. Scope

**Covers:** electric distribution utilities (investor-owned, municipal, cooperative), vertically integrated utilities with generation and transmission, transmission owners/operators, generation owners, and their control centres. RTO/ISO and reliability coordinators appear as outside parties.

**Aliases:** electric utility, power company, power utility, electric cooperative, co-op, rural electric, municipal electric, muni, public power district, PUD, IOU, transmission operator, distribution utility, grid operator, power plant, generation, substation, T&D, the grid.

**CISA sector:** Energy Sector (electricity subsector). **SRMA:** Department of Energy **[U: per NSM-22]**. Regulators: FERC (bulk power system reliability via NERC), state public utility commissions (distribution, retail service), NRC for nuclear generation.

## 3. Systems

### IT / business

- Customer information system and billing (CIS), meter data management (MDM)
- Outage management system (OMS) and public outage map, IVR
- GIS of the network, work management / mobile workforce management
- Enterprise asset management, ERP, email, SIEM, identity (AD)
- Energy trading and settlement (if applicable)

### OT / ICS

- Energy Management System (EMS) with SCADA, AGC, state estimator (e.g. GE, Hitachi Energy, Siemens, OSI)
- Advanced Distribution Management System (ADMS) / DMS, DERMS
- Substation automation: RTUs, protective relays (e.g. SEL), IEDs, data concentrators; protocols DNP3, IEC 61850, ICCP between control centres
- AMI head-end and smart meters (with remote connect/disconnect), field area network
- Distribution automation: reclosers, capacitor banks, FLISR
- Generation DCS and plant controls (if applicable)
- Backup control centre (NERC EOP-008), telecom (microwave, fibre, private LTE), GPS time sources
- Physical: substation PACS, perimeter fencing, CCTV, gunshot detection, transformer oil and temperature monitoring

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Cyber on EMS/SCADA:** anomaly (unexpected breaker operations, operator lose view) → conservative operations, notify RC → fail to backup control centre or manual operation of substations with field crews → CIP-008 IR plan → determination → E-ISAC + CISA within 1 hour → OE-417 Emergency Alert within 1 hour if criteria met → FBI → restoration from known-good configuration.
- **Physical attack on a substation:** alarms (transformer low-oil, relay trips, gunshot detection) → dispatch with police escort → OE-417 (criterion 1 or 16) → RC coordination for switching → mobile transformer / spare → JTTF.
- **Capacity emergency / cold weather:** RC issues Energy Emergency Alert levels → conservation appeal (OE-417 criterion 9) → rotating outages with critical-load exclusion → OE-417 for firm load shed ≥100 MW.
- **Major storm:** pre-staging, ICS activation, damage assessment, ETRs, mutual-aid crews, restoration priorities (transmission → substations → critical facilities → feeders → laterals → services).
- **Forms:** ICS 201/202/203/205/213/214/215; DOE-417; NERC EOP-004 Attachment 2 event report form **[U]**; switching orders; clearance / tag records; damage assessment forms; mutual-aid resource request.

## 7. Exercise scenarios

1. **cyber_physical:** Remote takeover of distribution SCADA opens breakers at several substations; operators lose control; firmware wiped on serial-to-Ethernet converters (pattern: Dec 2015 Ukraine grid attack **[U]**).
2. **cyber:** State actor pre-positioned in IT and OT networks via an edge device; discovery during a heat wave; whether to isolate and operate manually (pattern: CISA advisory AA24-038A on pre-positioning **[U]**; AA22-103A ICS tool capabilities **[V]**).
3. **physical_security:** Gunfire disables two distribution substations at night; 40,000 customers out for days (pattern: Dec 2022 North Carolina substation attacks **[U]**; CISA Electric Substation Sabotage CTEP **[V title]**).
4. **natural_hazard:** Winter storm with generation outages; RC directs firm load shed; critical customers (water plants, hospitals) on rotating circuits (pattern: Feb 2021 Winter Storm Uri; Dec 2022 Winter Storm Elliott **[U]**).
5. **insider:** Contract relay technician with standing access changes protection settings before leaving the company.
6. **infrastructure_failure:** Loss of the primary control centre (fire suppression discharge); move to backup control centre under EOP-008.
7. **uas_drone:** Drone with a conductive tether found near a substation fence (pattern: public DHS/FBI reporting of a 2020 Pennsylvania substation drone **[U]**).
8. **mass_gathering:** Stadium event and a heat dome; feeder overloads near the venue; a mobile generator request from the venue operator.
9. **cyber:** Ransomware in the CIS/OMS: no outage map, no ETRs, call-centre overload while the grid is unaffected; misinformation that "hackers turned off the power".
10. **natural_hazard:** Severe geomagnetic storm watch from NOAA SWPC; transformer heating and conservative operations.
