# Domain dossier: `commercial_facilities` (new: retail, lodging, casino, mall)

Researched 2026-10-24. Every URL in §9 was fetched in this session with a ranged GET (HTTP 206); type and size come from `Content-Range`, and page counts and dates come from `pdfinfo`/`pdftotext`. Regulation text came from the eCFR versioner API and, for Nevada gaming, from the adopted regulation PDF on gaming.nv.gov.

Boundary: stadiums, arenas, convention centers and outdoor events are `stadium_venue`. This domain is for **places people shop, stay and gamble**: enclosed and open-air malls, big-box and grocery retail, hotels and resorts, casinos (commercial and tribal), integrated resorts, mixed-use towers, and their corporate security / loss-prevention operations.

---

## 1. Scope

- **Covers:** regional malls and lifestyle centers (owner/manager plus tenants), big-box and department stores, grocery chains, convenience-store chains, distribution centers serving retail (warehouse operations only), hotels (full-service, select-service, resorts, timeshare), casinos and integrated resorts (gaming floor, hotel towers, showrooms, restaurants), office and mixed-use buildings run by property managers.
- **Aliases users might type:** retail, store, big box, mall, shopping center, outlet, grocery, supermarket, department store, hotel, motel, resort, lodging, hospitality, casino, gaming, integrated resort, tribal casino, card room, racino, property management, office tower, mixed-use.
- **CISA sector:** **Commercial Facilities Sector** (eight subsectors per CISA's Sector Profile, Aug 2026: Entertainment and Media, Gaming, Lodging, Outdoor Events, Public Assembly, Real Estate, Retail, Sports Leagues). **SRMA: CISA.** _Profile fetched; subsector names read from its layout._
- Overlaps: payment-card data (Financial Services dependencies), Food and Agriculture (grocery, restaurants), Healthcare (in-store pharmacies), Transportation (distribution).

## 3. Systems

**IT / business**

- Retail: point-of-sale (Oracle Xstore/MICROS, NCR, Toshiba), payment terminals and processor links (PCI scope), e-commerce, inventory / warehouse management, loyalty program, gift-card systems, self-checkout, electronic shelf labels, store Wi-Fi.
- Lodging: property management system (Oracle OPERA, Infor HMS), central reservations and channel managers, guest Wi-Fi, keycard / door-lock system (ASSA ABLOY Vostio, dormakaba), call accounting / PBX, F&B POS, spa/event booking.
- Casino: casino management system (CMS: slot accounting and player tracking, e.g., IGT Advantage, Konami SYNKROS, Aristocrat Oasis), table games management, cage and count systems, kiosks (ticket-in/ticket-out redemption), loyalty, sportsbook / mobile wagering platforms, AML/Title 31 compliance software.
- Common: identity provider and help desk (social-engineering target), email, ERP / finance, HR / payroll, SIEM / MDR.

**OT / building**

- CCTV / VMS (surveillance rooms with regulator-mandated retention in casinos), PACS and employee badges, elevators and escalators, fire alarm and mass notification / PA, BAS / HVAC, parking garage gates and LPR, loading-dock doors, refrigeration and freezer monitoring (grocery), slot machines on the slot data network, EAS anti-theft gates, panic buttons (hotel staff; several cities require them for housekeepers — _local ordinances, not verified_).

## 6. Common operational workflows

**Active assailant in a mall or store**: 911 and security radio → security dispatch locks doors / pulls PA message (Run-Hide-Fight-style "Avoid, Deny, Defend" language varies by program) → tenants close gates and shelter → police in unified command → casualty collection, guest reunification point (often an outlying lot) → tenant accountability call-down → media staging → recovery: crime-scene hold, tenant reopening plan, employee assistance. (CISA _Active Assailant EAP Instructional Guide_; CISA _Active Shooter Recovery Guide_ 2017.)

**Casino / hotel cyberattack (social-engineering help-desk reset → identity takeover → ransomware on hypervisors)**: SOC detection → CISO declares incident → isolate identity, disable affected accounts → **Nevada: notify the Board Chair within 24 h of activating the IR plan**, 5-day initial report, 30-day updates → FBI/CISA → guest operations fall back to manual: keycards, check-in on paper, slot floor down or "hand-pay only", ATM/cage cash handling → SEC 8-K decision if public → guest notices under state breach law. (CISA/FBI advisory AA23-320A _Scattered Spider_, which describes help-desk social engineering against commercial facilities and gaming.)

**POS / payment-card compromise**: processor or card-brand common-point-of-purchase alert → engage PFI under PCI rules → contain (isolate POS segment), preserve evidence → decide whether to take lanes offline (cash-only) → USSS/FBI → state AG and customer notices.

**Organized retail crime / flash mob theft**: store team follows non-confrontation policy → police → LP compiles video and loss totals → ORC task force → social-media spread drives reputational pressure.

**Hotel guest emergencies**: fire alarm in a tower → fire department, elevators recalled, PA in floors → front desk prints in-house guest list for accountability → relocation to other properties (brand reservations team).

**Forms used**: ICS 201, 202, 205, 213, 214; security incident report; trespass notice; LP case file; casino surveillance log and regulator incident report; FinCEN SAR; Nevada _Initial Cybersecurity Incident Response_ form (Board form, referenced in Reg 5.260); PCI incident report to acquirer; HSEEP AAR/IP.

## 7. Exercise scenarios (seeds)

1. **Cyber (casino/hotel)**: help-desk social engineering gives an attacker admin access; hypervisors are encrypted, slot floor and keycards fail on a holiday weekend (CISA/FBI AA23-320A _Scattered Spider_; Nevada Reg 5.260 timelines).
2. **Cyber (retail)**: POS malware at a regional chain discovered via a card-brand CPP alert during holiday season; e-commerce stays up but stores go cash-only (USSS/FBI POS malware advisories; pattern of large 2013–2014 retail breaches; no retailer named).
3. **Physical security (mall)**: active shooter in a food court on a Saturday; tenants with rolling gates, a movie theater and a children's play area (CTEP _Commercial Facilities_, _Large Box Retail Store_; USSS NTAC _Mass Attacks in Public Spaces 2016–2020_).
4. **Insider (casino)**: a cage employee colludes with an outside group to launder cash through chip walking; a surveillance operator deletes footage (FinCEN SAR rule; CTEP _Gaming Industry_ insider threads).
5. **Workplace violence (retail)**: a terminated employee returns to a grocery store and threatens staff; prior warning signs were reported (CISA _Pathway to Violence_ fact sheet 2025; CISA _De-escalation Series_).
6. **Vehicle ramming**: vehicle drives into a crowded outdoor holiday market at an open-air shopping center (CISA _Vehicle Ramming Action Guide_ 2024).
7. **Cyber-physical (hotel)**: keycard system compromise allows room access; guest in a high floor reports an intruder while the PMS is also down (vendor advisories on hotel lock systems; no hotel named).
8. **Natural hazard**: hurricane landfall with a full resort, guests unable to fly out, generator fuel running low, and a shelter-in-place decision for 4,000 guests (CISA _Venue Guide for Mitigating Dependency Disruptions_ 2025).
9. **Infrastructure failure**: regional power outage on Black Friday; generators run life safety only, refrigeration fails in grocery tenants, crowd frustration at a mall entrance (FEMA/NWS outage patterns).
10. **Bomb threat / swatting**: emailed bomb threats to multiple malls in a region at opening time (FBI public statements on hoax threat waves; CISA OBP _Bomb Threat Guide_, _not fetched_).
