# Domain dossier: `elections` (county or state election office) — NEW

Researched 2026-10-24. "Verified" means checked against the primary source this session; anything else is marked unverified. Political neutrality matters for this pack: scenarios use fictional jurisdictions and never name real candidates, parties or a real election's outcome.

## 1. Scope

- **Covers:** county / municipal election offices (county clerk, registrar of voters, board of elections, supervisor of elections, election commission), state election offices (Secretary of State elections division, state board of elections), and their facilities: warehouse, central count / tabulation center, vote centers and polling places, ballot drop boxes, and the election-night reporting site.
- **Aliases users might type:** elections, election office, board of elections, BOE, county clerk, registrar, registrar of voters, ROV, supervisor of elections, SOE, secretary of state, SOS, election administration, vote center, polling place, voting.
- **CISA sector:** Government Services and Facilities Sector, **Election Infrastructure Subsector** (DHS designated election infrastructure as critical infrastructure in January 2017; designation date from public DHS statements, not re-fetched this session).
- **Sector Risk Management Agency:** CISA (DHS). The U.S. Election Assistance Commission (EAC) is the federal clearinghouse and certifies voting systems against the VVSG. Note: in 2025 CISA reduced election-security staffing and federal funding for the EI-ISAC ended (secondary reporting; not verified against CISA primary statements this session). The CISA _topics/election-security_ page still resolves (200), but its resource listing exposes no PDF links. Newer documents come mostly through the EAC (the EAC pages list CISA and FBI co-products).
- **Overlap:** `municipal` (the county runs the office), `emergency_communications` (swatting, polling-place 911), `public_health` (white-powder mail response).

## 3. Systems

**IT:**

- Statewide voter registration database (VRDB), plus county election management system (EMS) and voter registration front end.
- Electronic pollbooks (e-pollbooks) at vote centers: check-in, ballot-style lookup, network sync.
- Online voter registration portal and "My Voter Info" / polling-place lookup.
- Election-night reporting (ENR) / results website.
- Ballot-on-demand printers; ballot design / layout software.
- Mail-ballot tracking (e.g. BallotTrax-type notifications), signature verification software.
- Email, county network, AD, phone hotline / call center, social media accounts.
- GIS / district mapping (precinct boundaries, redistricting).

**Voting system / OT-like (federally certified under the EAC's VVSG program):**

- Precinct-count optical scanners (tabulators), central-count high-speed scanners.
- Ballot-marking devices (BMDs) / accessible voting units.
- EMS server (air-gapped per most procedures), removable media (USB / memory cards), results upload workstation.
- Seals, seal logs, video surveillance of tabulation room, access control at warehouse / central count.
- Drop boxes with cameras.

**Identifiers seen in election documents (fact categories):**

- **Precincts** (`Precinct 0412`, `Pct 17-B`): proposed `precincts`, alnum, lint yes, complete yes.
- **Polling places / vote centers** (`Vote Center 12 – Riverside Library`): proposed `vote_centers`, alnum, lint yes.
- **Drop boxes** (`Drop Box 7`): proposed `drop_boxes`, number, lint yes.
- **Tabulators / devices** (serial-like `DS200-0457`, `BMD 3`): proposed `devices`, token, not linted.
- **Seal numbers** (`Seal #A0048213`): token, scene detail.
- **Ballot styles** (`BS-034`): token.
- **Districts / contests** (`District 4`, `Measure B`): reuse `districts`; contests fictional only.

## 4. Outside parties and media

- **Federal:** CISA (regional election security advisor / Cybersecurity Advisor; services), EAC (clearinghouse, VVSG, HAVA funds), FBI (election crimes coordinator in each field office; IC3), USPS / U.S. Postal Inspection Service (mail ballots, suspicious mail), DOJ Civil Rights Division (Voting Section), FCC (robocalls), DHS I&A, National Guard (via the state).
- **State / local:** Secretary of State / state board, state fusion center, county IT, county sheriff / police, county EM, fire / hazmat (suspicious mail), courts (hours extension), county board of supervisors / commissioners.
- **Vendors:** voting-system vendor (tabulators, EMS, BMDs), e-pollbook vendor, ballot printer, mail house, ENR hosting provider, VR system vendor, cloud / CDN, telecom.
- **Observers / stakeholders:** party observers and poll watchers (generic, no real parties), candidates (fictional), voting-rights and disability-rights groups, academic observers, the press.
- **ISAC:** EI-ISAC (formerly run by CIS with federal funding; current status after 2025 not verified).
- **Media channels:** a viral X post / TikTok claiming machines "flipped votes", a livestream from a central-count observation area, local TV live shots at a long line, a reporter's records request, a candidate's (fictional) statement, an AI-voice robocall telling voters the wrong date, a text-message spam campaign, a fake "polling place moved" Facebook post.

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Election-day operations center:** the chief election official activates it (often co-located with the county EOC or a dedicated room). Hotlines take poll-worker calls, rovers are dispatched, and an incident log is kept with a severity tier. Escalations go to the SOS, county counsel and law enforcement.
- **Polling-place disruption (power, flood, threat, equipment):** the poll worker calls the hotline and the rover is dispatched. Decisions: emergency paper ballots, relocating the polling place (signage at the old site, as state law requires), and asking a court to extend hours (counsel files; state-specific). The PIO notifies voters.
- **E-pollbook failure:** switch to paper backup pollbook / precinct register printouts, issue provisional ballots per HAVA §302, and reconcile after the polls close.
- **Cyber incident affecting VR or ENR:** isolate the system and notify the state election director, CISA and the FBI (EAC contact sheet, March 2026). Put up a public statement that voting systems are separate from the affected system (if true) and post results through an alternate channel. Records under 52 U.S.C. 20701 are preserved.
- **Suspicious mail (white powder):** don't open further. Isolate the room, evacuate per plan, call 911 / hazmat, and notify USPIS and the FBI. Staff decontamination and continuity of mail-ballot processing follow. (Pattern: FBI / USPIS statements reported in Sept 2024 (election offices in at least 16 states) and Oct 2026 (AP, election and congressional offices).)
- **Rumor control:** the PIO publishes a "rumor vs. reality" post. The source document, CISA's former "Rumor vs. Reality" page, was not checked this session.
- **Canvass / certification:** reconcile ballots cast vs. voters checked in, adjudicate provisionals, run the audit, and the board certifies by the statutory deadline (state-specific).
- **Reporting deadlines:** no federal cyber-incident reporting mandate for election offices (CIRCIA once in force may cover some government entities; scope pending). State requirements vary (many require reporting to the SOS; unverified per state). State breach-notification law applies to voter PII beyond public-record fields.
- **Forms:** incident log / trouble ticket, seal log, chain-of-custody transfer form, ballot accounting / reconciliation form, provisional ballot envelope, poll-worker oath, polling-place relocation notice, ICS 201/213/214 when integrated with the county EOC.

## 7. Exercise scenarios (seeds)

1. **Cyber (pre-ransomware):** 30 days out, the CISA Cybersecurity Advisor reports exfiltrated credentials found in a pre-ransomware notification. Days later the voting-system vendor loses service and tabulators show errors. (CISA CTEP _Voting Machine Compromise: Pre-Ransomware Notification_, Sept 2026.)
2. **Cyber (DDoS on results site):** On election night the results-reporting website is knocked offline by DDoS. A spoofed results page circulates on social media and observers demand answers. (CISA _Readiness and Resilience Checklist_ (Sept 2024); EAC/CISA _Incident Response Communications Guide_ (Oct 2024).)
3. **Disinformation / AI:** An AI-generated robocall in the voice of a (fictional) official tells voters to "save your vote" for a later date. The county must respond within hours. (Pattern: FCC $6M forfeiture against the consultant behind 2024 AI-voice primary robocalls, reported May / Sept 2024.)
4. **Physical security:** Letters containing white powder arrive at the elections office during mail-ballot processing. The office is evacuated, hazmat responds, and a decision is needed on whether processing continues at the alternate site. (FBI / USPIS investigations reported Sept 2024 and Oct 2026.)
5. **Insider:** A temporary election worker makes unauthorized copies of an EMS hard drive, and images appear online. Chain-of-custody questions follow, along with whether the equipment can still be used. (FBI / DHS I&A / CISA / EAC _2024 U.S. Federal Elections: The Insider Threat_, June 2024.)
6. **Natural hazard:** A hurricane makes landfall 5 days before Election Day. Vote centers are damaged, USPS is disrupted, poll workers are displaced, and an emergency relocation order is needed. (EAC _Disaster Quick Tips_, Feb 2026, drawn from the EAC Disaster Roundtable, Sept 2025.)
7. **Infrastructure failure:** A statewide e-pollbook sync outage on the morning of Election Day causes long lines and provisional-ballot shortages, and a court petition for extended hours follows.
8. **Threats to election workers / swatting:** The director's home address is posted online, followed by a swatting call to the residence and threatening voicemails to staff. (CISA _Physical Security Checklist for Election Offices_, Sept 2024; CISA swatting intro for ECCs, 2026.)
9. **UAS / mass gathering:** A drone hovers over the central count facility during the canvass while a large protest gathers outside. Staff safety and observer access are both at issue. (Ties to `unmanned_systems`.)
10. **Supply chain:** A ballot-printing vendor misprints a contest on 40,000 mail ballots. The office must choose between reprinting and remediation, all within the UOCAVA and mailing timeline.
