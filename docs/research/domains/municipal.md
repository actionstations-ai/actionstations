# Domain dossier: `municipal` (city or county government)

Researched 2026-10-24. Extends the existing profile `src/shared/domains/profiles/municipal.ts`. "Verified" means checked against the primary source this session; anything else is marked unverified.

## 1. Scope

- **Covers:** cities, towns, villages, counties, parishes, boroughs, consolidated city-county governments, and special districts run by them (city-owned utilities, transit, airports appear as partners). It is the whole-of-government view: the emergency operations center (EOC) plus the departments that run services.
- **Aliases users might type:** city, county, town, township, village, borough, parish, municipality, local government, government, city hall, county government, SLTT, metro government.
- **CISA sector:** Government Services and Facilities Sector (the SRMA is DHS (CISA) together with GSA; the 2024 NSM-22 renamed it from "Government Facilities", rename not re-verified this session). Elections are a separate pack (`elections`). Municipal utilities map to Water and Wastewater (EPA) and Energy (DOE).
- **Overlap:** `emergency_communications` (911), `water_utility`, `transit`, `elections`, `public_health`.

## 3. Systems

**IT:**

- ERP / financials / payroll / HR (e.g. Tyler Munis, Oracle, Workday, SAP; category examples).
- Utility billing and customer information system, online payments portal.
- Permitting, licensing, code enforcement, land management (e.g. Accela, Tyler EnerGov).
- Property tax / assessor / recorder of deeds.
- Courts / municipal court case management, parking citations.
- 311 CRM and mobile app, public website / CMS, open-data portal.
- GIS (enterprise, e.g. Esri), address management.
- Public safety: CAD / RMS (see `emergency_communications`), body-camera / evidence storage, jail management.
- Library ILS and public Wi-Fi.
- Email / Microsoft 365, AD / Entra ID, VPN, MDM, backups, SIEM / EDR (often MSSP), mass-notification (Everbridge / CodeRED-type), WebEOC-type EOC software.
- Council meeting streaming / agenda management.

**OT / physical:**

- Traffic signal central system and controllers, school-zone flashers, parking garages.
- Water / wastewater SCADA (if city-run; see `water_utility`), stormwater pumps, levee gates.
- Outdoor warning sirens (radio-activated).
- Building automation (HVAC, elevators) at city hall and facilities; access control and CCTV.
- Fleet fuel management, EV chargers.
- Street lighting control, smart-city sensors.
- Public-safety radio (shared with county / state).

**Identifiers seen in city / county documents (fact categories):**

- Wards / districts / precincts: existing `wards`.
- Fire / police stations: existing `stations`.
- Apparatus / units: existing `apparatus`.
- **City facilities** (`City Hall Annex`, `Service Center 2`, `Library Branch 5`): proposed `facilities`, alnum, lint yes.
- **Traffic signals / intersections** (`Signal #1147`, `Main & 5th`): proposed `signals`, token, not linted.
- **Siren sites** (`Siren 23`): proposed `sirens`, number, lint yes.
- **Shelters / cooling centers** (`Shelter 4 – Westside Rec Center`): proposed `shelters`.
- **ESF numbers** (ESF #1–#15 per the National Response Framework): vocabulary, not a fact category.
- **Lift stations / pump stations**: shared with `water_utility`.

## 4. Outside parties and media

- **Federal:** CISA (regional office, Cybersecurity Advisor, Cyber Hygiene / vulnerability scanning services), FBI field office (cyber squad, IC3), U.S. Secret Service (BEC / wire fraud), FEMA region (disaster declarations, Public Assistance), DOJ, EPA (if water), OFAC (ransom sanctions screening), National Guard (via the state).
- **State:** state emergency management agency, state CISO / state fusion center, state police, state auditor, state attorney general (breach notifications), state cybersecurity task force / National Guard cyber unit.
- **Regional / mutual aid:** neighboring jurisdictions, county EM, council of governments, intrastate mutual aid compact, EMAC (via the state), regional transit, school districts, utilities (power, gas, telecom), hospitals and healthcare coalition, Red Cross / VOAD.
- **ISAC:** MS-ISAC, run by the Center for Internet Security. CISA ended its cooperative agreement funding on 2025-09-30 and MS-ISAC moved to a fee-based membership model (secondary reporting: Route Fifty, The Register, Help Net Security). A bill to restore funding was introduced June 2026 (Nextgov; passage not verified).
- **Vendors:** ERP vendor, MSP / MSSP, cyber insurer and breach coach, IR firm, payment processor, bank (wire recall), cloud host, mass-notification vendor, traffic-signal vendor.
- **Media channels:** local TV and newspaper, a council member's Facebook post, Nextdoor post, a resident's X post complaining that bills can't be paid, a ransomware group's leak-site listing (template only), a public-records request from a reporter, a union statement, a talk-radio call-in.

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **EOC activation (cyber or all-hazards):** the EMD sets the activation level (e.g. Level 3 monitoring, Level 2 partial, Level 1 full; naming varies). The ICS 201 is opened, then a planning-P cycle (ICS 202/203/204/205/215/215A). WebEOC-type software is used, unless it is among the affected systems.
- **Local emergency declaration:** the chief elected official declares under state law and the council ratifies within the state-specified window (often 7 days; varies, unverified). The declaration unlocks emergency procurement and a request for state assistance. A cyber incident can be the basis for a declaration in many states (CISA Local Governments CTEP case study: "A large U.S. city declared a state of emergency in February 2023 after a ransomware attack").
- **Ransomware workflow:** IT isolates. Counsel is engaged, and the breach coach comes through the insurer. The FBI / CISA are notified, OFAC is screened, and decisions are made on payment, restore from backup, and staff notification. Payroll continuity comes through a manual / bank file, and utility billing is suspended along with shut-off notices (a common public decision).
- **BEC / wire fraud:** finance spots a vendor bank-change request that was fraudulent. Bank recall is requested immediately, followed by an IC3 complaint and the FBI Recovery Asset Team (IC3 guidance says to contact the bank and file with IC3 promptly; timing not re-fetched).
- **Joint Information Center:** the PIO leads, with a single message cadence, a council briefing, call-center scripts, and a rumor page.
- **Mutual aid / resource request:** request to county EM, then the state EOC, then EMAC / the National Guard cyber team. ICS 213RR is the resource request form.
- **Regulatory / external reporting deadlines:** state breach law (varies); CJIS incident to the state CSA (per CJIS policy); PCI incident to the acquirer (contractual); CIRCIA once in force (not yet; the final rule was reported at OMB 2026-10-01, secondary source). Federal grant reporting conditions are not covered here. EPA / state primacy notice if city water is affected (see `water_utility`).
- **Forms:** ICS 201, 202, 203, 204, 205, 205A, 206, 207, 208, 209, 211, 213, 213RR, 214, 215, 215A (FEMA ICS forms; booklet URL at training.fema.gov returned 404 this session); the local emergency declaration template; the state request for assistance; FEMA Project Worksheets (Public Assistance) for reimbursement.

## 7. Exercise scenarios (seeds)

1. **Cyber (ransomware):** Ransomware hits city servers on a Monday, with ransom notes printing on office printers. Police RMS, courts, libraries and payroll go down, and the group later posts employee PII. (CISA Local Governments CTEP case study "Royal Ransomware Attack", May 2023, large U.S. city.)
2. **Cyber + declaration:** A ransomware attack leads to a state of emergency. City Hall internet and payroll are out, followed by a secondary data leak. (CISA Local Governments CTEP case study, February 2023.)
3. **Cyber-physical:** Unauthorized radio activation sets off all outdoor warning sirens at 23:40, and 911 is flooded with calls. (Pattern: publicly reported 2017 siren activation in a large Texas city; source not re-fetched this session, so cite at build time.)
4. **Insider / fraud:** A business email compromise redirects a $1.2M construction payment. The finance director's mailbox has a forwarding rule, and a council member leaks it before law enforcement is told. (FBI IC3 BEC public service announcements; not fetched this session.)
5. **Infrastructure failure:** The traffic-signal central system loses communication citywide during the evening rush. Signals go to flash, there is a crash at a school-zone intersection, and a vendor remote-access session is under suspicion.
6. **Natural hazard:** A tornado or derecho, with the EOC activated while the mass-notification vendor is degraded. Shelters open, debris is managed, and a FEMA Public Assistance cost-tracking decision follows. (CPG 101 v3.1; Local Elected Officials Guide 2025.)
7. **Physical security / civil unrest:** A large protest at City Hall after a controversial council vote, a threat called into the council chambers, and a livestream of the meeting.
8. **Mass gathering:** A city-hosted festival, with a lost-child incident, a heat emergency, a drone over the crowd, and a fake "event cancelled" post. (Ties to `stadium_venue`, `unmanned_systems`.)
9. **Water / public works:** The city water SCADA shows an operator-workstation intrusion and a chemical setpoint change. The utility, public health and the EOC coordinate a do-not-drink advisory. (Ties to `water_utility`, `public_health`.)
10. **Third-party / supply chain:** A cloud ERP vendor serving many municipalities suffers an outage and breach. The city can't run payroll or take payments, and the vendor's communications lag. (CISA Vendor Supply Chain Compromise CTEP, Aug 2024.)
