# Domain dossier: `chemical` (NEW — chemical facility)

Research for issue #313. Researched 2026-10-24. **[V]** = checked this session against a primary source. **[U]** = not verified this session.

No profile exists. `industrial` currently lists `chemical` among its aliases; move it here and leave `industrial` for discrete/process manufacturing generally. The `industrial` dossier (by another researcher) bundles OSHA 3132 too; share the file between packs rather than shipping it twice.

## 1. Scope

**Covers:** chemical manufacturing plants (basic, specialty, agricultural), chemical distributors and terminals, toll processors, fertilizer plants and ag retailers (anhydrous ammonia, ammonium nitrate), petrochemical units, industrial gas plants, and other facilities holding RMP/PSM-regulated quantities (e.g. chlorine repackagers, refrigeration with large ammonia inventories). Refineries can use this or `industrial`.

**Aliases:** chemical plant, chemical facility, chemical manufacturer, chemical distributor, chemical terminal, petrochemical, specialty chemicals, fertilizer plant, ag retailer, ammonia plant, chlor-alkali, RMP facility, PSM facility, CFATS facility, tank farm, process plant.

**CISA sector:** Chemical Sector. **SRMA:** CISA **[U: per NSM-22]**. Safety/environment regulators: OSHA (PSM), EPA (RMP, EPCRA, CERCLA), state environmental agencies, the US Chemical Safety Board (investigation, not enforcement). Security: CFATS lapsed on 28 July 2023 and CISA cannot enforce it; ChemLock is the voluntary replacement **[V: CISA CFATS page]**. MTSA applies to waterfront facilities (USCG).

## 3. Systems

### IT / business

- ERP (production, inventory, quality), LIMS, MES
- Logistics: rail car tracking, truck scheduling, bills of lading, hazmat shipping papers
- SDS management, chemical inventory (Tier II reporting), EHS management system
- Access control / visitor management, CCTV VMS, email, SIEM

### OT / ICS

- Distributed Control System (DCS) (e.g. Honeywell Experion, Emerson DeltaV, Yokogawa CENTUM)
- Safety Instrumented System (SIS) — separate logic solver (e.g. Schneider Triconex, the platform targeted by TRITON/TRISIS **[U]**)
- PLCs for packaging, loading racks, utilities; burner management systems
- Fire and gas detection, toxic gas detectors, deluge and foam systems, water curtains
- Tank level gauging and overfill protection; relief systems and flares; scrubbers
- Emergency shutdown (ESD) and emergency isolation valves
- Historian (e.g. AVEVA PI), alarm management
- Community warning sirens, meteorological station (wind for dispersion), dispersion modelling (EPA/NOAA ALOHA, CAMEO)
- Remote access for vendors; engineering workstations

- **Federal:** National Response Center (NRC), EPA Region (RMP, CERCLA), OSHA Area Office, US Chemical Safety Board (CSB), CISA (ChemLock; Chemical Security Inspectors), FBI (WMD Coordinator, theft/diversion), ATF (explosive precursors), USCG (MTSA facilities), DOT/PHMSA (hazmat in transport), FRA (rail)
- **State/local:** SERC, LEPC (EPCRA §303 community emergency plan), county EM, fire department HAZMAT team, police, 911, schools (shelter-in-place), hospitals (decon), public health, state environmental agency
- **Industry:** CHEMTREC (24-h emergency call centre), industrial mutual aid association / CAER group, the railroad, carriers, the product's manufacturer, ACC/Responsible Care (association, copyrighted guidance)
- **ISAC:** Chemical sector has no stand-alone ISAC; IT-ISAC Chemical SIG / ONG-ISAC for petrochemicals **[U]**
- **Vendors:** DCS/SIS vendor, integrator, environmental contractor (cleanup), air-monitoring contractor
- **Media:** local TV with aerial footage of the plume, residents' videos of flares and smoke on TikTok/X, Facebook neighbourhood groups, environmental justice advocates, national outlets for large events

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Toxic release:** gas detector / operator report → board operator isolates, ESD → site IC declares emergency → sirens and site muster → protective-action recommendation to county (shelter-in-place vs evacuate) using wind and dispersion modelling → **NRC immediately** if RQ exceeded (302.6) → LEPC and SERC immediately (355.40/.42) → CHEMTREC if transport-related → OSHA within 8/24 h if injuries → written follow-up to SERC/LEPC → RMP accident history update within 6 months → incident investigation (PSM (m), within 48 h start **[U]**).
- **Cyber on DCS/SIS:** unexpected SIS trip or DCS anomaly → do not reset the SIS until cause known → manual/safe park → isolate engineering workstations → vendor engagement → CISA/FBI → decision on restart (TRITON pattern).
- **Theft / diversion:** inventory discrepancy or cut fence at the ammonia/AN storage → FSO → FBI WMD coordinator, ATF → review CCTV → security plan updates.
- **Hurricane / flood:** pre-storm safe shutdown, refrigeration of reactive chemicals, backup power, ride-out crew, evacuation zone with the county (Arkema pattern).
- **Forms:** ICS 201/202/203/205/208 (safety message)/213/214; NRC report record; EPCRA written follow-up; OSHA 300/301 and 1904.39 report; MOC form; hot-work permit; shipping papers; CHEMTREC call record; RMP*eSubmit accident history fields.

## 7. Exercise scenarios

1. **natural_hazard:** Hurricane flooding takes out power and backup refrigeration for organic peroxides; trailers cannot be moved; decomposition and fires over days; 1.5-mile evacuation zone (CSB Arkema Crosby report, 2018 **[V]**).
2. **cyber_physical:** Attackers in a petrochemical plant's network deploy malware against the SIS controllers; a fault trips the unit; is it a malfunction or an attack (TRITON/TRISIS, 2017; CISA MAR-17-352-01 **[U]**).
3. **infrastructure_failure:** Corroded elbow in an HF alkylation unit ruptures; fire and explosions; HF release risk to the community (pattern: CSB report on a 2019 Philadelphia refinery **[U]**).
4. **physical_security / insider:** Contractor with legitimate access diverts small quantities of a chemical of interest over weeks; inventory reconciliation reveals it (CISA Chemical Sector theft and diversion CTEP **[V title]**).
5. **cyber:** Ransomware on ERP and logistics: no shipping papers or SDS access; trucks queue at the gate with hazmat; production must slow or stop.
6. **uas_drone:** Drone hovering over the chlorine railcar spot, then a second drone near the control room (CISA Chemical Sector UAS CTEP **[V]**).
7. **mass_gathering:** Toxic release with wind toward a high-school football game; shelter-in-place vs evacuation decision with the county.
8. **physical_security:** Fire set on a loading rack by an intruder during civil unrest (CISA Chemical Sector fire-as-a-weapon / civil-unrest CTEPs **[V titles]**).
9. **infrastructure_failure:** Fertilizer warehouse fire with ammonium nitrate; responders arrive before the explosion risk is known (pattern: 2013 West, Texas — CSB report **[U]**).
10. **cyber:** Remote-access vendor account compromised; DCS operator screens show manipulated tank levels; overfill risk at a tank farm.
