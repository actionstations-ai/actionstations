Researched 2026-10-24. Every URL in §9 was fetched in this session with a ranged GET (HTTP 206) and the file type and byte size come from the server's `Content-Range`. Page counts and dates come from `pdfinfo`/`pdftotext` on downloaded copies. Regulation text was read from the eCFR versioner API (title 34, 2026-10-01 point-in-time).

**Fetcher note:** `rems.ed.gov` **does not resolve in DNS** as of 2026-10-24 (`Could not resolve host`). The REMS TA Center guides are now served from `www.ed.gov/media/document/...` (K-12 guide, IHE checklist) and the IHE guide from `www.fbi.gov/file-repository/...`. Any existing link in the repo to `rems.ed.gov/docs/...` is dead. `www.ed.gov` answers `curl -I` with 403 but a browser-style ranged GET returns 206; the pack fetcher needs a browser User-Agent and Accept headers.

## 1. Scope

- **Covers:** public and private institutions of higher education (IHEs): research universities, regional four-year universities, community and technical colleges, multi-campus systems, academic medical-center-adjacent campuses (the hospital itself belongs in `healthcare`), residential liberal-arts colleges. Includes residence halls, research labs (BSL-2/3, select agents, radiation), athletics venues on campus (big games may be better modelled with `stadium_venue` as a second domain), central utility plants, and campus police.
- **Aliases users might type:** university, college, campus, community college, IHE, higher ed, higher education, state university, research university, technical college, junior college, academy (service academies), residence hall, dorm.
- **CISA sector:** **Government Facilities Sector, Education Facilities Subsector** (covers both K-12 and higher education). Sector Risk Management Agencies: **CISA and the General Services Administration (Federal Protective Service)** for Government Facilities; the **Department of Education** is the lead federal partner for the Education Facilities subsector. _Verified from the CISA Government Facilities sector page structure in an earlier pass; the subsector SRMA wording was not re-fetched this session._
- Research labs touch the **Chemical** (CFATS lapsed in July 2023; not re-verified) and **Healthcare and Public Health** sectors; utility plants touch **Energy**.

## 3. Systems

**IT**

- Student information system (SIS): Ellucian Banner / Colleague, Workday Student, Oracle PeopleSoft Campus Solutions, Jenzabar.
- ERP / HR / finance: Workday, PeopleSoft, Banner Finance.
- Learning management system (LMS): Canvas, D2L Brightspace, Blackboard, Moodle.
- Identity: Active Directory / Entra ID, Shibboleth / InCommon federation, Okta, Duo MFA.
- Email and collaboration: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace.
- Financial aid: FSA systems access through SAIG / Student Aid Internet Gateway (the GLBA and FSA reporting obligations follow from this).
- Research computing (HPC clusters, research data storage, controlled unclassified information (CUI) enclaves for DoD-funded research).
- Campus card / one-card system (CBORD, Transact): meal plans, door access, vending, laundry.
- Mass notification / emergency alert: Rave Alert, Everbridge, Omnilert, Regroup (SMS, email, desktop pop-up, digital signage, sirens).
- Parking and transportation (shuttle AVL), ticketing for athletics, website / CMS, helpdesk / ITSM, SIEM / SOC (often REN-ISAC member).

**OT / building**

- Building automation systems (BAS): Siemens Desigo, Johnson Controls Metasys, Honeywell, Schneider EcoStruxure; lab fume hood monitors and airflow controls.
- Central utility plant: steam boilers, chillers, cogeneration, campus electrical distribution (substations and feeders), SCADA for the plant.
- Physical access control (PACS): Lenel OnGuard, Genetec, C•CURE, often tied to the campus card.
- CCTV / VMS, blue-light emergency phones, outdoor warning sirens / giant voice, fire alarm panels.
- Lab safety: BSL-3 access interlocks, freezer/-80 °C alarms, radiation monitors, gas detection.
- Residence hall door locks, elevators, emergency generators.

## 6. Common operational workflows

**Emergency notification (Clery 668.46(g))**

1. Report reaches campus police dispatch (911 transfer, blue-light phone, LiveSafe-type app, CSA).
2. Police supervisor or Chief **confirms** a significant emergency or dangerous situation involving an immediate threat on campus (the clock that matters is "without delay" after confirmation).
3. Pre-authorized senders (titles listed in the ASR: Chief of Police, EM Director, PIO) choose segments (whole campus, a building, a zone) and a pre-scripted template ("Shelter in place", "Avoid area", "Lockdown", "All clear").
4. Send via multi-modal alert (SMS, email, voice, desktop pop-up, digital signage, sirens / outdoor voice, social media, website banner).
5. Follow-up updates and **all-clear**; Clery Officer logs the notification for the ASR test record and decides whether a separate timely warning is still needed.
6. Larger community: city emergency management, local media, parents' page.

**Timely warning (668.46(e))**: Clery crime reported (e.g., armed robbery near campus, series of burglaries) → Clery Officer and police assess "serious or continuing threat" → warning issued "as soon as pertinent information is available" (institutional policies commonly target the same day; _no fixed federal deadline_), victim names withheld, crime prevention tips.

**ICS activation**: EM Director activates the EOC (levels 3/2/1), Policy Group convenes separately (President, Provost, VPA, GC, PIO), unified command with city police/fire at the scene, family assistance / reunification center (often the student union or arena), student accountability through housing rosters and the card system.

**Cyber incident** (ransomware on SIS/ERP/LMS during registration, finals or financial-aid disbursement): SOC alert → CISO declares incident → incident response plan → isolate identity plane → engage cyber insurer, breach coach, DFIR → FBI and CISA → **FSA notice same day**, FTC Safeguards notice ≤30 days if ≥500 consumers, state AG and individual notices under state law → academic continuity decisions (extend add/drop, delay grades) → Clery is not triggered by a pure cyber event unless there is an immediate threat to health or safety.

**Missing student**: report of 24 h absence → immediate referral to campus police → notify confidential contact within 24 h of determination; under-18 parent notice. _(668.46(h); verified.)_

**Lab incident**: EHS / Responsible Official → evacuate and isolate → Form 3 timelines (select agents) → NRC/Agreement State notice for radioactive material (_timelines not re-verified_) → public health.

**Forms used**: ICS 201 (incident briefing), 202 (objectives), 203 (organization), 205 (radio plan), 205A (contact list), 213 (general message), 214 (activity log), 209 (status summary); Clery daily crime log; timely-warning and emergency-notification templates; APHIS/CDC Form 3; FERPA disclosure record (99.32 recordation); after-action report / improvement plan (HSEEP).

## 7. Exercise scenarios (seeds)

1. **Cyber**: ransomware encrypts the SIS and ERP two days before financial-aid disbursement; leak-site post claims student SSNs (pattern: CISA K-12 / higher-ed ransomware reporting; FBI-CISA joint advisories on Vice Society and Rhysida that named education as a primary target, AA22-249A and AA23-319A).
2. **Cyber / identity**: compromised SSO via MFA-fatigue push leads to payroll direct-deposit diversion for faculty (pattern: FBI IC3 PSA on business email compromise of payroll, 2022–2024).
3. **Physical security**: active threat reported in a classroom building during midterms; conflicting social posts place a second shooter in a residence hall (CTEP _Higher Education Active Threat_ SitMan, June 2021; USSS NTAC _Mass Attacks in Public Spaces 2016–2020_).
4. **Cyber-physical**: building automation compromise disables fume-hood alarms and lab airflow in a chemistry building while classes are in session (CISA advisories on internet-exposed BAS; no specific campus case named).
5. **Insider**: graduate researcher exfiltrates export-controlled CUI from a DoD-funded lab and a sponsor demands a 72-hour incident report (DFARS 252.204-7012; NSPM-33 research security).
6. **Natural hazard**: hurricane track forces residence-hall evacuation decisions for 6,000 students with international students who cannot leave (Campus Resilience Program ESK _Hurricane_).
7. **Infrastructure failure**: central plant steam loss in January; residence halls lose heat and a -80 °C freezer farm warms (pattern: published campus utility failures; no named victim).
8. **Mass gathering**: football game-day crowd crush at a student gate combined with a bomb threat called into the press box (CTEP _Higher Education IED_, April 2022; CTEP _UAS Stadium Crash_).
9. **UAS**: drone repeatedly flies over a commencement ceremony; local police have no counter-UAS authority (CISA/FBI UAS guidance; FAA rules).
10. **Swatting / hoax**: coordinated swatting calls reporting active shooters at several campuses in one morning (FBI public statements on 2023–2025 swatting waves; seed names no institution).
