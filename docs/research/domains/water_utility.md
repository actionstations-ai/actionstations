## 1. Scope

**Covers:** municipal and regional drinking-water systems (community water systems, CWS), wastewater / water resource recovery utilities (POTWs), combined water-and-sewer utilities, special districts and authorities, rural water districts, and private investor-owned water companies. Raw-water reservoirs owned by the utility are in scope; the dam itself is better exercised with `water_dam`.

**Aliases users might type:** water department, water utility, water authority, water district, rural water district, municipal utility district (MUD), public utility district, water works, water and sewer, sewer district, sanitation district, wastewater treatment plant, WWTP, WTP, POTW, water reclamation, water resource recovery facility (WRRF), CWS, drinking water. (The current profile already has `water_department`, `drinking_water`, `wastewater`, `water_treatment`. Note that `water_dam` lists `water` and `water_utility` as its own aliases; the alias router should prefer this profile for "water utility".)

**CISA sector:** Water and Wastewater Systems Sector. **Sector Risk Management Agency:** US EPA **[U: per NSM-22, April 2024; EPA's own pages call it the SRMA]**.

## 3. Systems

### IT / business

- Customer Information System and billing (CIS; e.g. Oracle CC&B, Tyler, Itron-integrated systems)
- AMI head-end and meter data management (MDM) (e.g. Sensus FlexNet, Badger, Itron)
- Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)
- Computerized maintenance / asset management (CMMS/EAM, e.g. Cityworks, Maximo)
- GIS of mains, valves, hydrants (e.g. Esri)
- Hydraulic model (e.g. EPANET — a public-domain EPA tool — InfoWater, WaterGEMS)
- Customer portal and payment processor, IVR / call centre
- Email, ERP/finance, document management, backup, SIEM / MDR

### OT / ICS

- Plant SCADA / HMI servers and operator workstations (e.g. Wonderware/AVEVA, Ignition, iFIX, ClearSCADA)
- Historian (e.g. OSIsoft/AVEVA PI)
- PLCs at the plant (Allen-Bradley ControlLogix, Schneider Modicon) and small integrated HMI/PLCs at booster stations (e.g. Unitronics Vision — the device in AA23-335A **[V]**)
- RTUs and telemetry at tanks, wells, booster and lift stations (licensed radio, cellular, fibre)
- Chemical feed and dosing: chlorine gas / sodium hypochlorite, caustic (sodium hydroxide), fluoride, coagulant (alum/ferric), polymer, ammonia (chloramination), permanganate
- Online analyzers: chlorine residual, turbidity, pH, conductivity; filter effluent turbidimeters (CFE/IFE)
- Variable-frequency drives (VFDs), high-service and booster pumps, motor control centres
- UV disinfection and membrane skids; wastewater blowers, DO control, clarifiers, digesters
- Remote-access gateway / VPN / vendor remote tool for the integrator (TeamViewer-class tools figure in the 2021 Florida incident pattern **[U]**)
- PACS, intrusion alarms and CCTV at tanks, wells and pump stations

## 4. Outside parties and media

- **Regulators:** state drinking-water program (primacy agency), EPA Region, state environmental agency (NPDES permits for wastewater), county/state health department, state PUC (investor-owned utilities)
- **Federal:** EPA Water Security Division, CISA (regional Cybersecurity Advisor / Protective Security Advisor), FBI field office cyber squad, NRC (National Response Center) for hazardous-substance releases (chlorine)
- **ISAC / associations:** WaterISAC; state WARN (Water/Wastewater Agency Response Network) mutual aid; AWWA section; rural water association (e.g. NRWA state affiliate) **[U names vary by state]**
- **Local:** county emergency management / EOC, LEPC, fire and police, 911/PSAP, hospitals, dialysis centres, schools, large industrial users, the city council / mayor, neighbouring interconnected utilities
- **Vendors:** SCADA integrator, PLC vendor, chemical supplier, lab contract, AMI vendor, MSP/MSSP, cyber insurer and breach coach, bottled-water and tanker vendors
- **Media:** local TV assignment desk, local paper, Nextdoor and Facebook neighbourhood groups, X/Twitter, Reddit city sub, TikTok videos of discoloured water; the utility's own alert system (IPAWS/WEA via county, reverse-911, text alerts)

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Cyber on the control system:** detect (operator sees an HMI set-point change, analyzer excursion) → take affected process to manual/local → disconnect remote access → notify IT/ISO and SCADA → activate cyber IR plan and ERP → preserve logs → call CISA (888-282-0870 / report.cisa.gov), FBI field office, WaterISAC, EPA; notify state drinking-water program if water quality could be affected → decide on Tier 1 notice → restore from known-good PLC logic. Sources: EPA Cybersecurity IAC; CISA/FBI/EPA WWS IR Guide **[V both PDFs fetched]**.
- **Contamination / boil water:** positive E. coli or pressure loss/main break → repeat samples → consult primacy agency → Tier 1 notice (boil-water advisory or do-not-drink) within 24 h → flushing and resampling → "problem corrected" notice (Template 1-10 in the 2023 handbook **[U: numbering per secondary source]**).
- **Chlorine gas release:** shelter-in-place / evacuation call with fire and LEPC, NRC report (immediately), EPCRA §304 notice to SERC/LEPC.
- **Wastewater overflow:** SSO detected → contain → 24-h oral report to permit authority → 5-day written report.
- **Mutual aid:** WARN request for generators, crews, lab capacity.
- **ICS:** ICS 201 (briefing), 202 (objectives), 203 (organization), 205 (comms plan), 213 (general message), 214 (activity log), 215 (planning worksheet). Sector forms: EPA Incident Action Checklists ("My Contacts" page), public notice templates, sample chain-of-custody forms, state primacy report forms.

## 7. Exercise scenarios

1. **cyber_physical:** Remote-access session on an operator workstation raises the caustic (sodium hydroxide) set-point; operator notices and reverses it; is anything else changed? (pattern: Feb 2021 Florida incident, described in public FBI/CISA reporting **[U]**).
2. **cyber:** Hacktivist persona defaces internet-exposed booster-station HMI/PLCs with default passwords; station goes to manual (AA23-335A, Dec 2023 **[V]**).
3. **cyber:** Ransomware encrypts billing/CIS and the SCADA historian; plant runs but cannot bill or trend; decision on paying and on OT isolation (AA21-287A lists 2019–2021 WWS ransomware cases **[V]**).
4. **insider:** Former employee whose credentials were never revoked logs into the remote-access tool and shuts down disinfection (pattern: 2019 Kansas rural water district prosecution, DOJ press release **[U]**).
5. **infrastructure_failure:** Winter storm causes multiple main breaks and power loss at the high-service pump station; system-wide pressure loss triggers a boil-water notice for 100,000+ customers (pattern: Feb 2021 Texas winter storm **[U]**).
6. **natural_hazard:** Hurricane flooding at the WWTP; bypass of untreated sewage; generator fuel runs low; lift stations down.
7. **physical_security:** Intrusion at an elevated storage tank: hatch cut, unknown liquid possibly introduced; do-not-drink decision while waiting for lab results.
8. **uas_drone:** Repeated drone flights over the chlorine building and clearwell; one lands inside the fence carrying a package.
9. **mass_gathering:** Regional event doubles demand in one pressure zone during a heat wave and a pump failure; low pressure near the venue.
10. **cyber:** Third-party AMI/billing vendor breach exposes customer data and triggers state breach-notification law; public believes "the water was hacked".
