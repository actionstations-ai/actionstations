## 1. Scope

**Covers:** natural gas local distribution companies (LDCs: city gate, regulator stations, mains, services, meters), interstate and intrastate transmission pipelines (compressor stations, mainline valves, storage), underground natural gas storage, LNG peak-shaving and import/export terminals, and hazardous-liquid pipelines (crude, refined products, NGLs) where users want them. Gas utilities that are also electric utilities exercise the gas side here.

**Aliases:** gas, gas utility, gas company, natural gas, LDC, local distribution company, gas distribution, transmission pipeline, interstate pipeline, pipeline, pipeline operator, compressor station, gas storage, LNG, LNG terminal, midstream, oil pipeline, products pipeline, hazardous liquid pipeline.

**CISA sectors:** Energy Sector (oil and natural gas subsector; SRMA DOE) **and** Transportation Systems Sector (pipeline systems mode; SRMA DHS/TSA with DOT) **[U: per NSM-22]**. Security regulator: TSA (49 U.S.C. 114). Safety regulator: PHMSA (49 CFR Parts 190–199), with state pipeline safety programs for intrastate lines. Interstate rates/siting: FERC.

## 3. Systems

### IT / business

- CIS and billing, customer portal, call centre / IVR
- Work and asset management, mobile dispatch, GIS of mains/services/valves
- Gas nomination and scheduling, electronic bulletin board (EBB) for interstate pipelines, gas accounting
- One-call / 811 ticket management, locate management
- Email, ERP, SIEM, identity

### OT / ICS

- Pipeline SCADA (gas control) with HMI, historian, alarm management, leak detection (CPM for liquids)
- Remote terminal units at regulator stations, city gates, compressor stations, valve sites
- Remote-control / automatic shut-off valves (RCV/ASV) on transmission lines (rupture mitigation rule) **[U: 2022 PHMSA rule]**
- Compressor station unit control panels, station ESD (emergency shutdown) systems, fire and gas detection
- Electronic flow measurement (EFM), gas chromatographs, odorant injection systems
- Pressure recorders/telemetry on low-pressure distribution (lessons of Merrimack Valley)
- AMI/AMR gas meters (some with remote shut-off)
- Telecom: radio, cellular, satellite, leased lines; third-party EDI for customer communications

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Gas odour / leak call:** call centre or 911 → dispatch serviceperson → evaluate (grade leak), make safe, evacuate if needed, shut off service/meter → coordinate with fire → repair → relight.
- **Rupture / explosion:** controller sees pressure drop / flow change → notification of potential rupture → immediate and direct PSAP notification → rupture identification → close RCVs/ASVs and manual valves → isolate segment → NRC within 1 hour of confirmed discovery → revise within 48 h → PHMSA F 7100.x within 30 days → NTSB if investigation launched.
- **Overpressure on low-pressure distribution:** multiple fire/explosion calls across a district → shut down the regulator station(s) and the district → door-to-door shutoff of meters → evacuation → days/weeks of relights (Merrimack Valley pattern, NTSB PAR-19/02 **[V title]**).
- **Cyber on gas control:** detect → move to manual operations at stations → Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (SD 02G) → Cybersecurity Coordinator reports to CISA within 72 h (SD 01G) → TSA coordination → consider precautionary shutdown (Colonial pattern: IT-only compromise led to OT shutdown, AA21-131A **[V]**).
- **Supply emergency / curtailment:** OFO or critical notice on the EBB → curtail interruptible customers → appeal for conservation → gas-electric coordination with the RTO.
- **Forms:** ICS 201/202/205/213/214; PHMSA F 7100.1 / 7100.2; NRC telephonic report record; leak survey and leak grading forms; 811 locate ticket; valve closure log; relight sheets.

## 7. Exercise scenarios

1. **cyber:** Ransomware on the business network of a transmission pipeline; billing and scheduling down; company shuts the pipeline as a precaution; fuel shortages and panic buying (Colonial Pipeline, May 2021 — AA21-131A **[V]**).
2. **cyber_physical:** Spearphishing → IT → OT; commodity ransomware encrypts HMIs and historians at a compressor station; two-day shutdown (CISA AA20-049A, Feb 2020 **[V: HTML alert, per search summary]**).
3. **infrastructure_failure:** Sensor lines left on an abandoned main during a tie-in; regulators open fully; overpressure causes dozens of fires and explosions across three towns (NTSB PAR-19/02, Merrimack Valley 2018 **[V title]**).
4. **cyber:** Third-party EDI/communications provider for several pipelines hit by a cyberattack; customer nominations and notices interrupted (2018 public reporting **[U]**).
5. **physical_security:** Activists close manual mainline valves on a hazardous-liquid line after cutting chains (2016 public reporting **[U]**).
6. **natural_hazard:** Polar vortex: wellhead freeze-offs, low pressure at the city gate, curtailment of interruptible customers and gas-fired generators, risk of losing a low-pressure district (relights take days).
7. **insider:** Disgruntled gas controller changes alarm limits and disables a station ESD bypass alarm.
8. **uas_drone:** Drones repeatedly over a compressor station and LNG tank; a payload drop near the fence.
9. **infrastructure_failure:** Third-party excavation (no 811 ticket) strikes a transmission line in a suburb; rupture and fire; evacuation of a school.
10. **mass_gathering:** Gas leak near a stadium with 60,000 attendees; isolate a main without cutting service to the venue's hospital-grade kitchen and nearby care home.
