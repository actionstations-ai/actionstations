# Domain research dossiers

One dossier per domain, written on 2026-10-24 for the 0.4.0 domain packs (#313, ADR-0020). Each one covers scope and sector, roles, IT/OT systems and the identifiers documents state, outside parties and media, regulatory drivers and required plans, incident workflows and reporting deadlines, exercise scenario seeds, proposed inject templates, the public-domain federal documents worth bundling (with URLs, sizes and sha256 where recorded), useful links that are not bundled, and sources.

Owner rule: packs bundle public-domain documents only (US federal works, 17 U.S.C. §105, or CC0). Copyrighted references are named, not bundled.

Facts here were checked on the date above; regulations and editions change (for example TSA security directives expire, CIRCIA was not final). Re-check before relying on a deadline.

`generic.md` is the core pack: HSEEP, NIMS, CPG 101, ICS forms, CISA tabletop exercise packages and cross-sector material.
