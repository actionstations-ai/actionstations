# Domain dossier: `transit` (bus and rail transit agency)

Researched 2026-10-24. Every file URL in §9 was fetched in full on that date; size, page count, title and date come from the downloaded PDF, and each file was text-searched for copyright / SSI / FOUO / TLP markings. Claims marked **(unverified)** were not checked against a primary source.

> **Fetch note:** transit.dot.gov and rosap.ntl.bts.gov return HTTP 403 (Akamai) to plain `curl`. They serve normally with browser-like headers (`User-Agent`, `Accept`, `Accept-Language`, `Sec-Fetch-Mode: navigate`). tsa.gov and govinfo.gov serve to any client.

## 1. Scope

- **Covers:** a public transportation agency: fixed-route bus, paratransit/demand response, light rail, streetcar, heavy rail (subway/metro), commuter rail operated by or for the agency, bus rapid transit, ferries (as a mode). Also transit authorities, regional transportation districts and city transit departments.
- **Aliases:** transit, transit agency, bus, rail, metro, subway, light rail, LRT, streetcar, BRT, commuter rail, paratransit, transit authority, RTD, MPO-operated transit, public transportation.
- **CISA sector:** Transportation Systems Sector, Mass Transit and Passenger Rail mode. **SRMA:** DHS (TSA) and DOT. FTA is the safety regulator (49 CFR 670/672/673/674). TSA is the security regulator (49 CFR 1570/1582 and SDs). State Safety Oversight Agencies (SSOAs) oversee rail fixed-guideway systems.
- Freight rail is a separate domain (`freight_rail`). Commuter railroads on the general railroad system are FRA-regulated (49 CFR 239 passenger train emergency preparedness).

## 3. Systems

**IT:** CAD/AVL (computer-aided dispatch / automatic vehicle location), scheduling and runcutting, real-time passenger information feeds (GTFS / GTFS-Realtime), trip planner and rider app, fare collection back office (account-based ticketing, open payments, smart cards, mobile ticketing), paratransit scheduling and dispatch, maintenance management (EAM), timekeeping/payroll, email/identity, ITSM, SIEM, public website, rider alert system (SMS/email).

**OT:** rail signaling and train control (fixed-block wayside signals, CBTC on metros, PTC on commuter rail), traction power SCADA (substations, rectifiers, catenary/third rail breakers), tunnel ventilation and fire life safety, station PIDS and PA, station PACS and CCTV, fare gates and ticket vending machines, on-board vehicle logic units (VLU/MDT), radio (P25/LMR), transit signal priority with city traffic signals, fuel and battery-electric bus charging management, depot building automation, elevator/escalator monitoring, yard interlocking.

**Identifiers (fact categories):** profile has Bus routes, Rail lines, Vehicle/bus numbers, Stops, Depots/yards/transit centers. Candidates:

- **Stations** (named; "Central Station") and **platforms/tracks** ("Track 2", "Platform B")
- **Substations** ("TPSS 14", "Substation 7"): `token`/`alnum`
- **Interlockings / control points** ("CP Main", "Interlocking 3"), **signals** ("Signal 214")
- **Run / block numbers** (operator assignments, "Block 1042")
- **Fare devices** (TVM numbers, fare gate arrays)
- **Radio talkgroups / channels** ("Bus Ops 2")

## 4. Outside parties and media

- **Federal:** FTA (Office of Transit Safety and Oversight, regional office), the **SSOA** (state agency), TSA Surface Operations (Transportation Security Inspectors; the TSOC for significant security concern reports), CISA (Central, regional advisors), FBI (JTTF), NTSB (rail accidents, some bus crashes), DHS I&A (fusion centers), FEMA (Transit Security Grant Program).
- **State/local:** city/county emergency management (transit supports evacuations), local police and fire, the 911 PSAP, city traffic management center, state DOT, school districts and major employers, hospitals, the regional fusion center.
- **Vendors:** CAD/AVL vendor NOC, fare-system vendor and payment processor, paratransit contractor, signaling/CBTC supplier, radio system owner (often city/county), cloud/MSP.
- **Peers:** neighboring transit agencies (mutual aid buses), Amtrak/commuter railroad if shared corridors.
- **ISAC:** Surface Transportation, Public Transportation and Over-the-Road Bus ISAC (ST-ISAC / PT-ISAC, run with APTA; **name unverified against a primary page**); MS-ISAC for city/county-run agencies.
- **Media:** rider posts on X/Reddit/TikTok, a transit-advocacy blog, the local TV assignment desk, a city-council member's social account, the union's social account, real-time app reviews.

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Rail safety event:** train operator → OCC → (two-hour) FTA and SSOA notification (674.33) → NTSB notification for qualifying accidents (49 CFR 840) **(840 not fetched)** → agency investigation under the SSOA program.
- **Security concern:** operator/station agent → transit police and OCC → TSA report within 24 h (1570.203) → FBI/fusion center as needed.
- **Cyber incident:** IT/SOC → Cybersecurity Coordinator → CISA report as soon as practicable, ≤72 h (SD 1582-21-01E) → CIRP activation, isolation of IT from OT where the plan says → NTD S&S-40 if it meets the major-event threshold → CIRCIA (when final).
- **Service disruption:** OCC/dispatch declares a disruption → bus bridge requested from bus ops → rider alerts (app, SMS, PIDS, PA, social) → coordination with the city TMC and police for detours.
- **ICS:** the agency EOC or a unified command at the scene. Transit often supports the city EOC (ESF-1 Transportation).
- **Forms:** ICS 201/202/203/205/213/214; FTA NTD S&S-40 (major event) and S&S-50 (monthly non-major); agency incident report; operator incident report; bus bridge request. TSA significant-security-concern report (TSOC).

## 7. Exercise scenarios (seeds)

1. **Ransomware hits CAD/AVL and the rider app:** Dispatch falls back to radio and paper, real-time arrivals go blank, and paratransit trips are lost. _Pattern: FTA/TRACS cyber report background; public reporting of transit ransomware since 2016 (**URLs unverified**)._
2. **Fare system compromise:** The account-based fare back office is breached. Card readers fail closed and riders' payment data is exposed. Choose between fare-free service and a shutdown.
3. **Traction power SCADA anomaly, cyber-physical:** Remote breaker operations nobody in the OCC issued, during the evening peak. De-energize a segment with trains in a tunnel?
4. **Signal / train control failure:** Loss of CBTC or a wayside signal data link forces manual block operation at reduced speed, platform crowding and a heat event.
5. **Insider:** A recently terminated IT contractor's VPN account is still active and is used to change the GTFS-RT feed, sending "service suspended" alerts for one line.
6. **Physical security:** A suspicious package on a light-rail vehicle at a downtown transfer station. Station evacuation, service suspension and rerouting.
7. **Mass gathering:** A stadium event with an extended-service plan, then a crowd surge on a platform after a weather delay. Riders livestream it.
8. **Natural hazard:** A flash flood in a tunnel portal and a bus depot. Equipment relocation and service cuts; transit supports the city evacuation.
9. **Operator assault cluster / workforce:** A spike in operator assaults and a union safety stand-down on a key route during a festival. _Pattern: FTA General Directive on operator assault risk (2024, **unverified**)._
10. **UAS:** A drone is seen hovering over a rail yard and maintenance facility at night. A Remote ID broadcast is absent.
