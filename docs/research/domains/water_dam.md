Research for issue #313. Researched 2026-10-24. **[V]** = checked this session against a primary source. **[U]** = not verified this session.

An existing profile is at `src/shared/domains/profiles/water_dam.ts` (roles: ops, it, pio, security, dse, ema). This dossier proposes additions.

## 1. Scope

**Covers:** owners and operators of dams and their appurtenant works: non-federal hydropower licensees (FERC-regulated), federal dams (USACE, Reclamation, TVA), state- and locally-owned flood-control, water-supply and recreation dams, levee-protected reservoirs, navigation locks, tailings dams (mine waste) and pumped storage.

**Aliases:** dam, dam owner, hydro, hydropower, hydroelectric, hydro plant, powerhouse, reservoir, impoundment, spillway, lock and dam, flood control district, irrigation district, pumped storage, levee district. (The current profile's alias `water` and `water_utility` should move to `water_utility`; see that dossier.)

**CISA sector:** Dams Sector (dams, hydropower, navigation locks, levees, mine tailings, hurricane barriers). Hydro generation also sits in the Energy Sector. **SRMA:** CISA for Dams **[V: CISA Dams Sector CTS guide states CISA carries out SRMA responsibilities for the Dams Sector]**; DOE for Energy.

## 3. Systems

### IT / business

- Dam safety instrumentation database and ADAS (automated data acquisition) server
- Asset management / CMMS
- Energy trading / scheduling (for hydropower), outage management
- GIS with inundation maps; EAP document management
- Email, business ERP, SIEM, remote access

### OT / ICS

- Plant and remote SCADA / HMI (e.g. GE iFIX, Emerson Ovation, ABB, AVEVA), historian
- PLCs controlling spillway and sluice gates (radial/Tainter, vertical lift, crest gates, bascule), gate position encoders, hoists
- Generating unit controls: governors, exciters, unit protection relays, switchyard (IEC 61850 / DNP3)
- Instrumentation: piezometers, weirs (seepage), inclinometers, survey monuments, reservoir level / tailwater gauges, accelerometers (seismic), GPS displacement
- Siren / warning systems for downstream areas, public-address at recreation areas
- Telemetry (licensed radio, satellite such as GOES DCP, cellular), communication to the remote operations centre
- Backup power (diesel generators) for gates, emergency lighting
- PACS, CCTV, intrusion detection, waterside barrier booms, UAS detection

- **Regulators:** FERC Division of Dam Safety and Inspections (Regional Engineer) for licensed hydro; state dam safety program; USACE (for federal dams and Section 408); Reclamation; NRC (if a nuclear plant is downstream) **[U]**

- **ISAC:** E-ISAC (for NERC-registered hydro), WaterISAC (water-supply dams), Multi-State ISAC (municipal owners)
- **Local:** county/parish emergency management, sheriff, fire, 911/PSAP, downstream municipalities, hospitals and schools in the inundation zone, downstream and upstream dam owners, the balancing authority / reliability coordinator
- **Vendors:** gate and hoist contractor, SCADA integrator, instrumentation vendor, diving contractor, engineering consultant (Independent Consultant for Part 12D inspections)
- **Media:** local TV, regional paper, recreation-user social media (kayakers, anglers), Facebook community groups, emergency alerts (WEA/EAS)

## 6. Common operational workflows

- **Detection → level → notification (FEMA P-64 Steps 1–2):** operator observes seepage/boil or instrumentation alarm → CDSE evaluates → emergency level set (non-failure / potential failure / imminent failure; high flow for flood releases) → Notification Flowchart: county EMA and 911 first for imminent failure, NWS, state dam safety, FERC Regional Engineer, downstream dams.
- **Imminent failure:** EMA issues evacuation; NWS issues a flash flood warning / dam-failure warning; owner lowers pool if possible; sirens.
- **Cyber on gate/unit controls:** take gates to local/manual, station an operator at the gate house, validate reservoir levels by staff gauge, isolate remote access, report to CISA/FBI (and E-ISAC within the CIP-008 1-hour window if applicable), notify the FERC Regional Engineer if dam safety is affected (12.10).
- **Flood operations:** release schedule coordinated with RFC, USACE water control manual (federal projects), downstream notifications at each step.
- **Forms:** ICS 201/202/205/213/214; EAP Notification Flowchart; emergency level determination table (P-64 App. D example); dam safety incident report to Regional Engineer; inspection checklist; instrumentation reading log; EAP drill and exercise evaluation report.

## 7. Exercise scenarios

1. **cyber_physical:** An intruder in the SCADA network of a small dam monitors sluice-gate status (gate was disconnected for maintenance); later the same actor targets an operating gate (pattern: 2013 New York dam intrusion described in a 2016 DOJ indictment **[U]**).
2. **cyber_physical:** Hacktivists open a valve on a small dam through an internet-exposed HMI, raising discharge for hours before staff notice (pattern: 2025 Norwegian dam incident in public reporting **[U]**).
3. **natural_hazard:** Extreme rainfall pushes reservoir above the top of active storage; spillway erosion; potential failure declared; downstream evacuation decision (pattern: 2017 California spillway incident; 2020 Michigan dam failures, FERC Independent Forensic Team report **[U]**).
4. **infrastructure_failure:** Loss of offsite power and a failed backup generator during flood operations; gates cannot be moved remotely.
5. **physical_security:** Vehicle-borne threat on the dam crest road; bomb threat called in during a holiday weekend with boaters on the reservoir (CISA Dams Sector adversarial threat CTEP **[V: file title]**).
6. **uas_drone:** Repeated drone flights along the spillway and powerhouse; one crashes into the switchyard.
7. **insider:** Disgruntled operator changes alarm set-points on piezometers so a rising trend is suppressed.
8. **mass_gathering:** July 4 fireworks with 20,000 people below the dam when an unscheduled high-flow release is required.
9. **cyber:** Ransomware on the business network encrypts the EAP document server and contact lists the morning of a potential-failure condition.
