# Harrow Valley Water Authority — Emergency Response Plan

> Test fixture (#423). The Harrow Valley Water Authority, its people and numbers are fictional.

## Section 1 Purpose

This plan sets out how the Authority responds to emergencies that affect drinking water. It works with the Crisis Communications Annex (Annex C) and the Cyber Incident Plan.

## Section 3 Notification

### 3.1 Regulators

The Duty Manager must notify the State EPA within 1 hour of a confirmed loss of treatment. The Duty Manager must notify the County Health Department within 24 hours of any boil water advisory.

### 3.2 Law enforcement

The Duty Manager must notify the Harrow County Sheriff within 60 minutes of a confirmed intrusion at a storage site.

## Section 4 Activation

The Emergency Operations Center is activated at Level 2 when more than one pressure zone loses service. The Incident Commander decides when to stand it down.

## Section 5 Public Information

All public releases must be approved by the General Manager. The Public Information Officer drafts them from the templates in Annex C.

## Section 6 Hazardous Materials

The Harrow Fire Department is the lead agency for hazardous materials releases at Authority sites. Authority staff isolate the area and give the responders the chemical inventory.
