# Harrow Valley Water Authority — Cyber Incident Plan

> Test fixture (#423). The Harrow Valley Water Authority, its people and numbers are fictional.

## 1 Scope

This plan covers incidents on the control network and the business network.

## 2 Escalation

The Emergency Operations Center is activated at Level 3 when more than one pressure zone loses service. The IT Security Lead joins the EOC as the cyber advisor.

## 3 Notification

The Duty Manager must notify the Harrow County Sheriff within 1 hour of a confirmed intrusion at a storage site. The Duty Manager must notify the State Drinking Water Program within 2 hours of a confirmed data breach.

## 4 Hazardous Materials

The Harrow Police Department is the lead agency for hazardous materials releases at Authority sites. Operators shut down chemical feed pumps before leaving the building.
