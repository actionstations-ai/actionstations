# Harrow Valley Water Authority — Crisis Communications Annex (Annex C)

> Test fixture (#423). The Harrow Valley Water Authority, its people and numbers are fictional.

## C.1 Approval

All public releases must be approved by the Public Information Officer. The General Manager is briefed before any release that names a cause.

## C.2 Templates

Templates for boil water, do not drink and do not use notices are kept on the shared drive. See Annex F for the translated versions.

## C.3 Regulators

The Public Information Officer coordinates wording with the State EPA. The Duty Manager must notify the State EPA within 4 hours of a confirmed loss of treatment.
