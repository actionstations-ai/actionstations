# Election offices

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `elections` domain profile: a fictional county or state election office with voter registration, mail ballots, vote centers and drop boxes, certified voting systems at central count, election-night results reporting and the canvass.

Political neutrality: scenarios use fictional jurisdictions, contests and candidates, and never name a real party, candidate or election outcome.

Facts below were checked on 2026-10-24. Election law is mostly state law and varies, so check your state's rules before relying on a deadline.

| Accessibility                                                                 | HAVA §301(a)(3); ADA Title II                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Deadlines and retention

- Election records for federal elections must be kept for 22 months from the election (52 U.S.C. 20701); willful failure carries a fine of up to $1,000, up to one year of imprisonment, or both. Preserve them through any incident.
- There is no federal cyber-incident reporting mandate for election offices. Many states require reporting to the Secretary of State; check yours. Federal contacts are on the EAC Election Incident Response Contacts sheet (March 2026, v2.0).
