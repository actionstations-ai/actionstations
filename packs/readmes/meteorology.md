# Meteorology

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `meteorology` domain profile: a weather forecast and warning team, as a forecast office, a broadcast weather team or a meteorologist embedded with emergency management or a utility. It pairs with the Weather toolkit, which carries the product formats and realism rules; turn both on for a warning-operations exercise.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `meteorology`:** player roles for the three kinds of team (forecast office: lead forecaster, warning forecaster, mesoanalyst, decision support meteorologist, warning coordination meteorologist, hydrologist, science and operations officer, meteorologist in charge, communications forecaster; broadcast: chief meteorologist, on-air meteorologist, weather producer, digital producer, news director; embedded meteorologist), plus a county emergency manager and a public information officer. Systems cover forecaster workstations, radar, satellite, soundings, model guidance and dissemination.
- **Threat families:** convective outbreak, flash flood, tropical cyclone, winter storm, extreme heat, fire weather, aviation weather, and weather service degradation (radar outage, dissemination failure, office evacuation and backup, staffing shortfall, misinformation). Warning operations follow outlook, environment, watch, warnings, reports and verification, then survey and recovery.
- **Core capabilities** shown first in the evaluation guide: Public Information and Warning, Situational Assessment, Operational Communications, Planning and Operational Coordination.

## Notes

Products in an exercise are exercise products and are marked as such. The exercising organization, its people and its local businesses are fictional; real forecast office identifiers and real weather events can be used.

## Evaluation

The Public Information and Warning, Situational Assessment, Operational Communications and Planning capabilities are shown first in the evaluation guide. The writer is asked for checkable critical tasks in each hazard (a warning with the right hazard, polygon, duration and tags; a basis recorded before each decision; products transmitted and receipt confirmed; staffing and sectors planned ahead), and the guides cite your own plans and directives when you add them. The weather toolkit scores warnings with probability of detection, false alarm ratio, critical success index and lead time, and hurricane evacuation orders against the arrival of tropical-storm-force winds.
