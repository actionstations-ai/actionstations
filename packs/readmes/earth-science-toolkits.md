# Earth science toolkits

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds four toolkits any exercise can turn on, whatever kind of organization it is for: earthquakes and early warning, volcanoes and ash, tsunamis, and landslides and debris flows. Each carries the product templates, outside parties, advisor roles, decisions, timing measures and writing guidance of its field, and names the figures, public data sources and format checks the app provides for it.

Products are exercise products: every template says so, and simulated or replayed data is labeled as such wherever it appears. The exercising organization, people and private businesses in a scenario are fictional; real geography and the national scientific agencies appear as outside parties.

## Data services (contacted only when you fetch an event)

No account or key is needed, and nothing is requested until you agree to a fetch.

- USGS earthquake catalog, ShakeMap, "Did You Feel It?" and PAGER products, and the USGS ShakeMap scenario catalog (U.S. government work).
- FDSN data centers for seismograms: EarthScope, the Southern California Earthquake Data Center and the Northern California Earthquake Data Center. At most three downloads at a time; each dataset cites its network and data center.
- USGS Hazards Notification System (volcano notices for aviation, past year).
- NOAA tsunami database, tide gauges (CO-OPS) and DART buoys (National Data Buoy Center).
- USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database and USGS post-fire debris-flow assessments on ScienceBase.

## Copyrighted references (not bundled)

- ICAO Annex 3 and Doc 9691 (volcanic ash provisions and manual): https://www.icao.int/
- The USGS fact sheets on volcano alert levels and the HayWired scenario are carried by the Earth sciences reference library pack.
