# Campuses

Domain pack for a college or university: academic buildings, residence halls, research labs, a central plant, campus police and large events.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a campus; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `campus`:** buildings, lots and labs as fact categories. Systems: the SIS, LMS, ERP, identity and SSO, financial aid systems, the campus card, research computing, building automation, PACS, the central utility plant, sirens, mass notification and lab safety systems. Player roles: Emergency Manager, CISO, University Communications, Campus Police, Dean of Students, Clery Compliance Officer, Facilities and EHS (Responsible Official).

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Clery Act** (34 CFR 668.46): emergency notification without delay on confirmation of a significant emergency or dangerous situation, timely warnings, the daily crime log, missing-student notification, and the Annual Security Report by October 1.
- **Emergency operations plan** (REMS IHE guide, FEMA CPG 101 v3.1).
- **GLBA Safeguards Rule** (16 CFR 314): an incident response plan, and notice to the FTC within **30 days** of discovering an event that affects 500 or more consumers.
- **Federal Student Aid** breach reporting under the institution's participation agreement.
- **Select agents** (42 CFR 73.19): theft, loss or release reported immediately, Form 3 within seven days.
- **FERPA** health-or-safety emergency disclosures (34 CFR 99.36).
