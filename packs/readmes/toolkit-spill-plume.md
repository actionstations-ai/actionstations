# Toolkit: spill and plume

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `spill_plume` toolkit: the terms, product formats and realism rules for a release of oil or a hazardous substance, the outside parties and advisor roles (Scientific Support Coordinator, Wildlife Branch Director, Environmental Unit Leader, OSRO Duty Manager), and thirteen exercise products drawn as templates, so every word on them is exact and legible.

Products: ICS 202 incident objectives, ICS 204 assignment list, ICS 232 resources at risk summary, ICS 233 open action tracker, ICS 234 work analysis matrix, a Unified Command meeting agenda, an aerial observation report, a shoreline survey report, a countermeasure request and decision, a wildlife status report, a closure order (fishing, shoreline or water intake), a Joint Information Center claims notice and the National Response Center report record.

The ICS products are exercise products modeled on the structure of the public forms. They are marked as exercise products and are not the official forms; use the current Coast Guard or EPA version for a real incident. Operators, people and phone numbers in the samples are fictional.

Turn the toolkit on for a scenario from its **Toolkits** tab. It is suggested for the environmental response, port, chemical and industrial domains, and for briefs that mention an oil spill or a worst case discharge.
