# Earth sciences

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `geoscience` domain profile for exercising the teams that monitor, forecast and warn for earthquakes, volcanoes, tsunamis and landslides, and the partners who act on their warnings. One profile serves four kinds of organization, each a set of roles within it:

- a volcano observatory (scientist-in-charge, volcano monitoring scientist);
- a seismic network with earthquake early warning operations (duty seismologist, early warning operations lead);
- a tsunami warning center (tsunami warning duty scientist);
- a geological survey (landslide and geologic hazards geologist).

Network engineering, hazard communication, emergency management liaison and building inspection roles are shared. The exercising organization, people and private businesses in a scenario are fictional; real geography and the national scientific agencies appear as outside parties.

## Threat families

Earthquake, volcanic unrest and eruption, tsunami, post-fire debris flow, ground failure after an earthquake, and monitoring or alerting degradation (outages, false and missed alerts). Each has a four-phase arc, decision points and clue kinds written for the specialist, for example that a volcano alert level always travels with its aviation color code, that magnitude and intensity are never interchanged, and that a tsunami watch is not sent as a Wireless Emergency Alert.
