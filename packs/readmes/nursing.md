# Nursing and clinical care

Domain pack and toolkit for nurses and nursing programs: the unit and the classroom view of a hospital, a long-term care facility and a nursing simulation lab.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise at the unit level; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `nursing`:** builds on the healthcare profile (outside parties, systems and the hospital regulation list are repeated, so a project can run on either) and adds three role sets: a hospital nursing unit (nurse manager, charge nurse, house supervisor, staff nurse, rapid response nurse, infection prevention, nurse informaticist), a long-term care facility (administrator, director of nursing, wing charge nurse, maintenance) and a simulation lab (clinical instructor, simulation operator, learner lead, program director, placement coordinator).
- **Scenario families:** unit surge and mass casualty, unit evacuation, outbreak on a unit, medication error and disclosure, staffing crisis, active threat on a unit, downtime care, heat and power loss in long-term care, and a clinical event family for unfolding case studies.
- **Toolkit `clinical_care`:** the terms, product conventions and realism rules of bedside care (vital signs, lab results with reference ranges, SBAR handoffs, triage with START and SALT, staffing grids), the advisors a facilitator can add, and nine structured decisions: assign nurse, call rapid response, escalate a staffing shortage, set an evacuation order, triage an arrival, isolate or cohort, request a transfer, disclose an event and notify.

## Exercise content, not clinical guidance

Every patient, hospital and facility in an exercise is fictional. The pack is for rehearsing decisions and teaching; it is not a clinical reference, a drug reference or decision support for real patients, and it does not reproduce licensed exam items.

## Reference library

The separate **Nursing reference library** pack carries the AHRQ TeamSTEPPS pocket guide (SBAR, handoffs, briefs and debriefs), the AHRQ CANDOR implementation guide with its disclosure checklist and case scenarios, the ASPR TRACIE collections on evacuation and sheltering, mass casualty triage and patient movement, the NCSBN simulation guidelines, the Royal College of Physicians NEWS2 report and scoring chart, CMS Appendix Z and the FEMA guidance on healthcare facilities and power outages. Documents that are not US federal works carry their publisher's terms, stated in each entry. The AHRQ evacuation decision guide (archived copies only), the CDC isolation precautions guideline, the NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model and the INACSL standards are linked; the library README lists each with the reason.
