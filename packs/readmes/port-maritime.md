# Ports and maritime

Domain pack for a seaport or marine terminal regulated under MTSA: berths and piers, cranes and yard equipment, the terminal operating system, truck gates, vessels, and the Coast Guard Captain of the Port.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a port; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `port_maritime`:** berths, piers and terminals, cranes, truck gates and yard blocks as fact categories. Systems: the TOS, the gate appointment system, gate OCR and RFID, CBP-facing systems, ship-to-shore crane PLCs and vendor remote access, liquid bulk SCADA, PACS and TWIC readers, AIS and radar, and shore power. Player roles: Port Operations, Facility Security Officer, IT/OT, External Affairs, Terminal Operator, Cybersecurity Officer, Port Police and Emergency Manager.

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Facility Security Plan** (33 CFR 105): drills at least every three months; an exercise each calendar year, no more than 18 months apart, which may be a tabletop (105.220).
- **USCG cyber rule** (33 CFR 101 subpart F, effective 16 Jul 2025): a Cybersecurity Officer, a Cybersecurity Plan and a Cyber Incident Response Plan; reportable cyber incidents go to the **National Response Center without delay**.
- **Security incidents:** breaches of security and suspicious activity go to the National Response Center without delay (33 CFR 101.305); under 33 CFR 6.16-1, evidence of a cyber incident is reported immediately to the FBI, CISA and the COTP (NVIC 02-24 CH1).
- **Area Maritime Security Plan** (33 CFR 103), led by the COTP and exercised by the Area Maritime Security Committee.
- **Drones:** since 1 Jul 2026 (SAFER SKIES Act interim final rule), certified state and local law-enforcement agencies may detect and mitigate credible UAS threats to critical infrastructure, alongside DOJ and DHS.
