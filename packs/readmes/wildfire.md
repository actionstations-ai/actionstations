# Wildfire and power shutoff

Domain pack for wildland-urban interface fire, public safety power shutoffs and wildfire smoke: the organizations that plan for, fight or are affected by fire where wildland fuels meet communities, and the electric utilities whose operations can start or avoid fires.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise wildfire; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `wildfire`:** a fire district or agency, a county wildfire and evacuation program (emergency management with the sheriff) and a utility wildfire and power shutoff program, with their roles, systems, outside parties, media channels and fact categories for evacuation zones, circuits, shelters, traffic control points and community resource centers.
- **Threat families:** wildland-urban interface fire, red flag wind event and power shutoff, smoke and air quality episode, resource shortage, flood and debris flow after a fire, and degradation of alerting and communications.
- **Toolkits it suggests:** weather (red flag warnings and fire weather), flooding, debris flows and the media environment.

## Things the pack knows

- A perimeter is a mapped polygon at a time, not a continuous record of the fire.
- Evacuation status words (order, warning, shelter in place, none) must match on the map, in the zone table and in every message.
- Power shutoff notification windows are an example rule from one state (48 to 72 hours for public safety partners, 24 to 48 hours for customers, 1 to 4 hours before, at de-energization, just before re-energization begins and when it is complete). Change them for your project.

All organizations, people, circuits and customers in examples are fictional.
