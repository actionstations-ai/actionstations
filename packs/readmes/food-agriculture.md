# Food and agriculture

Domain pack for food processors and agricultural enterprises: meat and poultry plants under USDA FSIS inspection, FDA-regulated food and beverage manufacturers, cold storage and distribution, feed mills, grain and farm-supply cooperatives, and the farms, feedlots, dairies and poultry operations they serve. Restaurants and grocery retail are covered from the retail side in the Commercial facilities pack.

Facts below were checked on 2026-10-24 for the research dossier (`docs/research/domains/food_agriculture.md` in the Action Stations repository). Regulations and editions change; re-check a deadline before an exercise depends on it.

## Plans the exercise should draw on

- **Food defense plan** for FDA-registered food facilities (21 CFR part 121, the intentional adulteration rule): a written plan (121.126) with a vulnerability assessment of each point or step to find actionable process steps (121.130), mitigation strategies (121.135), food defense monitoring (121.140), corrective actions (121.145), verification (121.150), reanalysis at least once every 3 years and on significant change (121.157), qualified individuals (121.4) and records (121.301–121.330). Very small businesses, most farm activities and some holding and packing activities are exempt (121.5).
- **Food safety plan and recall plan** under preventive controls (21 CFR part 117 for human food, part 507 for animal food).
- **FSIS functional food defense plan** (voluntary; FSIS Form 5420-5 _General Food Defense Plan_).
- **Ammonia refrigeration process safety**: OSHA PSM (29 CFR 1910.119) and the EPA Risk Management Program (40 CFR part 68).
- **Grain handling facilities** (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.272): emergency action plan, grain-dust housekeeping, hot-work permits, bin entry.

## Reporting deadlines

- **FSIS notification (9 CFR 418.2)**: an establishment must "promptly notify the local FSIS District Office within 24 hours" of learning or determining that adulterated or misbranded product has entered commerce, with type, amount, origin and destination. Verified against eCFR.
- **Reportable Food Registry (21 U.S.C. 350f)**: report "as soon as practicable, but in no case later than 24 hours" after determining a food is a reportable food. The clock comes from FDA's 2008 Federal Register summary of the statute.
- **Select agent theft, loss or release (42 CFR 73.19)**: immediate notice to APHIS or CDC, and Form 3 within 7 days. Verified for 42 CFR 73.19; the APHIS parts parallel it.
- **CIRCIA**: the final rule was not published as of 2026-10-24.
