# Epidemiology and outbreak investigation

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `epidemiology` domain profile: a fictional epidemiology program, hospital infection prevention team or public health laboratory working an outbreak from the first report to control measures, communication and after-action. It extends the `public_health` profile, which describes the health department as an agency (operations center, health orders, dispensing).

Facts below were checked on 2026-11-04. Reporting rules are mostly state law and vary.

## Threat families

Foodborne outbreak, respiratory pathogen with community spread, vaccine-preventable exposure, healthcare-associated infection cluster, mass prophylaxis, surveillance signal, laboratory surge and surveillance service degradation. The foodborne arc follows the investigation steps in CDC's Field Epidemiology Manual: signal, verify and define cases, describe by time, place and person, hypothesis, control, communicate, close.

## Scope

The profile stays at public health response decisions: case definitions, counts, onset dates, exposures, control measures and messages. It gives no pathogen handling, culture or enhancement detail, and every person and place in a scenario is fictional.

## Exercise requirements the profile assumes

- CDC Public Health Emergency Preparedness (PHEP) 2024-2028 cooperative agreement and its Exercise Framework supplemental guidance (April 2024): Biological Incident and Capstone tracks, Critical Contacts Drill. Improvement plan data elements are due in CDC's reporting system within 90 days of an exercise.
- Hospital infection prevention exercises follow CMS emergency preparedness (42 CFR 482.15) and accreditation; see the `healthcare` pack.
