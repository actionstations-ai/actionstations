# Lab safety toolkit

Included with Action Stations. A toolkit any scenario can turn on, whatever the organization: a research university, a hospital laboratory, a public health laboratory, a school science department or a food and agriculture lab.

## What it adds

- Writing guidance: the roles and the notification vocabulary of a biosafety program, the select agent immediate notice and 7-calendar-day written report, the NIH reporting duties (with the draft policy marked as a draft), and what a records discrepancy usually turns out to be.
- Content guardrails: a documented rule set that keeps technique, growth, production, enhancement, modification, quantities, strains and agent-specific procedures out of every generated or bundled product. An inject that fails the check goes back to the model; a pack that fails it is not loaded.

## Limits

The toolkit exercises incident management, notification, continuity and compliance. It is not a biosafety manual, and its worksheets are not the official forms. A biosafety officer should review the clocks, templates and rules before use. Every person and place is fictional.
