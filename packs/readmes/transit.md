# Transit

Domain pack for a public transit agency: bus, light rail, metro and paratransit, with an operations control center, CAD/AVL, fare collection and transit police.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a transit agency; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `transit`:** routes, lines, vehicles, stops and depots as fact categories. Systems: CAD/AVL, the fare back office, GTFS-RT, paratransit dispatch, signaling and train control (CBTC, PTC), traction power SCADA, tunnel ventilation, fare gates and P25 radio. Player roles: Operations, IT/OT, PIO, Transit Police, Fleet Maintenance, Chief Safety Officer and Cybersecurity Coordinator.

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Public Transportation Agency Safety Plan** (49 CFR 673).
- **State Safety Oversight** (49 CFR 674): notify FTA and the SSOA within **two hours** of a qualifying rail safety event (a fatality, two or more injuries, a derailment, certain collisions, an evacuation for life safety, unintended train movement).
- **TSA SD 1582-21-01E** (effective 16 Jan 2026, expires 15 Jan 2027) for passenger rail and rail transit: a Cybersecurity Coordinator; cybersecurity incidents reported to CISA as soon as practicable and **no later than 72 hours** after identification (earlier editions said 24 hours); a Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan exercised at least once a year.
- **Significant security concerns** go to TSA within **24 hours** (49 CFR 1570.203).
- **National Transit Database:** cyber events are reportable security events; major events go on the S&S-40.
