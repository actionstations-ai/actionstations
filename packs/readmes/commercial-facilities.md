# Commercial facilities

Domain pack for places people shop, stay and gamble: malls and their tenants, big-box, grocery and department-store chains, hotels and resorts, commercial and tribal casinos, and mixed-use towers run by property managers. Stadiums, arenas, convention centers and outdoor events are in the Stadiums and venues pack.

Facts below were checked on 2026-10-24 for the research dossier (`docs/research/domains/commercial_facilities.md` in the Action Stations repository). Regulations and editions change; re-check a deadline before an exercise depends on it.

## Plans the exercise should draw on

- **Emergency Action Plan** for employees (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.38): reporting emergencies, evacuation procedures and routes, critical operations, employee accounting, rescue and medical duties, alarms.
- **Life-safety and fire plans** for assembly and hotel occupancies under the locally adopted fire and life-safety codes.
- **Active assailant emergency action plan**: voluntary; the CISA _Active Assailant EAP Template_ and _Instructional Guide_ (February 2025) cover notification, evacuation and lockdown, accountability, roles, training and recovery.
- **Casino cybersecurity incident response plan** (Nevada Gaming Commission Regulation 5.260 for Nevada licensees; other states set their own rules).
- **Casino BSA/AML program** (31 CFR 1021) and, for tribal gaming, the NIGC minimum internal control standards (25 CFR 543) and the tribal-state compact.
- **Payment-card incident response plan** (PCI DSS requirement 12.10, contractual through the card brands).

## Reporting deadlines

- **Nevada casino cyber incident (Regulation 5.260, amended and effective January 29, 2026)**: notify the Board Chair "as soon as practicable but no later than 24 hours after activating the response procedures" of the incident response plan; submit the initial cybersecurity incident response report within 5 calendar days; send written updates every 30 days until resolved; then an investigation report on root cause. Verified from the adopted regulation.
- **Casino suspicious activity report (31 CFR 1021.320)**: file "no later than 30 calendar days after the date of the initial detection". Verified against eCFR.
- **Public-company cyber disclosure (SEC Form 8-K Item 1.05)**: four business days after the materiality determination.
- **Customer data breach**: notice to individuals and attorneys general on each state's timeline (all 50 states have a statute).
- **Card brands**: acquirer and brand notice on contractual timelines once a compromise is suspected.
- **CIRCIA**: final rule pending; whether commercial facilities are covered depends on the final sector criteria.
