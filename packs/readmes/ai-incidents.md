# AI incidents and synthetic media

Domain pack for the teams that face the two new incident types of the AI era: synthetic media attacks (a deepfaked executive authorizing a payment, a fake spokesperson video, synthetic misinformation about the organization) and failures of AI systems (harmful output to customers, data poisoning, an AI vendor breach).

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise this field; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `ai_incidents`** with four variants as role sets: an enterprise security and fraud team, an AI governance and product team, a trust and safety team on a platform, and a board. Roles include the AI governance lead, CISO, fraud investigator, treasury lead, trust and safety lead, model owner, product owner, communications, general counsel, vendor risk manager, executive, board member and the impersonated executive.
- **Six threat families with phase arcs:** synthetic impersonation fraud, synthetic media about the organization, AI system malfunction or misuse, data poisoning and model tampering, AI vendor or supply chain breach, and AI service degradation (a monitoring blind spot, an expired evaluation pipeline, a detector outage).

The **AI incidents toolkit** pack adds the writing guidance, products, decisions and measures that any other domain (financial services, elections, campus, healthcare, municipal, telecom and more) can turn on. The **Media environment** toolkit supplies verification, social spread and press.

## Limits

This pack gives no legal advice. Which reporting duties apply depends on the organization's role (provider, deployer or victim of a fraud) and its jurisdiction; the exercise names the question and the plan to check. Detector scores in an exercise are simulated, and a detector can be wrong. Every person, bank, vendor and company in an exercise is fictional, and no real person's voice or face is imitated.
