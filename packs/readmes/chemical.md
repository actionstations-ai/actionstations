# Chemical facilities

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `chemical` domain profile: a fictional chemical plant, distributor, terminal or fertilizer site holding RMP- or PSM-regulated quantities, with a DCS and a separate safety instrumented system, tank farms and loading racks, an emergency response team and a fence-line community. The `chemical` alias now resolves here instead of to the industrial profile; general manufacturing stays `industrial`.

Facts below were checked on 2026-10-24. Rules change, so check the current text before relying on a deadline.

## Plans and programs the profile assumes

| Plan or duty                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Basis                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Risk Management Plan: hazard assessment, prevention program, emergency response program, 5-year accident history; corrections within 6 months of a reportable accident                                                                                             | Clean Air Act §112(r); 40 CFR part 68 (68.42, 68.195)                                                       |
| RMP emergency response exercises: annual notification exercise; a tabletop exercise before 21 Dec 2026 and then at least every 3 years; a field exercise before 15 Mar 2027 and then at least every 10 years; an evaluation report within 90 days of each exercise | 40 CFR 68.90 to 68.96. An EPA proposal published 24 Feb 2026 may change these dates if finalized.           |

## Reporting deadlines

- Release at or above a reportable quantity: the National Response Center immediately (CERCLA §103, 40 CFR 302.6), and the LEPC community emergency coordinator and the SERC immediately, with written follow-up (EPCRA §304, 40 CFR 355.42).
- Work-related fatality to OSHA within 8 hours; in-patient hospitalization, amputation or loss of an eye within 24 hours (29 CFR 1904.39).
- There is no sector cyber-incident reporting mandate.
