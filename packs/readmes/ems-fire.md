# EMS, fire and hazardous materials

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `ems_fire` domain profile (an EMS agency, a fire department, a hazmat team, the dispatchers who send them, and the healthcare coalition liaison on the hospital side) and toolkits any other kind of organization can turn on: a chemical plant, a stadium, a railroad or a hospital can add a realistic street response.

All people, agencies, hospitals and patients in the templates and examples are fictional. Everything here is exercise material: it is not clinical guidance, hazmat guidance or fire tactics for a real incident.

## Toolkits

| Toolkit                  | What it brings                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mass casualty and triage | Triage tags, MCI patient list, Medical Branch worksheet, treatment area assignments, patient transportation log, hospital call record, and the decisions a Medical Group makes. |

## Triage methods

The pack knows START and SALT and a free-text local method. No federal rule picks one method for the country, so a scenario names the method its players use. Check your state or regional protocol before relying on any of it.

## The Emergency Response Guidebook is not bundled

The guide pages and the table of initial isolation and protective action distances in the Emergency Response Guidebook are not included until the publisher's terms for redistribution are confirmed. Where an exercise needs those numbers, you enter them from your own copy of the guidebook, or you use a fictional material with invented, clearly labeled exercise distances. The guidebook is free from the US Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration: https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/hazmat/erg/emergency-response-guidebook-erg
