# Federal continuity (COOP)

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `federal_continuity` domain profile: a fictional federal executive branch department, agency or component exercising its continuity plan. Scenarios cover activation, relocation to an alternate site, devolution of command and control to another office, performance of mission essential functions (MEFs) and primary mission essential functions (PMEFs), orders of succession, delegations of authority, essential records, continuity communications and reconstitution.

The profile uses a fictional agency and fictional officials. Real geography is fine. The reference library (`ai.actionstations.federal-continuity-library`, catalog only) holds the public-domain FEMA directives and templates listed below.

Facts below were checked on 2026-11-02 against the August 2024 Federal Continuity Directive (FCD) text. FEMA revises these documents, so check the current edition before relying on a requirement.

## Exercise requirements the scenarios test

- Each year (federal fiscal year, 1 October to 30 September), personnel assigned to a PMEF validate that required capabilities work from the alternate site, devolution site or other assigned location.
- Each PMEF is exercised annually and each MEF at least every two years, using one or more continuity options in the plan.
- Each year the organization exercises its ability to sustain command and control.
- Findings go in an after-action report validated by the Continuity Coordinator and the mission owner of each function exercised, and the report is made available to FEMA's Office of National Continuity Programs (ONCP).
- Exercise plans, after-action reports and corrective action plans are kept for the biennial assessment.

## Continuity-focused evaluation capabilities

The profile lists the FEMA Core Capabilities the evaluation guide prompt shows first: Planning, Operational Coordination, Operational Communications, Public Information and Warning, Situational Assessment, Infrastructure Systems, Logistics and Supply Chain Management and Cybersecurity. The critical tasks come from the agency's own continuity plan.
