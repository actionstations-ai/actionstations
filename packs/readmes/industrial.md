# Industrial and manufacturing

Domain pack for a manufacturing or process plant: production lines and process units, a DCS/SCADA control room, safety instrumented systems, hazardous materials, a plant emergency response team and the fenceline community.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a plant; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `industrial`:** process units and lines, tanks, areas and controllers as fact categories. Systems: ERP/MES, WMS, LIMS and QMS, CMMS, label printing, DCS/SCADA and HMIs, PLCs and SIS, burner management, engineering workstations, the OT DMZ and remote-access broker, OT monitoring, robots and CNC, CEMS, utilities and muster accountability. Player roles: Plant Manager, Shift Supervisor, OT/IT Security, EHS, Community Relations, Process Control Engineer, Process Safety Engineer, ERT Chief and Site Security.

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Emergency action plan** (29 CFR 1910.38), **PSM** (1910.119: management of change, pre-startup safety review, incident investigation started within 48 hours) and **HAZWOPER emergency response** (1910.120(q)).
- **RMP emergency response program** (40 CFR 68): a notification exercise every year, a **tabletop exercise before 21 Dec 2026 and then at least every three years**, a field exercise before 15 Mar 2027 and then every ten years.
- **Releases:** the National Response Center immediately for a reportable quantity (40 CFR 302.6), the SERC and LEPC immediately (40 CFR 355.40); the Chemical Safety Board within **8 hours** of a release causing a fatality, serious injury or substantial damage (40 CFR 1604.3).
- **OSHA:** a fatality within **8 hours**, an in-patient hospitalization, amputation or eye loss within **24 hours** (29 CFR 1904.39).
- **Cyber:** defense suppliers report to DoD within **72 hours** (DFARS 252.204-7012); public companies disclose a material incident on Form 8-K. CFATS lapsed in July 2023; CISA's voluntary ChemLock program took its place.
