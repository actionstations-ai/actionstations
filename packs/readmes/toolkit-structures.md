# Toolkit: structures and building safety

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `structures` toolkit: the terms, product formats and realism rules for construction site emergencies and post-disaster building evaluation, three advisor roles (structural engineer, construction safety director, building safety evaluator), and twelve exercise products drawn as templates, so every word is exact and legible. It also offers the building safety placard that comes with the earthquake toolkit.

Products: daily excavation inspection record, crane inspection with lift plan and wind log, incident or near-miss report, stop-work order, OSHA report record, RFI or change request, unsafe building order, rapid and detailed evaluation forms, fixed equipment checklist, inspection assignment log and utility strike report. The Construction pack adds the OSHA reporting clocks (8 hours for a work-related fatality, 24 hours for an in-patient hospitalization, amputation or loss of an eye).

The evaluation forms and checklist are new exercise templates that follow the structure of the public evaluation methods; they are not the official forms. Every product is marked as an exercise and is not a safety evaluation of any real structure. Contractors, engineers, workers and buildings in the samples are fictional.

The toolkit checks placard words and colors (UNSAFE is red, never written by color alone), the OSHA clock for the event named, the excavation rules and that only the building official declares a building unsafe, and that a crane pick stops at the wind limit.

Turn the toolkit on for a scenario from its **Toolkits** tab. It is suggested for the construction domain, for industrial, utility, transport, campus, school, healthcare and municipal domains, and for briefs that mention a trench, a crane collapse or a building inspection.
