# Freight railroads

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `freight_rail` domain profile: a fictional Class I, regional or short-line railroad with its subdivisions, yards, dispatching center (CTC and PTC), hazmat traffic, railroad police and the towns along the line. Exercises are about the railroad's own people; real railroads appear only as interchange partners or vendors.

Facts below were checked on 2026-10-24. Security directives expire and rules change, so check the current text before relying on a deadline.

## Reporting deadlines

- Cybersecurity incident to CISA Central: as soon as practicable, no later than 72 hours after identification (SD 1580-21-01E). TSA accepts the CISA report. The earlier SD 1580-21-01C required 24 hours.
- Potential threats and significant security concerns to TSA: within 24 hours of initial discovery (49 CFR 1570.203).
- Qualifying hazmat incident to the National Response Center by phone: as soon as practical, no later than 12 hours (49 CFR 171.15).
- Listed accidents and incidents to the National Response Center by phone (49 CFR 225.9).

A TSA proposed rule, Enhancing Surface Cyber Risk Management (89 FR 88488, 7 Nov 2024), had no final rule as of 2026-10-24.
