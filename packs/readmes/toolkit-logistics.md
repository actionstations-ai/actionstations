# Supply chain and logistics toolkit

Included with Action Stations. A toolkit any scenario can turn on, whatever the organization: a manufacturer, a distributor, a hospital materials office, a utility's spare parts store, a retailer or a school's food service.

## What it adds

- Writing guidance: days of supply, reorder points, supplier tiers, force majeure, demurrage and detention, traceability lot codes, recall classes, depth and effectiveness check levels, with the rules that records must reconcile.
- Eight structured decisions: allocate supply, switch source, reroute or change mode, hold or quarantine product, recall decision, notify customers, escalate to the executive team, submit a trace result.
- Three advisers: supply chain, recall and traceability, freight and port.

## Limits

The toolkit stays at coordination level and gives no pathogen handling detail. The FSMA 204 compliance date is shown with its status, not as settled. A supply chain director and a food safety manager should review the content before use. Every company, carrier and customer is fictional.
