# Public health departments

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `public_health` domain profile: a fictional local, district or state health department with epidemiology and surveillance, a public health laboratory, environmental health, clinics and immunizations, medical countermeasure dispensing, and the health officer's orders. Hospitals and care delivery are the `healthcare` profile.

Facts below were checked on 2026-10-24. Reporting rules are mostly state law and vary, so check your state's rules before relying on a deadline.

## Plans and programs the profile assumes

| Plan                                                              | Basis                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All-hazards public health emergency operations plan (ESF-8 annex) | PHEP cooperative agreement (PHS Act §319C-1) and the CDC Public Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Capabilities (October 2018, updated January 2019; 15 capabilities); the county EOP |
| Medical countermeasure dispensing and distribution plan (PODs)    | PHEP Capabilities 8 and 9                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Crisis and emergency risk communication plan                      | PHEP Capability 4; CDC CERC                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Pandemic and infectious disease plan                              | PHEP; state law                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Continuity of operations plan                                     | FEMA continuity guidance                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Laboratory biosafety and select agent plans                       | 42 CFR part 73                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Communicable disease reporting                                    | State law and administrative code                                                                                                                                                             |

## Reporting deadlines

- Notifiable-disease reporting runs from the health department to the state under state rules, and from the state to CDC through NNDSS. Timelines vary by state.
- A clinic that is a HIPAA covered component has the breach-notification clock described in the `healthcare` pack.
- CIRCIA is not in force.
