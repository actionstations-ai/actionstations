# Flooding and hydrology

Domain pack and toolkit for floods: river floods with a forecast crest, flash floods, levee problems, urban flooding, ice jams, floods after wildfire, and the failure of the tools that warn about them.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise floods; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `flooding`:** a county flood program (emergency management, floodplain administration, public works), a levee or drainage district and a regional flood warning program, with their roles, systems, outside parties and the seven flood threat families (river flood, flash flood, levee or dam breach, urban flooding, ice jam, flood after wildfire, and loss of flood warning services).
- **Toolkit `flooding`:** terms, product formats and realism rules for flood exercises, the outside parties and advisor roles floods bring in, and the inject types below. Any organization can turn the toolkit on: a wastewater plant, a farm cooperative, a camp or a school does not need the flood response domain.
- **Templates:** hydrologic outlook and flood advisory, flood forecast discussion, road closure and low-water crossing status, sandbag and pump request, substantial damage letter and damage assessment summary. Flood watches, warnings and statements use the NWS warning template that is already included.

## Things the toolkit knows

- Flood categories (minor, moderate, major) are set for each forecast point from local impacts; a point may not have all of them, and flood stage is not the same as bankfull.
- Flash flood warnings carry a damage threat tag: base, CONSIDERABLE or CATASTROPHIC. Wireless Emergency Alerts go out only for the last two, and CATASTROPHIC only comes with flash flood emergency language.
- National Weather Service water resources products follow Ready, Set, Go: outlook, watch, warning.

Facts were checked against NWS Manual 10-950 and NWS Instruction 10-922 on 2026-11-04. Check the current documents before relying on a definition.
