# Behavioral health and human services

Domain pack and toolkits for the people who help others on the worst days: crisis line centers (such as 988 centers), mass care shelter teams, family reunification teams, and child welfare and social service agencies.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise these services; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `human_services`:** roles, systems and outside parties for a crisis center, a shelter, a reunification site and a child welfare agency, and six threat families: crisis line surge, shelter operations, reunification, displaced children in care, responder and staff stress, and service degradation. No state is assumed.
- **Toolkit `behavioral_safeguards`:** the rules for sensitive material, described below. Any domain can turn it on.
- **Toolkit `crisis_line`:** daily contact report, network surge and backup notices, queue wait-time board, staffing coverage summary, supervisor escalation, de-identified contact summary and state coordinator briefing, with the 988 rerouting rules (dated March 2026).
- **Toolkit `mass_care`:** shelter status report, open shelters list, functional needs support request, registration form, daily count, incident report, health referral, closing report and EOC mass care request, with the rule that general population shelters integrate people with access and functional needs.
- **Toolkit `reunification_child_welfare`:** reunification plan and site layout, roster, release log, parent notification text, "where is my child" call script, unreleased student tracker, displaced children in care list, state disaster plan activation notice and crisis counseling request.
- **Templates:** 26 products for the three toolkits, plus a content notice card, a facilitator wellbeing check script and a psychological first aid handout. Every one carries the EXERCISE marking and uses exercise references, never real names.

## Safeguards for sensitive material

Exercises in this field can touch suicide, self-harm, abuse and the loss of a child. The pack treats that as part of the product, not an afterthought.

- **Safe messaging.** Generated injects are checked for method or means detail next to a suicide reference, for suicide presented as a solution, and for language safe messaging avoids ("committed suicide", "successful attempt"). A flagged inject goes back to the model with the problem named. The rules follow the Recommendations for Reporting on Suicide (SAVE with partner organizations, 2024) and the AFSP ethical reporting guidelines.
- **Exercise banner.** A public message or handout that shows a real helpline number must carry the word EXERCISE. Exercise materials never tell a player to call a real line; they use a fictional 555 number.
- **Content notice.** When a scenario touches these topics, the player briefing opens with a content notice, the Exercise Plan repeats it, and the controller handbook gives facilitators a wellbeing check and a "stop and step out" rule.
- **Psychological first aid.** The eight core actions are available as knowledge for the generator and as an original-summary handout.

The pack is an exercise aid. It gives no clinical guidance and has not been reviewed by a clinician; a review by a clinician with crisis line experience is planned before any example ships.
