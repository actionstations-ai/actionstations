# Animal disease toolkit

Included with Action Stations. A toolkit any scenario can turn on, whatever the organization: a state animal health office, a poultry integrator, a dairy cooperative, a county fair board, a county emergency manager or a health department.

## What it adds

- Ten inject templates: quarantine order, hold order or control area notice; movement permit request and decision; presumptive positive notice and confirmation; premises investigation form; indemnity claim worksheet; depopulation and disposal plan; producer letter; fair or show cancellation notice; consumer questions and answers; worker health monitoring log. It also uses the laboratory result report, situation report and press release already in the app.
- Writing guidance: premises designations and the zones they sit in, the HPAI zone sizes, the 24 hour depopulation goal, FMD sampling intervals, and the order of presumptive and confirmed results. Each rule names the response plan and edition it comes from.
- Format checks that send a draft back when a zone is under the plan minimum, a premises becomes monitored without being at-risk, or a confirmation comes from a network laboratory.

## Limits

The rules follow the HPAI Red Book (May 2017) and the FMD Red Book (October 2020). Follow your own state plan, which governs. A state veterinarian's office and an extension specialist should review the products before use. Every person, farm and place is fictional, and there is no pathogen handling detail.
