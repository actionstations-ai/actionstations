# Construction and the built environment

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `construction` domain profile: a general contractor's project team (executive, project manager, superintendent, safety director and the competent persons for excavations, scaffolds and cranes), the owner and design team (owner's representative, engineer of record, geotechnical engineer, special inspector), the building department and its post-disaster building evaluation program (building official, team leaders, volunteer evaluators and the emergency operations center that asks for them), and an engineering or construction management class that runs the same cases.

It adds six threat families: site emergency (crane, trench, fall, fire, utility strike), structural collapse and rescue support, post-disaster building inspection, professional dilemma (schedule against safety), site security and theft, and service degradation (a silent monitoring logger, a dead radio channel, an evaluator shortage or two conflicting placards). Each follows an arc from the first warning to investigation or closeout.

The profile keeps authority specific: the competent person and the superintendent stop work, the engineer of record decides structural adequacy, the building official declares a building unsafe, and rescue belongs to the fire department. It uses the three placard categories in words as well as color: INSPECTED, RESTRICTED USE and UNSAFE.

The products an exercise needs (inspection records, stop-work orders, evaluation forms, monitoring figures and the like) come with the structures toolkit in a later release. The earthquake and weather toolkits are suggested for shaking and wind.

Facts below were checked on 2026-11-05. Rules change, so check the current text before relying on a deadline. Summaries are not legal advice.

## Rules the profile assumes

| Rule                                                                                                                                             | Basis                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| A work-related fatality is reported to OSHA within 8 hours; an in-patient hospitalization, amputation or loss of an eye within 24 hours          | 29 CFR 1904.39        |
| Excavations need a protective system unless in stable rock or under 5 feet deep with no indication of a cave-in                                  | 29 CFR 1926.652(a)(1) |
| A competent person inspects excavations daily and after rainstorms or other hazard-increasing events; workers leave the hazard until it is fixed | 29 CFR 1926.651(k)    |
| Trenches 4 feet or deeper need a safe way out within 25 feet of lateral travel                                                                   | 29 CFR 1926.651(c)(2) |
