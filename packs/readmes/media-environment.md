# Media environment

Toolkit pack: the news cycle and social feeds around an incident, for any kind of exercise. Turn it on for a scenario on the scenario's Toolkits tab; every domain profile suggests it.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never want media, social media or press in your exercises; scenarios that use it skip it quietly.

## What is in the pack

- **Outside parties:** reporters, the wire service desk, the local TV news desk, a fact-checking organization, a platform trust and safety team, the public on social media and community group administrators.
- **Advisor roles** a domain can add: a media relations liaison and a social media monitor.
- **Decisions players make:** issue a holding statement, approve a release, respond to a reporter (on the record, background, decline or no comment), issue a correction, schedule a press conference, escalate to legal or the executive, request a platform takedown or label, hold or publish, and request a second source.
- **Writing guidance:** news and wire conventions, holding statement structure, correction practice, CERC principles, verification and Content Credentials caveats.
- **Existing templates reused:** social posts, news articles, press releases, reporter emails, call slips, voicemails, status pages and alerts.

## Teaching models

Simulated spread and sentiment are teaching models with documented parameters. They do not predict real behavior, and every simulated figure and counter is labeled simulated.

Every outlet, handle, reporter and spokesperson in an exercise is fictional, and no false claim in an exercise is about a real person, a real organization or a real ongoing event.
