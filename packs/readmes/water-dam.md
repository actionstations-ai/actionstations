# Dams and water authorities

Domain pack for the owner of a dam, reservoir and powerhouse (hydro and/or flood control): spillway gates, intakes, penstocks and generating units, SCADA, the dam safety program and the downstream communities.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a dam; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `water_dam`:** spillway gates, generating units, penstocks, intakes, RTUs, gauges, inundation zones and downstream communities as fact categories. Systems: dam SCADA, gate PLCs, unit controls, switchyard relays, instrumentation and the ADAS server, sirens, telemetry and inundation-map GIS. Player roles: Dam Operations, SCADA/IT, PIO, Security, Dam Safety Engineer, County Emergency Manager, EAP Coordinator and Reservoir / Water Control Manager.

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Emergency Action Plan:** FEMA P-64 (2013) for all dams, with the emergency levels High Flow, Non-failure, Potential failure and Imminent failure, a notification flowchart and inundation maps; 18 CFR Part 12 subpart C for FERC-licensed projects.
- **Safety-related incidents** at FERC-licensed projects: report to the FERC Regional Engineer as soon as practicable, preferably within **72 hours** (18 CFR 12.10).
- If the hydro plant is a NERC-registered generator, the CIP-008 one-hour cyber reporting in the Energy utilities pack applies.
