# Municipal government

Domain pack for a city or county government: the emergency operations center, public works, police and fire, 911 dispatch, city IT and the services residents depend on.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a local government; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `municipal`:** wards, stations and apparatus as fact categories. Systems: 911 CAD and RMS, ERP and payroll, utility billing, permitting and GIS, 311, property tax and courts, WebEOC-type EOC software, traffic signals, water SCADA, stormwater pumps, sirens and public-safety radio. Player roles: Emergency Management Director, Public Works, CIO, PIO, Police Chief, Mayor / County Executive, Fire Chief and Finance Director.

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Emergency operations plan** with ESF annexes (FEMA CPG 101 v3.1, May 2025), a cyber incident annex (FEMA _Planning Considerations for Cyber Incidents_, Nov 2023), COOP/COG, THIRA (CPG 201) and the hazard mitigation plan (44 CFR 201).
- **911 outages:** the 911 service provider notifies the PSAP as soon as possible and **no later than 30 minutes** after discovering an outage that may affect it, by phone and in writing (47 CFR 4.9(h)).
- **Local emergency declaration** by the chief elected official under state law, ratified by the council.
- **CJIS** incident reporting to the state CSA for police systems; state breach-notification laws for residents' data.
