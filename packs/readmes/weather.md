# Weather toolkit

Included with Action Stations. A toolkit gives any exercise the expertise of one field. This one adds weather: what a forecast, a watch, a warning and an advisory look like, the outside parties that matter (the forecast office, the Storm Prediction Center, the National Hurricane Center, spotters), an advisor role for a meteorologist liaison, and the realism rules a forecaster would check.

Turn it on for a scenario on the scenario's Toolkits tab. The app proposes it when a brief mentions a storm, a hurricane, a flood, a heat wave, a ground stop and similar words, and for the kinds of organization that most often need weather (airports, utilities, venues, schools and others).
