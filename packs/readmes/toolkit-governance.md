# Governance decisions toolkit

Included with Action Stations. A toolkit any scenario can turn on, whatever the organization: a manufacturer, a bank, a hospital system, a university, a utility or a business school case.

## What it adds

- Eleven inject templates: board briefing memo, materiality worksheet, Form 8-K disclosure draft (Item 1.05 and Item 8.01), business impact analysis table, ransom payment decision paper, legal hold and privilege notice, regulator inquiry or subpoena summary, insurer notice and auditor inquiry, investor questions and call talking points, decision log, and executive succession notice and authority matrix. It also uses the press release and regulatory incident report already in the app.
- Writing guidance: materiality (quantitative and qualitative factors), what Item 1.05 and Item 8.01 reports must and must not say, the four business days after a materiality determination, no delay of the determination by delaying a meeting, the Attorney General delay, and that paying a ransom does not remove the duty to decide on materiality.
- Format checks: every document carries the exercise-aid line; the company is fictional with no real name or trading symbol; an Item 1.05 draft states nature, scope, timing and material impact; an Item 8.01 draft does not claim materiality; every decision records who decided, when and on what basis.

## Limits

Exercise aid, not legal advice: check the current rule and your counsel. The toolkit does not decide materiality for the user and files nothing. Every company, person, regulator and adviser in an exercise is fictional.
