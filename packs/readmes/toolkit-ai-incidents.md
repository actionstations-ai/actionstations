# Toolkit: AI incidents and synthetic media

Included with Action Stations. A toolkit any scenario can turn on, whatever the organization: a bank, a county, a campus, a hospital, a telecom carrier or a software company.

## What it adds

- Writing guidance for deepfake, voice cloning and AI system incidents: how a lure works, why a detector score needs its uncertainty, why a missing content credential proves nothing, why a callback uses the directory number, what an AI incident and a serious incident are, and why duties differ between a provider, a deployer and a fraud victim.
- Outside parties and two advisors: an AI governance advisor and a fraud advisor.
- It reuses the Media environment toolkit for verification, social spread and press.

## Limits

No legal advice. Every person, bank, vendor and company is fictional. The exercise never imitates a real person.
