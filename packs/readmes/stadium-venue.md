# Stadiums and venues

Domain pack for a stadium, arena or convention venue on event day: gates and sections, ticketing and credentialing, a command post with CCTV, the tenant or promoter, and police, fire and EMS in unified command.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise a venue; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `stadium_venue`:** gates, sections, concourses and lots as fact categories. Systems: ticketing and mobile entry, POS, credentialing, Wi-Fi and DAS, videoboards and IPTV, turnstiles and PACS, CCTV, PA and voice evacuation, smoke control, vehicle barriers, screening equipment and counter-UAS detection. Player roles: Event Operations, Security, Communications, Event Medical Director, Venue Technology, General Manager, Guest Services and Facilities.

## Plans and clocks the profile knows

- **Venue emergency action / operations plan** and **event security plan**, with pre-scripted hold, delay, evacuate, shelter-in-place and relocate messages.
- **Stadium temporary flight restriction:** at or below 3,000 ft AGL within 3 nautical miles of a stadium seating 30,000 or more during MLB, NFL, NCAA Division I football and major motor speedway events, from one hour before to one hour after (14 CFR 99.7).
- **Drones:** the venue may detect and report. Since 1 Jul 2026 (SAFER SKIES Act interim final rule, 6 CFR and 28 CFR part 124), certified state and local law-enforcement agencies may mitigate credible UAS threats to mass gatherings, alongside DOJ and DHS.
- **SAFETY Act** designation or certification of the venue's security program (6 CFR part 25).
