# Research laboratories and biosafety

Included with Action Stations. This pack adds the `research_labs` domain profile for exercising the people who run and oversee research laboratories: biosafety officers, responsible officials of select agent registrations, laboratory directors and principal investigators, environmental health and safety, institutional biosafety committees, occupational health, research compliance and research IT. One profile serves five settings, each a set of roles within it: a university biosafety program, a registered entity, a hospital or clinical research laboratory, an animal facility, and a company or core facility.

Facts below were checked on 2026-11-04. Follow your registration and your institution's own policy; a clock in this pack is an exercise aid.

## Threat families

Laboratory exposure, inventory discrepancy, freezer failure and sample loss, laboratory facility emergency, animal facility emergency, research data breach, workplace violence or threat, and monitoring or access system degradation. Every arc runs the same seven phases: discovery, immediate actions and containment, classify and notify, investigate, written reports, corrective action, return to work.

## Scope

Exercises are about what an organization decides and whom it tells: classification, stop-work, medical evaluation, notifications and their deadlines, continuity and messages. The profile gives no technique, growth, production, quantity, strain or modification detail for any agent. The Lab safety toolkit enforces this with content checks. Every person, institution and place in a scenario is fictional.

## Exercise requirements the profile assumes

- Registered entities test their incident response plan at least annually and review it after any drill, exercise or incident (42 CFR 73.14).
- Theft or loss of a select agent or toxin is reported immediately, and a release causing occupational exposure is reported immediately; the written report (APHIS/CDC Form 3) is due within 7 calendar days (42 CFR 73.19).
- Institutions that receive NIH funds for recombinant or synthetic nucleic acid research report through the institutional biosafety committee under the NIH Guidelines.
