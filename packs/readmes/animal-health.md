# Animal health, veterinary and agriculture

Included with Action Stations. A domain for the people who respond to animal disease and animal emergencies: state animal health officials, producers and integrators, diagnostic laboratories, extension, fairs and animal shelters.

## What it adds

- Thirteen roles in eleven teams, from the state veterinarian and incident commander to the shelter director, with variants for a state agency, a producer or integrator, a laboratory, an extension or fair organization and a disaster animal care program.
- Nine threat families with phase arcs: avian influenza in poultry and in dairy cattle, African swine fever, foot-and-mouth disease, New World screwworm, virulent Newcastle disease, zoonotic spillover to workers, disaster animal care and animal health service degradation.
- Suggested toolkits: animal disease, epidemiology, weather, flooding and media environment.

## Limits

The scenarios are exercise material with fictional premises and people. Follow your own state plan. Review the products with a state veterinarian's office before use.
