# Corporate governance, risk and business continuity

Domain pack for the people who decide for a whole organization when something goes wrong: the board and its audit or risk committee, the executive team, general counsel and the disclosure committee, risk and compliance, and the business continuity manager.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise corporate decisions; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `governance`** with three variants as role sets: a public company executive team and board, a business continuity and resilience program, and an academic case for business and law programs. Roles include the board chair, audit committee chair, CEO, CFO, general counsel, disclosure committee chair, CISO, chief risk and compliance officers, internal audit, the business continuity manager, a function owner, investor relations, outside counsel and a case facilitator.
- **Six threat families:** cyber incident disclosure decision, ransom payment decision, site or system loss against recovery objectives, supplier or cloud provider outage, executive succession and key-person loss, and insider threat, deal leak or regulatory investigation.

## Plans and doctrine the profile knows

- The crisis management plan and call tree, the disclosure controls and procedures, and the business impact analysis with recovery time objectives.
- SEC Form 8-K Item 1.05: the materiality determination must be made without unreasonable delay, and a material incident is reported within four business days of that determination. The reporting clock is included; the determination is a decision players make and the exercise times it.
- New York DFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500) notice and continuity testing duties, and the FFIEC business continuity booklet for banks; the Governance and business continuity reference library pack carries both.

The company, its people, advisers and any ticker in an exercise are fictional. Exercise aid, not legal advice: check the current rule and your counsel. Facts were checked against public sources on 2026-11-04.
