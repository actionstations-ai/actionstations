# Humanitarian and international response

Domain pack and toolkit for NGOs, UN agencies, cluster partners and development programs responding to crises outside a single national emergency system.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise humanitarian response; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `humanitarian`:** four variants as role sets (an international NGO country office, a coordination office with clusters, a response site, and a headquarters crisis management team), their systems and outside parties, and eight threat families: sudden-onset disaster abroad, refugee influx, outbreak in a displacement site, staff security incident, supply pipeline break, funding gap, beneficiary data breach and communications loss in the field.
- **Toolkit `humanitarian`:** terms, product conventions and realism rules for humanitarian exercises, the outside parties and advisor roles the field brings in, and the products, figures, decisions and measures that later releases of this pack add. Any organization can turn the toolkit on: a county voluntary organization active in disasters, a port or a food bank can use the situation report, 3W and coordination products without being a humanitarian agency.

## Things the toolkit knows

- A cluster is activated at the request of the Humanitarian Coordinator and decided by the Emergency Relief Coordinator with the endorsement of the IASC Principals, and is time-limited. Refugee settings use the refugee coordination model, not clusters.
- Taking part in the Saving Lives Together framework does not make the UN responsible for an NGO's security.
- Staff security and kidnapping scenarios stay at the level of decisions and communications. The pack never writes negotiation tactics, ransom mechanics or detention-resistance technique.

Facts were checked against IASC and UNHCR pages on 2026-11-05. The structure of the cluster system can change, so confirm the current one before relying on it. Every organization, person and crisis in the pack is fictional.
