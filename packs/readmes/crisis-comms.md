# Crisis communications and journalism

Domain pack for the people who tell the public what is happening and the people who decide what is true enough to print: an organization's communications team, a joint information center, a press office and a newsroom.

Included with Action Stations and enabled by default. Turn it off on the Packs screen if you never exercise communications or a newsroom; projects already on the domain keep working.

## What is in the pack

- **Domain profile `crisis_comms`** with four variants as role sets: the organizational communications team, the joint information center, the press office and the newsroom. Roles include the communications director, spokesperson, media relations lead, social media manager, writer, legal counsel, executive with release authority, Lead PIO and agency PIOs, rumor control, news director, desk editor, reporter, verification editor, producers and standards editor.
- **Five threat families:** organizational crisis, disaster public information, information disorder (viral misinformation, impersonation, hacked official account, manipulated media), newsroom breaking news, and communications service degradation (compromised social account, alert system outage, unavailable spokesperson).

The **Media environment** pack adds the toolkit that every other domain can turn on: simulated news coverage, social feeds, reporter calls and verification tasks.

## Plans and doctrine the profile knows

- The organization's crisis communications plan and its approval route for statements.
- The NIMS Joint Information System: the Joint Information Center, the PIO cycle (gather, verify, coordinate, disseminate) and release authority.
- CDC Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication principles: be first, be right, be credible, express empathy, promote action, show respect.
- SEC Form 8-K Item 1.05 for public companies that disclose a material cybersecurity incident (coordinate with legal; the clock starts when the company determines the incident is material).
