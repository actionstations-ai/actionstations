# Sources — public guidance the Marston County documents paraphrase

Marston County, the City of Ellery, the Marston County Health Department, and every person, partner, vendor, number, hostname and procedure in this folder are simulated for training use; no real health department is depicted. The shape of the documents and the obligations they describe follow the public guidance below. Nothing was copied; the plans paraphrase what these sources require or recommend, in the way a mid-size local health department's own plans would.

## Preparedness standards

- CDC, *Public Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Capabilities: National Standards for State, Local, Tribal, and Territorial Public Health* (October 2018, updated January 2019) — the 15 capabilities the Emergency Operations Plan is organized around (emergency operations coordination, emergency public information and warning, information sharing, medical countermeasure dispensing and administration, medical materiel management and distribution, nonpharmaceutical interventions, public health surveillance and epidemiological investigation, volunteer management and the others). https://www.cdc.gov/readiness/media/pdfs/CDC_PreparednesResponseCapabilities_October2018_Final_508.pdf
- The PHEP cooperative agreement (Public Health Service Act §319C-1) as the funding and standards basis for state and local preparedness, as summarized in the project's public_health research dossier.

## Risk communication

- CDC, *CERC: Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication — Introduction* (2018 update): the six principles (be first, be right, be credible, express empathy, promote action, show respect) used in Section 8 of the plan. https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Introduction.pdf
- CDC, *CERC: Messages and Audiences* (2018 update). https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Messages_and_Audiences.pdf
- CDC, *CERC: Crisis Communication Plans* (2014) and *CERC: Working with the Media* (2014). https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Crisis_Communication_Plans.pdf · https://www.cdc.gov/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Working_with_the_Media.pdf

## Cyber incidents in the health sector

- CISA, *Tabletop Exercise Package: Healthcare and Public Health Sector — Situation Manual* (updated October 2023): the ransomware-during-a-health-response pattern and the kind of discussion questions the scenario uses. https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-01/Healthcare-and-Public-Health-CTEP-Situation-Manual-102023-508.docx
- FEMA with CISA, *Planning Considerations for Cyber Incidents: Guidance for Emergency Managers* (November 2023). https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_planning-considerations-cyber-incidents_2023.pdf
- HIPAA breach notification (45 CFR 164.400–414) for the clinic components of a hybrid entity: notice to individuals without unreasonable delay and within 60 days of discovery, as summarized in the healthcare research dossier.

## Scenario pattern

The exercise follows seed scenario 1 of the public_health dossier: ransomware on county IT takes down local systems during a measles outbreak, laboratories revert to fax, and contact tracing goes to spreadsheets (pattern from the CISA HPH and Local Governments CTEP case studies). The measles exposure windows and post-exposure prophylaxis intervals in the plan follow standard public health guidance; the plan defers to the state's current investigation guideline. Kansas statutes, the KDHE reporting rule and the state system names are referred to generically; check the current state rule before relying on any timing.
