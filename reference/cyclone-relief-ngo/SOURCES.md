# Sources: public guidance the Harbourlight Relief International documents paraphrase

Harbourlight Relief International, its staff, offices, warehouse, customs agent and phone numbers are simulated for training use, as are the cyclone and the villages and sites named in the exercise. Mozambique, Beira, Sofala and Zambezia provinces, the United Nations system and the agencies named are real. A real disaster event from the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System may be used as case data for realism; no real victim, staff member or beneficiary is depicted. Nothing below is copied.

- Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC), Humanitarian Programme Cycle and the Transformative Agenda protocols: cluster activation, the role of the Humanitarian Coordinator and the Emergency Relief Coordinator, the Humanitarian Country Team. Public documents.
- IASC, Reference Module for Cluster Coordination at Country Level and the cluster lead agency arrangements (examples of global cluster leads, flagged for review).
- UN OCHA, flash update and situation report formats; 3W (who does what where) guidance; Humanitarian Data Exchange. Public documents.
- UN Department of Safety and Security and the Saving Lives Together framework: the principle that participation does not make the UN responsible for NGO security.
- The Sphere Handbook: Humanitarian Charter and Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Response (cited by name for WASH, shelter and health standards; nothing copied).
- Core Humanitarian Standard on Quality and Accountability; humanitarian principles in UN General Assembly resolutions 46/182 and 58/114 (cited by number).
- Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (GDACS) event descriptions, as case data. Public data.
- Global Logistics Cluster guidance on common services, customs facilitation and the supply pipeline. Public documents.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP) as the exercise method.
