# Sources: public guidance the Sawmill County documents paraphrase

Sawmill County, Pine Gulch, Kestrel Springs, the Sheriff's Office, fire districts, Foothills Electric Cooperative, the water district, every person and phone number are simulated for training use. Colorado, the Front Range, the National Weather Service Boulder office and the federal and state agencies are real. The exercise may replay the growth of a real foothills fire from public perimeter and detection data; no real victim, address or property is shown. Nothing below is copied.

- National Weather Service, Red Flag Warning and Fire Weather Watch criteria; fire weather forecasts. Public domain.
- National Wildfire Coordinating Group, Incident Response Pocket Guide and ICS 209 incident status summary; wildfire terminology. Public domain.
- FEMA, Fire Management Assistance Grant program guidance and the National Incident Management System. Public domain.
- FEMA, Integrated Public Alert and Warning System (IPAWS) guidance and Wireless Emergency Alert message length (360 characters). Public domain.
- US Environmental Protection Agency, AirNow Air Quality Index categories and wildfire smoke guidance for schools and for the public. Public domain.
- NOAA/NASA, active fire detections (FIRMS) and the National Interagency Fire Center perimeter data, as case data. Public domain.
- State public utilities commission rules and utility wildfire mitigation plans, as general practice for public safety power shutoff notification windows and medical baseline customers (the co-op's numbers are the exercise's own).
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP).
