# MSU Emergency Operations Plan, Annex F: Flash Flooding

Marlowe State University · Office of Emergency Management · Revision 4 · Course document 3

## 1. Purpose and scope

Annex F tells Marlowe State University (MSU) how it prepares for, responds to and recovers from flash flooding of Sugar Camp Run and the Hocking River floodplain that borders the campus in Marlowe, Athens County, Ohio. It applies to the Main Campus, Riverside Hall, the Sugar Run Apartments and the Dairy Lane service area. It is the plan the students in EMGT 4320 use in Exercise 3. Authority: the MSU Emergency Operations Plan (EOP) base plan, which adopts the National Incident Management System.

## 2. Flood profile

Sugar Camp Run drains 9 square miles of hills north of campus. It runs in an open channel through the Upper Quad, enters a 10-foot box culvert under Mill Street, and emerges across the Lower Quad before it joins the Hocking River at the southern edge of the campus. In heavy rain it can rise 1.5 feet per hour. The university reads two gauges: the MSU stage gauge at the Mill Street culvert, readable on the Facilities dashboard and on the campus operations phone line, and the USGS stream gauge on the Hocking River at the Route 33 bridge. The National Weather Service Charleston, West Virginia, forecast office issues flood and flash flood products for Athens County and the Ohio River Forecast Center issues river forecasts for the Hocking River.

Stages at the Mill Street gauge, in feet:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| 5.0 | Action stage. Emergency Management begins monitoring. |
| 6.5 | Dairy Lane crossing is closed by Campus Police (the crossing floods at 8.0). |
| 7.0 | Flood stage. Sugar Run Apartments are put on evacuation advisory. |
| 8.0 | Dairy Lane crossing under water. Riverside Hall evacuation order if the forecast reaches 10.5 within 3 hours. |
| 9.0 | Lower Quad floods. Pedestrian paths closed. |
| 9.5 | Parking Lot C (620 spaces, mostly commuter cars) floods. |
| 10.5 | Riverside Hall basement floods: mechanical room, laundry, the transformer vault and the hall's main electrical gear. |
| 12.0 | Whitcomb Library lower level floods: network distribution room and the Special Collections archive. |

## 4. Activation levels

| Level | Trigger | EOC |
|---|---|---|
| 3 Monitoring | NWS flood watch or flash flood watch for Athens County | Director and Facilities watch gauges; no EOC |
| 2 Partial | Mill Street stage at 5.0 or higher, or an NWS flash flood warning for the Sugar Camp Run basin | EOC opens in the Campus Services Building, room 108; Police, Communications, Facilities and Student Affairs report |
| 1 Full | Stage at 7.0 or higher, an evacuation order, or a shelter opening | All seats staffed, Marlowe Police and Fire liaisons invited |

The IC may raise or lower the level at any time and records the time and basis.

## 5. Protective actions

1. **Close the Dairy Lane crossing** at stage 6.5 (Campus Police). The crossing carries the central plant's delivery trucks and a Marlowe Fire access route.
2. **Evacuation advisory for the Sugar Run Apartments** (210 residents) at stage 7.0. Clearance time is 45 minutes. Residents go to Kessler Fieldhouse.
3. **Evacuation order for Riverside Hall** (680 residents, 11 floors) when the stage is 8.0 and rising, or when forecast crest at the Mill Street gauge is 10.5 or higher within 3 hours. Clearance time is 90 minutes, longer if the elevators are shut down. Order the evacuation so the building is empty at least 30 minutes before the basement stage.
4. **Move assets** before the stage that floods them: the Facilities truck fleet and mobile generators in Lot C by stage 8.5; library network gear and Special Collections boxes on the lowest shelves to the second floor by stage 10.5.
5. **Shelter in place** is the default for all other buildings. People are told to stay out of the Lower Quad, Lot C and the culvert area.
6. **Isolate electricity** to the flooded zone before the water arrives (see section 7).

## 6. Notification

Notification follows the Notification and Shelter SOP (course document 4): MSU Alert (text, email, voice, desktop banner and 18 outdoor sirens), the university home page banner, social media, RA phone tree, door-to-door by Campus Police and Residence Life. The PIO issues the first public message within 15 minutes of any evacuation decision. The Clery Compliance Officer is consulted on every message that might be a timely warning or an emergency notification.

## 7. Utilities and facilities

- **Electrical:** Facilities isolates Riverside Hall's main service at the transformer vault before the basement stage. Isolation takes 20 minutes and turns off elevators, lights and fire alarm primary power; the building's emergency lighting and fire alarm batteries last 4 hours. Do not evacuate by elevator once power is isolated.
- **Central plant (Dairy Lane):** sits at 11.5 feet above the Mill Street datum and does not flood below a stage of 14. It serves chilled water and steam to 14 buildings. The Dairy Lane crossing closure affects fuel delivery; the plant carries 3 days of fuel.
- **Whitcomb Library:** the network distribution room and the Special Collections archive are on the lower level. Moving the network gear needs 2 hours, the archive boxes 3 hours.
- **Pumps and barriers:** 6 portable pumps and 400 feet of water-filled barrier are in the Facilities yard; 90 minutes to deploy at Riverside Hall.

## 8. Sheltering

Kessler Fieldhouse is the primary shelter (1,800 capacity, 500 kW generator with 36 hours of fuel, on ground 40 feet above Lot C). The Student Union ballroom is the alternate (400 capacity). The Dean of Students opens a shelter; the American Red Cross (Southeast Ohio chapter) supports after the first 12 hours on request. Students with medical equipment needing electricity, mobility needs or communication access needs are met in the shelter's general area with support; the Disability Services Director advises. Pets in carriers are allowed.

## 9. Accountability

The Dean of Students and Campus Police account for residents of the evacuated halls using RA floor rosters, ID card swipes at the Fieldhouse door and a list of students not yet accounted for. The list is reviewed hourly. Students who cannot be found are reported to Campus Police.

## 10. Recovery and reoccupation

Facilities inspects each building before reoccupation: structure, electrical, water, mechanical, fire alarm, elevator. Riverside Hall reoccupation needs written clearance from the Marlowe building inspector. Student Affairs arranges temporary housing for displaced students. The IC records damage figures for the Ohio Emergency Management Agency.
