# EMGT 4320 Role Rubrics and Outcome Map

Marlowe State University · Department of Emergency Management and Homeland Security · Spring 2027 · Handout given to students before every session

## 1. How to read this handout

Each seat has a rubric with four criteria. Each criterion has four levels worth 0 to 3 points (Not shown 0, Developing 1, Proficient 2, Exemplary 3). Criteria have weights; a weight of 2 counts twice. A role rubric is worth 30 weighted points and the team rubric is worth 15, so a session is scored out of 45 and converted to the 12 course points. All levels are described in what a scorer can see in the exercise record: a message sent, a decision recorded, an observation noted. The rubric scores what a student did, never how the scenario ended.

The outcome codes LO1 to LO6 are those in the syllabus. The instructor links each criterion to an outcome, and the Classroom dashboard reports attainment for each outcome as the share of students at Proficient or above.

## 7. Student Evaluator (graded on observation quality)

| Criterion | Weight | Outcome | Not shown (0) | Developing (1) | Proficient (2) | Exemplary (3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Specificity | 3 | LO5 | Opinion only ("did well") | Describes a behavior without time or seat | Describes a behavior with the time and the seat | Time, seat, the inject it responded to and the words used |
| Tie to an objective | 3 | LO5 | No objective linked | Objective linked to some notes | Every observation linked to an objective or critical task | Linked, and notes what evidence would raise or lower the rating |
| Neutrality | 2 | LO5 | Names or judges a person's character | Mixed fact and judgment | Facts only, about actions | Facts only, and flags where the evaluator might be biased |
| Coverage | 2 | LO5 | One seat observed | Two seats | Every assigned seat observed at least twice | Every seat observed, with the quietest seat checked on |

Where two evaluators rate the same student and criterion, the Classroom dashboard shows agreement and the instructor uses it to calibrate scoring.

## 8. Team rubric (every student in the session)

| Criterion | Weight | Outcome | Not shown (0) | Developing (1) | Proficient (2) | Exemplary (3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shared situation picture | 2 | LO4 | Seats work from different facts | One shared summary at the start | Summary updated at each phase change | Summary updated, and discrepancies between seats resolved aloud |
| Timeliness of decisions | 2 | LO1 | Decisions made after the trigger passed | Decisions on the trigger with no lead time | Decisions with lead time over a stated trigger | Decisions ahead of the trigger and the lead time explained |
| Consistent public message | 1 | LO2 | Alert, police and student messages disagree | Messages agree on some facts | Messages agree on place, time and action | Messages agree and one owner is named for corrections |

## 9. Reflection rubric

Reflections are scored 0 to 3 on one criterion, evidence of learning (LO6): 0 restates the scenario, 1 describes what happened, 2 names a decision the student would change and why, 3 names the change, the information that would have supported it, and what the student's seat depended on from others.

## 10. Calibration

Before the first graded session Dr. Castellan and Joel Brandvold score the practice run's recorded evidence separately and compare. Differences of more than one level on a criterion are discussed and the descriptor above is clarified. Students receive the rubric a week before each session.
