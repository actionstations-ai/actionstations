# Tallis County and Cedar Ford: Facilities and Systems

Reference sheet for exercise players · Tallis County Emergency Management Agency · 2027

## 1. The county

Tallis County, Iowa: 31,000 residents, 520 square miles of river valley and farmland. Cedar Ford (11,400) is the county seat; Ferris Crossing (1,150) is 9 miles upstream. The Cedar River is the county's main hazard. The last major flood in the plan's records reached 22.8 feet at CFDI4 in a September; the design flood of the levee is 25.5 feet.

## 2. EOC and communications

The EOC at 220 Sycamore Street has 16 workstations, a generator with 3 days of diesel, an amateur radio station and a WebEOC connection with Iowa HSEMD. The Tallis County Communications Center (911) is in the Sheriff's Office at 100 Courthouse Square. TallisAlert reaches 8,700 subscribers by text and voice; voice calls take 25 minutes for the county. The county is an IPAWS alerting authority. The county's local radio station is called "Tallis Valley Radio" in the exercise.

## 3. Hollow Court Care Center

36 beds, 24 of them non-ambulatory, 41 staff on the day shift. Administrator Rosa Delgado. Evacuation takes 12 hours with the county's three paratransit buses and eight vans. Phone 319-555-0160.

## 4. Cedar Ford Wastewater Treatment Plant

Capacity 3.2 million gallons per day, built 1962, upgraded 2004. The plant sits on the river's right bank inside the Levee District. Superintendent Tran Nguyen (319-555-0162). The plant is behind the levee, so river water reaches it only if the levee fails; interior ponding and seepage reach the yard when the pump stations fall behind (equivalent to a river stage of 22.5 feet at CFDI4). The SCADA room floor is at that level, the generator pad at 23.0 feet and the main electrical gear at 23.5 feet. The plant's plan is to move portable pumps, spare parts, the chemical totes and the mobile generator above 24.0 feet and to shut down and bypass if the stage reaches 24.0 feet. A shutdown sends untreated flow to the river; the Iowa Department of Natural Resources must be notified.

## 5. Tallis Prairie Cooperative

A farmer-owned cooperative at the river bend on Route 38: five grain bins (about 420,000 bushels), a fertilizer plant and a feed yard with about 340 head of cattle. General Manager Ellen Strand (319-555-0165). The yard floods at 19.0 ft; the cooperative needs 24 hours and 14 cattle trucks to move the herd to higher pasture at the Weller farm. The fertilizer plant stores anhydrous ammonia tanks that must be secured.

## 6. Schools and shelters

Cedar Ford High School (shelter, 400 capacity, generator on site, access and functional needs area, field house for 60 pets). Tallis Elementary School is in the leveed area; the school district Superintendent Dr. Nina Faraj will close it when the stage forecast reaches 22.0 ft. Ferris Crossing Community Hall (80).

## 7. Utilities

Tallis Rural Electric Cooperative (the exercise's name for the electric utility) serves the county; Substation 4 at Hollow Road floods at 20.5 ft. Cedar Ford Municipal Water draws from wells on the river terrace; Well 3 floods at 21.0 ft. Natural gas from a regional distributor.

## 8. Bridges and routes

The Route 38 Cedar Ford Bridge is the only river crossing inside the county; the next crossing is 14 miles upstream and 20 miles downstream. The Mill Street underpass carries the short line railroad (called the "Prairie Southern Railway" in the exercise), which also closes at 20.5 ft.

## 9. Outside agencies

The National Weather Service (Quad Cities office and the North Central River Forecast Center); the USGS Iowa Water Science Center; the Army Corps of Engineers Rock Island District; Iowa DOT District 6; Iowa HSEMD; Iowa Department of Natural Resources; the Red Cross Iowa region; the Iowa National Guard; the neighboring county's EMA downstream (Weller County, fictional).
