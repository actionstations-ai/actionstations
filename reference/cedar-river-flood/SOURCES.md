# Sources: public guidance the Tallis County documents paraphrase

Tallis County, Cedar Ford, Ferris Crossing, the Levee and Drainage District No. 3, the wastewater plant, the care center, the cooperative, every person and phone number, and the forecast point CFDI4 are simulated for training use. The Cedar River, Iowa and the federal and state agencies are real. The exercise replays the stage record of a real Cedar River gauge as case data (USGS) and scales it to the plan's categories. Nothing below is copied.

- National Weather Service, Advanced Hydrologic Prediction Service and hydrograph products; flood categories (action, minor, moderate, major), forecast crest wording and flood warning products. Public domain.
- National Weather Service, flash flood warning impact-based tags (considerable and catastrophic). Public domain.
- US Geological Survey, Water Data and Water Science Center gauge descriptions (stage, discharge, datum). Public domain.
- US Army Corps of Engineers, flood fight guidance: sandbag construction, sand boil treatment, levee patrol (Engineer Manual on levee inspection and flood fight techniques). Public domain.
- FEMA, Flood response and recovery planning guidance; substantial damage estimation guidance; Ready, Set, Go flood messaging. Public domain.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP) and the National Incident Management System; ICS 213 General Message. Public domain.
- FEMA, Disaster Recovery Reform guidance on pet evacuation and sheltering (PETS Act). Public domain.
- Iowa Department of Natural Resources, wastewater bypass notification requirements (summarized; check current rule).
