# Sources: public guidance the Tallis documents paraphrase

Tallis Home Products, Inc., its people, its web store vendor, its retail partners, outlets, reporters, advisers and every phone number in this folder are simulated for training use. Dayton, Ohio, the Ohio Attorney General, the FBI, CISA, the SEC and the FTC are real places and agencies named as outside parties. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources describe.

- SEC, Release 33-11216, Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure (2023), and the staff fact sheet; Form 8-K Items 1.05 and 8.01. Public domain.
- CDC, Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC) manual: be first, be right, be credible; what is known, not known and being done. Public domain.
- FEMA, National Incident Management System, Joint Information System and Public Information guidance. Public domain.
- CISA, Cyber Incident Reporting guidance and tabletop exercise packages (ransomware). Public domain.
- FTC, Data Breach Response: A Guide for Business. Public domain.
- HSEEP, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program. Public domain.
- Associated Press Stylebook conventions on attribution and on the record, background and off the record (general practice described, nothing copied).
