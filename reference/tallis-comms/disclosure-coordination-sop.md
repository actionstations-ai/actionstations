# TLC-LEG-07: Cyber Incident Disclosure Coordination

Tallis Home Products · Office of the General Counsel with Corporate Communications, Information Security and Investor Relations · Revision 5

## 1. Purpose

When a cybersecurity incident may be material, this procedure keeps what Tallis says to customers, the press, regulators, investors and employees consistent, and makes sure the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Form 8-K is filed on time. It is an exercise aid; the determination of materiality is the company's, with counsel.

## 2. Triggers

The CISO calls the General Counsel within one hour of any of these: unauthorized access to a system holding personal data of more than 10,000 people; confirmed access to financial, payment or employee records; an outside party claiming to have Tallis data; ransomware on any production system. The General Counsel calls the Chief Communications Officer (CCO) and the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) the same hour, and the Disclosure Committee meets within four hours.

## 3. The Disclosure Committee

Chair: General Counsel Gerald Okafor. Members: CFO Alan Brightman, Chief Accounting Officer Nora Vasquez, CISO Lena Hartmann, CCO Marcus Delaney, Investor Relations Director Shauna Pike, Corporate Secretary Ellen Park. Outside counsel (Whitfield Lane LLP) and the forensic firm (Cardinal Forensics) attend by phone. The committee records, on one page, the facts known, the facts not known, the factors considered and the decision, with the name of the person who decided.

## 4. Materiality determination

The committee determines "without unreasonable delay after discovery" whether the incident is material. It does not wait for the investigation to end. It considers quantitative factors (estimated cost of response, notification and credit monitoring; revenue at risk; the number of records; the share of web store sales, which are 14 percent of net sales) and qualitative factors (harm to reputation, to customer and retail relationships, regulatory exposure, whether the data includes sensitive categories, the likelihood of litigation). If the facts are not enough, the record says "not yet determined" and lists what the committee is waiting for; it reconvenes at a fixed time. The CEO is told of the determination within an hour of it being made.

The audit committee chair, Dr. Helen Marsh, is told within 24 hours of any incident the Disclosure Committee treats as possibly material, and at once if the incident is determined material.

## 5. The 8-K

- **Item 1.05** is filed within four business days after the company determines the incident is material. It describes the material aspects of the nature, scope and timing of the incident and its material or reasonably likely material impact. Facts not yet determined are stated as such, and the company files an 8-K/A within four business days of learning them.
- **Item 8.01** may be used for an incident not yet determined material. The text does not claim materiality. If the incident is later determined material, Item 1.05 is filed within four business days of that determination.
- A request by the Attorney General to delay disclosure for national security or public safety is handled by the General Counsel alone; the days run from the end of the delay.
- Filing is by the EDGAR filing agent, Harbor Filing Services, before the 5:30 p.m. Eastern cut-off. Draft: General Counsel; review: outside counsel, CFO, CCO; approval: CEO and the General Counsel.

## 6. The communications timeline

| When | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Within 60 min of confirmation or a credible claim | Holding statement, employee message | COMMS |
| Before the statement | IR told; if the market is open the Disclosure Committee chair told | IR |
| Within 4 hours | Disclosure Committee meets, first record | General Counsel |
| Same day as scope is confirmed | Customer email and website notice; state notices as law requires | General Counsel and COMMS |
| Within 72 hours | Notices to state attorneys general and regulators as the facts require; the Ohio Attorney General is notified when Ohio residents are affected | General Counsel |
| Four business days after a materiality determination | Form 8-K Item 1.05 | General Counsel |
| Next investor call or earlier | Script approved by the committee | IR |

## 7. Consistency check

Before any release the General Counsel and the VP Corporate Communications compare the public statement, customer email, call center macro, retailer note, board briefing and Form 8-K line by line for the same numbers, dates and verbs. An 8-K may not say less than the press release says, and a press release may not say more than the 8-K can support. A number appears only if it is on the fact sheet.

## 8. Insurance, law enforcement and vendors

The CFO tells the cyber insurer's claims line (the broker is Midland Risk Partners, (937) 555-0147) within 24 hours. Security reports to the FBI Cincinnati Field Office and to CISA. The web store vendor, CartBridge Commerce, is informed in writing of the incident and asked to preserve logs; its public statements must be coordinated with Tallis, and Tallis does not blame it in public.

## 9. Records

All decisions are written with the name of the decider, the facts known at the time and the basis. The communications log, the committee record and the filing are kept for seven years.
