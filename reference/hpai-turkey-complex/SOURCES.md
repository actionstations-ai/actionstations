# Sources — public guidance the Calder County documents paraphrase

Calder County, Calder County Emergency Management, Larkin, Dunmore, the Prairie Gold Turkey Cooperative, every farm, person, phone number, address and identifier in this folder are simulated for training use. The real places and agencies named as outside parties (Minnesota; the Minnesota Board of Animal Health; the Minnesota Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory; the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency; the Minnesota Department of Health; Minnesota Homeland Security and Emergency Management; USDA APHIS Veterinary Services and its national laboratory) are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe.

- USDA APHIS, HPAI Response Plan: The Red Book (May 2017), including zone sizes (infected zone 3 km, buffer zone, control area at least 10 km, surveillance zone at least 10 km), premises designations and the depopulation goal of 24 hours. Public domain.
- USDA APHIS, Foreign Animal Disease Preparedness and Response Plan (FAD PReP) and the National Animal Health Laboratory Network description of presumptive and confirmed results. Public domain.
- USDA APHIS, Secure Egg Supply and Secure Poultry Supply plans on continuity of business and movement permits. Public domain.
- USDA APHIS, guidance on depopulation, disposal and cleaning and disinfection of HPAI premises. Public domain.
- CDC, Interim guidance on highly pathogenic avian influenza A(H5N1) for people exposed to infected birds: monitoring and antiviral prophylaxis for 10 days after exposure. Public domain.
- Minnesota Board of Animal Health, public HPAI response information (named only; the state plan governs distances and timings).
- FEMA, National Incident Management System and Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP). Public domain.
