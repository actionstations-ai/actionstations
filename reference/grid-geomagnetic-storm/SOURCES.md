# Sources: public guidance the Northern Lakes documents paraphrase

Northern Lakes Transmission Operations, the Upper Midwest Reliability Coordinator, Taconite Ridge, Mesabi Taconite Works, the neighboring operators, every person and phone number are simulated for training use. Minnesota, the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center and the federal and state agencies are real. A real storm's Kp and X-ray record may be replayed as case data. Nothing below is copied.

- NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, NOAA Space Weather Scales (G, S and R), product descriptions and message codes (watches, warnings, alerts, summaries), and the CME discussion. Public domain.
- NOAA SWPC and the National Weather Service, Kp index and the geomagnetic storm scale. Public domain.
- North American Electric Reliability Corporation, reliability standards EOP-010 (Geomagnetic Disturbance Operations) and TPL-007 (Transmission System Planned Performance for GMD Events), cited by name and in paraphrase only.
- FERC orders on GMD reliability standards and the NERC Geomagnetic Disturbance Task Force reports. Public documents.
- US Department of Energy and the national laboratories, geomagnetically induced current and transformer effects (general descriptions). Public domain.
- US Government, National Space Weather Strategy and Action Plan and the Space Weather Operations, Research and Mitigation framework. Public domain.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP).
