# Sources: public guidance the Wren County Sheriff's Office documents paraphrase

Wren County, Oregon, the Wren County Sheriff's Office, the city of Alder Falls, Alder Falls Police Department, Wren County Fire District 1, Pine Landing, Gray Butte, every person, phone extension, talkgroup, site and identifier in this folder are simulated for training use. The real places and agencies named as outside parties (Oregon, Deschutes and Jefferson counties, the Oregon State Police, Oregon Emergency Management, the Oregon Department of Transportation, the FBI Portland Field Office, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe, the way a county's own plans would. No child, family or case is depicted.

- U.S. Department of Justice, Office of Justice Programs, AMBER Alert program guidance: the five recommended AMBER Alert criteria and the voluntary partnership of law enforcement, broadcasters and transportation agencies. Public domain.
- U.S. Department of Justice, OJJDP, AMBER Alert Best Practices and the National AMBER Alert Network, including Wireless Emergency Alerts. Public domain.
- FEMA, Integrated Public Alert and Warning System (IPAWS) and Wireless Emergency Alerts (360-character message length). Public domain.
- FBI, Child Abduction Rapid Deployment (CARD) team description and the National Crime Information Center (NCIC) Missing Person File, including the Child Abduction flag. Public domain.
- Suzanne's Law and the Missing Children's Assistance Act, as summarized by OJJDP (no waiting period for reports of missing children). Public domain.
- Oregon State Police public descriptions of the state AMBER Alert Plan and the Oregon Missing Children Clearinghouse. State government publications.
- FEMA, National Incident Management System (NIMS) and Incident Command System: unified command, command post and joint information center concepts. Public domain.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP). Public domain.
- FEMA, National Qualification System position task descriptions for Public Information Officer. Public domain.
- CDC, Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC) principles for holding statements and corrections. Public domain.
- NCMEC public guidance for law enforcement on missing child cases (cited by name only; nothing copied).
- Commission on Accreditation for Law Enforcement Agencies, standards on critical incidents (cited by name only; nothing copied).
