# Halvorsen State University — Research Facilities and Biosafety Program

Reference description for HSU-EHS-BIO-01 and SOP-12 · Office of Environmental Health and Safety

## 1. The university

Halvorsen State University is a public research university of about 14,000 students in Marlow Falls, a city of 62,000 in central Iowa. The Office of Research manages about 380 active awards, with about 41 principal investigators who hold biological use authorizations. The university employs 1,150 staff in its research enterprise on the campus and the research farm. The funding comes from federal agencies, a state agricultural commodity board and two industry sponsors.

## 2. Buildings and containment

| Building | Floors | Laboratories | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lindgren Life Sciences | 5 | 46 BSL-1 and BSL-2 laboratories | Main microbiology and molecular biology teaching and research; shared autoclave room on the ground floor |
| Kessler Hall | 4 | 22 laboratories | Chemistry and biochemistry; 6 BSL-2 rooms on the third floor |
| Aldous Veterinary Research Annex | 2 | 14 laboratories and an animal facility | ABSL-2 rooms; separate air handling |
| Prairie Genomics Center | 3 | Core facility with sequencers and a cell culture suite | BSL-2 cell culture, 24-hour badge access |

There are 78 BSL-2 laboratories in total. No BSL-3 space and no select agents are held. Each BSL-2 room has a biosafety cabinet certified annually by a contract vendor, an eyewash, a sink, a self-closing door, and the biohazard sign with the PI's name and an emergency number.

## 3. The biosafety program

- **Institutional Biosafety Committee.** Twelve members, including two community members. It meets on the second Tuesday of each month in Lindgren 214 and reviews registrations. It is chaired by Prof. Dennis Okafor.
- **Biosafety Officer.** Marcus Tran, with Priya Raman as alternate, and one technician. The BSO is reachable by phone at all hours through Campus Police dispatch.
- **Training.** All BSL-2 workers complete the online course and a laboratory-specific orientation before getting badge access. Refresher training is annual.
- **Inspections.** Each BSL-2 laboratory is inspected by EHS once a year and by the PI quarterly with a checklist.
- **Incident file.** The EHS portal holds the incident log, the clock board and the corrective action tracker. The log recorded 31 reports last year: 22 near misses, 8 minor exposures and 1 event sent to the NIH.

## 4. Systems

- **EHS portal.** Web application for reports, registrations, inventories and the clock board. Single sign-on. Outage procedure: paper forms in the BSO's office and in each building's front desk.
- **Badge access.** Access control logs for the Genomics Center and Lindgren's third and fourth floors. The log can be queried by the BSO and by Campus Police.
- **Freezer monitoring.** Wireless sensors on 140 ultralow freezers report to the Facilities operations center. The alarm text goes to the laboratory manager and the BSO.
- **Mass notification.** HSUAlert sends text, e-mail and desktop messages to students and staff. Only University Communications and Campus Police can send.
- **Telephone.** Campus extensions are five digits starting with 5. Outside numbers use the 515-555-01xx range.

## 5. Occupational Health Clinic

The clinic is on the ground floor of the Student Health Center, open weekdays 7:30 to 17:00. After hours, workers go to Marlow Falls Regional Medical Center emergency department, which holds a standing arrangement with HSU. The clinic physician, Dr. Helen Mbeki, can be reached after hours through Campus Police dispatch. Post-exposure evaluation costs are charged to the PI's department and do not appear on the worker's insurance.

## 6. Outside parties

- Federal Select Agent Program (CDC Division of Regulatory Science and Compliance and APHIS), only if select agent evidence appears.
- NIH Office of Science Policy, for IBC reports.
- Iowa Department of Health and Human Services and Marlow County public health, for risks beyond the laboratory.
- Marlow Falls Fire Department hazmat team, for a spill that leaves a laboratory.
- Campus Police and Marlow Falls Police Department.
- Sponsors and program officers.
- The university's insurer.

## 7. People often in the room

Laboratory managers, graduate students and postdocs hold most hands-on roles. Undergraduate researchers work under supervision. In the Lindgren third-floor suite the laboratories of Dr. Anika Rao (bacterial genetics) and Dr. Tomas Eklund (cell biology) share a corridor, an autoclave and a centrifuge room.
