# Sources consulted

All documents are fictional. Halvorsen State University, its people, programs and sponsors are simulated. The public guidance below was paraphrased for realism; nothing was copied.

- CDC and NIH, Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories (BMBL), 6th edition: biosafety levels, laboratory practices, incident response in the laboratory.
- NIH Guidelines for Research Involving Recombinant or Synthetic Nucleic Acid Molecules: institutional biosafety committee roles and reporting of significant problems, violations, accidents and illnesses to the NIH Office of Science Policy.
- 42 CFR part 73, Select Agents and Toxins (Federal Select Agent Program): immediate notice, and written report within 7 days.
- Federal Select Agent Program, Incident Response Plan Guidance.
- OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030): post-exposure evaluation and follow-up.
- Standards named only: ISO 35001 biorisk management; ABSA International professional guidance.
