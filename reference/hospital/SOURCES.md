# Sources — public guidance the Ottawa River Regional Medical Center documents paraphrase

Ottawa River Regional Medical Center, every person, vendor, hostname, telephone extension, procedure and identifier in this folder are simulated for training use; no real hospital is depicted. Toledo, Lucas County, Ohio and the public agencies named as outside parties are real. The documents are original text. They paraphrase what the public sources below require or recommend, the way a hospital's own plans would. Nothing was copied.

## Preparedness requirements

- Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Emergency Preparedness Requirements for Medicare and Medicaid Participating Providers and Suppliers, 42 CFR 482.15 (hospitals): emergency plan, policies and procedures, communication plan, training and testing (twice a year, one of which may be a facilitated tabletop). Federal regulation, public domain. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-482/subpart-B/section-482.15
- CMS, State Operations Manual Appendix Z, Emergency Preparedness Interpretive Guidance. Public domain.
- FEMA, National Incident Management System and the Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP). Public domain.
- Hospital Incident Command System (HICS) guidebook, California Emergency Medical Services Authority (2014): the command structure and job action sheets referred to in the plan. Public.
- ASPR TRACIE (Technical Resources, Assistance Center, and Information Exchange): hazard vulnerability analysis, healthcare evacuation and sheltering in place, crisis standards of care, and surge topic collections. Federal, public domain.

## Cyber and downtime

- HHS, Healthcare and Public Health Sector Cybersecurity Performance Goals (CPGs) and Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices (HICP, 2023): the practices the plan lists. https://405d.hhs.gov
- CISA, Tabletop Exercise Package: Healthcare and Public Health Sector (Situation Manual) and CISA ransomware guidance: the ransomware-during-patient-care pattern. Public domain.
- HIPAA Security Rule contingency plan standard, 45 CFR 164.308(a)(7), and HIPAA Breach Notification Rule, 45 CFR 164.400–414 (individuals within 60 days of discovery; HHS and media for breaches of 500 or more). HHS, public domain.
- HHS OCR, Ransomware and HIPAA fact sheet (2016): ransomware encrypting protected health information is a presumptive breach.

## Surge and water

- ASPR, Healthcare Preparedness Capabilities and the Hospital Preparedness Program materials.
- CDC guidance on dialysis facility emergency preparedness and water treatment for hemodialysis (AAMI standards are referenced generically; the documents quote none of them).

## Scenario patterns

The four exercises follow patterns in the CISA HPH situation manual and ASPR TRACIE material: ransomware forcing EHR downtime, medical device network compromise, a mass casualty incident during an IT outage and a regional drinking-water advisory that reaches dialysis. All timings, counts and system behaviors are invented for the exercise.
