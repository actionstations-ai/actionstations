# Sources — public material consulted for realism

The documents in `reference/uas-ops/` describe **Harwick UAS Operations Group (HUOG)**, a simulated regional unmanned-aircraft operator (infrastructure inspection, agricultural and environmental survey, public-safety support). The organization, its people, sites, aircraft, hostnames, IP ranges, radio frequencies, telephone numbers, waiver numbers, clients and document numbers are exercise material and do not exist. No text from the pages below is reproduced; they were read so that a typical operator's equipment, field practice and regulatory environment would be portrayed plausibly.

## Deliberately not used

- Names of any real organization, program, person, building, property, road or town; the city of Harwick, Calder County, Dunmore, Birch Hollow Range, Horton Field and Harwick Regional Airport (HWK) are invented.
- Real tower or approach frequencies, real Class C dimensions or real UAS Facility Map ceilings; the sheet's 200 ft / 400 ft values are exercise values.
- Any real operator's policy text.
