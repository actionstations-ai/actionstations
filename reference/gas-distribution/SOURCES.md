# Sources — public guidance the Corwin Valley Gas documents paraphrase

Corwin Valley Gas Company, the City of Corwin, the Borough of Elm Falls, Lake Haven County, Tri-Ridge Interstate Pipeline, Halden Automation and PipeView, and every person, number, hostname and procedure in this folder are simulated for training use; no real utility is depicted. Real products (Emerson ROC800 and ROCLINK), federal and state agencies (TSA, CISA, PHMSA, NTSB, FBI, the Pennsylvania PUC) and sector organizations keep their names. The shape of the documents and the obligations they describe follow the public guidance below. Nothing was copied; the plans paraphrase what these sources require or recommend, in the way a mid-size gas distribution company's own plans would.

## Regulation

- 49 CFR 192.615, Emergency plans — written procedures for receiving and classifying notices; communication with the 911 PSAP, fire, police and officials; response to gas in or near a building, fire, explosion and natural disaster; protecting people first, then property; emergency shutdown, valve shut-off or pressure reduction; making safe; immediate and direct PSAP notice of a potential rupture; restoration; failure investigation; training; liaison. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title49-vol3/pdf/CFR-2025-title49-vol3-sec192-615.pdf
- 49 CFR 191.3 and 191.5 — incident definition; telephonic notice to the National Response Center at the earliest practicable moment and no later than one hour after confirmed discovery, revised or confirmed within 48 hours. 49 CFR 191.9 — written report on Form PHMSA F 7100.1 within 30 days. 49 CFR 191.25 — safety-related condition reports. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-191

## Pipeline security

- TSA, Security Directive Pipeline-2021-01G (effective 16 January 2026 to 15 January 2027) — Cybersecurity Coordinator and alternate available 24/7; report cybersecurity incidents to CISA as soon as practicable and no later than 72 hours after identification. https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/signed_security-directive-pipeline-2021-01g-and-transmittal-memo_508c.pdf
- TSA, Security Directive Pipeline-2021-02G (effective 3 May 2026 to 2 May 2027) — Cybersecurity Implementation Plan; Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan with the four objectives (containment, segregation with memory capture and labeling, backup integrity, IT/OT isolation capability and governance), exercised at least annually testing at least two objectives with the responsible positions participating; Cybersecurity Assessment Plan. https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/signed_security_directive_pipeline-2021-02g_and_transmittal_memo_508c.pdf
- TSA, *Pipeline Security Guidelines* (March 2018, Change 1 April 2021) and *Pipeline Security Smart Practice Observations* (April 2026). https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/pipeline_security_guidelines.pdf — https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/tsa_pipeline_security_smart_practice_observat.pdf

## Incidents the scenario is modeled on

- NTSB, *Overpressurization of Natural Gas Distribution System, Explosions, and Fires in Merrimack Valley, Massachusetts, September 13, 2018*, NTSB/PAR-19/02 — high-pressure gas entered a low-pressure distribution system after a main was abandoned without relocating the regulator sensing lines; fires and explosions damaged 131 structures, one person was killed, and the operator shut down the low-pressure system serving 10,894 customers; a contributing factor was a low-pressure system without adequate overpressure protection. https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/PAR1902.pdf (The Corwin worker/monitor stations, sensing-line procedure CP-17, district-end recorders and the "field gauges govern" rule follow this case.)
- CISA and FBI, *DarkSide Ransomware: Best Practices for Preventing Business Disruption from Ransomware Attacks*, AA21-131A (May 2021) — a ransomware compromise of a pipeline company's IT network led the company to shut down pipeline operations as a precaution; the advisory recommends robust IT/OT segmentation and the ability to run OT manually. https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/AA21-131A_Darkside_Ransomware.pdf (The isolation levels I-1 to I-4 and manual-operations staffing follow this case.)
