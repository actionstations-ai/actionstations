# Sources — public guidance the Brennan County documents paraphrase

Brennan County, the Brennan County Emergency Management Agency, Ashford, Corwin, Hollis, Channel 6 StormTeam, Purchase Area Broadcasting, Ridgeline Distribution, Corwin Wax & Candle, every person, site, frequency assignment and identifier in this folder are simulated for training use. The real places and agencies named as outside parties (western Kentucky, the Jackson Purchase, McCracken, Graves, Marshall and Calloway counties, NWS Paducah and NWS Louisville, Kentucky Emergency Management, Kentucky State Police, the American Red Cross) are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe, the way a county's own plans would.

- National Weather Service, Impact-Based Warnings for tornado and severe thunderstorm warnings (damage threat tags: CONSIDERABLE, CATASTROPHIC, DESTRUCTIVE) and the NWS Directives 10-511 (WFO severe weather products). Public domain.
- NOAA Storm Prediction Center, convective outlook categories (Marginal, Slight, Enhanced, Moderate, High) and watch products. Public domain.
- FEMA, Integrated Public Alert and Warning System (IPAWS) program guidance for alerting authorities; Wireless Emergency Alerts message length (360 characters) and Spanish-language support. Public domain.
- FEMA P-361, Safe Rooms for Tornadoes and Hurricanes, and FEMA P-320 (community and residential safe rooms). Public domain.
- FEMA, Outdoor Warning Systems Guide (CPG 1-17) on the role of sirens as outdoor warning. Public domain.
- NWS, service assessment of the December 10–11, 2021 tornado outbreak (western Kentucky): night-time tornadoes in fast-moving storms, warning reception and manufactured-home and warehouse vulnerability. Public domain. The exercise draws its lessons from the assessment; it does not depict that event, its places or its victims.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP) and the National Incident Management System. Public domain.
- American Meteorological Society, guidance on broadcast meteorology during severe weather (general practice described, nothing copied).
