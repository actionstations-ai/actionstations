## SECTION 6: NETWORK AND HOSTING

- Data center: District Service Center Room 104. Virtual host cluster HUSD-VH01, HUSD-VH02, HUSD-VH03 (VMware) runs HUSD-DC01, HUSD-DC02, HUSD-FS01, HUSD-FS02, HUSD-ERP01, HUSD-SQL01, HUSD-PBX01, HUSD-VMS01, HUSD-BAS01 and HUSD-BK01.
- Core firewall HUSD-FW01; remote access VPN HUSD-VPN01 (staff and two vendors: Tyler support and the building-controls integrator).
- Schools connect over a leased fiber WAN; each school also has a local internet breakout for Chromebook and cloud traffic, so classroom cloud services survive an isolation of the data center (CIRP Section 7).
- Wi-Fi: cloud-managed access points at every school; testing SSID HUSD-TEST on the state testing allow-list.
- The 2025 external scan by CISA's free vulnerability scanning service found the VPN appliance and an exposed building-automation web page; the web page was removed, the VPN finding is open (CIRP Annex A, CIRP-4).
