# Sources — public guidance the Mariner Bay documents paraphrase

Mariner Bay Medical Center, its people, units, vendors, extensions and building details are simulated for training use. Corpus Christi, Nueces County, the Corpus Christi Bay, San Antonio, Victoria, the National Weather Service, the National Hurricane Center, the Texas Division of Emergency Management and the Coastal Bend Regional Advisory Council are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe.

- NOAA National Hurricane Center, forecast advisories, cone, storm surge and wind speed probability products, and the 34-knot, 50-knot and 64-knot wind thresholds. Public domain.
- NOAA/NWS, Storm Surge hazard maps and SLOSH model overview. Public domain.
- ASPR TRACIE, Hospital Hurricane Planning and Response Toolkit and healthcare evacuation and shelter-in-place resources. Public domain.
- CMS, emergency preparedness conditions of participation (42 CFR 482.15) and State Operations Manual Appendix Z. Public domain.
- HHS ASPR, Healthcare Facility Evacuation and Shelter-in-Place guidance (Hospital Evacuation Decision Guide, AHRQ). Public domain.
- FEMA, HSEEP and NIMS/ICS. Public domain.
- AHRQ, TeamSTEPPS handoff tools (SBAR). Public domain.
- Named only: state nurse-to-patient ratio guidance, Joint Commission emergency management standards.
