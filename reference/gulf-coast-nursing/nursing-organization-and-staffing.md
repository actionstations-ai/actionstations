To clear 7 West and 6 West, their current patients are consolidated onto 6 East, 7 East and telemetry; the House Supervisor tracks this on the bed board. 6 West and 7 West can receive no more than 40 patients between them, so any further patients from surge or from the ambulatory wing are staged on 5 telemetry.

## 3. Staffing for storm ride-out

- 120 nurses, 14 respiratory therapists, 9 pharmacists and technicians, 10 advanced practice providers and 6 intensivists and neonatologists are scheduled in two 12-hour shifts for 72 hours.
- In a ride-out, nurses may bring one family member and a pet to the dedicated family area only after approval by the Staffing Coordinator.
- The Staffing Coordinator keeps a call list of 40 more nurses who live inland and are asked to return after the storm; relief begins when roads reopen, typically 24 to 48 hours after landfall.
- A contract staffing agency can supply up to 15 critical care nurses in 36 hours after roads reopen.
- A nurse who is the only one in a unit with the competency (for example, NICU nurses with neonatal resuscitation program certification) is named on the roster so that an off-unit nurse is never assigned to a ventilated infant alone.

## 4. Competencies

Critical care nurses complete annual training on: transport ventilators, evacuation sleds and stair chairs, neonatal transport incubators, the structured handoff, and disaster triage. The NICU cross-trains six 6 West nurses to support infants. A vertical evacuation drill was last held in April 2026 and moved 8 ICU patients in 74 minutes, with two delays at the elevators.

## 5. Equipment

- Transport incubators: 6, each with a battery lasting about 2 hours.
- Transport ventilators: 8 adult, 4 neonatal.
- Portable monitors: 24 with 4-hour battery.
- Infusion pumps: 140 with 6-hour battery, pump library locked to unit profile.
- Evacuation sleds: 40; stair chairs: 12.
- Oxygen: bulk liquid tank with 5 days at average use; 30 E cylinders and 6 H cylinders in the central supply.
- Handheld radios: 60; satellite phones: 4.

## 6. Communication

Nurses use hospital-issued phones on the Wi-Fi; if Wi-Fi fails they use radio channel 3. The nursing leadership group chat is on a secure messaging application. Family communication is by phone from the unit or by the family liaison nurse; no photographs of patients are allowed.

## 7. Local setting

Corpus Christi and Nueces County: the Corpus Christi Bay, the Harbor Bridge and the causeways to Padre Island; the Texas A&M-Corpus Christi campus is a nearby shelter site; the nearest major receiving hospitals are in San Antonio (about 140 miles) and Victoria (about 90 miles). The National Weather Service Corpus Christi forecast office and the National Hurricane Center issue the forecasts; the Nueces County Judge orders evacuations of zones; the hospital follows the county order but decides independently on patient evacuation.
