# Sources: public guidance the Larkspur Constructors documents paraphrase

Larkspur Constructors, Inc., the Stillwater Regional Sewer District, Harlan & Weir Printing, Valley Gas Company, Miami River Power, Pinnacle Mutual Construction Insurance, Landmark Instrumentation Services, every person, phone number, project and monitoring value in this folder are simulated for training use. The real places and public agencies named (Dayton, Ohio; Dayton Fire Department; the City of Dayton building inspection division; OSHA; Ohio811) are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe.

- OSHA, 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, Excavations (competent person, protective systems, soil classification, access and egress, utilities, hazardous atmospheres). Public domain.
- OSHA, "Trenching and Excavation Safety" (OSHA 2226). Public domain.
- OSHA, 29 CFR 1904.39, reporting fatalities, hospitalizations, amputations and eye loss (8 and 24 hours). Public domain.
- OSHA, 29 CFR 1926.20 and 1926.32 on competent person and safety and health programs. Public domain.
- Common One Call and 811 damage prevention practice, as described by the Common Ground Alliance's best practices (named only).
- PHMSA, pipeline safety guidance on excavation damage to natural gas distribution lines. Public domain.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP) and the National Incident Management System. Public domain.
- FEMA, Urban Search and Rescue and technical rescue resource typing. Public domain.
