# Sources: public guidance the Marlow County documents paraphrase

Marlow County, its agencies, people, utilities, businesses, sites and phone numbers are simulated for training use. The real places named (Oregon, the Cascadia Subduction Zone, Tillamook and Lincoln counties, Portland) and the federal and state agencies are real. Nothing below is copied.

- US Geological Survey, ShakeAlert earthquake early warning system fact sheets and the description of how alerts are delivered (alert delivery, time to shaking, limits near the epicenter). Public domain.
- USGS, PAGER, ShakeMap and Did You Feel It? product descriptions; the Guide to Communicating Aftershock Forecasts. Public domain.
- NOAA National Tsunami Warning Center, tsunami message definitions (Warning, Advisory, Watch, Information Statement) and the local versus distant source guidance. Public domain.
- Oregon Department of Geology and Mineral Industries (DOGAMI), Cascadia scenario tsunami inundation maps and the evacuation zone sizes (S, M, L, XL, XXL); Oregon Emergency Management Cascadia planning scenarios. Public documents, summarized only.
- Applied Technology Council, ATC-20 postearthquake safety evaluation of buildings (placard categories Inspected, Restricted Use, Unsafe), cited by name only.
- FEMA P-646, Guidelines for Design of Structures for Vertical Evacuation from Tsunamis. Public domain.
- FEMA, Cascadia Rising 2016 exercise after-action material. Public domain.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP) and the National Incident Management System. Public domain.
