# Sources consulted

All documents are fictional. Great Plains Crisis Network and its people, systems and sites are simulated. Public guidance below was paraphrased for realism; nothing was copied.

- SAMHSA, 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: program overview and center operations, including network administrator, state coordinator and backup routing concepts.
- SAMHSA, Disaster Distress Helpline and Disaster Behavioral Health Concept of Operations: community support after a tragedy.
- Recommendations for reporting on suicide (reportingonsuicide.org) and the National Action Alliance for Suicide Prevention safe messaging guidance: used for the public statement rules.
- CDC, Preventing Suicide: a Technical Package of Policy, Programs and Practices.
- FEMA HSEEP (Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program): exercise structure.
- Standards named only: contact-center service level conventions (percent answered within a threshold).
