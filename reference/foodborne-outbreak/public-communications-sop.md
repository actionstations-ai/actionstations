# MCHD-COM-03 — Outbreak Communications and Notice Standard Operating Procedure

Marlow County Health Department · Public Information Office · Effective September 2026

## 1. Purpose

Describe who says what to whom, when, in an enteric outbreak, so that the health department, the hospital and the school district publish the same facts and the same numbers. The Public Information Officer (PIO), Davin Soto, owns this procedure. The Director, Renata Okafor, approves every public notice before release.

## 2. Principles

- State only what is known and the time it was known. Every count carries the words "as of" and a time.
- Counts come from the line list. If the line list and a notice disagree, the notice is wrong and is corrected.
- Name a place or a business only when the Director has decided that the benefit to the public outweighs the harm and the facts support the link. Implication is not enough.
- Never publish a name, an age combined with a small place, or a detail that could identify a patient.
- Recommend what people should do: seek care for bloody diarrhea, fever above 102 F, signs of dehydration or symptoms lasting more than three days; stay home from work or school while ill; wash hands.

## 3. Products

| Product | Audience | Owner | Approval | Target after decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Holding statement | Press | PIO | Director | 2 hours |
| Clinician health alert | Clinicians, urgent care, long-term care | Medical Advisor and Epidemiology Lead | Director | 4 hours |
| Exposure notice | Public | PIO | Director | 4 hours |
| School and employer letter | Parents and staff | District nurse and PIO | Director | 6 hours |
| Hotline script | Hotline staff | PIO | Epidemiology Lead | 2 hours |
| Media update | Press | PIO | Director | By 16:00 on active days |
| Social media post | Public | PIO | Director | Same time as the notice |

## 4. The exposure notice

An exposure notice has a headline stating what is known without naming a cause that is not proven, then: who may be affected (people who attended the event named, with the date and times), what illness to watch for and when it starts, what to do, where to call (hotline 555-0150, with hours), whether specimens are wanted, the as-of time and the count, and the time of the next update. Text is at a reading level of grade 8 or lower; the Spanish version is released within four hours of the English.

## 5. Coordinating with the hospital and the school

1. Before a notice is released, the PIO sends the final text to the Marlow Regional Medical Center communications manager and the Fenwick Community School District nurse coordinator, with the count and the as-of time.
2. Partners may add their own contact information and local instructions but not change numbers, dates, symptoms or recommendations. If a partner holds a different number, they call the PIO before publishing; the PIO and the Epidemiology Lead resolve the difference and send a single corrected figure.
3. The hospital's statement about its own patients (how many were treated, how many admitted) is its own to give and is added to the MCHD count only after the infection preventionist confirms the patients meet the case definition.
4. The school district does not announce closures or event cancellations as a response to the outbreak unless MCHD recommends it; the superintendent decides and tells the PIO before telling the press.

## 6. Press and rumors

All press questions go to the PIO. The standard lines: what is known, what is not known, what MCHD is doing, what people should do, when the next update will be. The PIO does not guess at the cause. If a social media post names a restaurant or a food without evidence, the PIO replies with the facts and the approved notice; the Director decides whether to ask the platform for correction.

## 7. Corrections

A wrong figure is corrected in the same channel it was published, within two hours of discovery, with the words "Correction" and the old and new values. The decision log notes who found it and when.

## 8. Public data releases

The Epidemiology Lead prepares a summary table (count of cases by status tier, hospitalized, epidemic curve by onset date) for release. A release needs the Director's approval and a statement of the limits: counts are provisional, reports lag, and tiers change as laboratory results arrive. Small cells (fewer than five in any age or place group) are suppressed.

## 9. Hotline

Six lines, 07:00 to 21:00 while the outbreak is active. Staff use the script and the call note form: caller's code, symptoms, onset, attendance, items eaten, whether a specimen can be provided, and whether the caller is a food handler or works in child care or health care. Calls from clinicians go to the Nurse Manager. Calls from press go to the PIO. Calls reporting an additional event with illness go straight to the Epidemiology Lead.

## 10. Timeline guide for the first 24 hours

- Hour 0: first report logged; holding statement drafted.
- Hour 4: clinician health alert out once the case definition is adopted.
- Hour 8: case definition in use; hotline script ready.
- Hour 12: outbreak declared if criteria met; Board of Health chair told.
- Hour 18: exposure notice out.
- Hour 24: school and employer letters out; first media update; state epidemiologist briefed on the count, the curve and the leading hypothesis.
