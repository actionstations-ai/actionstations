# Sources — public guidance the Marlow County documents paraphrase

Marlow County, the Marlow County Health Department, Fenwick, Dunmore, Kessler, Ostrander, Marlow Regional Medical Center, the Fenwick Community School District, the Heritage Days Committee, Prairie Table Catering, every person, phone number, event and identifier in this folder are simulated for training use. The real places and agencies named (Iowa, the Iowa Department of Health and Human Services, the State Hygienic Laboratory at the University of Iowa, CDC, FDA, USDA FSIS) are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe.

- CDC, Foodborne Outbreak Investigation guidance and Steps of an Outbreak Investigation. Public domain.
- CDC, Council of State and Territorial Epidemiologists case definitions for nationally notifiable conditions (Salmonellosis, STEC, norovirus). Public domain.
- CDC, Guide to Confirming the Etiology of Foodborne Disease Outbreaks and the Foodborne Disease Outbreak Surveillance System (NORS) reporting guidance. Public domain.
- CDC, PulseNet national molecular subtyping network, general description. Public domain.
- CDC, Epi-Aid program description (assistance requested through the state). Public domain.
- FDA Food Code (exclusion and restriction of ill food employees, Chapter 2) and FDA/USDA FSIS roles in foodborne outbreak traceback. Public domain.
- CDC and FDA, Environmental Assessment of Foodborne Illness Outbreaks guidance. Public domain.
- CDC, Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC) manual. Public domain.
- Council to Improve Foodborne Outbreak Response (CIFOR), Guidelines for Foodborne Disease Outbreak Response (named only).
- Iowa Administrative Code, reportable diseases and outbreak reporting rules (named only; the exercise paraphrases the general requirement).
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP). Public domain.
