# Sources: public guidance the Ironcrest documents paraphrase

Ironcrest Machinery Corporation, its subsidiaries, customers, lenders, insurer, auditor, advisers, directors, executives and every phone number in this folder are simulated for training use. Rockford, Illinois, Beloit, Dubuque and Joliet are real places; the SEC, the FBI, CISA, the Department of Defense and state attorneys general are real agencies named as outside parties. The documents are original text that paraphrases the public guidance below.

- SEC, Release 33-11216, Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure (2023); the staff fact sheet; Form 8-K Items 1.05 and 8.01 and the four business day rule; Regulation S-K Item 106. Public domain.
- SEC Division of Corporation Finance, statements and guidance on Item 1.05 and Item 8.01 usage (public staff statements).
- CISA, Tabletop Exercise Package: Ransomware, and Stop Ransomware guidance. Public domain.
- Treasury OFAC, Updated Advisory on Potential Sanctions Risks for Facilitating Ransomware Payments. Public domain.
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, Govern function (standard cited by name only).
- ISO 22301 (business continuity management systems), terms MTPD, RTO and RPO (standard cited by name only; nothing copied).
- FEMA, Continuity Guidance and business continuity templates for the private sector (Ready.gov business). Public domain.
- HSEEP, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program. Public domain.
