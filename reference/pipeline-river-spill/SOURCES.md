# Sources: public guidance the Ironbark and Sterling Bluff documents paraphrase

Ironbark Pipeline Company, Prairie Spill Response Cooperative, the Prairie Line, Prairie Bend County, Sterling Bluff and its Water Department, Prairie Bend Rural Water District, every person and phone number are simulated for training use. The Kansas River, Kansas, EPA Region 7, the Kansas Department of Health and Environment, the National Response Center and the federal and state agencies are real. Nothing below is copied.

- US Coast Guard and EPA, National Contingency Plan (40 CFR Part 300): the roles of the person in charge, the National Response Center, the Federal On-Scene Coordinator, Regional Response Teams and Unified Command. Public domain.
- Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, pipeline response plan rules (49 CFR Part 194): Qualified Individual, worst case discharge and response resources, by name and in paraphrase. Public domain.
- National Preparedness for Response Exercise Program (PREP) Guidelines: the core components, the exercise cycle and the exercise record. Public domain.
- NOAA Office of Response and Restoration, Environmental Sensitivity Index and oil spill response tools, boom use in currents and the response to inland spills. Public domain.
- EPA, Oil Spill Program guidance and the Area Contingency Plan concept; dispersant and in-situ burn authorization under the National Contingency Plan. Public domain.
- FEMA and EPA, drinking water utility response and source water contamination planning guidance (EPA Water Security Division). Public domain.
- US Fish and Wildlife Service, piping plover listing and nesting-season guidance. Public domain.
- FEMA, Incident Command System forms 202, 204, 232, 233 and 234, and the Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP).
