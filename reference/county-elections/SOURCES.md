# Sources — public guidance the Lisle County documents paraphrase

Lisle County, the City of Carrow, the Lisle County Board of Elections, and every person, polling location, precinct, contest, number, hostname and procedure in this folder are simulated for training use; no real election office, candidate, party or election result is depicted. The contests on the simulated ballot are fictional local measures and offices. The shape of the documents and the obligations they describe follow the public guidance below. Nothing was copied; the plans paraphrase what these sources require or recommend, in the way a county board of elections' own plans would. State-specific details (board structure, early voting at the board office, a single drop box, poll hours) are written as a plausible Ohio county would describe them; check current state law and Secretary of State directives before relying on any of them.

## Federal law

- 52 U.S.C. 20701 — officers of election must retain and preserve for 22 months from a federal election all records and papers relating to voting in it. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/52/20701
- HAVA §302 provisional ballots and §301(a)(3) accessible voting, as summarized in the project's elections research dossier.
- 52 U.S.C. 20302(a)(8) — military and overseas ballots transmitted no later than 45 days before a federal election (as stated in the dossier).

## Scenario pattern

The exercise combines patterns from the elections dossier: phishing of election staff and account takeover, voter-record integrity questions during early voting with provisional ballots as the fallback, impersonation of the election office (a fake "polling place moved" notice) and false claims about voting equipment on social media. Products named (ES&S DS200, DS850, ExpressVote, Electionware; KnowInk Poll Pad; Microsoft 365) are real products used as realistic equipment; nothing in the scenario attributes a flaw to them. The county voter registration system "VoterTrack" and its vendor are simulated.
