# Cedar Ridge Dam Site Map

> **Fictional site** created as source material for tabletop exercise generation. No map image is provided; the facts below are drawn from the EAP, Security Plan and O&M Manual and can be used to validate generated injects.

Known layout facts (use to validate generated injects):

- **Location:** Cedar River mile 38.2, Pike County, Ohio; City of Milford is 4.5 river miles downstream. Operator: Cedar Valley Water Authority (CVWA).
- **Dam:** concrete gravity, 22 monoliths, 142 ft high, crest el. 812.0, crest length 1,180 ft, oriented west (right bank) to east (left bank). Crest Road runs the full length with a keyed West Crest Gate and East Crest Gate.
- **Spillway:** center of the dam (monoliths 9–14), **Spillway Gates 1–5** numbered west to east; Spillway Hoist House on the crest above the gates (Doors P-6 east, P-7 west); quick-connect panel QC-1 for portable generator PG-1 on the crest outside the hoist house; stilling basin below with Gauge G-2. Opening sequence 3-2-4-1-5.
- **Intake:** **Intake Tower 1** on the upstream face of monoliths 4–5, reached by a 90-ft access bridge from the west abutment (Door P-5); headwater Gauge G-1 on the tower.
- **Penstocks 1–2:** steel, 12 ft diameter, from Intake Tower 1 down the west side to the powerhouse.
- **Powerhouse:** west bank at the toe; **Units 1 and 2** (18 MW each); diesel generator DG-1 inside; Doors P-3 (north personnel, fail-safe) and P-4 (generator floor from tailrace deck, fail-safe); CRD-HMI-02.
- **Control building:** west bank, upslope of the powerhouse; Door P-1 entrance, Door P-2 Control Room (CRD-HMI-01), Door P-8 Server/Telecom Room (CRD-SCADA-SRV01, CRD-HIST-01, CRD-JUMP-01, CRD-FW-01).
- **Switchyard:** 69 kV, west bank beside the control building, padlocked gate.
- **Low-level outlet:** 8-ft conduit through monolith 9, discharging to the stilling basin.
- **Tailrace:** below the powerhouse; tailwater Gauge G-3; public bank fishing to a fence 300 ft downstream.
- **Reservoir:** Cedar Lake upstream; buoy line 500 ft upstream of the dam (no physical boom); public boat ramp 0.6 mi upstream on the east shore.
- **East Overlook:** public, open dawn to dusk, east abutment, 150 ft from the Spillway Hoist House behind a 4-ft railing; unlit parking after 2300. AeroSentry UAS detector on the hoist house roof.
- **Access:** Dam Access Road (1.8 mi) from State Route 41 with a motorized gate (closed 2200–0600; CAM-01) and a culvert crossing of Pine Run that floods at Milford Gauge ~16 ft. Alternate: Ridge Fire Road (gravel, 4WD) from County Road 12.
- **Off-site:** CVWA maintenance yard in Milford (portable generator PG-1), 6.5 road miles away; Milford Gauge at the Main Street Bridge (RTU-08, cellular).
- **Cameras:** CAM-01 access gate · CAM-02 Door P-1 · CAM-03 Control Room · CAM-04 Door P-3 · CAM-05 generator floor · CAM-06 switchyard · CAM-07 Intake Tower 1 bridge (no night image) · CAM-08 West Crest Gate · CAM-09 East Overlook · CAM-10 Spillway Hoist House · CAM-11 spillway face · CAM-12 tailrace · CAM-13 upstream face/buoy line · CAM-14 server room.
- **Controllers:** PLC-1 (Spillway Gates 1–3), PLC-2 (Spillway Gates 4–5, LLO); RTU-01/RTU-02 gate positions, RTU-03 Intake Tower 1, RTU-04 Penstocks 1–2, RTU-05 LLO, RTU-06 weirs W-1 to W-4 and piezometers, RTU-07 Gauges G-2/G-3, RTU-08 Milford Gauge. SCADA network 10.84.x.x; business LAN 10.85.x.x.
