## SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

### 1.1 Purpose

This Emergency Action Plan (EAP) defines the actions the Cedar Valley Water Authority (CVWA) and its partner agencies take to detect, classify, and respond to an emergency at Cedar Ridge Dam, and to warn and evacuate people at risk downstream on the Cedar River. It is written to protect life first and property second.

### 1.2 Authority

Prepared under 18 CFR Part 12, Subpart C (Emergency Action Plans), the FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 6, and Ohio Revised Code Chapter 1521 as administered by the Ohio Department of Natural Resources Division of Water Resources (ODNR-DWR), the state dam safety regulator. Cedar Ridge Dam is classified HIGH hazard potential: failure or mis-operation will probably cause loss of human life.

## SECTION 2: DAM DESCRIPTION

### 2.1 Location and Purpose

Cedar Ridge Dam is located at Cedar River mile 38.2 in Pike County, Ohio, 4.5 river miles upstream of the City of Milford. Completed in 1961, it impounds Cedar Lake for flood control, hydroelectric generation, municipal water supply (Milford intake at river mile 33.9) and recreation.

### 2.2 Principal Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Dam type | Concrete gravity, 22 monoliths, founded on sandstone and shale |
| Structural height | 142 ft (streambed el. 670.0 to crest el. 812.0, NAVD88) |
| Crest length | 1,180 ft; Crest Road (single lane, gated at both abutments) |
| Spillway | Central gated ogee, crest el. 774.0; **Spillway Gates 1–5** (radial/Tainter, 40 ft wide × 32 ft high, numbered west to east); capacity 182,000 cfs at el. 810.0 |
| Gate hoists | Electric wire-rope hoists in the Spillway Hoist House on the crest; controlled by **PLC-1** (Gates 1–3) and **PLC-2** (Gates 4–5) |
| Intake | **Intake Tower 1**, upstream face of monoliths 4–5, reached by a 90-ft access bridge from the west abutment; trash racks, two wheel-mounted intake gates |
| Penstocks | **Penstocks 1–2**, 12-ft diameter steel, 410 ft each, Intake Tower 1 to the powerhouse |
| Powerhouse | West bank at the toe; **Units 1 and 2**, vertical Francis, 18 MW each, 1,650 cfs each at full gate |
| Low-level outlet (LLO) | 8-ft conduit through monolith 9, hydraulic slide gate plus emergency bulkhead; 2,900 cfs at el. 798.0 |
| Reservoir | Cedar Lake; normal summer pool el. 798.0 (2,340 acres, 61,500 acre-ft); top of flood-control pool el. 806.0 (79,800 acre-ft) |
| Downstream control point | **Milford Gauge** at the Main Street Bridge; action stage 11.5 ft, flood stage 14.0 ft |

### 2.3 Access

The site is reached from State Route 41 via the 1.8-mile Dam Access Road (paved, one entrance). The Dam Access Road crosses Pine Run on a single culvert; when Pine Run overtops (approx. 2 ft over road at Milford Gauge 16 ft), the site is reachable only by the Ridge Fire Road from County Road 12 (gravel, 4WD recommended). See Site Map.

## SECTION 4: EMERGENCY CONDITION LEVELS

### 4.1 Overview

CVWA uses three emergency levels. A level may be declared directly at any level; it is not necessary to pass through lower levels. Any employee who observes a condition in this section reports it immediately to the Control Room or, after hours, to the on-call operator.

### 4.2 Level 1 — Non-Failure / High Flow Event

An unusual event that is not expected to threaten the dam but requires monitoring, or high releases that may cause flooding downstream without dam failure.

- Total release greater than 20,000 cfs, or Milford Gauge forecast to exceed action stage (11.5 ft).
- Reservoir above el. 801.0 and rising.
- New seepage, or flow at any seepage weir (Weir W-1 to W-4) above its Level 1 threshold in O&M Manual Table 6-2.
- Single Spillway Gate inoperable during flood season (March 1 – June 30).
- Earthquake felt at the site or reported magnitude 4.0+ within 50 miles.
- Security event without damage: intrusion into a restricted area, unidentified UAS over the crest or Spillway Hoist House, credible threat.
- Loss of SCADA control for more than 30 minutes during releases above 8,000 cfs.

**Declared by:** DOM or DSE (Control Room Operator may declare in their absence).

### 4.3 Level 2 — Potential Failure Developing

A condition that could lead to dam failure if not corrected, with time available for analysis and remedial action.

- Reservoir above el. 806.0 (top of flood-control pool) and forecast to continue rising.
- Two or more Spillway Gates inoperable when releases are required.
- Seepage carrying sediment (cloudy discharge) at any weir, or a new boil downstream of the toe.
- Piezometer readings above Level 2 thresholds in two or more adjacent monoliths.
- Unexplained movement of a Spillway Gate, the LLO gate, or a unit wicket gate, from any cause including suspected unauthorized control.
- Confirmed damage to a gate, hoist, Intake Tower 1, or a penstock from any cause.

**Declared by:** DOM or DSE only. GM notified within 30 minutes.

### 4.4 Level 3 — Failure Imminent or in Progress

- Reservoir at or above crest el. 810.0 with the spillway at capacity (overtopping imminent).
- Rapidly increasing seepage, sediment-laden flow, sinkhole, or cracking with displacement of a monolith.
- Uncontrolled release through a failed Spillway Gate, the LLO, or a ruptured penstock.
- Explosion or deliberate attack causing structural damage.

**Declared by:** DOM, DSE, GM, **or the on-call operator** when the DOM cannot be reached within 5 minutes. Do not delay Level 3 notifications to confirm with management.

## SECTION 5: NOTIFICATION PROCEDURES AND FLOWCHART

### 5.1 General

Notifications are made by telephone with a voice read-back; email and text are supplementary only. Each call is logged on Form EAP-3 (time, person reached, message, call-back number). If a primary contact does not answer within 2 minutes, call the alternate and continue down the list — do not wait.

### 5.2 Notification Flowchart (Level 3)

| Step | From | To | Time Limit (from declaration) | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Person observing | Control Room / On-Call Operator | Immediately | Radio CVWA-1 or phone |
| 2 | Control Room / On-Call Operator | Dam Operations Manager (Glen Haskins) | Within 5 minutes | Phone; if no answer, declare Level 3 per 4.4 |
| 3 | DOM (or operator) | Pike County 911 Communications Center → Pike County EMA and Pike County Sheriff's Office | **Within 15 minutes** | Phone (dedicated dam line); 911 Center activates sirens S-1 to S-9 and PikeAlert |
| 4 | DOM (or operator) | City of Milford EOC / Milford Fire Dispatch | **Within 15 minutes** | Phone |
| 5 | Pike County EMA | Town of Harlow; Village of Stone Bend | Within 30 minutes | Phone and PikeAlert officials group |
| 6 | DOM | General Manager (Teresa Okafor); DSE (Mei-Lin Chao) | Within 30 minutes | Phone |
| 7 | DOM or DSE | National Weather Service forecast office | Within 30 minutes | Phone (NWS issues Flash Flood Warning / Dam Break) |

### 5.3 Level 2 Notifications

Steps 2, 3 and 4 within **30 minutes**; Pike County EMA decides on public warning. NWS, ODNR-DWR and FERC within 2 hours. Sirens are **not** sounded at Level 2 unless EMA orders a precautionary evacuation of Zone A.

### 5.4 Level 1 Notifications

DOM notifies Pike County EMA (business line) and the GM within 2 hours when releases or forecasts affect downstream areas. Security-caused Level 1 events follow Security Plan Section 9 (Sheriff immediately; FERC within 6 hours).

### 5.5 Pre-Scripted Messages

**Level 3 (to 911 Center):** "This is [name], [title], Cedar Ridge Dam. We have declared a Level 3 emergency: failure of Cedar Ridge Dam is [imminent / in progress]. Activate dam sirens and PikeAlert for Inundation Zone A immediately. Flood wave arrival at Milford Main Street Bridge is estimated at [time]. My call-back number is [number]."

**Level 2:** "…We have declared a Level 2 emergency: a potentially hazardous condition is developing at Cedar Ridge Dam. Failure is not imminent. Be prepared to warn and evacuate Inundation Zone A. Next update by [time]."

### 5.6 Notification Gaps Known to CVWA

- After hours there is **no operator on site**; detection depends on SCADA alarms routed through the AlarmLink autodialer to the on-call operator's phone (O&M Manual Section 3.5). The on-call operator may be up to 60 minutes from the site.
- Cellular coverage at the powerhouse is unreliable; use the control room landline, satellite phone, or radio.
- The 911 Center's dedicated dam line terminates on a single console; if busy it rolls to the general 911 queue.

## SECTION 6: INUNDATION ZONES

### 6.1 Basis

Inundation mapping (2023 study) models a sunny-day failure of monoliths 8–12 at normal pool and a PMF failure at el. 810.0. Travel times below are for the sunny-day case (shortest warning). Zone boundaries follow the PMF-failure extent.

### 6.2 Inundation Zones A–C

| Zone | River Miles Below Dam | Communities / Key Facilities | First Arrival / Peak | Population at Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Zone A** | 0 – 6.5 | City of Milford (river-front and downtown); **Milford Community Hospital** (112 beds, River Road); **Cedar Valley Elementary School** (410 students, Mill Street); Milford Water Treatment Plant; Main Street Bridge; SR-41 (River Road) | 38 min / 1 h 20 min at Main Street Bridge | 4,350 |
| **Zone B** | 6.5 – 14.0 | Town of Harlow; Harlow Senior Living (64 residents); County Road 12 low-water crossing; SR-41 Harlow Bridge; Pike County Fairgrounds | 1 h 55 min / 3 h 10 min at SR-41 Harlow Bridge | 2,100 |
| **Zone C** | 14.0 – 23.0 | Village of Stone Bend; Stone Bend Campground (seasonal, up to 300); Cedar Valley Railroad bridge | 3 h 45 min / 5 h 30 min at Stone Bend | 620 (+300 seasonal) |

### 6.3 Notes

- Milford Community Hospital requires a minimum of 3 hours to evacuate non-ambulatory patients — more than the Zone A warning time in a sunny-day failure. The hospital's plan is vertical evacuation to floors 3–4 (above PMF depth of 14 ft at the building).
- Cedar Valley Elementary School relocates to Milford High School (outside Zone A) by bus; school-day warning is sent directly by the Milford EOC.
- A non-failure release of 40,000 cfs floods SR-41 at three low points between the dam and Milford, cutting the Dam Access Road route for responders.

## SECTION 8: EVACUATION ROUTES

- **Zone A (Milford):** north and east away from the river via Main Street to SR-7 (Milford High School shelter); Oak Hill Road to the Pike County Fairgrounds is **not** to be used (Fairgrounds are in Zone B).

## SECTION 9: PREVENTIVE AND EMERGENCY ACTIONS

### 9.1 Authority to Operate

The DOM has sole authority to direct Spillway Gate, LLO and unit operations under this EAP, including releases outside the rule curve to protect the dam. The GM must approve a deviation lasting more than 24 hours. Pike County EMA must be told before total release is increased above 8,000 cfs (O&M Manual Section 2.4). In a Level 3 emergency, protecting the dam takes precedence over downstream ramping limits.

### 9.2 Actions by Condition

| Condition | Emergency Actions |
|---|---|
| High reservoir / flood | Open Spillway Gates in sequence 3-2-4-1-5 per O&M Manual 2.3; run Units 1 and 2 at full gate; open LLO; request upstream USACE reservoir hold-back |
| Seepage / piping | Lower pool at maximum safe rate; place inverted filter (stockpile at west abutment laydown yard: 200 tons graded filter sand and gravel) |
| Spillway Gate failure (stuck) | Shift releases to other gates; attempt local control at the Spillway Hoist House; manual handwheel operation (O&M 4.4) |
| Loss of SCADA | Transfer to local manual control (Security Plan 8.4; O&M 4.3); station operator in Spillway Hoist House and powerhouse |
| Loss of station power | Diesel generator DG-1 supports control building only; **gate hoists require portable generator PG-1 from Milford yard** (O&M 5.3) |
| Penstock rupture | Close intake gates at Intake Tower 1 (local or SCADA); trip affected unit; evacuate powerhouse |
| Security / sabotage | Security Plan Section 9; Sheriff; preserve evidence; DSE inspects before resuming operations |

## SECTION 10: TERMINATION AND ALL-CLEAR

### 10.1 Termination

The DOM, with concurrence of the DSE, terminates the EAP event when the dam is stable and releases are within the rule curve. Pike County EMA issues the public all-clear; CVWA does not message the public directly about re-entry.

### 10.2 All-Clear Checklist

- [ ] DSE inspection complete and documented (Form EAP-5)
- [ ] Reservoir below el. 801.0 and falling, or stable at a level approved by the DSE
- [ ] All Spillway Gates and the LLO tested or locked out and tagged
- [ ] SCADA control returned per O&M Manual Section 8 verification checklist
- [ ] Pike County EMA, City of Milford, NWS, ODNR-DWR and FERC informed of termination
- [ ] Written FERC incident report submitted (within 24 hours of Level 3; 7 days for Level 2)
- [ ] After-action review scheduled within 30 days
