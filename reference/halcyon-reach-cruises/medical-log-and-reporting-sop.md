# HR-HSP-02: Medical Log, Case Counting and Authority Reports

Halcyon Reach Cruises · MV Halcyon Dawn · Revision 7

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes how the ship counts cases and reports them to the CDC Vessel Sanitation Program (VSP), the CDC quarantine station and others, and who signs what. It paraphrases the VSP operations manual requirements and 42 CFR 71.21.

## 2. Persons on board at embarkation (Halcyon Dawn)

Passengers: 2,980 (the February 14 sailing was full). Crew: 1,120. All percentages use these denominators. The Purser's office confirms the denominator at embarkation and posts it on the Hotel Director's board.

| Threshold | Passengers (2,980) | Crew (1,120) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 percent | 30 | 12 |
| 2 percent | 60 (59.6 rounded up) | 23 (22.4 rounded up) |
| 3 percent | 90 (89.4 rounded up) | 34 (33.6 rounded up) |

## 3. The medical log

The medical center keeps the medical log electronically and on paper. For each case: cabin or crew number (never a name in any report), date and time of presentation, symptom onset, the symptoms, the date of embarkation, and whether the person is a passenger or crew. A case is entered once. Cases reported by stewards are entered even if the person does not come to the medical center. The log is closed to editing; corrections are by a new entry that references the old one. Stool specimen results and the lab are recorded when available.

## 4. Counting

At each count (06:00, 12:00, 18:00, 24:00) the nurse in charge totals: new cases in the period, cumulative passenger cases, cumulative crew cases, and the percentages to one decimal place. The Chief Medical Officer signs the count. The figures are communicated to the Hotel Director, the Master and shoreside Public Health. No other number is communicated outside the ship.

## 5. Reports to the VSP and the quarantine station

- **24-hour report:** Sent to the VSP 24 to 36 hours before arrival at a US port. It gives the ship, the voyage dates, passengers and crew on board, and the cumulative number of cases among passengers and among crew since embarkation, even if the number is zero. For the Galveston arrival at 07:00 on Sunday, the window is 19:00 Friday to 07:00 Saturday ship time.
- **4-hour update:** If the number of cases has changed since the 24-hour report, a cumulative update is sent at least 4 hours before arrival (by 03:00 on Sunday for a 07:00 arrival). Another is sent only if the ship is still more than 4 hours from arrival.
- **Special report at 2 percent:** Within 15 days of a US port, when cumulative cases reach 2 percent of passengers or of crew, the Chief Medical Officer telephones the VSP and sends the written special report; policy is within 1 hour of the count that reached the threshold. A second special report is sent at 3 percent. After that the VSP asks for daily updates; routine reports continue.
- **Immediate report to the quarantine station:** The Master reports any death on board, or any ill person with a fever and rash or other signs of a communicable disease, in the 15 days before arrival, to the CDC quarantine station that serves the arrival port (Houston, for Galveston) by phone and electronic message.
- **Content:** counts, percentages, the symptoms, the actions taken (isolation, cleaning, itinerary) and the lab results. The Chief Medical Officer signs, the Master countersigns, and the shoreside Public Health director confirms receipt.

## 6. Other authorities

- The port health authority and the local health department are sent a copy of each report on the day it is filed (Galveston County Health District and Texas Department of State Health Services).
- The flag state (the Bahamas Maritime Authority) receives the outbreak notification when the 3 percent level is reached.
- The terminal operator and the port are told the expected arrival and any delay.
- The CDC may board at the next port to inspect, sample and review the plan; the Master and the Environmental Officer receive them at the gangway with the plan, the log, cleaning records and food and water records.

## 7. Testing

Stool specimens for norovirus testing are collected from at least 10 ill persons at the first reports and sent ashore at a port with a contracted laboratory; results are reported to the Chief Medical Officer by phone and are logged. A positive norovirus result changes nothing in the counting and does not change the reporting.

## 8. Accuracy rules

Every count in every outward message (guest letter, family hotline script, media statement, crew notice) matches the signed count with the time. Percentages are given with the denominator. Nobody rounds down. If counts change between messages, the later message states the old and new count and the time of each.
