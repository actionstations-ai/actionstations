# HR-COM-03: Guest, Family, Travel Partner and Media Communications

Halcyon Reach Cruises · Corporate Communications and Guest Care · Revision 6

## 1. Principles

We tell guests what is known, what is not known and what we are doing. We tell them before a reporter does. We never give a medical diagnosis or advice in a guest letter beyond the ship's public health instructions. We use the signed count and its time in every message. We do not speculate on cause, and we do not name an individual.

## 3. Guest letters

The Hotel Director issues a letter delivered to every cabin within 4 hours of the 2 percent level. It states the number of guests with symptoms, the percentage, what the ship is doing (cleaning, isolation, changes at the buffet), what guests can do (hand washing with soap, report symptoms to the medical center, stay in the cabin if ill), the nature of the illness (stomach illness, common on ships and ashore), and who to ask. It carries no medical advice beyond that.

## 4. Families and the hotline

When more than 1 percent of passengers are ill or when media are covering the voyage, Guest Care opens the family hotline 800-555-0120 (staffed 06:00 to 24:00 Central). Agents follow the script: confirm the ship is sailing as scheduled or what has changed, give the current signed count and its time, say the ship's medical team is caring for ill guests, and take messages for guests (the ship passes them in 2 hours). Agents never quote a count that is not the latest signed count.

## 5. Media

The first holding statement is issued within 60 minutes of the 2 percent special report or the first media inquiry, whichever is first, and says: the ship, the voyage, the number of passengers and crew with symptoms with the percentage, what is being done, that the ship has reported to the CDC and will cooperate, and where to find updates. The statement is posted on the Halcyon Reach newsroom page and sent to the wire service and to Galveston and Houston reporters. A reporter's question about a count, a death or the cause is answered with the signed count only; "we do not know yet" is an acceptable answer.

## 6. Social media and passenger posts

The social listening desk watches the cruise review forums and the main platforms for posts from the ship. Passenger posts are never answered with a denial; they are answered with the statement and the count. A post that claims a death, a quarantine or a count that does not match is checked against the log by Public Health within 30 minutes, and if false a correction is posted, with the facts. The ship's Wi-Fi is not shut off; the Captain may ask the shoreside to limit it only for safety reasons.

## 7. Travel partners and next voyage

If boarding is delayed, the first notice goes to booked guests of the next voyage by text and email at least 4 hours before the original boarding time, with the new time. Travel agents receive a call from the sales desk and an email. The terminal operator and the port are told. If the voyage is canceled, guests receive a full refund and a future cruise credit of 100 percent.

## 8. Crew

Crew receive the same facts as guests and see the same count. The Hotel Director holds a crew meeting within 6 hours of the 2 percent level. Crew who are ill are cared for, paid and not penalized for reporting.

## 9. Consistency check

Before any message is released, the Communications duty officer compares its counts, times and actions with the latest signed count in the Public Health log, and a second person initials the check.
