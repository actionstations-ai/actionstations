# Sources: public guidance the Halcyon Reach Cruises documents paraphrase

Halcyon Reach Cruises, MV Halcyon Dawn and MV Halcyon Tide, every person, phone number, count and itinerary in this folder are simulated for training use. The real places and public agencies named (Galveston and Houston, Texas; the Port of Galveston; the CDC Vessel Sanitation Program and the CDC quarantine station; Galveston County Health District; the Texas Department of State Health Services; the Bahamas Maritime Authority; Roatán, Costa Maya and Cozumel as ports of call) are real. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe.

- CDC Vessel Sanitation Program, Operations Manual (outbreak prevention and response plans, definitions of reportable acute gastroenteritis, 24-hour and 4-hour reports, special reports at 2 and 3 percent). Public domain.
- 42 CFR 71.21, Radio and electronic reporting of illness and death in the 15 days before arrival. Public domain.
- CDC, Norovirus guidance for cruise ships and environmental cleaning (chlorine solutions, isolation after symptoms stop). Public domain.
- CDC, Quarantine station jurisdictions and notification of ill travelers. Public domain.
- 46 U.S.C. 3507, Cruise Vessel Security and Safety Act (reporting of crimes). Public domain.
- CDC, Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC) principles. Public domain.
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP). Public domain.
