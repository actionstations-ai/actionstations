# Sources — public guidance the Prairie Ridge documents paraphrase

Prairie Ridge Health, its people, extensions, vendors and building details are simulated for training use. Fort Dodge, Webster County, Mason City, Des Moines, Iowa, the FDA, the Iowa Board of Pharmacy, the Iowa Hospital Association and the regional poison center are real; the neighboring hospitals and wholesalers in the documents are fictional or unnamed. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources below describe.

- FDA, Drug Shortages (database and the CDER Drug Shortage Staff reporting email), including the FDA's guidance on temporary importation during intravenous solution shortages. Public domain.
- FDA, Drug Supply Chain Security Act (DSCSA) summary for trading partner licensure. Public domain.
- ASPR TRACIE, resources on healthcare supply chain and medical surge. Public domain.
- HHS ASPR, resources on IV solution shortage and conservation strategies. Public domain.
- FDA MedWatch for adverse event reporting. Public domain.
- CMS, emergency preparedness conditions of participation (42 CFR 482.15). Public domain.
- FEMA HSEEP. Public domain.
- Named only, not copied: ASHP guidelines on managing drug product shortages, USP chapter 797 (sterile compounding), ISMP safe practice guidance.
