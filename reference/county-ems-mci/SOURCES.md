# Sources: public guidance the Larkfield County documents paraphrase

Larkfield County, Brandt Lake, LCEMS, the fire department, the hospitals (including Larkfield Regional Medical Center), Tri-County Coach Lines, every person and phone number are simulated for training use. Wisconsin and the federal and state agencies are real. Nothing below is copied.

- US Department of Health and Human Services, ASPR TRACIE, mass casualty incident triage and hospital surge resources; Hospital Preparedness Program healthcare coalition guidance. Public domain.
- HHS Chemical Hazards Emergency Medical Management (CHEMM) and the Radiation Emergency Medical Management resource for START and JumpSTART triage descriptions. Public domain.
- National Highway Traffic Safety Administration and the CDC, National Guideline for the Field Triage of Injured Patients and the SALT mass casualty triage guideline (named as the standard, nothing copied). Public domain.
- FEMA, National Incident Management System and the Incident Command System (ICS) forms, including the ICS 201 and 214 and the Medical Branch worksheets. Public domain.
- US Fire Administration and NFPA standards by name only (NFPA 1561 incident management, NFPA 1670 technical rescue; mayday and accountability practice described generally).
- Wisconsin Department of Health Services EMS rules and regional trauma system descriptions (summarized only).
- FEMA, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP).
