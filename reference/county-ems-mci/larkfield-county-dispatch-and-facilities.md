# Larkfield County: Dispatch, EMS Resources and Facilities

Reference sheet for exercise players · LCEMS · 2026

## 1. County

Larkfield County, Wisconsin: 63,000 residents, 880 square miles, county seat Brandt Lake (14,200 residents). Highway 21 is the main road; it carries commuter traffic and milk and fuel tankers. Larkfield County Transit has four 35-foot buses.

## 2. Larkfield County Communications Center

The center is at 150 Justice Drive, Brandt Lake, and handles 911 for the Sheriff, 11 fire departments and LCEMS. Supervisor Keiko Tanaka (920-555-0188). It runs a computer-aided dispatch (CAD) with a unit status board, an incident detail screen and a pending calls queue. Radio: LARK DISP 1 (primary dispatch), LARK CMD 2 (command), LARK TAC 3, LARK TAC 4, MED 1 (hospital communications), and the regional interoperability channel. Station alerting (tones and voice) is automatic. Mutual aid requests go from the center to the neighbors by phone and are logged: requested resource, requested from, time of request, estimated arrival.

## 3. EMS resources

LCEMS has 7 ambulances (5 ALS, 2 BLS) on a normal day staffed from Brandt Lake, Pell Harbor and Wausau Road bases, 2 supervisors, 1 mass casualty trailer (stored at Station 1, with 100 triage tags, tarps, cots and a command kit) and the on-call response of 20 volunteer EMTs. Chief Paula Whitaker; Deputy Chief for Operations Luis Navarro; MCI Coordinator Hannah Moreau. Mutual aid is from neighboring counties' EMS (15 to 25 minutes away) and Northwoods Air Medical (one helicopter, call sign Air 7, based on the LRMC roof).

## 4. Hospitals and coalition

See SOP-MC-02. LRMC MEDCOM: 920-555-0190. LRMC Emergency Department Medical Director Dr. Alicia Boateng; Nursing Supervisor on duty at night. Larkfield Healthcare Coalition coordinator Greg Wilmot: 920-555-0192.

## 5. Other agencies

The Larkfield County Sheriff and the Wisconsin State Patrol (crash investigation and traffic); the Medical Examiner's office (Dr. Bernard Okoro, 920-555-0196); the American Red Cross (reunification); Larkfield County Public Health (the Health Officer, Dr. Anita Cho); the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (fuel spills); Wisconsin Emergency Management.

## 6. Reunification and public information

Brandt Lake High School gymnasium (capacity 300) is the reunification center, with a family room and a private room for notifications. The county PIO is Sandra Kowal (920-555-0185). The county's mass notification system "LarkfieldAlert" (about 22,000 contacts) is used for traffic and road closure notices.

## 7. A typical weekday evening

Highway 21 carries 9,000 vehicles in the evening commute. A senior center charter bus (Tri-County Coach Lines, a fictional company) makes a weekly trip with 31 passengers and one driver to the casino in the next county. Fuel and milk tankers use the road.

## 8. Training

LCEMS and the coalition hold an annual MCI drill in the autumn and a tabletop in spring. The coalition last exercised the SALT triage method in 2025.
