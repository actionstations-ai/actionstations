# Sources: public guidance the Pinecrest documents paraphrase

Pinecrest Fresh Cut, LLC, its growers, customers, carriers, co-packer, insurer, people and every phone number in this folder are simulated for training use. Salinas, Stockton, Fresno, Phoenix, Dallas and Yuma are real places; the FDA, the California Department of Public Health, the CDC and other state agencies are real agencies named as outside parties. The documents are original text that paraphrases what the public sources describe.

- FDA, Requirements for Additional Traceability Records for Certain Foods (Food Traceability Rule, 21 CFR part 1 subpart S), including the Food Traceability List (leafy greens), traceability lot codes, critical tracking events, key data elements and the 24-hour sortable spreadsheet requirement. Public domain. The compliance date has changed; the documents state the practice, not the date.
- FDA, 21 CFR part 7, Enforcement Policy, subpart C, Recalls (including voluntary recalls), recall classes I, II and III, depth of recall and effectiveness checks. Public domain.
- FDA, Reportable Food Registry guidance (reports within 24 hours). Public domain.
- FDA, Guidance for Industry: Initiation of Voluntary Recalls Under 21 CFR Part 7, Subpart C. Public domain.
- FDA and CDC, outbreak investigation pages for leafy greens and E. coli O157:H7 (general practice described, no outbreak depicted). Public domain.
- FDA, Food Safety Modernization Act, Preventive Controls for Human Food (21 CFR part 117) on recall plans. Public domain.
- Leafy greens industry practice on cold chain (34 to 38 degrees Fahrenheit) and lot coding (described generally, nothing copied).
- FEMA, Community Lifelines Implementation Toolkit (food, water, shelter lifeline). Public domain.
- HSEEP, Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program. Public domain.
