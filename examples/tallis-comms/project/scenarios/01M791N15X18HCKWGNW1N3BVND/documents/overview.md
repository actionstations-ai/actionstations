# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Sample Rows** (Web Store Export, Correction & Disclosure)
Location: Tallis Home Products, Inc., Communications Cell, Conference Room 3B, Dayton, Ohio · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Chief Communications Officer, VP Corporate Communications (Writer), Media Relations Manager, Social Media Manager, General Counsel and CISO. The SimCell plays reporters, the wire desk, fact-checkers, platform trust and safety teams, Brightwater Markets and other retail partners, customers and social media, state attorney general staff, investors, the CEO, Investor Relations and Customer Care.

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Speaking first without speaking wrong, correcting fast when you do, keeping the statement, customer email and filing on the same numbers, and deciding on materiality before all the facts are in.

**The Threat:** At 06:40 ET on Tuesday, March 9, 2027, the Tallis security operations center in Dayton saw a bulk export of customer records from the web store. The export ran through the API credentials of the web store vendor, CartBridge Commerce, and CISO Lena Hartmann suspended the key at 07:05. At 07:15 a breach-forum post claimed that 1.9 million Tallis customer accounts were for sale with passwords and cards, and it showed 200 sample rows. In fact the exposed data covers web store account records, including bcrypt-hashed passwords and Lakeview Payments payment tokens but no full card numbers. Forensics will later show that 412,000 accounts were exported between March 2 and March 8. The incident also affects Tallis Rewards and customer care, the press and social channels, the retailer relationship and the company's SEC disclosure duties.

**The Operational Impact:** The Communications Cell opens in Conference Room 3B at 07:30 under Chief Communications Officer Marcus Delaney. A holding statement is due within 60 minutes, while the main facts are still unknown.
- **07:52:** TechSignal Daily emails, with a 10:30 deadline.
- **08:10:** The CISO confirms that 38 of the 40 checked sample rows are real accounts but cannot say how many were taken.
- **08:20:** The holding statement goes out saying no payment information was involved. That is wrong, because the export included payment tokens. A correction stating what was wrong and what is right is needed by about 11:40.
- **08:40:** A viral post claims passwords were stored in plain text, and look-alike-domain phishing emails offer customers a "refund".
- **09:00–09:45:** The market opens and the stock falls 6 percent.
- **09:20:** Midwest Business Wire runs a bulletin.
- **09:30:** Brightwater Markets' category manager Fiona Ledesma calls.
- **Through the morning:** 240 calls wait in the customer care queue.
- **10:15:** Forensics changes the count from 1.9 million to 412,000 accounts.
- **11:00:** The Disclosure Committee under General Counsel Gerald Okafor must decide whether the incident is material or not yet determined, and whether to file Item 1.05, Item 8.01 or nothing yet before the 5:30 p.m. EDGAR cut-off.
- **Late morning to afternoon:** Miami Valley 9 News asks for a live noon interview, and Open Ledger Check asks about the plain-text claim. The customer email to 412,000 people takes about an hour at the 400,000-per-hour send limit. A press conference decision is due by 14:00, and The Valley Courier's deadline is 16:00.

Spokesperson Dana Whitcombe must give one set of numbers across the newsroom page, the company accounts, the customer email and any filing.

**Core Plan Sections Tested:**
- TMPCDI › 3. Holding statement template
- TMPCDI › 4. Hard questions and the answers
- TMPCDI › 5. Rumor and false claim protocol
- TCIDC › 4. Materiality determination
- TSCDMD › 2. Customer data systems
- TCIDC › 8. Insurance, law enforcement and vendors

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to issue a holding statement within 60 minutes that separates what is known, not known and being done, then publish a correction by about 11:40 that names the wrong 'no payment information' line and states that Lakeview Payments tokens were exported.
- **Use of Doctrine:** Demonstrate the ability to move every external statement through the TCCP 3 approval route (Writer drafts, CISO confirms facts, General Counsel clears, CCO approves, CEO sees, IR told) and record the approver and time on the statement log, even under the TechSignal Daily 10:30 deadline.
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to reach and record a materiality decision ('material' or 'not yet determined') at the 11:00 Disclosure Committee meeting, using the factors in TCIDC 4, and to choose Item 1.05, Item 8.01 or no filing before the 5:30 p.m. EDGAR cut-off.
- **Information Consistency:** Demonstrate the ability to keep one time-stamped fact sheet, so that the newsroom page, company accounts, customer email and any 8-K all use the 412,000-account figure after the 10:15 forensic update, and to answer the plain-text-password claim and the 'refund' phishing under the TMPCDI 5 rumor protocol.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Chief Communications Officer (COMMS) (COMMS):** Owns communications strategy, approval of every external statement, the decision on a press conference by 14:00 and the choice of who speaks.
- **VP Corporate Communications (Writer) (WRITER):** Owns the time-stamped fact sheet and drafts holding statements, corrections, message maps, Q&A and the customer email.
- **Media Relations Manager (MEDIA) (MEDIA):** Manages every reporter call and email, the reporter log, deadlines, and the choice of on the record, background or decline with COMMS.
- **Social Media Manager (SOCIAL) (SOCIAL):** Manages company accounts, social listening, the listening report and platform takedown requests for impersonation and phishing.
- **General Counsel (Disclosure Committee Chair) (LEGAL):** Owns clearance of statements on cause, scope, number of people affected, legal duty and fault, and chairs the Disclosure Committee on materiality and the 8-K.
- **CISO (CISO):** Owns verified technical facts on the CartBridge export, the work with Cardinal Forensics, and the reports to the FBI and CISA.
