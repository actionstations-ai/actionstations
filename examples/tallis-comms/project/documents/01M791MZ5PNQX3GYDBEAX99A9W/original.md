# TLC-COM-01: Crisis Communications Plan

Tallis Home Products, Inc. · Corporate Communications · Dayton, Ohio · Revision 4

## 1. Purpose and scope

Tallis Home Products makes small kitchen appliances and cleaning products, sells them through retail partners and through its own web store at tallishome.example, and employs about 6,800 people in Dayton, Ohio, and at two plants in Ohio and Tennessee. About 1.9 million customers hold a web store account. Tallis is a public company, so what it says in public, to employees, to retailers and to investors must agree with what it files.

This plan tells the Corporate Communications team who may speak, how a statement is approved, how fast each kind of statement goes out, and how the team works with Legal, Information Security and Investor Relations when the incident is a cyber event. It applies to any event that could draw press or social media attention: a data incident, a product safety event, a plant accident, an executive matter.

## 3. Approval route

Nothing goes out unless it follows this route, and the approver and the time are written on the statement log.

1. **Writer drafts** from the fact sheet.
2. **CISO confirms facts** (technical statements only), by reply on the draft.
3. **General Counsel clears** anything on cause, scope, number of people, regulatory duty, or fault. Target: 20 minutes for a holding statement, 90 minutes for a full statement.
4. **CCO approves** every external statement. In the CCO's absence the VP Corporate Communications approves holding statements only.
5. **CEO sees** every statement about a data incident before it is posted, by text or in the cell; the CEO does not edit for facts.
6. **IR is told** before anything goes out that could move the stock. When the market is open, IR also tells the Disclosure Committee chair (the General Counsel).

Employees hear before or at the same time as the public. Retail partners' account managers hear within 30 minutes of the first public statement.

## 4. Speed targets

| Product | Target |
|---|---|
| Holding statement on the website newsroom and on company accounts | within 60 minutes of the first credible public claim or confirmed incident |
| Reply to a reporter with a deadline | before the deadline, even if it is "we are looking into it and will update at 12:00" |
| Customer email, after Legal and the CISO agree what to say | same day as the facts are confirmed, not before they are confirmed |
| Employee message | before or with the holding statement |
| Social listening report | every 30 minutes while the cell is open |
| Next update | always stated in the previous one |

## 5. Rules of speech

- Say what is known, what is not known and what is being done, and when the next update comes.
- Never state a number of affected people until the CISO and General Counsel confirm it and the figure is on the fact sheet. If a claim is made by someone else, say "we are investigating this claim", not "the claim is false" or "the claim is true".
- Never say "no evidence" when the truth is "we have not looked yet".
- Do not speculate about who the attacker is. Do not say a cause until the forensic firm gives it to us in writing.
- Every statement carries the exercise time on the log, the same wording for the same fact, and the same numbers everywhere. If a fact changes, issue a correction that says what was wrong and what is right.
- On the record, on background and off the record are stated by Media Relations before the answer. Nothing is off the record from Tallis.
- No one outside the cell posts or replies publicly on the incident. Employees who are asked refer the person to the newsroom page.

## 7. Press conferences

A press conference is held only if the CCO decides that a written statement cannot answer the questions, there is something new and verified to say, and the spokesperson has been rehearsed on the five hardest questions. It is announced by a media advisory with the who, what, when and where. Dana Whitcombe speaks; the CEO appears only if customers' safety is at stake.

## 8. Closing

When the last update is issued the CCO closes the cell, the VP Corporate Communications archives the log, and a hot wash is held within five working days with Legal, Security, Customer Care and IR.
