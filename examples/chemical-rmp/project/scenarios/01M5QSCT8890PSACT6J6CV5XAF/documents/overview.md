# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Ghost Line** (SCADA Deception & Chlorine Release)
Location: Teague Valley Chemical Company, Corbin Falls Works, Corbin Falls, Teague County, West Virginia · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Site Command (Incident Commander), Operations, Process Control / OT, EHS and Compliance, Security, Community Relations / PIO

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Field over screen, cyber integrity, and coordinated emergency response

**The Threat:** An unexplained control-system state occurs during a chlorine release at Rail Spot 2: the DCS shows ESD-310 closed and the release stopped, but the field reports a growing yellow-green cloud and a continuing rise on the railcar scale WT-302. The OT Engineer later discovers a remote session from TVC-JMP-01 to TS-EWS-01 with no log entry and the Triconex key switch in PROGRAM mode, indicating possible unauthorized access and system manipulation.

**The Operational Impact:** The board operator sees a closed ESD-310 on the DeltaV screen but cannot stop the release. The loader at Rail Spot 2 observes a visible chlorine cloud, continues to rise on WT-302, and calls the CCR. The ERT must enter Level A suits and manually close HV-312. Fence-line detectors AT-904 and AT-905 alarm as the plume drifts east toward the Riverside Addition and Corbin Falls Elementary School. The Incident Commander must declare a General Emergency, sound sirens S-1 to S-6, and request shelter-in-place from Teague County OEM. The EHS Manager must report to the NRC, WV SERC, LEPC, and DEP with a release estimate. OSHA must be notified for a worker with potential exposure. The TVIMA mutual aid team is activated. The media and neighbors begin calling the community line. The school district asks if classes can open at 06:30. The cell room trip decision must be made under pressure. No unit restarts until Section 10 is complete.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Field vs. Screen Integrity:** Demonstrate the ability to trust field observations over control-system displays when a process release continues despite a DCS indication of isolation, and to initiate a cyber-incident triage under Section 11 of the RMP.
- **Cyber-Physical Coordination:** Demonstrate the ability to activate the OT Engineer and FSO roles in parallel with the EHS Manager and PIO to report to CISA, FBI, and regulators, and to manage the dual response of emergency operations and cyber-investigation.
- **Regulatory Notification Discipline:** Demonstrate the ability to report a chlorine release to the NRC, WV SERC, LEPC, and OSHA with factual data only, and to avoid speculation on cause or health impact, per the holding statement template and Section 9 of the RMP.
- **Siren and Community Activation:** Demonstrate the ability to declare a General Emergency, sound sirens S-1 to S-6, and initiate the CAER-Text and community line updates within 30 minutes of the first release call, as required by Section 8.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Emergency Coordinator / Site Incident Commander (IC):** Manages the declaration of emergency class, calls for mutual aid, and approves all notifications and actions.
- **Operations Superintendent / Shift Supervisor (OPS):** Owns the unit shutdown sequence, isolation of inventory, safe-park decisions, and the cell-room trip decision.
- **Process Control / OT Engineer (OT):** Owns DCS and SIS integrity, isolates the control network, and validates SIS logic before any reset.
- **Environmental, Health and Safety Manager (EHS):** Owns the reportable-quantity call and the NRC, SERC, LEPC and OSHA notifications.
- **Facility Security Officer (FSO):** Owns the site security plan, access control, theft and diversion response, and the law-enforcement liaison.
- **Community Relations / Communications Manager (PIO):** Owns sirens and the community notification line, neighbors and schools, and the media.
