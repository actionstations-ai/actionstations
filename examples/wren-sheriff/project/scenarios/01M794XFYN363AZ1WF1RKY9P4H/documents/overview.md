# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**North Gate** (Fairgrounds Abduction, AMBER Decision & Unified Command)
Location: Wren County Fairgrounds (command post at the Fairgrounds Exhibit Hall B office) and Wren County Communications, Wren County Sheriff's Office, Alder Falls, Wren County, Oregon · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Wren County Sheriff's Office command and support staff: Incident Commander (Patrol Lieutenant), Communications Center Supervisor, Investigations Sergeant, Public Information Officer, Victim and Family Liaison, and the Undersheriff (Sheriff's Office executive). The SimCell plays OSP, FBI Portland, NCMEC, Alder Falls PD, Fire District 1, the neighboring sheriffs, Oregon Emergency Management, the family, the media and social media users.

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Deciding on an AMBER Alert when the facts keep changing, setting up command across jurisdictions, and keeping one accurate public message while rumors and posts from the family spread.

**The Threat:** On a cool, dry Saturday in mid-October, about 3,000 people are at the Wren County Fall Festival at the Wren County Fairgrounds. A six-year-old girl (exercise name Lily Ashby) was last seen at 13:40 near the Exhibit Hall B petting area. A festival volunteer reports that a man led a child to a dark pickup truck that left by the north gate at about 13:50. Her grandparent called Wren County Communications at 14:00, and the CAD incident was flagged "missing child" before STARTEX at 14:05. The north gate road is inside Alder Falls city limits, so jurisdiction is shared. Witness accounts conflict: the truck is first dark, then dark blue, then gray. The volunteer is unsure of the time, and a second caller places the child at the food booths. As a result, the AMBER criteria are only partly met at first. Over the next four hours, this pressure spreads to the LEDS/NCIC entry, the OSP AMBER request, 911 call-taking, the ground search and public information.

**The Operational Impact:** A family is waiting at the fairgrounds without knowing where the child is. The Victim and Family Liaison must find them a private room away from the command post and explain the AMBER Alert before it goes out. At the same time, the grandparent posts the child's photo and a different truck description on a community Facebook group. Festival visitors, the festival organizer and the Wren County School District call in. A viral post names a local man as the abductor. A local TV reporter and a regional wire service ask whether there is a suspect and why the alert was not issued sooner. Deputies and Alder Falls PD officers work the fairgrounds and the north gate road. Wren County Search and Rescue volunteers start a ground search that must not take resources away from the vehicle search. The Incident Commander must name a building-level command post at the Fairgrounds Exhibit Hall B office. Unified command with APD, Fire District 1 and OSP must be formed within 30 minutes. The Communications Supervisor must get the Child Abduction-flagged LEDS/NCIC entry made and read back before any alert. The Supervisor must also work through the OSP Northern Communications Center coordinator, who checks the criteria and asks for more description. After the alert, tips flood the 911 center: the queue goes over 12 and hold times go over 30 seconds. The tip line moves to the conference room, staffed by three call-takers. WrenAlert cannot carry an AMBER Alert. At about 16:30, a tip puts a truck on Highway 26 heading toward the county line, which forces an extension of the alert area through OSP. The command group then has to decide when to cancel the alert and what the closing statement says.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to complete and update the AMBER worksheet under WCSO-SOP-12 section 4, recording each of the five criteria as met or not with a reason as the truck description and timeline keep changing, and to decide with the Undersheriff on the phone whether to request the OSP AMBER Alert or use a search-area Wireless Emergency Alert or Endangered Missing Person advisory instead.
- **Unified Command:** Demonstrate the ability to announce a building-level command post at the Fairgrounds Exhibit Hall B office within 20 minutes and form unified command with Alder Falls PD, Fire District 1 and Oregon State Police within 30 minutes, with every change of command announced on WREN-1 and logged in the CAD chronology.
- **Use of Doctrine:** Demonstrate the ability to get the Child Abduction-flagged LEDS/NCIC entry made and read back before any alert request, and to protect the 911 queue during the tip surge by moving tips to extension 4450, following the Communications Center plan (WCCFSP 5).
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to open the joint information center and issue one accurate, agreed message that corrects the grandparent's conflicting truck description and answers the viral post naming a local man within 30 minutes, without releasing case detail the Investigations Sergeant has not cleared.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Patrol Lieutenant (Incident Commander) (IC):** Manages the incident objectives, the Exhibit Hall B command post, unified command with APD, Fire District 1 and OSP, and the AMBER worksheet decision.
- **Communications Center Supervisor (COMMS):** Manages Wren County Communications (911), including the LEDS/NCIC entry, the contact with the OSP Northern Communications Center, the WREN-1 talkgroup and the tip-line surge.
- **Investigations Sergeant (INV):** Owns the scene boundary, the witness interviews, the evidence plan and the facts that decide AMBER criteria 1 to 3.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO):** Manages the media, social media monitoring, the joint information center and the release log for the Sheriff's Office.
- **Victim and Family Liaison (VFL):** Manages the family's care, a private room away from the command post, family contact and notification, and briefing the family before public alerts.
- **Undersheriff (Sheriff's Office executive) (USH):** Owns the agency's position, the executive input on the AMBER decision by phone, activation of the EOC and the requests to the state and partners.

## Phase 3: The AMBER Call (Hour 2:00 – 3:00)

Goal: Tests the one high-consequence decision of the exercise. Command must either request the OSP AMBER Alert on criteria that are now barely met, accepting a flood of tips and the risk of a wrong description, or use a search-area Wireless Emergency Alert or an Endangered Missing Person advisory and risk losing time. It then tests whether the 911 center and the alert area hold up after the call.
