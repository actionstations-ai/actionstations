# Hot Wash Guide
## North Gate

### Hot Wash Guide (Debrief)

**The "3-Up, 3-Down" Method:** Ask the room for three things that went well (3-Up) and three things that broke or failed (3-Down). Go around the room one team at a time (Command Post, Communications Center, Investigations, PIO/JIC, Alder Falls PD, Fire District 1, OSP) and write every item on the whiteboard in two columns before anyone discusses them.

**Key Discussion Questions for North Gate:**
- **The Call:** At I-11 (16:07), Trooper Ellen Hask asked you to choose between an OSP AMBER request, a search-area WEA and an Endangered Missing Person advisory, and to give a decision time. Talk us through your AMBER worksheet at that moment. Was each of the five criteria in WCSO-SOP-12 section 4 marked yes or no with a reason? Did you review it every 30 minutes as the truck went from black to dark blue to gray? For the criteria that were only partly met, did you list what information was missing and who would get it? And when did the Undersheriff get on the phone: before the decision or after it? _(Objective: Decision Making)_
- **Trust:** Between I-03 (the volunteer changing her account to 'dark blue, maybe a Ford') and I-07 (the parking volunteer's photo IMG_5126 timestamped 13:51:22), when did you stop relying on the witness accounts and start relying on the photo? Did you handle that photo as evidence, by preserving the original file and metadata, identifying the volunteer and telling him not to post it? Or did it just go around the command post as another update? How did it shape the one vehicle description your sergeant was willing to stand behind? _(Objective: Decision Making)_
- **The Gap:** At I-14 (16:32), Jefferson County dispatch asked who the IC was, what channel to use and whether your alert area would include their county. Meanwhile a tipster was still behind a gray-blue pickup on Hwy 26. Could everyone in the room have answered that call right then? When was the Exhibit Hall B command post announced, and when did unified command with Alder Falls PD, Fire District 1 and OSP actually form? Was each change of command announced on WREN-1 and logged in the CAD chronology? What would have let you bring in a neighboring jurisdiction faster? _(Objective: Unified Command)_
- **Doctrine Under Load:** At I-03 (14:35), OSP asked whether the LEDS entry carried the Child Abduction flag and who did the read-back. Did the command post know the answer, or did the Communications Center have to tell you? Later, at I-17, 7 callers in 5 minutes asked the tip line whether the alert was cancelled. Did moving tips to extension 4450 under WCCFSP 5 actually keep the 911 queue clear all afternoon? If so, who was watching the 4450 line for real leads like the Hwy 26 tip, and who was answering the people calling because of rumors? _(Objective: Use of Doctrine)_
- **The Message:** Three posts got ahead of you: the grandparent's 'silver-gray Chevy' post at I-03b, the 'black Dodge toward Redmond' update at I-06, and the false 'they found her' post at I-17. KWRN was also going live at 15:00, and a camera was filming the family through the lobby glass. What did you tell the public before you knew the facts, and who agreed to it? Did the JIC put out one message within 30 minutes that corrected the vehicle description and dealt with the viral post without releasing anything the Investigations Sergeant hadn't cleared? What did the Liaison tell the mother at 17:11 before the public heard it? _(Objective: Communication)_
