# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Wet Stock** (Cyclone Response, Coordination & Pipeline Integrity)
Location: Harbourlight Relief International (HRI) country office, Beira, Mozambique · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Country Director, Emergency Coordinator, Information Management Officer, Logistics Manager, Security Focal Point and the Headquarters Crisis Management Team Chair (Emergency Operations Director, Geneva). The SimCell plays the UN Humanitarian Coordinator, OCHA, the cluster coordinators, INGD, customs and port officials, donors, other agencies' security focal points, the shipping agent, the Nhamissa community committee, reporters and the other HQ CMT members.

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Duty of care for staff when communications are down, numbers that agree across every product, committing only the stock, staff, access and funds HRI actually has, and safe distributions under crowd and rumour pressure.

**The Threat:** Before STARTEX, fictional Tropical Cyclone Tevra made landfall near Beira, Mozambique, at about 22:00 on Thursday 11 February 2027. It brought winds of around 190 km/h and a storm surge, and the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System showed a red alert. The cyclone damaged the roof of HRI's Beira warehouse, leaving about 20 percent of the prepositioned stock wet or missing. It also cut the EN6 crossing at Dondo, leaving Buzi district reachable only by boat or helicopter with communications down. The exercise opens at 06:00 CAT on Friday 12 February and covers the first 72 hours across Sofala and Zambezia. Pressure points include cluster activation, a US$ 96 million flash appeal, a customs hold at Beira port on 6,000 tarpaulins, and on Saturday a crowd surge, a cash rumour and rising acute watery diarrhoea at the Nhamissa displacement site.

**The Operational Impact:** At 06:00, 61 of 64 staff are accounted for. Three staff members are in Buzi district with no radio or phone contact until they are found at 11:20, and their families are calling the Beira office. The logisticians find that the stock balance (3,000 shelter kits, 2,800 jerrycans, 4,000 hygiene kits and 12 portable water treatment units) is now overstated by about a fifth. The stock already reported to the clusters must be corrected before HRI can commit to any gap. Drivers cannot use the EN6 crossing at Dondo. Field teams for Buzi need boat or helicopter access and a movement approval before they can leave. The Emergency Coordinator does not arrive from Nairobi until noon, so the Country Director carries the first HCT meeting at 08:30 alone. At that meeting, INGD's 412,000 people affected conflicts with OCHA's 385,000. By 17:00 the Information Management Officer must submit the first 3W entries and make them match the situation message to Geneva and the flash update. The WASH cluster gap list shows no partner in Buzi district and shelter uncovered in Nhamatanda, with 12 hours to assign the gaps and 48 hours to cover them. At 15:00 customs hold a container of 6,000 tarpaulins at Beira port. Demurrage starts on day 5, an INGD waiver letter is needed, and the shelter-kit pipeline will break within 30 days unless a shipment is rerouted. On Saturday, displaced families and site managers at Nhamissa face a crowd surge at the distribution, and the Security Focal Point must stop movements in the area and share a security alert. A WhatsApp rumour promising cash on Sunday for a registration fee leads to calls from the community committee and reporters. The safeguarding focal point receives a complaint, and health and WASH staff report rising acute watery diarrhoea at the site.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Duty of Care:** Demonstrate the ability to account for the three out-of-contact staff in Buzi district, report the incident to the Security Focal Point and Country Director within 1 hour, notify the HQ Crisis Management Team chair within 2 hours of activation, and give families only cleared information until the staff are found at 11:20.
- **Information Integrity:** Demonstrate the ability to correct the overstated Beira warehouse stock balance with the clusters and submit the first 3W entries by 17:00, so that they agree with the situation message to Geneva and the flash update and explain the gap between INGD's 412,000 and OCHA's 385,000 affected.
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to decide which WASH and shelter gaps in Buzi and Nhamatanda HRI can take within the 12-hour assignment target, committing only the stock, staff, access and funds it actually has, and to choose between an INGD customs waiver and rerouting a shipment for the held container of 6,000 tarpaulins.
- **Safe Programming:** Demonstrate the ability to suspend movements and share a security alert without staff names under Saving Lives Together during the Nhamissa crowd surge, while countering the cash-for-registration rumour with one consistent message to the community committee and reporters.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Country Director (CD):** Owns the decision to activate the plan, staff security in Mozambique, commitments up to US$ 150,000 and HRI's position at the 08:30 HCT meeting until the Emergency Coordinator arrives.
- **Emergency Coordinator (EC):** Manages the scope and sites of HRI's response from noon on day 1, including the choice of which cluster gaps to take and how distributions at Nhamissa are run.
- **Information Management Officer (IMO):** Owns HRI's 3W entries, maps and flash updates, and makes sure the figures match the situation message to Geneva.
- **Logistics Manager (LOG):** Owns the Beira and Dondo warehouses, the fleet and the pipeline, including the recount of wet stock and the customs hold on the tarpaulin container.
- **Security Focal Point (SFP):** Manages the security risk assessment, movement approvals, incident reports and security alerts, and advises the Country Director.
- **Emergency Operations Director (HQ Crisis Management Team Chair) (CMT):** Owns the HQ Crisis Management Team in Geneva, its decision log, and the line to the country office, staff families and donors when staff are missing or evacuation is considered.

## Phase 3: Gaps and the Container (Hour 2:00 – 3:00)

Goal: Tests the exercise's one decision point: which Buzi and Nhamatanda gaps HRI commits to within the 12-hour target using only real stock, staff, access and funds, alongside the choice between an INGD customs waiver and rerouting the held tarpaulins.

Beats: DECISION POINT (13:00): The WASH cluster asks HRI, as a WASH cluster member, to take the Buzi district WASH gap. Accepting means sending teams by boat or helicopter under the HMSSSP 5 movement authorization into an area the three staff have only just left, and committing scarce water treatment units. Declining leaves Buzi with no partner past the 12-hour assignment target and damages HRI's standing with the cluster. Nhamatanda shelter must be weighed against the corrected shelter kit balance.; Mutual aid: the Logistics Cluster at Beira airport offers shared helicopter slots and boat access. Players must set out who authorizes HRI staff movements (the Country Director at Level 3 under HMSSSP 3) and how HQ CMT oversight fits with the in-country decision now that the Emergency Coordinator has arrived.; (15:00) Customs at Beira port hold a container of 6,000 tarpaulins. Demurrage starts on day 5, an INGD waiver letter is needed, and the shelter-kit pipeline breaks within 30 days. The choice is between pursuing the INGD waiver and rerouting a shipment through the Nairobi hub or Dubai under HMSCLP 4. Players must apply the approval thresholds: an Emergency Coordinator waiver up to US$ 50,000, the Country Director above US$ 50,000, and the Country Director's signing limit of US$ 150,000.; (17:00) The Information Management Officer must submit the first 3W entries at administrative post level. Under SOP-COORD-01 7, they must agree with the situation message to Geneva and flash update no. 1, or explain the 412,000 versus 385,000 difference and the stock correction. The HCT also confirms the US$ 96 million flash appeal, and HRI's request may include only activities that appear in its 3W and costing (SOP-COORD-01 8)..
