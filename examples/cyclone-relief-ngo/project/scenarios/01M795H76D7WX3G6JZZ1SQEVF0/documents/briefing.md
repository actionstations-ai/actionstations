# Player Briefing Packet
## Wet Stock — Cyclone Response, Coordination & Pipeline Integrity

Location: Harbourlight Relief International (HRI) country office, Beira, Mozambique · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 06:00 AM (Exercise Time) · Weather: Overcast over Beira with heavy rain bands that are now easing, gusts of 60–70 km/h and about 26 °C. This is the morning after Tropical Cyclone Tevra made landfall overnight.

### 1. Situation Brief

It is **06:00 Central Africa Time on Friday 12 February 2027** at the Harbourlight Relief International (HRI) country office in Beira, Sofala Province. Tropical Cyclone Tevra made landfall near Beira at about 22:00 on Thursday, with winds near 190 km/h and a storm surge along the coast. GDACS shows a **RED alert** for Mozambique. INGD's 05:30 bulletin (Boletim n.º 1) confirms widespread flooding and reports that the **EN6 crossing at Dondo is cut**. Mains power is off across the city. The office is running on its generator, HF radio works to Beira and Dondo, and mobile data comes and goes. A **skeleton crew of 9 staff** is in the office. Nobody in Beira has reported an injury, and the vehicles are parked in the compound. Today the first-72-hours timetable starts. In the first 6 hours, every staff member and their dependents must be contacted, the office and vehicle status confirmed, HQ notified and the warehouse stock checked. The Country Director, Alberto Matsinhe, will represent HRI alone at the **08:30 Humanitarian Country Team meeting**, because the Emergency Coordinator, Mariam Sesay, is flying in from Nairobi and will not arrive before **noon**. Partners will expect HRI to share its capacity and prepositioned stock. The Beira warehouse at Rua do Porto 18 holds stock for 15,000 people, and the Dondo sub-warehouse is 30 km away on the EN6. Geneva and the Regional Director expect an early first word from the office.

- Beira country office: OPEN on the generator; skeleton crew of 9 on site; no injuries reported
- Communications (HF radio, satellite phones, mobile data): OPERATIONAL: HF radio working to Beira and Dondo; satellite phones available; mobile data intermittent
- Staff accountability (roll call board): IN PROGRESS: 61 of 64 confirmed at 05:55; the 3-person Buzi WASH team has not yet checked in today
- Beira warehouse (Rua do Porto 18) and Dondo sub-warehouse: ON RECORD: prepositioned stock for 15,000 people; Dondo base reports the guard is fine
- Fleet (9 4x4 vehicles, 3 trucks, 2 water tankers): AVAILABLE: Beira vehicles parked in the compound; truck 2 at the Dondo base
- Information management (3W, flash update, HQ situation message): READY: templates prepared; the first situation message to HQ is due within the 6–12 hour window

**The Anomaly:** **06:15, phone call relayed in person.** Fátima Langa, the receptionist at the Beira office, comes to the Country Director's door: *"Senhor, I have had three calls in ten minutes from the families of the Buzi WASH team. The first was the wife of Abílio, the driver. The second was the mother of Graça, the mobiliser. The third was the brother of Celso, the WASH officer. They heard on the radio news that there is water near Guara-Guara, and they want to know if their people are well. Celso's brother asks which road our car took, because he wants to drive to Buzi himself this morning. I told them to wait. What do I say when they call back? One of them is still holding on line 2."*

**YOUR MISSION:** Keep HRI's duty of care to all 64 staff and their families, and its credibility with the HCT, the clusters and Geneva, while you investigate and resolve problems as they come up in the first hours after Tevra. You must balance **speed against verification**: partners, families and the media want answers now, but HRI may only commit stock, staff, access and figures it has confirmed. No team moves until its safety has been assessed.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- Start and end every radio, satellite phone, phone, WhatsApp or email message with "This is an exercise", because Beira really is a cyclone-prone city and staff families, partners or reporters who overhear a message must never mistake it for a real alert.
- If a real emergency or safety issue happens in this room or anywhere in the office, anyone may say "REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD", and the exercise stops immediately so that real staff, real people and real resources come first.
- The facilitator may announce "Time Jump: it is now [time]" to skip ahead, so the exercise can cover the first 72 hours in one session. When this happens, take stock of what would have happened in the gap and act from the new time.
- Treat printed injects, such as radio logs, emails, WhatsApp screenshots, bulletins, photos and reports, as real intelligence arriving through that channel. Check, verify and act on them as you would in a live response, because how you handle unconfirmed information is part of what is being tested.
