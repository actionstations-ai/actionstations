# Harbourlight Relief International: Mozambique Country Emergency Response Plan

Harbourlight Relief International (HRI) · Mozambique Country Office, Beira · Revision 4 · Effective December 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

This plan sets out how HRI's Mozambique country office responds to a sudden-onset disaster: a cyclone, flood or earthquake. It covers the first 72 hours, when the office decides what it will do, whom it works with and what it asks of headquarters. HRI is an international non-governmental organization (INGO) with headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland. In Mozambique it has 64 staff, 58 of them national, in programs for water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH), community health, shelter and livelihoods in Sofala and Zambezia provinces.

## 3. Activation triggers

The Country Director activates the plan when: the national disaster management agency, INGD (the National Institute for Disaster Management and Risk Reduction), or the national meteorological service declares an alert or a disaster for provinces where HRI works; or when 5,000 or more people are reported affected in HRI areas; or when HRI's own facilities or staff are affected. The Country Director notifies the Regional Director and the HQ CMT chair within 2 hours of activation.

## 4. The first 72 hours

| Time after the event | Action | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 6 hours | Staff accounted for (every staff member and dependents contacted); office and vehicles status; HQ notified; stock check at the warehouse | Country Director, Security Focal Point |
| 6 to 12 hours | First situation message to HQ and the coordination partners; rapid information from district focal points and community committees | Information Management Officer |
| 12 to 24 hours | Join the Humanitarian Country Team (HCT) and cluster meetings; share HRI's capacity and stock; first flash update; request surge staff | Country Director, Emergency Coordinator |
| 24 to 48 hours | Multi-sector rapid assessment with the clusters in the worst affected districts; start distribution of prepositioned items; submit the first "3W" (who does what where) entry | Programme Managers |
| 48 to 72 hours | Response plan; funding request (internal emergency fund and donors); confirm staffing, supplies and access | Emergency Coordinator |

## 5. Programs to scale

HRI scales: emergency water supply and water treatment; hygiene kits; latrines; community health teams and oral rehydration points; emergency shelter kits and tarpaulins; and cash for essential needs where markets work. HRI does not run general food distribution; it coordinates with the food security cluster lead.

## 6. Principles and accountability

HRI follows the humanitarian principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence. It consults affected communities on priorities and has a feedback line (+258 84 555 0188, with a toll-free SMS short code) and community committees in each site. Complaints about aid workers are handled by the safeguarding focal point and are escalated to Geneva within 24 hours. The country office reports on distributions with the number of people reached by sector and site against the response plan target.

## 7. Funding

HRI's internal emergency response fund releases up to US$ 350,000 within 24 hours of the CMT chair's approval. Institutional donors are approached through the Regional Director. HRI also contributes data to the Humanitarian Country Team's flash appeal and does not publish a funding request to the public until the CMT has approved the figure.

## 8. Reporting

HRI sends a daily situation message in the first week to headquarters and a weekly flash update to partners. A flash update has the sections: situation, humanitarian needs, response, gaps and constraints, and contacts. The 3W entry gives the organization, the activity, the location at administrative level 3 (the district post), the number of people, and start and end dates.
