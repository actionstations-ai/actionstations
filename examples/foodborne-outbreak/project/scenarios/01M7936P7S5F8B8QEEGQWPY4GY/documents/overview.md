# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Side Dish** (Supper Outbreak, Line List & One Count)
Location: Marlow County Health Department, second floor of the Marlow County Services Building, Fenwick, Marlow County, Iowa · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Public Health Director, Epidemiology Lead, Environmental Health Supervisor, Nurse Manager, Public Information Officer, Hospital Infection Preventionist (Marlow Regional Medical Center)

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Defining and counting cases by onset, not by report. Acting on a caterer before the vehicle is proven. Keeping one count and one message across the health department, the hospital and the school.

**The Threat:** On Saturday October 10, 2026, about 412 people ate at the Marlow Heritage Days Community Supper at the Fenwick Fairgrounds Exhibition Hall (service 17:00 to 19:30). Prairie Table Catering cooked it, a Dunmore baker supplied the sheet cake and a Kessler produce vendor supplied the coleslaw and pasta salad. Before STARTEX, attendees started falling ill with vomiting, diarrhea and fever, and from Sunday 20:00 they came into the Marlow Regional Medical Center emergency department. STARTEX is 08:40 on Monday October 12, when infection preventionist Sunita Reddy phones the MCHD epidemiology line to report 11 ED patients, several of whom ate at the supper. The illness is later identified as Salmonella. It points to a point-source exposure at the supper that may tie back to a vendor who also sold to two other communities. Meanwhile Prairie Table Catering is due to serve a senior meal on Tuesday. Pressure builds on case finding, laboratory testing, food establishment control and public messaging. The exercise runs 4 hours from 08:40. Facilitators compress Monday afternoon and evening into it: the hospital stool culture growing Salmonella at 15:20, and the line list reaching about 90 cases by evening.

**The Operational Impact:** Supper attendees, their families and about 140 students and 30 staff of the Fenwick Community School District who attended are getting sick. By 10:30 the district nurse coordinator Margo Teller reports 23 students and 6 staff absent whose families attended. An 81-year-old is admitted with dehydration and a positive blood culture. Callers fill the six hotline lines with symptoms, questions about the menu and rumors.

The Epidemiology Lead is the single owner of the line list. It grows from 17 coded cases to 38 by noon. Within 8 hours the team needs a three-tier case definition based on onset date and hour, and an epidemic curve built from onset times (06:00 to 30:00 after the meal, with a tail), never from report dates. The Nurse Manager has six interviewers on day one, eight once the Garrison and Pell nurses arrive, and has to hand out Cary-Blair stool kits. The State Hygienic Laboratory courier runs only Tuesday through Friday unless the Director authorizes a special courier.

The Environmental Health Supervisor must choose a voluntary hold, an embargo or a closure order for Prairie Table Catering before Tuesday's senior meal, and collect leftover food under chain of custody. The attack-rate table puts the coleslaw first, the pasta salad nearly as high and the chicken thighs as a plausible distractor, so nothing can be named publicly yet.

Message problems arrive at the same time:
- A Facebook post names the caterer.
- The hospital's statement says 14 cases while the MCHD line list says 17.
- The school district wants to cancel Tuesday's homecoming game and send a letter MCHD has not approved.
- Spanish translation takes four hours.
- The Board of Health chair calls the Director.
- Sequencing shows matching isolates in two neighboring counties, which raises whether to request CDC Epi-Aid through Iowa HHS.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Case Definition and Epidemic Curve:** Demonstrate the ability to adopt a three-tier (confirmed, probable, suspect) case definition approved by the Director within 8 hours of the 08:40 hospital report, and build the Heritage Days supper epidemic curve from onset date and hour, not report date, using the coded line list.
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to choose between a documented voluntary hold, an embargo signed by the Director or a closure order for Prairie Table Catering before Tuesday's senior meal, while the attack-rate table still cannot separate the coleslaw from the pasta salad and the chicken thighs.
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to issue a holding statement, a clinician health alert, an exposure notice and an approved school letter that carry one count with an as-of time matching the MCHD line list, that settle the gap between the hospital's 14 cases and MCHD's 17, and that answer the Facebook post naming the caterer without naming an unproven food vehicle.
- **Use of Doctrine:** Demonstrate the ability to apply the Level 3 outbreak declaration criteria, specimen and courier procedures and escalation steps in the MCHD plans: declaring and recording the outbreak, authorizing a special State Hygienic Laboratory courier for Cary-Blair stool kits, and deciding with the state epidemiologist whether to request CDC Epi-Aid once isolates match in two neighboring counties.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Public Health Director (DIR):** Manages the MCHD outbreak response: declares the outbreak, approves the case definition and every public notice, signs embargo or closure orders, and briefs the Board of Health chair, the county administrator and the press.
- **Epidemiology Lead (EPI):** Owns the case definition, the coded line list, the onset-based epidemic curve, the attack-rate analysis and contact with the state epidemiologist.
- **Environmental Health Supervisor (EHS):** Manages food establishment inspection, Prairie Table Catering and vendor records, the voluntary hold or embargo recommendation, and the collection of leftover food under chain of custody.
- **Nurse Manager (NM):** Manages the interviewer pool, distribution of Cary-Blair stool kits, exclusion counseling for food handlers, child care and health care workers, and the only key that links case codes to names.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO):** Manages the holding statement, exposure notice, hotline script, school and employer letter, social media posts, Spanish translation and rumor control.
- **Infection Preventionist, Marlow Regional Medical Center (Hospital Infection Preventionist) (IP):** Manages hospital case reporting to MCHD, stool and blood culture results, enteric precautions, staff exposure assessment and the hospital's public case count.
