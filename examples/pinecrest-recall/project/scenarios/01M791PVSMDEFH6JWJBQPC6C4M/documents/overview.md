# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Presumptive** (Lot Trace, Holds & Recall Notification)
Location: Pinecrest Fresh Cut, LLC — Plant 1 conference room and Recall Team bridge · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Food safety and quality (Recall Coordinator), supply chain planning and allocation, logistics and distribution, traceability and enterprise systems (MES/ERP/WMS), customer service, and corporate communications

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Deciding before the facts are confirmed, getting one set of numbers across MES, WMS and paper records, and keeping the required notification order while regulators, customers and the media push to go first.

**The Threat:** At 05:50 Pacific Time on Tuesday, October 12, 2027, during the Salinas Valley harvest, Coastal Analytical Labs emailed a presumptive positive for E. coli O157:H7 on a retained sample of lot PF1-27278-L2-B (Plant 1, line 2, shift B, produced October 5). Confirmation will take 24 to 48 hours. The scope covers every lot made on line 2 within 24 hours of the positive, plus raw material lot FLD-SLG-R14-C-27277 from Santa Lucia Growers Ranch 14, Block C. That makes five lots (PF1-27277-L2-B, PF1-27278-L2-A, PF1-27278-L2-B, PF1-27279-L2-A and PF1-27279-L2-B) totaling 38,600 cases. They shipped as 18,900 to the Stockton DC, 9,700 to the Phoenix DC, 7,400 to the Dallas DC and 2,600 direct to the co-packer Rivermark Kits in Fresno. Use-by dates run to October 19, and about 40 percent has already sold through. The weak points are the MES-to-WMS interface and Rivermark's partly paper records.

**The Operational Impact:** At 06:00 the Recall Team call opens on the bridge, and the hold and trace targets have been running since 05:50. Plant 1 stops line 2 for sanitation and a swab series. DC crews in Stockton, Phoenix and Dallas have to find, count and segregate held pallets. Dallas has already shipped 2,100 cases to 38 Cornerstone Grocers stores since 04:00. A Sonoran Cold Freight trailer near Tucson carries 1,320 cases and is 14 hours from Dallas, so dispatchers have to decide whether to reroute or hold it. The counts don't agree: MES shows 38,600 cases, a WMS export shows 38,540 and a draft press release says "about 40,000". Rivermark cannot quickly match its paper receiving records to the lots (the last mock recall left 4 percent unmatched), and its Caesar kits have gone on to national retail accounts. At 09:30 CDPH calls about three reported illnesses with a genetic match pending and asks for the distribution list. An FDA records request starts a 24-hour clock for the sortable spreadsheet. At 11:00 a food safety blogger posts that "romaine is about to be recalled." The President must decide the recall class, depth and public warning before confirmation arrives. Customer service reps must reach Harvest Table Markets (212 stores), Cornerstone Grocers (140 Texas stores) and Westline Foodservice (31 delivery points, including hospitals and school districts) within 4 hours of that decision. Cornerstone threatens to drop Pinecrest and demands replacement romaine, but supply chain can cover only about 60 percent of romaine volume within 48 hours. A Central Coast Courier reporter calls before the press release, which needs the retailers' agreement, can go out.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to build and present a recall recommendation to the President (proposed class, depth, the five PF1 lot codes, quantity and whether a public warning is needed) on a presumptive E. coli O157:H7 result, before lab confirmation, as PPHTRP 4 requires.
- **Technical Analysis:** Demonstrate the ability to bring the MES (38,600 cases), the WMS export (38,540) and Rivermark Kits' paper receiving records into one reconciliation table, as PLCTR 8 requires, within the 4-hour internal trace target, and to deliver the FDA sortable spreadsheet within its 24-hour clock.
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to keep the PFC-FS-04 2 notification order (plant and DCs, then FDA and CDPH, then customers, then the press release) while CDPH asks for the distribution list, a blogger posts early and a Central Coast Courier reporter calls before the release has retailer agreement.
- **Operational Coordination:** Demonstrate the ability to confirm physical holds at the Stockton, Phoenix and Dallas DCs within 4 hours, decide what to do with the Sonoran Cold Freight trailer near Tucson and the 2,100 cases already sent to Cornerstone stores, and allocate the roughly 60 percent replacement romaine supply among customers.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **VP Food Safety and Quality (Recall Coordinator) (RECALL):** Owns the hold, the recall recommendation to the President, contact with FDA and CDPH, the contact log and the reconciled case counts for the incident.
- **VP Supply Chain (VPSC):** Manages stopping shipments of lots that may share the cause and filling unaffected demand from other lots, including allocating limited replacement romaine among customers.
- **Director of Logistics (LOG):** Manages DC holds and segregation, trailers in transit, and the return or destruction of held product with certificates.
- **Traceability and Systems Lead (TRACE):** Owns the lot trace across MES, WMS and co-packer records, the reconciliation table and the FDA sortable spreadsheet.
- **Customer Service Manager (CS):** Manages customer phone calls, recall letters, acknowledgment tracking and effectiveness checks across Harvest Table, Cornerstone, Westline and Rivermark.
- **Communications Manager (COMMS):** Manages the press release, the consumer notice on the website, holding statements and media enquiries.
