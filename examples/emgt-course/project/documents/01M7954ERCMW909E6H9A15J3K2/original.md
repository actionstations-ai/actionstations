# MSU-EM-SOP-03: Emergency Notification and Shelter Operations

Marlowe State University · Office of Emergency Management, University Communications and Student Affairs · Revision 5 · Course document 4

## 1. Purpose

This procedure tells the Public Information Officer, the Chief of Campus Police, the Clery Compliance Officer and the Dean of Students how MSU warns the campus community, how it decides what kind of message is required, and how it opens and runs a shelter. It carries out Sections 6 and 8 of Annex F (Flash Flooding) and Annex A (Communications) of the Emergency Operations Plan.

## 2. The MSU Alert system

- **Reach:** about 13,600 subscribers (all students, faculty and staff by default; parents opt in). Channels: text, email, voice call, desktop banner on lab computers, the university home page banner, and 18 outdoor sirens with voice (6 on the Main Campus, 4 at Riverside, 3 at Sugar Run, 5 on the athletic fields).
- **Timing:** text and email reach nearly everyone in 2 minutes. Voice calls take up to 8 minutes. Sirens and banners are immediate.
- **Who can send:** the PIO or the PIO's deputy, Maya Okonkwo (740-555-0109), through the MSU Alert console. The Chief of Campus Police can send from the console when the PIO cannot be reached within 5 minutes.
- **Templates:** the console holds templates for "Flood: avoid area", "Flood: evacuate", "Shelter open", "Shelter closing" and "All clear". Text messages are limited to 160 characters.
- **Language:** every Alert is also sent in Spanish. Messages for students with hearing impairments are also sent by text and by the desktop banner, and the Disability Services Director reviews the template wording once a year.

## 3. Which kind of message is it

The Clery Act requires two kinds of campus messages. The Clery Compliance Officer decides with the Chief of Campus Police and the IC; the decision, the time and the reason go in the notification log.

| Message | When | Clery section | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency notification | An immediate threat to health or safety of people on campus, such as a flood requiring evacuation | 34 CFR 668.46(g) | Without delay, unless the notice would compromise efforts to contain the emergency |
| Timely warning | A Clery crime that is a continuing threat (for example a robbery series) | 34 CFR 668.46(e) | As soon as the pertinent information is available |
| Informational | Road closures, class cancellations, updates | Not Clery | At the PIO's judgment |

A flood evacuation is an emergency notification. The Clery Officer confirms; the PIO does not wait for confirmation if the IC orders an evacuation, and the Clery Officer completes the log afterward. MSU tests its notification system once each fall and spring semester and records the test.

## 4. Message content

Every message answers four questions: what is happening, where, what to do, and when the next update comes.

- **Flood, avoid area (text):** "MSU ALERT: Flash flooding at Lower Quad, Lot C and Dairy Lane. Avoid these areas now. Stay indoors elsewhere. Updates every 30 min at marlowestate.example/alert."
- **Flood, evacuate (text):** "MSU ALERT: Riverside Hall evacuation ordered now. Leave by stairs. Walk to Kessler Fieldhouse. Take medication and ID. Do not drive."
- **Shelter open (text):** "MSU ALERT: Kessler Fieldhouse shelter open. Enter at Gate 2. Pets in carriers allowed."

The PIO checks every message against what the police and the shelter lead have decided before sending. A message that disagrees with another message is held until the difference is settled.

## 5. Sequence for a flood evacuation

1. **IC orders the evacuation** and states the area, the clearance time and the shelter.
2. **PIO** sends the MSU Alert within 15 minutes and posts the banner. A holding line goes out on social media if the full message is not ready.
3. **Campus Police** and **Residence Life** go door to door and by RA phone tree; each RA reports floor clear to the Residence Life Director.
4. **Dean of Students** confirms the shelter is open (see section 7).
5. **Facilities** isolates the building's power and tells the IC when elevators stop.
6. **PIO** sends the update message every 30 minutes until the stage falls below 8.0, and an "All clear" only when the IC authorizes it.

## 6. Media, parents and rumors

- **Media:** The PIO is the only spokesperson. Reporters from Channel 9, the Marlowe Daily Ledger and MSU's student newspaper, The Hilltop, are given the same statements. Statements say what is known, what is not known and what is being done, and are posted on the university website.
- **Parents:** The Dean of Students and the PIO publish a parent line (740-555-0150) and a web page. FERPA limits what is said about an individual student: the university can tell a parent that the campus is evacuating and where students are being sheltered, and can say that a student has been accounted for only with the student's consent or under the health and safety emergency exception, decided by the Dean of Students with University Counsel.
- **Rumors:** The PIO monitors social media for posts about the flood and checks each rumor with Campus Police or Facilities before answering. A correction goes out through the channel the rumor spread on and states what was wrong and what is right.

## 7. Opening the shelter

The Dean of Students decides to open Kessler Fieldhouse. Steps:

1. Call Fieldhouse Operations (Lena Park, 740-555-0124) to unlock Gate 2, turn on the generator transfer switch and open the main floor. Time to open: 25 minutes by day, 40 at night.
2. Set up check-in at Gate 2 with 4 tables, ID card readers and the shelter log (name, ID, hall, time in, time out, needs).
3. Set up the areas: general sleeping area, a quiet area with power outlets for medical devices, a pets-in-carriers area, a supply table with water, blankets and charging stations. The Disability Services Director (Cora Whitfield, 740-555-0127) and a nurse from the Student Health Center staff the support table.
4. Request supplies from the Commons dining hall (meals for up to 1,000: 2 hours' notice).
5. Report "doors open" to the EOC, with capacity and count.
6. After the first 12 hours, ask the American Red Cross (Southeast Ohio chapter) to take over or share management, and record what was requested and when it arrived.

Capacity is 1,800; when the count reaches 1,500 the Dean opens the Student Union ballroom (400) as the alternate and tells the PIO to update the Alert.

## 8. Closing and consolidation

The shelter closes when the Riverside Hall and Sugar Run residents are rehoused, or when Facilities and the building inspector clear the buildings. The Dean tells the PIO to send the "Shelter closing" message 60 minutes ahead. Students with no safe housing are placed in temporary housing in Harlan Hall (120 beds held for this purpose).

## 9. Record keeping

The notification log holds every message with its time, channel, author and Clery decision. The shelter log holds each person's arrival and departure. Both logs are reviewed in the hot wash and the after-action report. Neither log leaves the university except to the Ohio Emergency Management Agency or as the Clery regulations require.
