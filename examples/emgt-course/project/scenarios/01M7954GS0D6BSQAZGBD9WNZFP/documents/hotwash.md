# Hot Wash Guide
## Ninety Minutes

### Hot Wash Guide (Debrief)

**The "3-Up, 3-Down" Method:** Ask the room for three things that went well and three things that broke or failed during the flood response. Go around the room one team at a time (EOC command, Facilities, Police, Communications, Student Affairs/shelter), write each item on the whiteboard in two columns, and don't let anyone repeat an item until every team has spoken.

**Key Discussion Questions for Ninety Minutes:**
- **The Call:** At 07:35 (I-12) the Vice President for Student Affairs asked you to hold the Riverside evacuation until the Mill Street gauge actually reached 8.0 and the 9 AM forecast came out. The Hall Director had just told you a full sweep takes about 90 minutes, and longer for floors 9 to 11 and the three residents on the access list. Did you decide on the 10.8 ft crest forecast, or did you wait for the gauge? What time did you give the order, and which forecast issuance did you write down as the basis, as EOP 4 requires? What would have let you decide faster? _(Objective: Decision Making)_
- **The Gap:** At 06:10 (I-04) the gauge passed action stage at 5.02 ft and was rising 1.2 ft/hr. Then the central plant needed an answer about the 08:00 diesel tanker by 06:45, and the fleet coordinator asked whether to start the 40-minute Lot C move now or wait for 8.5 (I-05). Did you work back from the 6.5 Dairy Lane closure and the 9.5 Lot C stage to a start time, or did you wait for each threshold to arrive? When Tech 4 reported two inches in the low corner of Lot C at 07:20, were the trucks and generators already moving? Did the tanker reach Kessler's half-full day tank? _(Objective: Infrastructure Protection)_
- **The Rumor:** At 07:50 (I-13) the 'trapped on the roof' post had 900 reposts in 8 minutes. Channel 9 was going live at 8:00, The Hilltop was publishing at 08:15, and 64 voicemails were waiting on the parent line. What had you already told the public when the flash flood warning came out, and was it within 15 minutes? Who checked the roof claim with Campus Police and Facilities before you answered? Was your correction one message, on the same channel, saying the same thing to Channel 9, The Hilltop and parents, or did different versions go out? _(Objective: Communication)_
- **The Count:** When Riverside emptied into Kessler Fieldhouse, how did you keep the shelter count tied to the 680-resident roster? How did you support the two students on oxygen concentrators and the deaf student, both during the evacuation and at the shelter? When the missing-student report came in at 09:30, what were you able to confirm, and to whom, within the FERPA limits in MENSO 6? Where did the shelter team depend on the residence hall staff or the police for information, and what was missing? _(Objective: Mass Care)_
- **Trust:** At 10:32 (I-18) the gauge read 10.81 ft with a 'CREST?' flag, and the 10:15 forecast said the crest was over but a second rise toward 12.0 ft was possible late in the afternoon. Did operations and the technical team read that forecast the same way? Did the 48 boxes still on the lower level and the floor-level UPS and switches in Whitcomb get moved, or did 'not in this forecast' lead you to stand down? When did you bring in the Marlowe and Athens County emergency management liaisons and NWS Charleston, and was that early enough? _(Objective: Infrastructure Protection)_
