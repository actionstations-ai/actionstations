# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Silent Calls** (CAD/AVL Integrity & Service Continuity)
Location: Rook County Public Safety Center, 250 West Jefferson Street, Halden, Rook County, Indiana · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Command / 911 Director, Floor Operations, Technology / CAD and County IT, Radio, Field Agencies (Sheriff, Police, Fire/EMS), Public Information

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Trust in the Data, Public Trust, and Crowd Management

**The Threat:** A cyber incident involving credential theft and malware beaconing from RCECD-POS09 and CAD-MDS01 compromises the CAD network, causing CAD to freeze, misreport unit status, and disrupt ALI and location data. This leads to call delays, false unit availability, and a surge of spoofed and silent calls through the 10-digit lines and 911 SIP trunk group A, which the GateKeeper appliance attempts to filter using the TDoS-strict rule set. The incident triggers a loss of confidence in 911, misinformation on social media, and a false report of a hostage situation, all while the state ESInet NOC and Tamarack County Communications must be engaged for rerouting.

**The Operational Impact:** Call takers on Positions 4 and 9 experience a frozen CAD interface, with Medic 3 shown as available while on a call. The ACD wallboard displays 9 calls in queue for 911, and a structure-fire call from Marlow waits 70 seconds. The 10-digit lines are flooded with silent and recorded-message calls, causing conference calls across positions. A calm VoIP caller reports a hostage situation at County Commissioner Ellen Strand’s home, but the call is not verified. A false social media post claims '911 is down, call 555-0177' (a non-RCECD number), prompting public confusion. The Technical Lead must manually initiate firewalling at CAD-FW01 (CYB-DR-1), and the supervisor activates the GateKeeper TDoS-strict rule set. The EOC is notified, and the PIO must activate the alternate 10-digit number via WEA and social media. The floor transitions to manual operations using cards (RCECD-F-11), and evidence preservation begins on REC-01.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to coordinate the activation of the GateKeeper TDoS-strict rule set and the rerouting of 911 calls to Tamarack County Communications via OPS-DR-2, ensuring continuity of emergency call handling under cyber-induced service degradation.
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to declare a Level 1 — Critical incident and authorize evacuation to the backup PSAP using OPS-DR-5, based on CAD freeze, ALI corruption, and sustained silent call flood.
- **Technical Analysis:** Demonstrate the ability to manually initiate firewalling at CAD-FW01 (CYB-DR-1) and validate system isolation from the compromised network segment, preventing further lateral movement.
- **Use of Doctrine:** Demonstrate the ability to activate manual dispatch using run cards (RCECD-F-11) and maintain call-handling continuity under degraded CAD and ALI conditions, per RCECD-OPS-2026.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **911 Director (Incident Commander) (Dir):** Manages center-level command, evacuation to the backup PSAP, overtime, and policy-group requests.
- **Floor Supervisor (Communications Supervisor) (Sup):** Owns moment-to-moment call handling, the switch to manual dispatch with run cards, and talkgroup patches.
- **911 Systems / Technical Services Manager (Technical Lead) (Tech):** Owns the call-handling equipment, CAD, ALI, mapping and the recorder, and escalation to the 911 service provider and vendors.
- **Radio System Manager / Communications Unit Leader (COML) (Coml):** Owns the trunked radio system, consoles, interoperability channels and the ICS 205.
- **Police / Sheriff Liaison (Patrol Commander) (Le):** Owns the field response, the swatting response posture and BOLO dissemination.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO) (Pio):** Owns the call-this-10-digit-number message, social media and the press.

Goal: Test the organization's readiness to recognize and respond to ambiguous system anomalies that may signal a cyber incident, focusing on hesitation to declare an incident and the initial triage of conflicting signals.
