# Player Briefing Packet
## Scenario 3: "Flight to Nowhere"

Location: North Coast International Airport (NCIA) · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 09:00 AM (Exercise Time) · Weather: Clear, 65°F. Winds calm.

### 1. Situation Brief

It is a busy Thursday morning at North Coast International Airport. Passenger volume is approximately 15% higher than average due to the start of a holiday weekend.

- Terminal Status: Normal Operations.
- Airfield Status: Green / Open.
- IT Status: Green / Normal.
- Staffing: Full shifts are present in Operations, Maintenance, and IT.

**The Anomaly:** At 09:00 AM, the Airport Operations Center (AOC) received a non-emergency call from a Skyline Air Gate Agent at B4. She reported that the screen above her gate was displaying the wrong destination. She believes it is a simple display glitch.

**YOUR MISSION:** Maintain the safety and security of the traveling public while investigating and resolving operational anomalies. You must balance the need for efficiency (keeping planes moving) with security (protecting infrastructure).

### 2. Team Assignments & Standing Orders

| Team | Role | Standing Orders |
|---|---|---|
| **Team A: Airport Operations (Command)** | You own the physical reality. You are responsible for the safety of people and aircraft. | **Safety First:** If data conflicts with reality, trust reality. Do not allow passengers to board incorrect aircraft. **Continuity:** Keep the airport open unless safety is compromised. **Coordination:** You are the "client." You must tell IT what you need (e.g., "I need the screens off") to manage the crowd. |
| **Team B: IT & Cybersecurity (Technical)** | You own the digital reality. You are responsible for the integrity of airport systems (FIDS, BHS, AODB). | **Verification:** Do not accept "it's a glitch" as an answer. Verify logs. **Containment:** If a system is compromised, your priority is to stop the spread, even if it means breaking operations (with approval). **Forensics:** Preserve evidence. Do not reboot servers unless absolutely necessary for life safety. |
| **Team C: Public Information (PIO)** | You own the narrative. You are responsible for protecting the airport's reputation and calming the public. | **Monitor:** Watch social media (Twitter/X) closely for passenger sentiment. **Truth:** Do not confirm "Cyber Attack" until IT confirms it. Use holding statements ("We are investigating a technical issue"). **Clarity:** If screens fail, you must provide clear, alternative instructions to passengers via PA and social media. |
| **Team D: Security & Law Enforcement** | You own the enforcement. You are responsible for physical order. | **Crowd Control:** If passengers become hostile or confused, deploy resources to de-escalate. **Perimeter:** Ensure the confusion inside the terminal does not lead to a breach of the sterile area or ramp. |

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- **"This is an Exercise":** Start and end all radio/phone communications with this phrase to prevent real-world panic.
- **Real-World Emergencies:** If a real emergency occurs (e.g., a student medical issue), use the phrase "REAL WORLD – REAL WORLD" to stop the exercise immediately.
- **Simulated Time:** The exercise may jump forward in time. The facilitator will announce "Time Jump: It is now [Time]."
- **Artifacts:** You will be handed physical "injects" (tweets, logs, photos). Treat them as real intelligence.
