# ICS 201 — Incident Briefing (FEMA)

Source: https://training.fema.gov/icsresource/icsforms.aspx (public domain). The original fillable PDF ("ICS Form 201 – Incident Briefing v3.pdf") should be placed in `reference/originals/`. The app's `ics_201_prefill` template must reproduce this four-page layout.

## Page 1

1. Incident Name · 2. Incident Number · 3. Date/Time Initiated (Date, Time)
4. **Map/Sketch** — total area of operations, incident site/area, impacted and threatened areas, other graphics depicting situational status and resource assignment.
5. **Situation Summary and Health and Safety Briefing** — for briefings or transfer of command; recognize hazards and protective measures.
6. Prepared by: Name · Position/Title · Signature · Date/Time

## Page 2

7. **Current and Planned Objectives**
8. **Current and Planned Actions, Strategies, and Tactics** — table: Time | Actions

## Page 3

9. **Current Organization** — org chart: Incident Commander(s); Safety Officer; Public Information Officer; Liaison Officer; Operations Section Chief; Planning Section Chief; Logistics Section Chief; Finance/Admin Section Chief. (Split the IC box for Unified Command and indicate agency.)

## Page 4

10. **Resource Summary** — table: Resource | Resource Identifier | Date/Time Ordered | ETA | Arrived | Notes (location/assignment/status)

## Purpose and use

Provides the IC and Command/General Staff with basic information on the incident situation and resources; serves as an initial action worksheet and permanent record of the initial response; can be part of the initial Incident Action Plan (IAP). Prepared by the IC for the incoming IC with an oral briefing. Pages 1–2 go to the Situation Unit; pages 3–4 to the Resources Unit.

### Block instructions (abridged)

- 3: month/day/year and 24-hour clock.
- 4: use ICS map symbology; north at top; geospatial references go on ICS 209.
- 7: objectives and specific problem areas.
- 8: actions and when they occur; continue on another ICS 201 page 2 if needed.
- 9: names in each position; add Command Staff Assistants, Agency Representatives, section organization as needed.
- 10: number and kind/type of resource; agency/resource designator; ordered date/time; ETA; arrived check; notes.
