# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Digital Lockdown** (Cyber Incident & School Continuity)
Location: Halverson Unified School District, Halverson, Brandt County, Oregon · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: District Command / School Safety, Policy Group, Technology / Information Security, Communications, School Site / Halverson High School, Law Enforcement (School Resource Officer)

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** System Integrity, Trust in Data, and Crisis Leadership

**The Threat:** A ransomware actor infiltrated Halverson Unified School District through an unpatched VPN appliance (HUSD-VPN01) and a shared local-admin password, compromising the district's core IT systems. The attacker exfiltrated sensitive student data—including IEPs, health logs, threat assessments, and custody files—from the HUSD-FS02 file server before encrypting the on-premises systems. The attack occurred during the spring state assessment window, threatening test continuity and public trust.

**The Operational Impact:** At 06:40, IT staff detect anomalous Defender alerts on HUSD-FS02 and HUSD-VH02. By 07:30, file shares, Tyler Munis, and the hosted VoIP system are encrypted, and a ransom note appears on staff laptops. PowerSchool, Google Workspace, and Chromebooks remain functional via local internet breakouts, but the district’s internal communication and data systems are crippled. At midday, the actor emails parents and staff from a spoofed domain, posting sample IEPs to a leak site and threatening to release more. Parents post screenshots in the Halverson Parents Facebook group, and Channel 6 calls the PIO. At 13:15, a 911 call reports a shooter in C Wing at Halverson High School. The school goes to LOCKDOWN via CENTEGIX badges; district phones to the District Service Center are down. Students in the library and Commons during grade 11 testing must be evacuated via bus to the Reunification Site (Fairgrounds Exhibit Hall) using 05:00 offline rosters. The Assessment Coordinator must pause testing. The Policy Group must make rapid decisions on closure, ransom negotiation, legal reporting, and family notification.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **System Integrity:** Demonstrate the ability to assess and contain a multi-system cyber incident involving data exfiltration, encryption, and spoofed communications, prioritizing the protection of student and staff data as defined in FERPA and Oregon breach-notice laws.
- **Trust in Data:** Demonstrate the ability to validate and rely on offline, pre-incident student rosters and accountability records during a system-wide outage, ensuring accurate reunification and safe student movement during an evacuation.
- **Crisis Leadership:** Demonstrate the ability to make rapid, coordinated decisions on school closure, ransom negotiation, legal reporting, and family notification under pressure, balancing legal, ethical, and operational imperatives.
- **Inter-Team Coordination:** Demonstrate the ability to synchronize actions across the Policy Group, EOC, Technology, Communications, and Student Services teams during a cascading incident involving cyberattack, false alarm, and physical safety response.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Director of School Safety / District Emergency Manager (Incident Commander) (DSS):** Manages the activation and operations of the District EOC, sets incident objectives, and calls standard response actions (lockdown, evacuate, shelter) across the district.
- **Superintendent (Policy Group chair) (Supt):** Makes final decisions on school closures, early release, and district-wide statements; chairs the Policy Group and briefs the Board of Education.
- **Building Principal (Site Incident Commander) (Prin):** Owns the first actions at Halverson High School: lockdown, evacuation, student accountability, and staff assignments until responders take over.
- **School Resource Officer / School Police Chief (Law Enforcement Liaison) (SRO):** Owns the initial law-enforcement response, threat investigation, and liaison to city police or the sheriff.
- **Chief Technology Officer (Technical Lead) (CTO):** Owns the SIS, LMS, phones, Wi-Fi and 1:1 devices, containment, vendor coordination and the MS-ISAC / K12 SIX / CISA liaison.
- **Chief Communications Officer (Public Information Officer) (PIO):** Owns parent notification through the mass-notification system, social media, media staging and rumor control.

## Phase 1: The Glitch (Hour 0:00 – 1:00)

Goal: Test the team's readiness to recognize and respond to early signs of a cyber incident, particularly the reluctance to escalate when anomalies appear in routine operations and data integrity is subtly compromised.
