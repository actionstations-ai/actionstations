# LC-REF-04: Site, Utilities and Adjacent Properties

Larkspur Constructors, Inc. · Stillwater Creek Interceptor, Phase 2 · Reference sheet for supervisors

## 1. Geography

The pipe corridor follows the former Stillwater Creek culvert for 3,800 feet through the Eastside neighborhood of Dayton, Ohio. Stations are measured from the west end at Corliss Avenue (0+00) to the connection chamber at the rail spur (38+00). The work is divided into 400-foot open-cut segments; each segment is excavated, piped, bedded, backfilled and the street restored within 3 weeks. This week's segment is stations 14+00 to 18+00, a trench 13 feet deep and 8 feet wide at the bottom, running along the south side of Ninth Street.

## 2. The trench

The trench through stations 12+00 to 20+00 passes through saturated sandy fill. The trench box is 10 feet by 20 feet, rated for 15 feet in Type C soil, and is moved forward as the pipe is laid. A 12-inch pump (Cascade Dewatering) runs from a sump at station 15+20, discharging into a settling tank. Two ladders are at every box. Spoil is stored on the north side, 4 feet from the trench edge, and trucks load at a ramp at 16+50.

## 3. Equipment on site

- Excavators: a 40-ton (Unit E-7, operator Gordon Pruitt) and a 22-ton (Unit E-4).
- A 60-ton crawler crane (Unit C-2), lift director Walt Dunmore, used for setting 20-foot pipe sections and the trench box.
- Vacuum excavator (hired from a subcontractor) for utility pot-holing.
- Two dump trucks, a compaction roller, a light tower fleet, a diesel generator at the trailers.
- Rescue and safety equipment: two hydraulic aluminum shores, a trench rescue kit (backboard, cribbing, ladders, a trench jack), a four-gas meter, an AED in the trailer and a second AED on the crane truck.

## 6. Hospitals and responders

- Dayton Fire Department Station 12 is 0.8 mile from the west gate; the department's technical rescue company (Rescue 2) brings trench rescue equipment and responds with a battalion chief.
- The regional trauma center is 4.5 miles away; the project's pre-plan lists it as the destination for any crush injury. A medical helicopter can land at the baseball field across Corliss Avenue.
- The Dayton Division of Building Inspection has an inspector on call.

## 7. The monitoring system

The monitoring dashboard in trailer 2 shows the crack gauges and the settlement prisms with time stamps. The dashboard sends a text to the superintendent, the Safety Director and the Engineer of Record when a reading reaches the alarm level. The monitoring contractor is Landmark Instrumentation Services, whose technician Kyle Sandoval can be reached at 937-555-0190 for sensor questions only; decisions belong to the Engineer of Record. The readings on the morning of the last weekly review: CG-1 0.03 inch, CG-2 0.05 inch, CG-3 0.06 inch; prisms all within 0.10 inch.
