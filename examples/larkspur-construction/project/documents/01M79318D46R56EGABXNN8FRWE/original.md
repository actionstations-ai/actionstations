# LC-EAP-03: Project Organization, Notifications and Reporting Clocks

Larkspur Constructors, Inc. · Stillwater Creek Interceptor, Phase 2 · Revision 3

## 1. Company and project

Larkspur Constructors, Inc. is a heavy civil and utility contractor of about 240 employees in Dayton, Ohio, with annual revenue near $180 million. The Stillwater Creek Interceptor Phase 2 is a $24.6 million contract awarded by the Stillwater Regional Sewer District. Notice to proceed was February 9, 2027, with substantial completion due in November 2027. Crews work Monday to Friday, 6:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., with about 42 Larkspur workers and 30 subcontractor workers on site on a normal day. The subcontractors include Pike & Marlow Paving, Hollis Electric and Cascade Dewatering.

The project office is a four-unit trailer compound at the east staging yard, with a monitoring station (a laptop and a wall display) in trailer 2, a sign-in board at the gate, and a first-aid room with an AED. Mobile phone coverage is good across the site. The two-way radios use channel 1 (general) and channel 2 (emergency).

## 3. OSHA and building official clocks

- **OSHA:** The Safety Director reports a work-related fatality within 8 hours of learning of it. An in-patient hospitalization, an amputation or the loss of an eye is reported within 24 hours of learning of it. The report goes to the OSHA area office by phone, to 1-800-321-OSHA, or through the online form. The report names the employer, the location, the time, the number of employees affected and a contact. OSHA's clock starts when any Larkspur supervisor or manager learns of the event, not when the Safety Director does.
- **Building official:** The Safety Director notifies the City of Dayton Division of Building Inspection (building official Fiona Castellanos, 937-555-0170) whenever any adjacent building is evacuated or damaged, or if a monitoring action level is reached. Only the building official declares a building unsafe or posts it.
- **Subcontractor employees:** A subcontractor reports to OSHA for its own employees, but Larkspur, as the controlling employer, coordinates and makes the call for the site if a sub's employee is killed or hospitalized, and records that the sub was notified.
- **Owner contract:** The contract requires written notice to the owner within 24 hours of any incident that injures a person or damages property of a third party, and a written report within 5 working days.

## 4. Public and family communication

No Larkspur employee speaks to media. The Project Executive or her designee is the only spokesperson, and speaks only after the owner's representative has been told. The family of an injured or dead worker is contacted in person, by the Safety Director and the worker's foreman, with the Project Executive, before any name is released. Names are not released until the family has been told and the employer of record agrees. Statements say what is known, what is not known and what is being done; they do not speculate on cause.

Neighbors within 300 feet receive a door-hanger or a visit from the community relations lead, Imani Duclos, with a hotline: 937-555-0180. The District's public information officer, Teresa Whitcomb, approves anything that names the District.

## 5. Records Larkspur starts at once

The Safety Director starts the incident log: time of the first call, time each person was notified, who ordered what and when, the headcount, the weather, the one-call ticket numbers, the monitoring readings before and after, and the names of witnesses. Cell phone photographs are taken by the Safety Director only after the responders release the scene. Everything is retained for OSHA and the insurer.
