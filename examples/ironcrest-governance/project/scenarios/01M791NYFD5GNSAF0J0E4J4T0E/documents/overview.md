# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Six Days Out** (MES Ransomware, Materiality & Disclosure)
Location: Ironcrest Machinery Corporation headquarters, Rockford, Illinois · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: CEO, CFO, General Counsel, CISO, Business Continuity Manager and Audit Committee Chair. The SimCell plays the COO, the Chief Communications Officer, Investor Relations, the CIO, the insurer and broker, outside counsel, the forensic negotiator, the FBI Chicago field office, customers, the external auditor, the agent bank, the plants, reporters, analysts and the attackers.

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Recovery measured against the RTOs, not against hope; a timely, documented materiality determination; a ransom decision made under policy, sanctions and insurer rules; and one set of facts for the board, the filing, customers, employees and the press.

**The Threat:** On Thursday, March 25, 2027 (Central Time), six days before quarter end, the Dubuque plant reported a hung manufacturing execution system at 13:40. At 14:05 the SOC found ransomware encrypting the file servers and the MES at the Rockford data center. CISO Anil Deshmukh isolated the network at 14:25, and the CEO, Thomas Aldridge, declared Level 3 at 14:50, all before STARTEX. The attackers demand 18 million dollars in cryptocurrency within 72 hours and threaten to publish 1.2 TB of stolen data. Their group name partly matches a sanctioned affiliate. Lot tracking is down at all three plants, the Joliet warehouse system is offline and the ERP integration is locked. The forensic firm Blackwater Cyber reports that the last clean MES backup is 31 hours old and that the Dubuque replica is partly encrypted.

**The Operational Impact:** The CMT convenes at 15:00 on the bridge at (815) 555-0121.

**Operations:** Plants 1 to 3 in Rockford, Beloit and Dubuque run controllers in manual mode. They have no work orders and no lot tracking. Shipping and parts distribution at Joliet stop at 15:30, and the dealer portal cannot show order status.

**Recovery:** Business Continuity Manager Ravi Menon's tracker shows the MES heading past its 8-hour RTO. Recovery must start from a backup 31 hours old against a 15-minute RPO, and nothing can be restored until the forensic firm clears the backups. Each day of lost production is worth about 8.5 million dollars of sales, and CFO Priya Raghavan must give an impact estimate against the 60-million-dollar planning threshold.

**Board and disclosure:** General Counsel Marguerite Daley must tell the Audit Committee Chair, Dr. Samuel Obuya, within hours. The Disclosure Committee meets by 19:00 and must record a materiality determination (material, not material or not yet determined), the Form 8-K choice (Item 1.05, Item 8.01 or no filing yet) and the trading-window closure. The board meets by video at 07:00 on Friday.

**Ransom:** The ransom decision requires sanctions screening, the consent of the insurer Blue Heron Mutual, notice to the FBI and consultation with the Audit Committee Chair. Company policy is not to pay.

**Who is calling:**
- Fieldmark Equipment and Granite Peak Machinery want supply status, which triggers the 72-hour supply notice.
- Northbridge Payroll needs the payroll file by 10:00 Friday.
- The agent bank, the external auditor and analysts are calling.
- A Rock River Gazette reporter calls at 18:30, and the story breaks at 20:40.
- At 22:10 the attackers post a sample that includes engineering drawings of a drive component made for a defense contractor and 3,100 employee records. This raises Department of Defense and state attorney general notices and puts employees on edge.

**Timing:** The story runs from 15:00 Thursday to Friday morning; the time-jump cards compress it into the four hours of exercise time.

**Core Plan Sections Tested:**
- IBIARO › 3. Critical functions
- IBIARO › 4. Recovery order
- IBODAI › 3. Delegation of authority (extract)
- IBODAI › 2. How the board learns of an incident
- IDCCI › 1. Purpose
- IBODAI › 5. Insurance

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to reach and record a timely materiality determination at the 19:00 Disclosure Committee (material, not material or not yet determined), with the Form 8-K choice between Item 1.05, Item 8.01 or no filing yet and the trading-window closure, each stating who decided, when, what was known and the basis.
- **Use of Doctrine:** Demonstrate the ability to handle the 18-million-dollar ransom demand under the IBODAI 3 delegation of authority, completing sanctions screening of the partly matching affiliate, Blue Heron Mutual consent, FBI notice, Audit Committee Chair consultation and the board approval needed above 5 million dollars, while company policy is not to pay.
- **Recovery Management:** Demonstrate the ability to judge MES, Joliet warehouse and ERP recovery against the IBIARO 3 RTOs and the 4, 8, 12, 24, 48 and 72-hour checkpoints, using the 31-hour-old backup and forensic clearance, and to set a recovery order that protects payroll funding and largest-customer supply before the 5-day finished-goods buffer runs out.
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to keep a single fact sheet consistent across the 07:00 Friday board briefing, the 8-K draft, the Fieldmark and Granite Peak supply notices, the employee message after the 3,100-record leak and the Rock River Gazette response.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Chief Executive Officer (Chair, Crisis Management Team) (CEO):** Owns the Level 3 crisis: leads the CMT, approves or refuses the ransom, approves public statements, regulator contact and the 8-K with the General Counsel, and keeps the Chair of the Board informed.
- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO):** Owns the financial impact estimate against the 60-million-dollar planning threshold, along with liquidity, agent bank contact, the insurance claim with Blue Heron Mutual and certification of related filings.
- **General Counsel and Corporate Secretary (Disclosure Committee Chair) (GC):** Manages privilege, outside counsel and Blackwater Cyber, the Disclosure Committee and the 8-K draft, the legal hold, and contact with the FBI, regulators, the Department of Defense and state attorneys general.
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):** Owns the technical facts on scope and impact, containment of the ransomware, coordination with Blackwater Cyber and restoration of systems once backups are cleared.
- **Business Continuity Manager (BCM):** Owns the BIA and the recovery tracker, records recovery time against each RTO at the 4, 8, 12, 24, 48 and 72-hour checkpoints, and recommends the recovery order.
- **Audit Committee Chair (AUDIT):** Manages board oversight of the incident disclosure process, the auditor relationship and the consultation on the ransom decision, and reviews whether the disclosure is complete and timely.
