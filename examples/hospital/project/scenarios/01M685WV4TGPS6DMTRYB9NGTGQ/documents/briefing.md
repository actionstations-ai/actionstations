# Player Briefing Packet
## Ghost Pumps — Infusion Pump Integrity and Clinical Trust

Location: Ottawa River Regional Medical Center, Toledo, Lucas County, Ohio · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 14:20 PM (Exercise Time) · Weather: Overcast with light rain

### 1. Situation Brief

It is a busy Thursday afternoon at Ottawa River Regional Medical Center, with a total inpatient census of 326 and 19 patients in the Emergency Department. The campus is operating at full capacity, with ICU 3 North and ICU 3 South both at full occupancy (16 patients each). The hospital is managing routine clinical workflows across Tower A, Tower B, and Pavilion C, including active surgical cases in ORs 1 through 12 and ongoing care in the Outpatient Dialysis Center. Clinical Engineering and IT teams are monitoring standard operations, and no incidents have been reported to the Administrator on Call (AOC) as of 14:20.

- Campus Status: Normal
- Building Automation (HVAC, Medical Gas, Generators): Normal
- PACS and RIS Access: Normal
- Infant Security (6 West): Normal
- CCTV and Access Control: Normal
- Elevators: Normal
- Medical Devices on Network (Pumps, Monitors, Imaging): Normal
- Pneumatic Tube System: Normal
- Fire Alarm Panels: Normal
- Water Treatment for Dialysis: Normal
- Duress Buttons: Normal
- IT Infrastructure (EHR, LIS, Pharmacy, Revenue): Normal
- Security and Surveillance: Normal
- Staffing Levels (Clinical, Support): Normal

**The Anomaly:** At 14:17, the charge nurse in ICU 3 North, Mara Voss, reports via internal phone to the Chief Nursing Officer and Clinical Engineering that four patients on the central telemetry display (beds 3, 7, 9, and 12) have gone blank. At the bedside, monitors show normal tracings, and no alarms have been triggered. Additionally, a pump on bed 7 displays 'library update pending'—a message not previously seen. No further system disruptions or alarms have been reported.

**YOUR MISSION:** Maintain uninterrupted patient care and clinical trust while investigating and resolving the integrity failure in the infusion pump and telemetry systems, balancing the need for operational continuity against the risk of relying on potentially compromised medical device data.

### 2. Team Assignments & Standing Orders

| Team | Role | Standing Orders |

You assess the root cause of system anomalies and determine whether any systems can be safely restored. You enforce two-person checks for high-alert medications and blood, manage unit census and acuity reporting, and ensure staffing meets patient needs under degraded conditions. | **Patient safety first: two-person checks replace barcode scanning for high-alert drugs and blood.** **Units report census, acuity, and open beds at the top of every hour at Level 1.** **Never override a bedside device without CNO approval.** |

You ensure that no device is traded for central visibility without CNO approval. You assess functional integrity and manual operation readiness. | **Never trade a device that is working at the bedside for central visibility without the CNO agreeing.** **Verify manual fallbacks for all critical devices in ICU 3 North and 3 South.** **Report any deviation from approved device configurations immediately to the CNO and ISO.** |
| **Team E: Communications** | You own all public and internal messaging. You release information only from facts cleared by the Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer. You maintain a media log and ensure no patient-specific details are released without approval. | **Never say patient data was not accessed unless the Chief Privacy Officer has cleared the words.** **Keep a media log; patient-specific information is released only as the Chief Privacy Officer allows.** **All external communications must be pre-approved by the HCC and the CPO.** |

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- All radio and phone communications must begin and end with 'This is an exercise' to prevent real-world panic.
- If a real emergency occurs, say 'REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD' immediately and the exercise will stop.
- The facilitator may announce 'Time Jump: it is now [time]' to skip ahead to a later point in the scenario.
- Printed injects (posts, logs, photos, reports) are intelligence and must be treated as real.
