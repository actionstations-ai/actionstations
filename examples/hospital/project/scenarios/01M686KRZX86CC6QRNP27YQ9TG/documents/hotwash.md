# Hot Wash Guide
## Water Line, No Drink

### Hot Wash Guide (Debrief)

**The "3-Up, 3-Down" Method:** Use the '3-Up, 3-Down' method by asking each team to share three things that went well and three that broke or failed during the exercise. Go around the room by team, capturing responses on a whiteboard to ensure all voices are heard. Keep the discussion focused on operational performance, communication, and decision-making under pressure.

**Key Discussion Questions for Water Line, No Drink:**
- **Trust:** When did you first stop trusting the water test results from the dialysis loop, and what changed your mind about whether to trust the city’s field samples or the hospital’s internal lab data? _(Objective: Dialysis Continuity)_
- **The Gap:** At the moment you decided to close the city fill valve, what information was missing that made it hard to decide whether to protect stored water or risk contamination for conservation? How did that gap affect your confidence in the decision? _(Objective: Stored Water and Conservation)_
- **The Call:** When you received the Facebook post about a patient stuck in a wheelchair with no ride, who did you call, and what would have allowed you to respond faster and prevent misinformation from spreading? _(Objective: One Voice)_
- **The Evidence:** When the field report said the city water smelled stronger and the RO outlet conductivity was normal, did you treat that as evidence or as a ticket to keep operating? At what point did you decide to stop relying on sensory or partial data and act on public health guidance? _(Objective: Infection Prevention and Sterile Processing)_
- **The Partner:** When you learned the neighboring hospital had open beds but no transport plan, what would have allowed you to decide faster to activate a regional transport protocol or involve the regional healthcare coalition? _(Objective: One Voice)_
