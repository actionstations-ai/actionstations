# Player Briefing Packet
## Lost Link — A flyaway after a field firmware update

Location: Horton Field and the Millbrook Road hangar complex, Harwick, Calder County, New York · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 09:00 (Tuesday, September 15) (Exercise Time) · Weather: Clear, 64°F, wind light from the west

### 1. Situation Brief

Crew 1 launched UAS-02, a heavy-lift hexacopter carrying the PL-05 lidar, from Horton Field at 08:57 on GCS-01 for a site survey along the field’s south edge. The flight is authorized through LAANC to 200 ft AGL under the HWK Class C outer shelf. DAA-1 is on. The rest of the fleet is in Hangar 1. Client flights this week: CP&L substation inspections Wednesday, a CVAC collection Thursday, a CP&L line patrol Friday.

- UAS-02 / GCS-01: Airborne since 08:57
- DAA-1: Receiving
- Fleet in Hangar 1: UAS-01, UAS-03 to UAS-12
- Firmware baseline: 03.2026-B (OEM aircraft)
- Client flights: CP&L Wed and Fri; CVAC Thu

**The Anomaly:** 09:03 — GCS-01 loses the C2 link to UAS-02, and the lost-link timer runs out without a return-to-launch.

**YOUR MISSION:** Make the right calls in the first minutes, recover the aircraft lawfully, find out why it flew away, decide what is safe to fly, and tell the people affected the truth.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- The facilitator will announce time jumps; treat the new time as real.
- Treat printed injects as real information; ask the SimCell for anything you would normally be able to look up.
