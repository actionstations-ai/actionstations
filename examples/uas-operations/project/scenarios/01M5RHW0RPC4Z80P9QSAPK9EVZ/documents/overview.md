# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Ten Days Before D-3** (Ransomware on the NAS and the processing workstation before a client deadline)
Location: Millbrook Road hangar complex (Hangars 1–3), Harwick, Calder County, New York · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Leadership, Systems / Cybersecurity, Flight Operations, Payload & Calibration, Corporate Communications

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Ransomware against a small operator’s mission data: grounding what touched it, scoping what was read, recovering in order from stale backups, and telling clients the truth inside their contract clocks

**The Threat:** On Monday morning, October 5 — ten days before Calder Power & Light Deliverable D-3 is due — the processing team finds HUOG-NAS01 and the processing workstation HUOG-PROC01 encrypted, with a ransom note on the shares. The NAS snapshot schedule was disabled on Saturday night by the backup service account, the off-site backup job has been failing since a credential expired, and the offline drive OB-1 in the General Manager’s safe was last written three weeks ago. GCS-02 mounted the NAS at 08:44 to upload last week’s flight logs and is now infected too. Overnight on Sunday the same account read the controlled share, which holds CP&L task-order data and the PL-02 calibration files.

**The Operational Impact:** Processing for D-3 stops. The Data VLAN has to be isolated at HUOG-FW01 and every ground control station that mounted the NAS since Saturday grounded until the managed SOC (SecureWatch MSSP) clears it, which also threatens the CVAC growing-season collections due within seven days. The General Manager must decide within four hours whether to tell CP&L and CVAC that deadlines are at risk, and whether CP&L’s 24-hour exposure notice applies because its data may have been read and not only encrypted. Recovery follows HUOG-ISP Section 9.3: OB-1 is not connected until the MSSP confirms a clean environment, the calibration share comes back first and is verified against the repository hashes, and the last three September collection days for D-3 may survive only on the aircraft payload media. The Group does not pay, and records that decision.

- ISP › Section 6 › 6.2 Backups

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Data Integrity Verification:** Keep Leadership, the Systems & Cybersecurity Lead, Flight Operations, Payload & Calibration and Corporate Communications on one set of facts, and route every external statement through Corporate Communications (UC-CP-2025).
- **Client Notification Authority:** Within four hours of discovery, decide whether to notify CP&L and CVAC that deadlines are at risk and whether CP&L’s 24-hour exposure notice applies, on the evidence available (HUOG-ISP 9.3 step 4), and record the decision not to pay.
- **Operational Continuity vs. System Containment:** Contain the ransomware (isolate the Data VLAN at HUOG-FW01, ground every GCS that mounted the NAS during the infection window), scope which shares were read, and preserve evidence for the MSSP.
- **Incident Command Authority:** Recover in the HUOG-ISP 9.3 order from OB-1 and the off-site service once the MSSP confirms a clean environment, verifying restored data against manifests before any processing.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Director of Flight Operations (Flight Operations) (FOM):** Owns the flight schedule, crew rosters and the fleet register; decides which flights go and on which GCS.
- **Systems & Cybersecurity Lead (Systems / Cybersecurity) (SCL):** Owns the GCS baseline, the networks, HUOG-FW01, the NAS and backups; incident lead for cyber events and liaison to the managed SOC.
- **General Manager (Leadership) (GM):** Accountable executive; decides on client and regulator notification, records the decision not to pay, and is the spokesperson with Corporate Communications.
- **Corporate Communications (Communications) (COMMS):** Owns all external messaging under crisis protocol UC-CP-2025, including the media and social media; the Group never speaks to the media directly.
- **Payload & Calibration Lead (Payload & Calibration) (PCL):** Owns payload integration, calibration data integrity, ground-truth records, processing for client deliverables and PL-02 export-control handling.
