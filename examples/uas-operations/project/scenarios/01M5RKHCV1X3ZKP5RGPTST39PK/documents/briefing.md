# Player Briefing Packet
## Over the Fairgrounds — A drone over the Harwick Fairgrounds crowd on a NO FLY day

Location: Millbrook Road hangar complex and Horton Field, beside the Harwick Fairgrounds, Harwick, Calder County, New York · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 11:00 (Saturday, September 19) (Exercise Time) · Weather: Clear, 68°F, light wind

### 1. Situation Brief

It is Saturday of the Harwick Fall Festival. About 6,000 people are on the fairgrounds next to Horton Field, so today is a NO FLY day for the Group (HUOG-FOM 7.6): at 0800 the flight operations desk logged all twelve aircraft in Hangar 1. The desk in Hangar 2 is staffed; the Director of Flight Operations, the ASO and the General Manager are on call; the Fleet & Logistics Manager is on site. Harwick Police runs the event command post with its contracted drone detection vendor. Parking crews have the fair-week temporary code for the Group’s perimeter-road gate.

- Fleet (UAS-01 to UAS-12): All in Hangar 1 since the 0800 count
- Horton Field: NO FLY — public event
- Hangar 1 alarm: Set
- Perimeter road gate: Fair-week temporary code active
- Staffing: Desk staffed; managers on call

**The Anomaly:** 11:05 — Harwick Police’s detection console shows a drone broadcasting Remote ID circling the crowd, with its operator position beside the Group’s fence. The sergeant asks whether any Group aircraft is airborne.

**YOUR MISSION:** Prove fast and with evidence where every Group aircraft is, help Harwick Police without touching anything that is not yours, and say only what is true.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- Begin and end every radio and phone message with “This is an exercise.”
- If a real emergency occurs, the facilitator says “REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD” and the exercise stops.
- The facilitator will announce time jumps; treat the new time as real.
- Treat printed injects as real information; ask the SimCell for anything you would normally be able to look up.
