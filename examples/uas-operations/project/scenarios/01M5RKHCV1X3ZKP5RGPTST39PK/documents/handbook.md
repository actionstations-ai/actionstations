# Controller / Evaluator (C/E) Handbook
## Over the Fairgrounds — A drone over the Harwick Fairgrounds crowd on a NO FLY day

Exercise Date: [Date] · Location: Millbrook Road hangar complex and Horton Field, beside the Harwick Fairgrounds, Harwick, Calder County, New York · Duration: 4 Hours


<!-- section:overview -->
### 1. Exercise Overview & Objectives

This is a discussion-based tabletop exercise for the Harwick UAS Operations Group. On a NO FLY day, during the Harwick Fall Festival next door, a drone broadcasting Remote ID circles 6,000 people. It looks like one of the Group’s aircraft and its operator position is beside the Group’s fence. The Group has no counter-UAS capability and must not interfere; its job is to prove its own fleet is grounded and help Harwick Police read the data.

Phase 1 tests the five-minute answer to police and the first public line. Phase 2 puts the operator on the Group’s own perimeter road. Phase 3 carries the decision point: the operator is a part-time Group crew member flying his own drone, and the Group must decide what to say. Phase 4 closes with records, the FSDO, a client and the press. Evaluators should watch for evidence-backed answers, hands off the aircraft and the operator, and public statements that are true and not misleading.

**Objectives:**
- **Communication:** Answer Harwick Police within five minutes from the schedule and the fleet register, and keep Flight Operations, Aviation Safety, Leadership, the police liaison and Corporate Communications on one set of facts.
- **Decision Making:** Decide what the Group says publicly once its own crew member is identified as the operator, and how the Group handles him, without misleading statements (decision point).
- **Use of Doctrine:** Help Harwick Police interpret the Remote ID data and compare it with the fleet register, without launching, tracking with Group aircraft or interfering with the drone (HUOG-ERP 8.4).
- **Technical Analysis:** Follow HUOG-ERP 8.4 and HUOG-FOM 7.6, the Section 6 notifications (Harwick Police, the FSDO, Corporate Communications) and the UC-CP-2025 rule that only Corporate Communications speaks to the media.

**Plan sections exercised:**

### 3. Rules of Play (Instructor Guide)

You are the SimCell: the desk, Harwick Police, the FSDO, the festival chair, CP&L, the press and the operator. Deliver injects with “This just came in.”

If any player proposes launching a Group aircraft to look for, follow or identify the drone, stop and ask which plan section allows it (HUOG-ERP 8.4: none does).

Harwick Police leads. The FSDO advises and records. Neither tells the Group what to say publicly or what to do with its employee.

Time jumps at 1:00, 2:00 and 3:00. At 2:00 ask: “What would you say if the operator turned out to be on your property?”

If a real emergency occurs, say “REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD” and stop the exercise.

**SimCell Scripts (play these when the trigger happens — do not make it easy):**

- **Harwick Police sergeant (event command post)** — _Trigger: The Group calls the command post, or the liaison is asked for anything._
  - Ask: "Is it yours? I need a yes or no with a time on it."
  - Ask: "Who can open the gate on your perimeter road?"
  - Ask: "Can your pilot tell me whether that Remote ID is real or spoofed?"
  - Concede only when: Accepts the 0800 count and the register as proof the fleet is grounded.
  - Concede only when: Keeps the drone and controller as evidence; asks only for copies of Group records.
- **FAA Harwick FSDO duty inspector** — _Trigger: The ASO calls, or no one has by 13:10._
  - Ask: "Was this a Group operation? How do you know?"
  - Ask: "What is your policy on employees flying their own aircraft near your sites?"
  - Concede only when: Accepts a written follow-up with the records; does not tell the Group what to do with the employee.
- **Kyle Brandt (part-time visual observer)** — _Trigger: Any Group manager calls him._
  - Ask: "I was off duty with my own drone. Why is this the company’s business?"
  - Ask: "Am I fired?"
  - Concede only when: Accepts being taken off Monday’s roster pending review if told plainly why.

### 5. Hot Wash Guide (Debrief)

**The "3-Up, 3-Down" Method:** Each team names three things that went well and three that did not; the facilitator writes them up and looks for the same issue in more than one team.

**Key Discussion Questions for Over the Fairgrounds:**
- **Proving a Negative:** How long did it take to tell Harwick Police that every Group aircraft was grounded, and what evidence backed it? _(Objective: Communication)_
- **The Line:** Was “no Group aircraft were flying” still the right public line once the operator was found? What did you decide to say, and why? _(Objective: Decision Making)_
- **Hands Off:** Did anyone want to launch, follow or approach the drone or its operator? What stopped them? _(Objective: Use of Doctrine)_
- **Our Own People:** What should the Group’s rule be on staff flying their own drones near Horton Field and during public events? _(Objective: Use of Doctrine)_
- **Clients:** What did CP&L hear, from whom, and before or after the news? _(Objective: Communication)_
