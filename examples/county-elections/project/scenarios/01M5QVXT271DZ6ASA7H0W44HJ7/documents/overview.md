# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Ghost Poll Pads** (VoterTrack Compromise & Public Trust)
Location: Lisle County Board of Elections, Carrow, Lisle County, Ohio · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Command (Director and Deputy Director), Elections IT and County Security, Voter Registration and Mail Ballots, Voting Systems, Security and Sheriff's Liaison, Public Information

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Credential Theft, Data Tampering, and Rapid Public Communication

**The Threat:** An attacker compromised the account of Voter Registration Clerk Nina Ruiz by impersonating the Ohio Secretary of State in a phishing email. The attacker used her credentials to alter 1,186 voter records in VoterTrack between 02:05 and 02:40 on Wednesday, 10/29/2026. The changes included falsely marking voters as having received mail ballots, enabling unauthorized edits to pollbook data, and enabling a forwarding rule to exfiltrate her inbox. The attacker then used her account to send a spoofed notice to poll workers at Polling Location 6 (Carrow Community Church), falsely claiming the location had moved back to Carrow Middle School. This incident impacts the integrity of the voter registration database, the accuracy of pollbooks, and the operational readiness of election staff across multiple polling locations and the Early Vote Center.

**The Operational Impact:** At the Early Vote Center (18 South Market Street, Carrow), 23 voters are falsely shown as having received mail ballots; poll workers are unable to verify eligibility and must manually process checks, creating a line out the door. At Polling Location 6 (Carrow Community Church), poll workers receive a spoofed email from Nina Ruiz’s account directing them to a non-existent location, causing confusion and delays. The results website (results.lislevotes.example) shows a degraded load due to corrupted data feeds from VoterTrack. The hotline (555-0140) receives 42 calls in 30 minutes from voters reporting they were 'wrongly issued' ballots. The Board website (lislevotes.example) shows a 'Voter Registration Update' banner, but the underlying data is compromised. The Election Operations Center is activated, and staff begin to detect anomalies in the status board. The public information team must respond to a viral video (200,000 views) falsely claiming 10,000 voters were deleted and that scanners are internet-connected.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Credential Integrity:** Demonstrate the ability to detect and isolate a compromised user account in VoterTrack by verifying login patterns, disabling access, and initiating forensic preservation of logs within 15 minutes of first anomaly report.
- **Pollbook Accuracy:** Demonstrate the ability to validate and correct pollbook data for Polling Location 6 using a trusted, air-gapped copy of the VoterTrack export, ensuring no unauthorized changes are applied during the emergency load.
- **Public Trust Management:** Demonstrate the ability to issue a unified public statement via the PIO that corrects a viral misinformation claim about voter deletion, using the EAC/CISA communications guide and citing verified data from the Voter Registration Manager.
- **System Containment:** Demonstrate the ability to isolate the VoterTrack database from the network and block external data exfiltration by disabling the forwarding rule and revoking API access from the compromised account within 10 minutes of confirmation.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Chief Election Official (Incident Commander) (CEO):** Manages activation of the contingency plan, polling location relocation, and public statements in coordination with counsel and the PIO.
- **Deputy Director (Election-Day Operations Center) (Dep):** Runs the election-day operations center, hotline, rovers, staffing and logistics, and maintains the incident log and status board.
- **Elections IT Manager / County CISO (IT):** Owns the network, email, identity, voter registration system access, results website, and isolation decisions.
- **Voting Systems Manager (VSM):** Owns logic and accuracy testing, tabulator chain of custody, seals, and vendor escalation.
- **Facility Security Manager / Sheriff's Election Liaison (Sec):** Owns warehouse access, ballot transport escorts, threats to staff, and protest response.
- **Communications Director (PIO) (PIO):** Owns voter information, rumor control, media messages, and the Board’s public narrative.

## Phase 1: The Glitch (Hour 0:00 – 1:00)

Goal: Test the team's ability to recognize and respond to ambiguous signs of compromise, particularly the reluctance to act on anomalous data from a trusted system, and to initiate basic forensic checks on a user account.

Beats: At 08:12, a front-line poll worker at the Early Vote Center (18 South Market Street, Carrow) calls the hotline (555-0140) reporting that 23 voters in the queue are marked as 'Mail Ballot Received' in the system, but they have not requested one; the worker notes, 'This is not right — these people are here to vote in person.'; At 08:18, the IT team receives a SIEM alert from LCBOE-FS01 showing an unexpected outbound connection from the Microsoft 365 tenant to a foreign IP (104.213.142.77) via a non-standard port, but no associated user alert; the alert is logged as 'Low Priority' by the on-call engineer and not escalated.; At 08:25, the VoterTrack system logs show a series of rapid edits to 1,186 voter records in the 'Absentee Ballot Status' field, all timestamped between 02:05 and 02:40 on 10/29/2026; the edits were made from IP address 10.100.45.12, which is not in the county’s internal network range, and the user account 'nruiz@lislevotes.example' was used, which belongs to VR Clerk Nina Ruiz.; At 08:30, the mail ballot tracking service (linked to VoterTrack) sends a spoofed notice to poll workers at Polling Location 6 (Carrow Community Church), falsely stating that the location has reverted to Carrow Middle School (the old site), with a fake signature from 'Nina Ruiz' and a modified link to a non-existent web portal.; At 08:42, the Board website (lislevotes.example) displays a 'Voter Registration Update' banner with a headline that reads, 'Voters in Precincts 111 and 112 have been re-registered to a new location'; the underlying data is corrupted and not synchronized with the statewide database, but no one has yet questioned its accuracy..

## Phase 2: The Crisis (Hour 1:00 – 2:00)

Goal: Test the team's ability to transition from reactive triage to unified command under pressure, recognize a critical public misinformation event, and force an official incident declaration based on operational disruption and external pressure.
