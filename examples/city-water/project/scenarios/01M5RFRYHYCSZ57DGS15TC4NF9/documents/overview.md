# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Stale Signal** (A Telemetry Outage, a Transmission-Main Break and a Boil-Water Decision)
Location: Riverside Water Treatment Plant, 1400 River Road, Millbrook, Carver County, Ohio · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Water Operations, Distribution, SCADA / IT, Water Quality / Lab, Public Information

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Running blind: a main break during a telemetry outage, the hydraulics of isolation in a heat wave, and a boil-water decision without pressure data

**The Threat:** On the third day of a July heat wave, with demand near 14 MGD, the 24 VDC power supply in the Hilltop Tank radio cabinet fails at 05:30. It feeds both the repeater RP-1 and the cellular modem of RTU-01, so Hilltop Tank, Orchard Standpipe, the Orchard Wellfield, Northgate and Mill Road Booster Station all go stale on the HMI at once. At 06:10 the 1968 cast-iron, 24-inch Hilltop Transmission Main breaks near Summit Drive. Hilltop Tank starts to drain, and nobody at the plant can see it.

**The Operational Impact:** The break first shows at the plant as a jump in FIT-501 and a fall in PIT-501, then in calls from Summit Drive. Isolation needs valves V-2410 and V-2418, and V-2418 was last exercised in 2023. Holding Zone 2 means starting Mill Road Booster Station, which draws on Zone 1, where Millbrook General Hospital has no storage, and probably opening the Carver County Water District interconnect IC-1, whose chloraminated water matters to dialysis patients. Part of Zone 2 falls below 20 psi, which triggers the advisory decision in ERP 7.1, but with the telemetry down nobody knows exactly which streets. Northgate Reservoir, filled from Zone 2, starts to fall too.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Telemetry Trust:** Keep the Control Room, the field crews, the lab and the PIO on one picture of the system while the HMI shows stale data, using radio DWS-1, field readings every two hours and the OT Plan 10.6 checklist.
- **Zone Isolation:** Decide, from incomplete pressure data, the area and timing of a Boil Water Advisory for the depressurized part of Zone 2 (ERP 7.1 and 9), and whether and when to open IC-1.
- **Boil Water Decision:** Recognize the main-break signature (ERP 6.6), isolate the break with the fewest valves, and manage the hydraulic trade-offs between Zone 2, Zone 1 and Zone 3.
- **Inter-Team Coordination:** Follow ERP 7.1, the OT Plan 10.6 stale-data checklist, the ERP 9.5 notification sequence and the DDAGW consultation, with critical customers called before the public.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Director of Water Operations (Incident Commander) (IC):** Commands the response: declares the incident level, approves isolation, booster and interconnect operations, and decides on advisories with the Water Quality Manager.
- **SCADA / IT Security Manager (Technical Lead) (TL):** Owns control-system integrity, remote access, and evidence preservation; investigates telemetry failure and confirms no cyber compromise.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO) (PIO):** Owns public messaging, social media, press, and the MillbrookAlert system; releases advisories and updates.
- **Water Quality Manager (Compliance) (WQ):** Owns sampling, lab results, regulatory reporting, and public-notice triggers; confirms loss of disinfection and lifting criteria.
- **Distribution Superintendent (Distribution) (DIST):** Owns the distribution system: main breaks, valves, interconnects IC-1 and IC-2, boosters in the field, flushing, door hangers and field pressure readings.
