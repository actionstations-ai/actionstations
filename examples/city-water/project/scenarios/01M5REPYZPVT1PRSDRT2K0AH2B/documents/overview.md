# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Cut Lock** (Tampering at Orchard Standpipe and a Precautionary Advisory)
Location: City of Millbrook Department of Water & Sewer, Millbrook, Carver County, Ohio · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Water Operations, Distribution, Water Quality / Lab, Public Information, Security

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Physical intrusion at a storage facility, sampling before conclusions, isolation without losing pressure, and a precautionary advisory decided within two hours

**The Threat:** Overnight on Saturday someone cut through the fence at Orchard Standpipe (870 Orchard Road), cut the ladder-cage and roof-hatch padlocks and opened the roof hatch. The site’s intrusion detection is thin: the hatch switch ZS-611 has been out of service since a lightning strike in 07/2024 (WO-24-1187), so RTU-02 still reports the hatch closed; the gate contact ZS-612 tripped and cleared twice and was acknowledged as a nuisance alarm; CAM-13 covers the gate only. The outlet chlorine residual AIT-611 has drifted down since about 02:00, and an empty plastic jug lies at the foot of the ladder. Nothing has been confirmed in the water.

**The Operational Impact:** Orchard Standpipe and the Orchard Wellfield feed part of Zone 2 (Hilltop). The department must sample under ERP-3 before anything is drained, isolate the standpipe without dropping Zone 2 below 20 psi (possible while Hilltop Tank stays above 15 ft, with the wells stopped), preserve evidence for Millbrook Police and the FBI, consult Ohio EPA DDAGW and Carver County Public Health, and decide within two hours of the sign of entry on a precautionary advisory — boil water or do not drink, for which streets — without waiting for lab results that take 18 hours (coliform) to three days (chemical screen). A Zone 2 advisory reaches about 42,000 people on a Sunday and needs the City Manager’s approval; lifting it needs two rounds of satisfactory samples 24 hours apart.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Communication:** Keep Operations, Distribution, the lab, Security and the PIO on one set of facts from the first look at the overnight alarm log to the advisory release, and reach the Annex B critical customers before the public.
- **Decision Making:** Decide within two hours of the sign of entry whether to issue a precautionary advisory, of which type (Boil Water or Do Not Drink) and for which streets, after consulting DDAGW and Public Health, and record the basis on ERP-2 (ERP Sections 8.2 and 9).
- **Technical Analysis:** Treat the AIT-611 drift and the site evidence as a possible contamination event: sample per ERP-3 before draining anything, isolate the standpipe without dropping Zone 2 below 20 psi, and preserve video and RTU logs for law enforcement.
- **Use of Doctrine:** Follow ERP Section 8.2, the ERP-3 sampling plan, the PCP 10.3 precautionary template and the Section 9.5 notification sequence, and keep the department, not an outside agency, as the decision-maker for operations and advisories.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Director of Water Operations (Incident Commander) (IC):** Manages the water system, including treatment plants, pump stations, tanks, and the distribution network, and is responsible for declaring incident levels and issuing advisories in coordination with the Water Quality Manager.
- **Distribution Superintendent (Distribution) (DIST):** Owns the distribution system, the storage sites and their valves, field crews, flushing and hydrant sampling access, and pressure in each zone.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO) (PIO):** Owns customer notices, boil-water advisories, social media, and the press; responsible for releasing all public statements and managing the Joint Information Center.
- **Water Quality Manager (Compliance) (WQ):** Owns sampling, the lab, regulatory reporting, and public-notice triggers; confirms health-effects content and sampling plan behind every advisory.
- **Security & Emergency Manager (Security) (SEC):** Owns site security, the law-enforcement liaison, and the emergency response plan; responsible for physical order and evidence preservation.
