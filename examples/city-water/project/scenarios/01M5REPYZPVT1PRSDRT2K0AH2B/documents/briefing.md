# Player Briefing Packet
## Cut Lock — Tampering at Orchard Standpipe and a Precautionary Advisory

Location: City of Millbrook Department of Water & Sewer, Millbrook, Carver County, Ohio · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 07:00 (Sunday, October 25) (Exercise Time) · Weather: Clear and cool, 52°F

### 1. Situation Brief

It is a quiet Sunday morning. The day operator has just relieved the night operator in the RWTP Control Room; demand is low at about 6 MGD. Hilltop Tank reads 27 feet and Orchard Standpipe 84 feet, with the Orchard wells cycling normally. Distribution has one crew on call and the SecureLine patrol is on its weekend route. Orchard Standpipe’s roof-hatch switch ZS-611 has been out of service since a lightning strike in July 2024, and its camera CAM-13 covers the gate only. The next routine visit to the standpipe is Tuesday.

- Treatment plant: Normal (automatic)
- Hilltop Tank: 27 ft
- Orchard Standpipe: 84 ft
- Orchard Wellfield: Cycling normally
- Site security at Orchard Standpipe: Degraded: ZS-611 out of service; CAM-13 gate only
- Staffing: One operator; one Distribution crew on call; SecureLine patrol

**The Anomaly:** 07:15 — Reviewing the overnight alarm summary, the day operator finds that the Orchard Standpipe gate contact tripped twice after midnight and was acknowledged as wind, and a camera clip shows a figure with a flashlight near the ladder cage.

**YOUR MISSION:** Find out what happened at Orchard Standpipe, protect the people it serves without losing pressure in Zone 2, decide on a precautionary advisory within the time your plan allows, and preserve what the police will need.

### 2. Team Assignments & Standing Orders

| Team | Role | Standing Orders |
|---|---|---|
| **Team A: Operations (Command)**— Director of Water Operations (Incident Commander) | Command; declares incident levels and decides, with the Water Quality Manager, on advisories. | **Any sign of entry at a storage site is a Level 2 condition.** **Decide on a precautionary advisory within two hours of the sign of entry.** **Record every advisory decision on ERP-2 before release.** |
| **Team B: Distribution**— Distribution Superintendent (Distribution) | Storage sites, valves, crews, flushing and zone pressure. | **Isolate a site only if no zone drops below 20 psi.** **Stop the Orchard wells before isolating Orchard Standpipe.** **Sample before draining or flushing anything.** |
| **Team C: Public Information**— Public Information Officer (PIO) | Advisories, media, social media and MillbrookAlert. | **Critical customers before the public.** **Describe advisory areas by streets.** **Never conclude “no contamination” without results.** |
| **Team D: Water Quality / Lab**— Water Quality Manager (Compliance) | Sampling, the lab, DDAGW and Public Health consultation, advisory triggers and lifting. | **Sample the outlet and the first two hydrants downstream first.** **Know the turnarounds: 15 minutes, 18–24 hours, 48–72 hours.** **Consult DDAGW and Public Health on any precautionary advisory.** |
| **Team E: Security**— Security & Emergency Manager (Security) | Site security, Millbrook Police and FBI liaison, evidence. | **Call Millbrook Police for any sign of entry.** **Preserve camera footage and RTU logs before they overwrite.** **Keep a scene log of every entry.** |
