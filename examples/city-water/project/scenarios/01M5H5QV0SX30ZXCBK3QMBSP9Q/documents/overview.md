# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**pH on the Edge** (Caustic Overfeed & Public Health)
Location: Riverside Water Treatment Plant, Millbrook, Carver County, Ohio · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Water Operations, SCADA / IT, Public Information, Water Quality / Lab, Security

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Trust in the Data, Control System Integrity, and Public Health Decision-Making

**The Threat:** A remote-support session through the unapproved DosaTech ScreenBridge agent on MBW-HMI-02 in the Chemical Building results in a stepwise increase of the NAOH-FC-203 caustic dose setpoint toward the PLC hard limit of 40 mg/L. The HMI displays normal operation, but field instruments (AIT-302, PSH-302) and grab samples indicate finished-water pH is rising toward 9.5, creating a mismatch between screen and reality. This cyber-physical compromise of the chemical feed system triggers a public health emergency.

**The Operational Impact:** The night operator on rounds observes a rising pH trend on the HMI but notes that the local high-high switch PSH-302 has not tripped and that grab samples show pH above 9.5. The chemical feed pump continues to run. The Control Room receives conflicting data: HMI shows 14 mg/L (within normal band), but lab results show 9.3 pH and 38 mg/L caustic. The operator attempts to manually override the feed via the local panel but finds the PLC is still issuing commands. The distribution system in Zone 1 experiences a sudden rise in pH, triggering customer calls to the surge line about bitter-tasting water and chemical odors. The Water Quality Manager confirms the lab results, and the Director must decide whether to issue a Do Not Drink advisory. The SCADA team isolates the control network to stop remote access, but the network is already compromised. The Ohio EPA is notified under Section 12, and the Carver County EOC is activated.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Control System Integrity:** Demonstrate the ability to detect and respond to a mismatch between HMI data and field instruments, prioritizing physical evidence over screen displays when a hard-wired safety switch has not tripped and lab results indicate a hazardous condition.
- **Public Health Decision-Making:** Demonstrate the ability to declare a Level 3 emergency and issue a Do Not Drink advisory within 2 hours of confirming lab results showing finished-water pH above 9.5, based on the Water Quality Manager's verification and the Director's approval.
- **Cyber-Physical Incident Response:** Demonstrate the ability to isolate the control system, engage the FBI/CISA liaison, and prevent further remote access while preserving evidence, despite the network being compromised.
- **Public Information Consistency:** Demonstrate the ability to release a pre-scripted, fact-based advisory through MillbrookAlert, the website, and social media within 45 minutes of the advisory decision, using approved language and avoiding speculation.

- **SCADA / IT Security Manager (Technical Lead) (TL):** Owns the control system, remote access, network integrity, and evidence preservation.
