# Brennan County — Facilities, Populations and Warning Systems

Supporting description for Annex H and BCEMA-SOP-02 · Brennan County Emergency Management Agency

## 1. Places and people at risk

| Site | Location | People | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ridgeline Distribution warehouse | Corwin Industrial Park, KY 80 | Up to 310 on the overnight shift (22:00–06:30) | Tilt-up concrete walls, long-span roof; designated refuge areas in the restroom cores; plan reviewed 2025 |

## 2. Community safe rooms

- **Ashford Middle School safe room:** 300 people, entrance on the east side, opened by the school facilities director or BCEMA. Takes 20 minutes from the call to unlocked doors at night.
- **Corwin Community Center:** 300 people, opened by the Corwin city clerk or Fire District 3. Takes 15 minutes at night.
- Pets in carriers are allowed. No overnight sheltering; the safe rooms are for the duration of a warning.

## 3. Warning systems

- **Sirens:** 46 sirens in four zones (North 12, Central 16, South 10, East 8). Encoder at 911 Position 2, backup at the EOC. Polling reports battery and rotation faults; two North zone sirens (N-07 at Pine Hollow and N-11 at Clarks River bridge) failed the November test and are awaiting parts.
- **BrennanAlert:** about 14,200 subscribers; text, voice and email; voice delivery takes up to 12 minutes countywide.
- **IPAWS:** BCEMA is an alerting authority (county WEA and EAS, English and Spanish templates).
- **NOAA Weather Radio:** the Paducah transmitter covers the county; 800 radios were given to manufactured home residents and the care home in 2024.
- **Radio:** county VHF repeater for the spotter net (BCEMA SKYWARN net, 147.315 MHz); KYEM uses the Kentucky Emergency Warning System and state radio.

## 4. Emergency operations

- **EOC:** Brennan County Public Safety Building, 900 Industrial Parkway, Ashford. Positions for 14; generator; satellite phone; NWSChat, WebEOC (Kentucky's statewide instance) and BrennanAlert consoles.
- **Alternate EOC:** Corwin Fire Station 3.
- **911:** Brennan County Communications, six positions at night (two telecommunicators, one supervisor on the overnight shift). Fails over to Marshall County 911.

## 5. Utilities and roads

- Brennan Rural Electric Cooperative serves about 9,000 meters outside the cities; Ashford Municipal Utilities serves the city. Outage counts are reported to the EOC hourly.
- Cell service: three carriers; tower batteries last four to eight hours; one carrier has a cell on wheels staged in Paducah.
- Main routes: Interstate 69 through the east of the county, US 641, KY 80 and KY 94. The Clarks River bridges on KY 80 are the only routes into Corwin from Ashford.

## 6. Partners

- NWS Weather Forecast Office Paducah: warning-coordination meteorologist, NWSChat room "pahchat", EM hotline.
- Kentucky Emergency Management, Area 1 Manager; Kentucky State Police Post 1.
- McCracken, Graves, Marshall and Calloway County EMAs (neighbors and mutual aid).
- American Red Cross of Western Kentucky.
