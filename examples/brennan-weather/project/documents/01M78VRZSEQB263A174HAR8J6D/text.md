# BCEMA-SOP-02: Warning and Notification

Brennan County Emergency Management Agency · Brennan County Communications (911) · Revision 6

## 1. Purpose

This procedure tells the 911 telecommunicator and the BCEMA Warning Officer exactly how to warn the public of severe weather: outdoor sirens, BrennanAlert, county Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA) through IPAWS, social media and contact with the media. It carries out Annex H of the Emergency Operations Plan.

## 2. Sources of warning information

- **NWS Paducah products:** tornado warning (TOR), severe thunderstorm warning (SVR), severe weather statement (SVS), tornado and severe thunderstorm watches, and special weather statements. Products arrive on the NOAA Weather Wire receiver at the 911 console, NWSChat, the EAS receiver and NOAA Weather Radio.
- **Impact-based tags:** warnings carry tags. A tornado warning may say "RADAR INDICATED" or "OBSERVED", and a tornado damage threat of "CONSIDERABLE" (a particularly dangerous situation) or "CATASTROPHIC" (a tornado emergency). A severe thunderstorm warning may carry a damage threat of "DESTRUCTIVE" (winds 80 mph or more, or hail 2.75 inches or larger).
- **Spotters and the public:** reports go to NWS Paducah; they do not replace NWS warnings.

## 3. Outdoor warning sirens

The county's 46 sirens are in four zones: North (Corwin and the Clarks River bottoms, 12 sirens), Central (Ashford, 16), South (Hollis and the state line, 10) and East (Lake area, 8). Activation is from the siren encoder at Position 2 of the 911 center, with a backup encoder in the EOC.

| Situation | Who activates | What |
|---|---|---|
| NWS tornado warning polygon touches a zone | 911 telecommunicator on Position 2, without waiting | Zones inside the polygon, 3-minute steady tone; repeat every 10 minutes while the warning is in effect |
| Tornado reported by law enforcement, fire or trained spotter, before an NWS warning | 911 supervisor | Zone of the report and the zone downstream |
| Severe thunderstorm warning with DESTRUCTIVE tag or winds 80 mph or more | EM Director or Deputy | All zones |
| Test | BCEMA | First Wednesday of the month at 11:00, except during severe weather |

No "all clear" tone is sounded. Telecommunicators note each activation in the CAD event and the warning log (time, zones, reason). A siren that fails its polling check is listed in the log and reported to the Warning Officer.

## 4. BrennanAlert (mass notification)

BrennanAlert reaches about 14,200 subscribers by text, voice call and email; voice calls take up to 12 minutes to complete for the whole county. The Warning Officer sends:

1. When a tornado warning includes Brennan County: the zone, the NWS expiry time, "take shelter now", and the safe room status.
2. When a safe room opens or closes: location, time, pets policy (pets in carriers allowed).
3. After the storm: road closures, shelter locations, how to report damage (not by 911 unless there is an emergency).

Templates are in the BrennanAlert console under "WX". Messages are under 160 characters for texts; voice messages are recorded by the Warning Officer or use text-to-speech.

## 5. County WEA through IPAWS

BCEMA is an IPAWS alerting authority. The NWS sends WEA for tornado warnings itself, so the county never duplicates a tornado warning by WEA. The county may send its own WEA for:

- opening of community safe rooms during a night tornado watch;
- a shelter-in-place or evacuation order for a hazardous materials release caused by storm damage;
- road closures or a boil-water notice covering a large part of the county after the storm.

A county WEA needs the EM Director's or the Deputy's approval. The county sends IPAWS messages from its mass notification console. Messages are 360 characters in English and Spanish; the county's Spanish template set covers safe rooms and shelters.

## 6. Media and partners

- **Channel 6 StormTeam:** the duty broadcast meteorologist has the Warning Officer's direct line and is in the county's partner chat room. BCEMA tells Channel 6 when safe rooms open, when sirens fail and about damage it can confirm. The county does not ask the station to delay or withhold any NWS warning.
- **Radio:** Ashford 98.1 FM and the Purchase Area Radio Group carry county messages; the Lake area relies on Lake Country 103's emergency broadcasts.
- **Neighbors:** McCracken, Graves and Marshall County EMAs are told of siren activations near their borders by the partner chat room.

## 7. Failures and backups

- **Siren encoder failure at Position 2:** use the EOC encoder; if both fail, ask fire districts to sound station sirens and patrol with vehicle PA systems in manufactured home parks.
- **BrennanAlert outage:** post to social media and ask Channel 6 and radio partners to carry the message; ask NWS Paducah to include safe room information in a severe weather statement if timely.
- **Power or phone loss at the 911 center:** generator transfers automatically; calls fail over to Marshall County 911 per the mutual aid agreement.
- **NWS Paducah outage:** NWS Louisville provides backup warning service; products continue to arrive under Paducah's identifier.

## 8. Records

Keep the warning log, siren activation records, BrennanAlert and IPAWS message logs and NWSChat transcripts for three years. They are reviewed after each activation and given to the NWS storm survey and to the after-action report.
