# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Ghost Pressure** (SCADA vs. Field Gauges & Life Safety)
Location: Corwin Valley Gas Company, City of Corwin, Lake Haven County, Pennsylvania · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Command, Gas Control, Cybersecurity / OT-SCADA, Field Operations (M&R and Distribution), Corporate Security, Communications and Public Awareness

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Trust in the Data, Command Authority, and Public Trust During a Cyber-Physical Overpressure

**The Threat:** An unauthorized command to regulator setpoints PC-21 and PC-22 was executed via the OT jump host CVG-OT-JMP01 using the integrator account halden-support, resulting in a sustained overpressure condition in Pressure District 7 (10 in. w.c. nominal, 14 in. w.c. max). The SCADA system shows no change, but field gauges and district recorders confirm overpressure. The cyber-physical breach has created a false sense of normalcy in the control room while field conditions are dangerous.

**The Operational Impact:** Gas Control sees no alarms; SCADA shows 10 in. w.c. at RS-21 and RS-22. Field personnel report 1.1 psig (13.2 in. w.c.) at RS-22 outlet. PR-7B shows 15 in. w.c. and climbing. PR-7A is offline. Three high-flame and pilot-outage calls from downtown Corwin (Riverside Manor, Front Street Senior Residence, downtown businesses) trigger emergency response. Corwin Fire reports a basement fire on Canal Street. The nursing home reports a stove flare-up. Call center agents are overwhelmed. Field crews are dispatched with no clear authority. The gas control room is unaware of the true pressure, but field teams know something is wrong. The IC must decide whether to shut in a district with two nursing homes, schools, and 5,120 customers, all under 14 °F, while media report explosions that never occurred.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Trust in Data:** Demonstrate the ability to resolve conflicting pressure data between SCADA and field gauges by validating the field report and overriding the false SCADA display to initiate emergency isolation.
- **Command Authority:** Demonstrate the ability to authorize a district-wide shutdown of Pressure District 7 despite conflicting data and external pressure to maintain service, based on field evidence and life-safety risk.
- **Cyber-Physical Containment:** Demonstrate the ability to isolate the OT network from the business network and disable remote access via the jump host after identifying unauthorized commands from a compromised integrator account.
- **Public Trust Management:** Demonstrate the ability to issue a timely, accurate evacuation notice and media statement that acknowledges the overpressure risk while correcting false reports of explosions, to prevent panic and maintain public confidence.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **VP Gas Operations (Incident Commander) (IC):** Manages the overall incident response, activates the emergency plan, and approves major actions such as district shutdowns and mutual assistance.
- **Gas Control Manager (GCM) (GCM):** Owns system pressure, line pack, remote valve operations, and manual control if SCADA fails; responsible for the 1-hour NRC call.
- **Cybersecurity Coordinator (TSA) (CYB):** Owns the OT/SCADA system, CISA reporting, IT/OT isolation, and the incident response plan; responsible for declaring a cyber incident.
- **Field Operations Supervisor (M&R) (FOS):** Owns regulator station checks, manual valve closures, field gauges, and relights; manages servicepersons on odor calls.
- **Corporate Security Manager (SEC):** Owns the physical security of city-gate and compressor stations, and the law enforcement liaison.
- **Corporate Communications / Public Awareness Manager (PIO):** Owns media, evacuation and relight notices, and the smell-gas-act-fast messaging.
