## Two Intervals Behind — 988 Surge, Backup Routing & Safe Messaging

Location: Great Plains Crisis Network — contact floor and executive command room · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 01:00 PM (Exercise Time) · Weather: 4 °C and overcast in Lincoln, with a light northwest wind. It is a grey March afternoon with no precipitation forecast.

### 1. Situation Brief

It is **Thursday, 12 March 2026, 13:00** at the Great Plains Crisis Network contact floor in Lincoln, Nebraska. Day shift is back from lunch with **16 counselors on the floor (9 on calls, 7 on texts and chats)**. Both shift supervisors are at the raised desks, and **Priyanka Venkat** is the surge commander. Staffing follows Tamsin Reyes's normal Thursday forecast of about **520 calls, 140 texts and 90 chats a day**. The service level target is 90 percent answered within 120 seconds, measured every 15 minutes by contact type. The operations wall board shows OK on every queue. The early afternoon is a steady, ordinary stretch: callbacks are going out, coaches are reviewing contacts and admin staff are working their normal tasks. The evening shift lead, **Calvin Whitehorse**, is due at **14:00 with 14 counselors**. The wellbeing contact, Dr. Anneliese Park, is available, and the quiet room is open. *All material in this exercise is fictional. If anyone needs to step out at any time, they may.*

- Contact floor (Lincoln): GREEN: normal operations. 16 counselors are logged in (calls 9, texts/chats 5+2), and 2 supervisors are on shift with Priyanka Venkat as surge commander. Surge level: none.
- Telephony platform and secondary carrier route: GREEN: the primary platform is up and the secondary carrier route is on standby. No routing changes are active.
- Shared text and chat platform: GREEN: messages are delivering normally, and the 13:00 queues are within target.
- Operations wall board: GREEN: all queues read OK, and the board is refreshing on its normal interval.
- Backup routing (out-of-state centers and state backup center): GREEN: not in use. In-state contacts are handled locally with standard national rerouting only.
- Staffing, breaks and quiet room: GREEN: breaks are on schedule and the quiet room is available. The evening shift of 14 is confirmed for 14:00, and the on-call list is on file in the workforce tool and the paper binder.

**The Anomaly:** **13:20, at the supervisor desk.** Counselor **Joaquin Reale** leans over the rail and says to the surge commander: *"Pri, I've had three new texts land before I closed the last one. The board says we're fine?"*

The supervisor desk has two screens side by side:

- **Text/chat platform interval report (13:00–13:15).**
 - Texts: offered 14 (same interval last Thursday: 7). Answered within 120 s: 82%. Oldest waiting text: **6:00**, status WATCH.
 - Chats: normal.
- **Operations wall board.** Texts OK, oldest wait 1:40, 100% within 120 s.

Nothing else on the floor looks unusual.

**YOUR MISSION:** Maintain safe, timely answering of every call, text and chat reaching Great Plains Crisis Network while investigating and resolving whatever is driving the change in the queues. Balance speed against verification: act early on what the floor and the platform tell you without trading a working service for an unconfirmed fix. Protect the counselors who carry the load and the public's trust in the line.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- Start and end every radio, phone, chat or text exchange with "This is an exercise." Anyone overhearing a crisis line team talk about queues and backup could believe the 988 service is really failing.
- If a real emergency happens, including a real person in distress in this room or on the real floor, say "REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD." The exercise stops immediately so everyone can deal with it.
- The facilitator may announce "Time Jump: it is now [time]." Accept the new time and the conditions described, because a four-hour surge cannot be played minute by minute.
- Treat printed injects (platform reports, wall board screenshots, logs, texts, posts and reporter emails) as real intelligence and act on them. They are fictional and de-identified, so never dial a real helpline or number from them; use only the fictional numbers provided.
