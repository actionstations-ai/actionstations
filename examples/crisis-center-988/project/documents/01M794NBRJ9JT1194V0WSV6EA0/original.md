# Great Plains Crisis Network — Staffing, Facilities and Systems

Supporting description for GPCN-OPS-01 and GPCN-OPS-02 · Great Plains Crisis Network

## 1. Organization

GPCN began in 1998 as a local crisis line and became the state's 988 center in 2022. It is a nonprofit governed by a board of nine. It is based in an office building in Lincoln, Nebraska, on the second floor, and a secondary leased office in Kearney, Nebraska, holds 8 workstations for continuity. The Executive Director, Dr. Wilhelmina Strand, reports to the board. The Division of Behavioral Health provides state funding and a state 988 coordinator.

## 2. Staffing by shift

| Shift | Hours | Counselors | Supervisors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day | 06:00 to 14:00 | 16 | 2 |
| Evening | 14:00 to 22:00 | 14 | 2 |
| Night | 22:00 to 06:00 | 8 | 1 |

Counselors work 8-hour shifts with two 15-minute breaks and a 30-minute meal. Clinical coaches work days. Counselors hold a bachelor's degree or equivalent experience and 80 hours of training and shadowing before they take live contacts alone. Last year turnover was 28 percent.

## 3. The floor

The contact floor has 34 workstations and a quiet room with 4 chairs for counselors after a hard contact. The supervisors sit at two raised desks with the operations wall board. A conference room is the executive command room. There is a 24-hour security desk in the building lobby.

## 4. Systems

- **Telephony platform.** A hosted contact-center platform from a commercial vendor receives 988 calls through two carriers. Failover to the secondary carrier takes about 20 minutes. Call recordings are kept for 90 days, and the exercise records none.
- **Text and chat platform.** A web-based multichannel platform shared with other centers. Supervisors can see every queue.
- **Operations board.** A web dashboard that reads the platform report every 60 seconds.
- **Workforce management tool.** Forecasts and schedules; the call-in list lives here and in a paper binder.
- **Secure remote client.** Remote counselors use a company laptop and a virtual desktop with multi-factor sign-in.
- **Internal chat.** An operations channel, with a mobile client for the executives.
- **Public website and social account.** Run by the Communications Lead, Jerome Bell.
- **Employee e-mail.** The domain is gpcn.example.

## 6. Surge history

GPCN's highest day was 1.9 times normal volume after a national news event in 2025, and the service level fell to 72 percent for three hours. The lessons recorded: the backup request was made 95 minutes after the queue passed its threshold; the platform dashboard lagged by two intervals; and counselors reported not having a break for 5 hours. The plan was revised to set a 60-minute target for backup requests and the 3-hour break rule.

## 7. Funding and constraints

GPCN's budget is $6.8 million. Overtime and a surge fund of $85,000 are available. The Executive Director can approve spending up to $25,000 without the board. Staff may not be asked to work more than 12 hours or to answer a contact without a break on the clock.

## 8. Training and readiness

All counselors complete annual refresher training and two drills a year. Surge scripts are reviewed twice a year by clinical coaches. The call-in list is verified every quarter, and 11 of 38 were unreachable at the last test. The workforce coordinator is working to improve it.
