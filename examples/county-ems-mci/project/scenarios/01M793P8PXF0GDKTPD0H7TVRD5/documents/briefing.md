### 3. Rules of Engagement

- Begin and end every radio call, phone call and message, including anything on LARK DISP 1, CMD 2, TAC 3 or MED 1, with "This is an exercise", so that no one in the Communications Center or at a hospital mistakes exercise traffic for a real crash.
- If a real emergency happens to anyone in this room or on a real channel, say "REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD". The exercise stops at once so that real patients and real responders come first.
- The facilitator may announce "Time Jump: it is now [time]" to skip ahead. When this happens, accept the new clock and the situation described, so the exercise can cover the full four hours of the evening.
- Treat every printed inject as real information you would get on duty: CAD screens, radio logs, capacity board printouts, triage tags, photos, social media posts and news reports. Act on them, check them and challenge them as you would in a real incident.
