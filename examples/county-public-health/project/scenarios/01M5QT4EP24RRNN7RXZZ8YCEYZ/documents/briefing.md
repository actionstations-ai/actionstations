# Player Briefing Packet
## Vaccine Vault Breach — Data Integrity & Public Trust

Location: Marston County Health Department, Ellery, Marston County, Kansas · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 09:00 AM (Exercise Time) · Weather: Clear skies, mild morning temperatures

### 1. Situation Brief

It is a typical weekday morning at the Marston County Health Department. The Ellery Health Center, serving as headquarters for Health District 1, is bustling with staff preparing for the day’s routine operations. The North District Clinic in Brandt and the South District Clinic in Coyle are conducting scheduled immunization visits, while the West District Office in Lindell handles administrative and outreach tasks. The public health lab at the Ellery Health Center is running routine diagnostics, and the Departmental Operations Center (DOC) in Conference Room B is staffed by the PHEP Coordinator and Planning Section personnel. All clinics are operating under normal capacity, with vaccination appointments flowing smoothly through the SlotWise system. The IIS export file has been processed and verified earlier today. No incidents have been reported. The morning shift has just begun, and all systems are operating within standard parameters.

- Ellery Health Center (Headquarters): Normal / Green
- North District Clinic (Brandt): Normal / Green
- South District Clinic (Coyle): Normal / Green
- West District Office (Lindell): Normal / Green
- Public Health Lab BSL-3 Suite — HVAC and directional airflow: Normal / Green
- −80 °C freezers and autoclaves with alarms: Normal / Green
- Vaccine refrigerators with remote temperature monitoring: Normal / Green
- Access control and CCTV at lab and clinics: Normal / Green
- Temporary IT at POD sites (POD 1–6): Normal / Green
- IIS export and SlotWise interface engines: Normal / Green
- Public mood (social media sentiment and hotline volume): Normal / Green

**The Anomaly:** At 09:00 AM, a routine call comes in from Nurse Carla at the North District Clinic. She reports a discrepancy in a patient record: a 4-year-old named Eli Hernandez, from Brandt, appears in ClinicTrack with an MMR dose administered today. The clinic staff did not administer any shots today, the lot number (MMR-26-8834) is not in their current inventory log, and the record was created 45 minutes prior — but no visit was scheduled or recorded.

**YOUR MISSION:** Maintain uninterrupted access to essential public health services while investigating and resolving a potential data integrity breach that may have compromised patient vaccination records, contact tracing, and public trust, all under pressure from conflicting information and growing public concern.

### 2. Team Assignments & Standing Orders

| Team | Role | Standing Orders |
|---|---|---|
| **Team A: Operations (Command)**— Health Officer / Health Director (Incident Commander), Director of Environmental Health (Environmental Health) | You own the overall command and control of the response, including declaration of a local public health emergency, issuance of health orders, and coordination with the policy group. You are responsible for ensuring that all actions align with ICS structure, activation level, and the standing orders of all teams. | **Safety First: if the screen and the field disagree, trust the field.** **Truth Over Reassurance: when public trust is at stake, prioritize accuracy over speed in messaging.** **Authority of the Line: the Incident Commander's word overrides all conflicting internal guidance during a Level 2 or Level 1 activation.** |
| **Team B: Surveillance and Investigation**— Lead Epidemiologist / Communicable Disease Manager (Surveillance and Investigation), Informatics Lead / IT Director (IT and Informatics) | You own case definitions, contact tracing, outbreak declaration, and data release. You are responsible for verifying all case data against paper records and initiating investigations for any anomalies in case reporting or line list integrity. | **Case Integrity First: never assign a case to a contact without verifying the contact’s name, date of birth, and location via paper record.** **No Data Release Without Verification: all case data must be cross-checked against paper line lists and clinic logs before any public or partner release.** **Field Over Screen: if field staff report a case not in the system, accept it and initiate a manual investigation.** |
| **Team C: Environmental Health**— Director of Environmental Health (Environmental Health) | You own facility closures, drinking-water advisories, and food recalls. You are responsible for implementing immediate actions when public panic threatens community health, and for ensuring all decisions are formally approved by the Incident Commander before release. | **Environmental Precedence: if a facility closure is needed due to public panic, act immediately and report to the IC within 15 minutes.** **No Action Without IC: all facility closures must be approved by the IC before being announced.** **Paper Is Authority: if the EnviroTrack system is down, use paper inspection forms and log all actions in the DOC kit.** |
| **Team E: IT and Informatics**— Informatics Lead / IT Director (IT and Informatics) | You own local systems, state system accounts, interfaces, and network isolation with County IT. You are responsible for ensuring system integrity, data trust, and controlled restoration of critical IT functions. | **System Integrity Before Restoration: no system may be restored until County IT confirms it is clean and the owner has run the data-integrity check.** **Pause Before Proceed: if the IIS export or SlotWise shows anomalies, pause the interface immediately and notify the IC.** **No Vendor Access Without Approval: all vendor access must be approved via ticket and routed through the jump host.** |
| **Team F: Communications**— Public Information Officer (Communications) | You own HAN wording with the medical director, press, social media, rumor control, and translation. You are responsible for ensuring all public messaging is unified, timely, and legally vetted, especially under pressure from viral misinformation. | **One Voice: all public messaging must use approved HAN language and be cleared through the IC and Legal.** **Rumor Control First: when a viral post emerges, immediately activate the call center script and publish a 'What's True' statement within 30 minutes.** **No Speculation: never speculate on the cause of the outage or the validity of a data claim without IC and Legal approval.** |

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- All radio and phone communications must begin and end with the phrase 'This is an exercise' to prevent real-world panic.
- If a real emergency occurs during the exercise, immediately say 'REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD' and the exercise will stop.
- The facilitator may announce 'Time Jump: it is now [time]' to skip ahead in the scenario timeline.
- Printed injects (including posts, logs, photos, and reports) are to be treated as real intelligence and must be analyzed for context and impact.
