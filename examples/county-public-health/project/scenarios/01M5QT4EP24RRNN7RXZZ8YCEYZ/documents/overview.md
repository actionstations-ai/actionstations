# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Vaccine Vault Breach** (Data Integrity & Public Trust)
Location: Marston County Health Department, Ellery, Marston County, Kansas · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Command / Health Officer, Epidemiology and Disease Investigation, Environmental Health, Informatics and County IT, Public Information

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Trust in the Data, Public Trust, and Crowd Management

**The Threat:** Ransomware on the county network compromised the ClinicTrack EHR, Epi fax server, and SharePoint line list, and the IIS export file was altered to include 412 MMR doses with non-existent lot numbers. The attacker also used a phishing email to gain access to Jenna Fowler's account and bulk-cancelled 640 SlotWise appointments for a measles POD clinic in Brandt, triggering a viral social media post alleging data breach and clinic cancellation.

**The Operational Impact:** Clinic staff in Health District 2 are unable to access patient records or verify vaccine doses; contact tracers cannot update line lists or assign cases; the North District Clinic and POD 2 are operating in paper mode; the public is receiving conflicting messages via social media and local news, with parents calling the hotline demanding confirmation about their children's vaccination status; the Epi team cannot validate case data; the IIS export is paused; the public information team is under pressure to respond to a viral post claiming MCHD leaked children’s health data; the DOC is activated at Level 2, but paper fallbacks are incomplete and not all teams have access to the DOC kit.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Data Integrity Verification:** Demonstrate the ability to verify the integrity of immunization data and identify falsified entries in the IIS export before releasing any public statement or restarting the system.
- **Communication Consistency:** Demonstrate the ability to synchronize public messaging across social media, press, and hotline staff to prevent misinformation, using approved HAN language and a unified narrative.
- **Paper Mode Continuity:** Demonstrate the ability to sustain critical functions—case investigation, contact tracing, and vaccine administration—in paper mode with complete documentation and double-checks, ensuring no loss of accountability.
- **System Restoration Coordination:** Demonstrate the ability to coordinate the stepwise restoration of systems under County IT's guidance, including data-integrity checks and verification of the IIS catch-up file before resuming automated data flows.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Health Officer / Health Director (Incident Commander) (HO):** Manages the declaration of a local public health emergency, issues health orders, and oversees policy group decisions.
- **Lead Epidemiologist / Communicable Disease Manager (Surveillance and Investigation) (Epi):** Owns case definitions, contact tracing, outbreak declaration, and data release.
- **Informatics Lead / IT Director (IT and Informatics) (IT):** Owns local systems, state system accounts, interfaces, and network isolation with County IT.
- **Public Information Officer (Communications) (PIO):** Owns HAN wording with the medical director, press, social media, rumor control, and translation.
- **Director of Environmental Health (Environmental Health) (EH):** Owns restaurant and pool closures, drinking-water advisories, and food recalls.
