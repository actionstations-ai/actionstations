# Player Briefing Packet
## Hairline Crack — Centrifuge Failure, Exposure Clocks & Rumor

Location: Lindgren Life Sciences Building, Halvorsen State University, Marlow Falls campus, Iowa · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 09:15 AM (Exercise Time) · Weather: Mild autumn morning in Marlow Falls, Iowa: 54°F, clear skies, light wind.

### 1. Situation Brief

It is Tuesday, 13 October 2026, week 8 of the fall semester at Halvorsen State University. The research buildings are running a normal weekday load. About 40 people are working in the BSL-1 and BSL-2 laboratories of Lindgren Life Sciences. Graduate students and postdocs are working through mid-semester experiments, and several groups have booked runs on the shared equipment on the third floor. The ABSL-2 rooms in the Aldous Veterinary Research Annex are on routine husbandry. The Prairie Genomics Center cell culture suite is running under its usual 24-hour badge access. The Office of Environmental Health and Safety is fully staffed. The Biosafety Officer is at their desk (ext. 5-0142) and the Director of Research Compliance is in the office. University Communications is in its regular Tuesday planning meeting. The Institutional Biosafety Committee's next scheduled meeting is in two weeks. The Occupational Health Clinic on the ground floor of the Student Health Center is open its normal weekday hours, 7:30 to 17:00. The EHS portal shows no open incident files.

- Lindgren Life Sciences laboratories, including the shared centrifuge room on the 3rd floor: GREEN: open, normal operations, runs booked as scheduled
- Occupational Health Clinic (Student Health Center, ground floor): GREEN: open 7:30–17:00; Marlow Falls Regional Medical Center ED available after hours
- EHS portal (clock board, incident log, inventories): GREEN: online, single sign-on working, no open incident files or clocks
- Freezer monitoring (140 ultralow freezers reporting to Facilities operations center): GREEN: all sensors reporting, no alarms
- Badge access (Lindgren 3rd and 4th floors, Prairie Genomics Center): GREEN: logging normally, no exceptions
- HSUAlert mass notification and campus public mood: GREEN: no messages sent, nothing unusual on the campus forum or from student media

**The Anomaly:** **09:20, Tuesday 13 October 2026. Phone call, conferenced from the Lindgren third-floor corridor phone to the Principal Investigator and to the BSO at ext. 5-0142.**

Maya, lab manager of the bacterial genetics laboratory on Lindgren's third floor, calls with her PI on the line. A tube broke at about 09:02 during a run in the shared centrifuge room on three, which her group shares with the cell biology laboratory. The graduate student running it rinsed off at the eyewash and washed her hands as a precaution. She is now sitting in the group's office. She says she feels fine and would like to get back to her data. Maya says nobody has touched the centrifuge since, and asks the safety office what it needs from them next.

**YOUR MISSION:** Keep research running, protect people and keep public trust while you investigate and resolve the morning's report from the Lindgren third floor. Balance continuity against containment: the laboratories want their equipment, data and schedules back, but every exposure, notification and records clock must be verified, not assumed. Put the worker first, follow the stricter deadline, and say nothing in public that has not been checked.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- Start and end every phone call, radio call and message with "This is an exercise", so that no one outside this room mistakes a call about a Lindgren laboratory, the clinic or the campus forum for a real event.
- If a real emergency happens during the exercise, anyone may say "REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD". Play stops at once and everyone deals with the real situation, because real safety always comes before the exercise.
- The facilitator may announce "Time Jump: it is now [time]" to move the clock forward. Update your clocks, deadlines and the clinic's 17:00 closing to match, so the exercise can test decisions that come later in the day.
- Treat printed injects (calls, texts, forum posts, logs, photos and reports) as real intelligence and act on them as you would on the job, because the facilitator uses them to bring you the facts you are expected to find.
