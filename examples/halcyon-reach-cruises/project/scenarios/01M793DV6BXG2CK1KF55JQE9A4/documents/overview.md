# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Same Count, Every Channel** (Norovirus Thresholds, Reporting & Turnaround)
Location: MV Halcyon Dawn (Halcyon Reach Cruises), at sea between Cozumel, Mexico, and the Port of Galveston, Galveston, Galveston County, Texas · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Master (Captain), Chief Medical Officer, Hotel Director, VP Fleet Operations (shoreside crisis lead), Director of Public Health and Vessel Sanitation, VP Communications. The SimCell plays:
- the CDC VSP and the Houston quarantine station
- the port, the flag state, the laboratory and the terminal operator
- guests, a guest's family, reporters and travel agents

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Every channel uses one signed count, reporting clocks run against arrival time, and the decision on whether the next voyage can board on time.

**The Threat:** MV Halcyon Dawn sails from Cozumel at 18:00 on Thursday, February 18, 2027. Acute gastroenteritis is already spreading on board: the signed medical log shows 41 passenger cases (1.4 percent of 2,980) and 9 crew cases (0.8 percent of 1,120). Stool specimens from 12 guests are at a contracted lab ashore, with results due Friday at 14:00. No one caused this, but the cases keep rising. Passengers cross 2 percent at about 05:10 Friday (case 60). Crew cross 2 percent at 22:00 Friday (case 23). Passengers reach 3 percent, the outbreak threshold, at 08:30 Saturday (case 90). At 14:00 Friday the lab confirms norovirus GII, which changes no count and no report. A guest in her eighties with a cardiac history dies in the medical center at 15:00 Saturday, and the ship does not know the cause. The Galveston arrival at 07:00 Sunday sets the reporting clocks, and the VSP and the Houston quarantine station board on arrival. The outbreak then runs into the 3.5-hour turnaround for 2,980 booked guests.

**The Operational Impact:** The facilitator compresses the timeline: the 4-hour exercise covers Thursday 18:00 through the Sunday boarding decision.

**Passengers**
- Ill guests stay in their cabins until 48 hours after symptoms stop.
- Self-service stations close. Players must decide whether to cancel the show lounge matinee and the kids club shared craft, and whether Friday's formal-night gala buffet goes ahead with crew serving.

**Cabin stewards and housekeeping**
- About 40 stewards run dedicated meal-delivery routes to isolated cabins.
- Stewards cannot serve and clean at the same time, so cleaning public areas every 2 hours pulls them from cabin work.

**Medical center**
- The medical center has 20 beds and 3 isolation rooms. It keeps the signed log that every report relies on and manages the death and the cabin code.

**Guest Services and communications**
- At 09:00 Friday a passenger posts a video from the Lido deck claiming "300 sick" when the signed count is 61. A Houston TV desk calls, and the family hotline opens.
- After the death, a reporter asks whether it is linked to the outbreak.
- The guest letter, the hotline script, the media statement and the crew notice must all give the same signed count and time.

**Sunday turnaround (shoreside)**
- Last guest ashore is 10:30 and the next boarding is planned for 14:00, with 1,490 cabins and 380 stewards.
- A full terminal clean at chlorine 1,000 ppm cannot be finished in that window.
- The boarding options are:
 - board on time with an attestation
 - delay in 2-hour blocks
 - shorten or cancel the voyage
- The first guest message must go out at least 4 hours before the planned boarding time. Travel agents, the terminal operator at the Port of Galveston and the Galveston County Health District must also be told.

- HR-HSP-02: Medical Log, Case Cou… › 2. Persons on board at embarkation (Halcyon Dawn)
- HR-COM-03: Guest, Family, Travel… › 6. Social media and passenger posts
- HR-HSP-02: Medical Log, Case Cou… › 6. Other authorities
- HR-ORG-04: The Company, MV Halcy… › 4. The voyage in the exercise (Sunday, February 14 to Sunday, February 21, 2027)

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Use of Doctrine (Reporting Clocks):** Demonstrate the ability to file the 2 percent and 3 percent special reports, the 24-hour and 4-hour pre-arrival reports and the immediate death report to the CDC Quarantine Station for Houston from the signed medical log, each timed against the 07:00 Sunday Galveston arrival and confirmed by the Director of Public Health and Vessel Sanitation before it is sent, as HOPRPG 8 and HR-HSP-02 require.
- **Containment:** Demonstrate the ability to carry out the HOPRPG 5 stage actions as passengers and crew pass 1, 2 and 3 percent: closing self-service, deciding on the show lounge matinee, the kids club shared craft and the formal-night gala buffet, and splitting about 380 stewards between meal-delivery routes, cabin work and cleaning public areas every 2 hours.
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to give one signed count and time in the guest letter, family hotline script, media statement and crew notice, to answer the "300 sick" Lido video within 30 minutes with the count and no denial, and to answer the reporter's question about the death without guessing at the cause and only after the family has been told.
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to decide by 10:00 Sunday whether the next voyage boards at 14:00 with an attestation, is delayed in 2-hour blocks, or is shortened or canceled, and to notify the 2,980 booked guests, travel agents, the Galveston terminal operator and the Galveston County Health District as HR-COM-03 7 requires.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Master (Captain) (MSTR):** Owns the final on-board decisions on cancelling activities, the whole-ship outbreak announcement, the immediate report of the death to the quarantine station, and the reception of the VSP and quarantine station at the gangway on arrival.
- **Chief Medical Officer (CMO):** Owns the signed medical log and the case definition, isolation of cases, stool specimens, medical center capacity, care and records for the death, and the count behind every report.
- **Hotel Director (HOTEL):** Manages cleaning and disinfection, food and beverage controls, steward allocation, meal delivery to isolated cabins, guest services and the guest letter on board.
- **Vice President, Fleet Operations (shoreside crisis lead) (OPS):** Owns the shoreside crisis team, the 08:00 daily shoreside call, any itinerary change or early return, and the decision on boarding the next voyage in Galveston.
- **Director of Public Health and Vessel Sanitation (PH):** Owns the confirmation of every VSP and quarantine station report, coordination with the CDC, the port health authority and the flag state, and checks of passenger posts against the log.
- **Vice President, Communications (COMMS):** Owns the media statement, the family hotline script, social listening, the crew notice and the messages to the next voyage's guests and travel agents.
