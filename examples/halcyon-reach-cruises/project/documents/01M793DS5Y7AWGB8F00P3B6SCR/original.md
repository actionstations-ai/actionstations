# HR-ORG-04: The Company, MV Halcyon Dawn and the Galveston Turnaround

Halcyon Reach Cruises · Reference sheet · Revision 4

## 1. Company

Halcyon Reach Cruises is a mid-size cruise line headquartered in Galveston, Texas, with a fleet of two ships (MV Halcyon Dawn and MV Halcyon Tide) and about 2,200 employees ashore and at sea. The Dawn carries 2,980 passengers and 1,120 crew on seven-night western Caribbean voyages sailing round trip from Galveston every Sunday. The Tide sails from New Orleans. The line is privately held. The Dawn is registered in the Bahamas.

All times are Central. The Dawn's next voyage embarks guests at 14:00 on the same Sunday, giving a turnaround of about 3.5 hours from last guest ashore to first guest aboard. The turnaround normally covers provisioning (12 trucks), cabin cleaning (about 1,490 cabins in 3.5 hours by 380 stewards), the garbage and waste offload and the crew changeover. About 2,980 booked guests for the next voyage have been told that check-in opens at 13:00 at the cruise terminal.

- The local TV news desks in Galveston and Houston and a wire service.

## 6. What the ship can and cannot do

The ship can isolate up to 150 sick guests in cabins with food delivery by about 40 stewards in dedicated routes, can run full cleaning only with the cabin stewards' work (they cannot also serve and clean at the same time), has laundry capacity of 14 tons a day, and can sanitize with chlorine in a shipboard mixing station. It cannot test stool specimens on board. If guests who are well want to leave the ship at an intermediate port because of illness, the port may refuse to let them, and the line pays for their travel.
