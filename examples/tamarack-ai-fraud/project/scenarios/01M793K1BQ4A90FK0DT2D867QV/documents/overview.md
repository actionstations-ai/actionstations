# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Escrow Echo** (Deepfaked Executives, Wires & Public Trust)
Location: Tamarack Precision Components, Inc., Greenville, South Carolina · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: Treasury Manager, Controller, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Information Security Officer, General Counsel, Director of Corporate Communications

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Payment controls under executive pressure, verifying identity when the executive can't be reached, keeping fraud recovery and AI incident decisions separate, and public trust when a second deepfake reaches the press.

**The Threat:** On Tuesday, September 14, 2027, an outside fraudster who knows details of Tamarack's secret talks to buy a machining company in Querétaro sends a calendar invitation from an external address at 09:40 Eastern. At 10:05 Treasury Analyst Ines Okonkwo-Hart joins a video meeting on the company's video meeting service. In it, synthetic videos and cloned voices of CFO Marcus Delacroix-Hume and General Counsel Priscilla Aranguren tell her to wire a confidential "escrow deposit." The CFO boarded a flight at 09:10, before STARTEX. He has no Wi-Fi and can't take a callback. The fake "CFO" tells her to skip the callback and approves on video. A chat message from a lookalike account pushes the Controller to give the second approval. Four international wires totaling $2,640,000 are requested through the Treasury Workstation. Three are released. The fourth is put on HOLD after the real CFO calls the Controller at 14:05. At 16:20 a synthetic "CEO" clip announcing the acquisition and layoffs at the Spartanburg plant appears on social media.

**The Operational Impact:** Treasury and accounts payable staff see a routine-looking Workstation queue. The meeting-platform logs show the "CFO" joined as an external guest. Wires go out at 10:55 ($480,000), 11:40 ($1,150,000, which needs executive-level approval that the CFO never gave in the Workstation with his own token) and 13:15 ($620,000, before the 15:00 Eastern international cutoff). The Treasury Manager is away at a plant visit, and the real CFO is out of reach until he turns on his phone at 13:55. After the CISO declares the incident at 14:12, staff must preserve the recording, the invitation, the chat and the Workstation audit log. SynthCheck scores the video at 0.71 (6 percent false positive rate, 11 percent false negative rate), and the video has no Content Credentials, so the detector is only one piece of evidence. At 14:20 the bank fraud desk is asked to recall and freeze the funds, with the written recall due within 30 minutes. The beneficiary bank freezes only the $620,000 from the third wire. The first two wires' funds have already moved on. Staff must work against several clocks:
- the FBI IC3 report within 72 hours, plus a call to the field office about the Financial Fraud Kill Chain
- the insurer and broker within 24 hours
- the Audit Committee chair within 24 hours because the loss is over $1 million

The Controller signs the reconciliation: $2,640,000 requested, $2,250,000 released, $390,000 stopped and $620,000 frozen. At 16:20 a Greenville business reporter emails for comment, with a 17:00 deadline. Spartanburg employees are calling, and the acquisition counterparty and suppliers may be contacted by the same fraudsters. Communications must check with the CEO, issue a holding statement within 60 minutes, send a takedown request to the platform, and message employees before or alongside the public statement. Every figure must match the Controller's signed reconciliation.

**Core Plan Sections Tested:**
- TPC-FIN-04: Payment Authorizatio… › 2. Who may initiate, approve and release
- TEIVES › 3. Executives' own safeguards
- TPC-FIN-04: Payment Authorizatio… › 5. If a payment is suspected to be fraudulent
- TPC-SEC-02: AI and Synthetic Med… › 2. Definition of an AI incident
- TPC-SEC-02: AI and Synthetic Med… › 6. AI system incidents
- TEIVES › 6. Media

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Payment Control Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to apply the TPC-FIN-04 Section 3 callback rule under executive pressure by holding the fourth pending international wire in the Treasury Workstation, refusing to treat the video 'CFO' or a lookalike chat message as verification, and calling the absent CFO and Treasury Manager only on directory numbers.
- **Fraud Recovery:** Demonstrate the ability to get a complete written recall-and-freeze request (originating bank, amount, date, beneficiary account, reason) to the bank fraud desk within 30 minutes of the 14:20 call, and to track the FBI IC3, insurer and Audit Committee chair clocks against the Controller's signed reconciliation.
- **Use of Doctrine and Evidence:** Demonstrate the ability to declare and run a combined fraud and AI incident under TPC-SEC-02 Sections 2 and 3, preserving the meeting recording, external calendar invitation, chat and Workstation audit log, and presenting the SynthCheck 0.71 score with its 6 percent false positive and 11 percent false negative rates and the absent Content Credentials as one piece of evidence, not proof.
- **Public Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to issue a General Counsel-approved holding statement on the synthetic 'CEO' acquisition-and-layoffs clip before the Greenville reporter's 17:00 deadline, send a platform takedown request, and message Spartanburg employees no later than the public statement, using only figures signed by the Controller.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **Treasury Manager (Fraud and Treasury Lead) (TREAS):** Owns first approval of payments, the hold and recall of released wires, the callback discipline in Treasury, and the payment reconciliation records under TPC-SEC-02 Section 3.
- **Controller (CTRL):** Owns second approval of payments of $250,000 and above, executive approval with the CEO's delegate when the CFO is absent, and the signed reconciliation that every loss figure must match.
- **Chief Financial Officer (Finance Executive) (CFO):** Owns executive approval of payments of $1,000,000 and above, approval of loss figures and bank steps, and the notice to the Audit Committee chair.
- **Chief Information Security Officer (Incident Commander) (CISO):** Manages the declaration and technical response of the combined fraud and AI incident, evidence preservation, the takedown decision, and liaison with the FBI field office and the meeting-platform vendor.
- **General Counsel (LEGAL):** Owns legal hold, privilege, the FBI IC3 report, the insurer and broker notice, and approval of any statement that mentions a loss, fraud, an investigation or an AI system.
- **Director of Corporate Communications (COMMS):** Owns the holding statement, the employee and Spartanburg messages, scripted outreach to suppliers and the counterparty, social listening, and the takedown request for the synthetic 'CEO' clip.
