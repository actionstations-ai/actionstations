# TPC-REF-01: Company, Systems and People

Tamarack Precision Components, Inc. · Reference sheet · Revision 4

## 1. The company

Tamarack Precision Components, Inc. is a privately held manufacturer of precision fasteners and machined components for automotive and aerospace customers, headquartered in Greenville, South Carolina. It has about 3,100 employees and annual sales of about $640 million. Plants: Greenville and Spartanburg, South Carolina; Monterrey and Querétaro in Mexico; and Poznań, Poland. The corporate office on the Greenville campus houses finance, treasury, legal, IT and security, communications and the executive team. Business hours are 08:00 to 17:00 Eastern; the Monterrey and Querétaro plants are two hours behind Eastern daylight time.

## 3. Finance systems

- **Treasury Workstation:** a cloud treasury system for initiating, approving and releasing payments, with multi-factor tokens and a full audit log; connects to First Meridian Commercial Bank by secure link. Treasury Manager's approval limit $250,000; Controller's second approval above that; CFO at $1,000,000.
- **ERP:** supplier master, purchase orders and accounts payable. Vendor bank detail changes require a callback by Accounts Payable, logged in the ERP.
- **Payments calendar:** weekly steel and tooling payments to approved vendors (about $3.2 million each Thursday); payments to new beneficiaries are rare, perhaps 12 a month.
- **Banks:** First Meridian Commercial Bank (operating accounts, U.S. dollar wires, 800-555-0141 fraud line) and a Mexican bank for peso accounts. Typical daily wire volume is 25 to 40 payments.

## 4. Communications and security tools

- **Meeting platform:** Tamarack uses a commercial video meeting service. Meetings created inside the Tamarack tenant show authenticated participants; guests join as "external". The platform's admin logs show the invitation source and the joining address.
- **Security tooling:** an email security gateway, an endpoint protection platform and a security operations center (a managed service plus two in-house analysts). SynthCheck, a deepfake detector, was licensed in 2026 and is used by security staff on request; Content Credentials (C2PA) validation is available in the security analyst's browser.
- **AI systems in use:** a supplier-quality vision model on the Poznań and Spartanburg lines (Tamarack is a deployer; the model is supplied by a vendor), a procurement assistant that drafts purchase orders (internal, with human sign-off), and a policy chatbot for HR.

## 5. The acquisition cover story

Tamarack has in fact been discussing a small acquisition of a Mexican machining company with a bank advisor; knowledge of this is held by seven people. The strategy is public in the sense that trade media rumor Tamarack is seeking to expand in Mexico. A fraudster could claim "an escrow for the acquisition" and be plausible.
