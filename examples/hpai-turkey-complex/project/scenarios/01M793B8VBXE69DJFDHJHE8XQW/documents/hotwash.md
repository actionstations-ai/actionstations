# Hot Wash Guide
## Flyway

### Hot Wash Guide (Debrief)

**The "3-Up, 3-Down" Method:** Ask the room for three things that went well and three that broke or failed during the response. Go around the room one team at a time (county EOC, sheriff and checkpoints, cooperative, communications), write each item on the whiteboard in two columns, and don't debate items until every team has spoken.

**Key Discussion Questions for Flyway:**
- **The Gap:** At I-02, the Board asked you to confirm IP-1's address, barns and access roads by 09:30. The complex manager said the CR 12 drive was the only way in. The County Highway Engineer said loaded trucks had been using an ungated field lane off 230th Street. Whose answer did you send to the Board, when did you send it, and who put a checkpoint or gate on the 230th lane? Did you treat the engineer's reply as evidence that changed the quarantine order, or as a side note in an email thread? Under Annex K 3, the clocks started at 07:50. Which of them did you actually meet? _(Objective: Use of Doctrine)_
- **The Call:** At I-14 (10:15), the state EOC told you that tonight you would have 11 checkpoint sites and about 4 people. They also said a Guard request with a county declaration had to reach them by noon to get soldiers there around 18:00. Did you decide on the declaration and the Guard request before noon? If not, what stopped you? When the commissioners chair warned that reporters would ask for the farm name, who decided what could be said? Did you keep that call with the Board and the cooperative, or did the county end up answering it? _(Objective: Decision Making)_
- **Thin Lines:** At I-07b, Pell could send two deputies starting at 11:30, Ritter could send nobody until tomorrow, and the Pell deputies had never run an ag checkpoint. Who briefed them on what a Board permit looks like, where the cleaning and disinfection station is, and what PPE to wear? How did you staff the first four checkpoints by 10:50 without putting anyone on a shift longer than 10 hours? What would have had to be ready before this morning for you to fill the night shift? _(Objective: Logistics and Continuity)_
- **Before You Knew:** At I-05, a Facebook post with 84 shares claimed that every turkey within 10 miles was being killed and that eggs and turkey were unsafe. Meanwhile, a 60-vendor poultry swap meet was going ahead unless someone official said otherwise. What did you tell the public, and the fair board, while the result was still only presumptive? Who decided about the swap meet: the Board or the county? Did the county, the cooperative and the Board all use the same counts and control-area distance, or did each one put out its own numbers? _(Objective: Communication)_
