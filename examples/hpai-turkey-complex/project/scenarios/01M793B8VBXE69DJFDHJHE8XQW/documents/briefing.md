# Player Briefing Packet
## Flyway — HPAI Control Area, Checkpoints & Depopulation

Location: Calder County Emergency Operations Center, Training Room, Calder County Public Safety Building, 410 Elm Street, Larkin, Calder County, Minnesota · Exercise Duration: 4 Hours
Current Time: 07:50 AM (Exercise Time) · Weather: Cold, clear fall morning: 29°F at the Larkin airport, light northwest wind, no precipitation expected.

### 1. Situation Brief

It is Tuesday, October 20, 2026, in Calder County, Minnesota. Fall waterfowl migration is at its peak, and residents are posting photos of big flocks of geese and ducks on the sloughs north of Larkin. The county EOC in the Public Safety Building Training Room is dark: there are no active incidents, so nobody is on the incident command post, permit desk or checkpoint desk. No premises in the county is under quarantine. Highway crews are out on routine fall maintenance, and the Sheriff's day shift has six deputies on patrol. Calder County Emergency Management staff are at their normal desks with a normal Tuesday workload. In the turkey sector, the Prairie Gold Turkey Cooperative's Larkin feed mill is running its usual Tuesday delivery schedule. Its Dunmore processing plant (410 employees) is taking finished birds as planned, which keeps feed, shavings and livestock trucks busy on county roads. County Public Health, the Larkin Fire District and the Calder County Regional Landfill are all running normal weekday operations.

- County EOC (Public Safety Building Training Room): GREEN: dark, on standby, no active incidents
- Sheriff's Office patrol and county road network: GREEN: six deputies on day-shift patrol; highway crews on routine fall maintenance
- Prairie Gold Larkin feed mill and Dunmore processing plant: GREEN: normal Tuesday feed deliveries and processing schedule
- Larkin Fire District and Calder County Regional Landfill: GREEN: normal operations, no special requests
- County Public Health: GREEN: routine clinic and program schedule
- County phones, email and IT systems: GREEN: all lines and the shared inbox working

**The Anomaly:** **07:50 AM, Director's desk, Public Safety Building.** The Director's inbox has a routine weekly flock-health note from the Prairie Gold Turkey Cooperative. It says that on Monday, cooperative veterinarian Dr. Anders Whitfield collected samples from a turkey flock after its daily mortality ran above normal for two days and its water use dropped. The samples went to the Minnesota Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory, and the result is "pending." At the same moment, the Director's desk phone shows a missed call from the Minnesota Board of Animal Health duty line, with no voicemail left.

**YOUR MISSION:** Your mission is to keep county services, road traffic and normal agricultural commerce running while you investigate and resolve whatever this morning's reports turn out to be. You must balance continuity against containment and speed against verification. Act fast enough to meet any plan clocks, but do not get ahead of the agencies that own the decisions or of facts you have not confirmed.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

- Start and end every radio and phone message with "This is an exercise." This stops a dispatcher, farmer or reporter who overhears from taking it as a real event.
- If a real emergency happens in this building or to anyone in the room, say "REAL WORLD, REAL WORLD." The exercise stops at once so everyone can deal with the real event.
- The facilitator may announce "Time Jump: it is now [time]." When that happens, treat the new time as current and work from the situation as it stands at that hour.
- Treat printed injects (emails, phone call cards, social media posts, logs, photos and reports) as real information. Act on them as you would if they arrived on a normal working day.
