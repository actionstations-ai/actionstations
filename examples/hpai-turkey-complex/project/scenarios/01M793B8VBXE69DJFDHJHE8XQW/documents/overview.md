# Master Scenario Events List (MSEL)

**Flyway** (HPAI Control Area, Checkpoints & Depopulation)
Location: Calder County Emergency Operations Center, Training Room, Calder County Public Safety Building, 410 Elm Street, Larkin, Calder County, Minnesota · Duration: 4 Hours
Target Audience: County Emergency Management Director (Marcus Lindqvist), Deputy Director / Operations (Alina Petrova), Sheriff / Checkpoint Commander (Dale Rademacher), County Public Health Director (Naomi Eshleman), Public Information Officer (Colette Marsh) and the Prairie Gold Turkey Cooperative Biosecurity Manager (Gretchen Oyelaran). The SimCell plays the state, federal, laboratory, regulatory, industry, media and public parties.

## Scenario Overview

**Theme:** Beating the plan clocks that start at the presumptive result, supporting the state's animal health decisions without making them, moving unaffected birds and feed under permit, and keeping one set of numbers across the state, the cooperative and the county.

**The Threat:** Highly pathogenic avian influenza, carried into Calder County by wild waterfowl during the fall migration, has infected the Larkin Ridge Turkey Complex (IP-1; four barns, 62,000 tom turkeys). Before STARTEX, on Monday, Prairie Gold Turkey Cooperative veterinarian Dr. Anders Whitfield sampled the flock after two days of rising deaths and falling water use. At 07:50 on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, a cold, clear morning, the Minnesota Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory reports an H5 presumptive positive, and the Minnesota Board of Animal Health calls Director Marcus Lindqvist. The national reference laboratory has not yet confirmed the result. The outbreak threatens the county's other 47 commercial turkey premises, including two Prairie Gold premises that share IP-1's catch crew. It also threatens the Larkin feed mill inside the planned control area, the Dunmore processing plant outside it, and roughly 190 small flocks.

**The Operational Impact:** From 07:50 the county EOC in the Public Safety Building Training Room runs against the Annex K clocks. The Board's quarantine on IP-1 is due by 11:50 and the first four checkpoints must be staffed by 10:50. The full control area and all 11 checkpoints are due by hour 8, and the 24-hour depopulation goal arrives through injects that move the clock forward. The federal plan's planning values are a 3 km infected zone, a 7 km buffer and a 10 km surveillance zone, and the Minnesota plan governs. Players must decide whether to ask the Board's epidemiologists to widen the zone because of the shared catch crew. Sheriff Rademacher does not have enough deputies for the checkpoints, so he asks Pell and Ritter counties for mutual aid while the Director weighs a request for National Guard support. Barn 3 loses power during depopulation, so county generators and the Larkin Fire District water tanker go to the site. The incident management team chooses between foam and carbon dioxide while crews are short and capped at 10-hour shifts. Composting is not possible on the farm. The Calder County Regional Landfill can take 120 tons a day but needs Minnesota Pollution Control Agency approval, and after 12 hours a line of trucks forces loads to Pell County Landfill. Permit requests pile up against a 24-hour service target: feed for barns with 4 to 6 days left, a load of finished toms, a rendering truck and egg carriers. On 11 premises, market-age toms must move within 14 days, and an at-risk premises cannot be shown as monitored until the Board says so. County Public Health must reach 37 barn workers, some of them catch-crew members who do not speak English, for 10 days of monitoring and antivirals through the Minnesota Department of Health. A grocery distributor and an export broker call the cooperative before the state has published anything. Social media claims that eggs and turkey are unsafe. The fair board asks about Saturday's poultry swap meet, and the farm family needs contact the same day.

**Exercise Objectives:**
- **Use of Doctrine:** Demonstrate the ability to meet the Annex K 4 clocks from the 07:50 presumptive result: open the EOC at Level 2 within 60 minutes, confirm the IP-1 address, barn count and access roads before the Board's 11:50 quarantine, and staff the first four checkpoints by 10:50 and all 11 by hour 8.
- **Decision Making:** Demonstrate the ability to support the Minnesota Board of Animal Health's decisions without making them: send the shared catch-crew evidence to the Board's epidemiologists for a decision on widening the zone, route disputed permits through the permit coordinator under CCACMP 8, and decide on Pell and Ritter mutual aid, a National Guard request and a county emergency declaration under CDSDSL 10.
- **Logistics and Continuity:** Demonstrate the ability to keep IP-1 depopulation and disposal moving: send county generators and the Larkin Fire District tanker when Barn 3 loses power, staff crews on shifts of 10 hours or less, escalate when the landfill queue passes 12 hours, and help process feed, finished-tom, rendering and egg-carrier permits for unaffected premises within the 24-hour service target.
- **Communication:** Demonstrate the ability to keep one set of counts, dates and distances across the Board, the cooperative and the county by issuing the 12-hour producer and consumer notice with the state, answering the social media claims that eggs and turkey are unsafe, coordinating replies to the grocery distributor and export broker, and reaching all 37 barn workers in a language they understand.

## Roles & Responsibilities (For Students)

- **County Emergency Management Director (EMD):** Manages the county EOC as a supporting node to the Board and USDA APHIS unified command, serves as county liaison to the state, and owns escalation, mutual aid, National Guard and emergency declaration decisions.
- **Deputy Director / Operations (OPS):** Manages the EOC floor, the IP-1 site liaison, logistics for staging at the fairgrounds, generators, water, crew welfare and the permit desk seat alongside the Board's permit coordinator.
- **Sheriff (Checkpoint Commander) (SHF):** Manages the 11 control area checkpoints, traffic control on the boundary roads and security of IP-1, working with the County Highway Engineer.
- **County Public Health Director (CPH):** Manages follow-up of exposed workers, symptom checks at the staging area and antiviral prophylaxis with the Minnesota Department of Health for every person who entered the IP-1 barns.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO):** Manages county consumer and producer notices, rumor control and social media response in coordination with the Board's public information officer and the cooperative.
- **Prairie Gold Turkey Cooperative Biosecurity Manager (Industry Liaison) (PROD):** Owns cooperative biosecurity, access to IP-1 for depopulation, tracing of the shared catch crew, member permit requests and continuity of the feed mill and Dunmore plant from the EOC industry liaison seat.
