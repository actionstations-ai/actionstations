Action Stations is source-available under the [PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md). This page explains in plain words what that license allows for free and what needs a separate commercial license. If this page and `LICENSE.md` ever seem to disagree, `LICENSE.md` governs.

## Free: your own organization's internal use

Any person or organization (a company, public agency, utility, airport, school, hospital or nonprofit) may, at no cost:

- download, install and run Action Stations;
- read and change the source code for its own use;
- design and run tabletop exercises **for its own people**: staff, contractors working inside its own exercise program, and the members of its own organization (including organizations under common control, as `LICENSE.md` defines "your company");
- keep, use and share the exercise materials it makes that way inside the organization and with the outside agencies that take part in its own exercises.

## Needs a commercial license

These are not internal use, so they need a written commercial license from the licensor:

- **Consulting, training and facilitation for others.** Using Action Stations to design, run, facilitate, evaluate or deliver exercises, training or exercise materials for another organization, whether paid, bundled into other services, or offered free as part of winning other work.
- **Selling or redistributing the software**, changed or unchanged, including bundling it into another product or installer.
- **Hosting it for others**, for example as a web service, managed service or shared workstation offered to other organizations.
- **Publishing a modified version** for others to use.

Packs (template, prompt, domain, document and example bundles) carry their own licenses, chosen by each pack's author and shown in the pack's manifest. This page covers the Action Stations software only.

## Asking for a commercial license

Email **licensing@actionstations.ai** with your organization, what you want to do, and roughly how many people or exercises it involves. Commercial licenses are available for consultancies, training providers, integrators and resellers.

## Not sure?

Ask before you start; a short email usually settles it. A good rule of thumb: if the people taking part in the exercise work for your own organization, or join an exercise your organization runs, it is internal use. If you are running the exercise for someone else, it is not.
